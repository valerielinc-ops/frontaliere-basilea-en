Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • You develop, maintain and optimize the group-wide AML/CFT framework, including the relevant guidelines, standards and processes.
  • You will analyse regulatory developments in the field of AML/CTF in Switzerland and Europe and accompany their implementation within the Group.
  • You prepare group-wide money laundering and terrorist financing risk assessments and critically question local risk analyses.
  • You support our local subsidiaries with your expertise in complex and risky cases.
  • You monitor the design and effectiveness of the local AML/CTF frameworks and independently carry out controls (also on site).
  • You will prepare reports, management updates and decision templates for committees, Group management and Board of Directors.
  • You will work proactively into new topics and continuously expand your expertise through further training (e.g. participation in specialist conferences).

Requirements

  • Completed studies in law or economics with specialization in compliance, finance or risk management.
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of AML/CFT, ideally in the banking, insurance or financial services environment.
  • In-depth knowledge of the legal requirements in Switzerland and the EU (GwG, GwV-FINMA, R SRO-SVV, EU AML guidelines/regulations) as well as the FATF requirements.
  • Experience working with supervisory and regulatory authorities
  • Ideally, a recognised AML/Compliance certification (e.g. ACAMS).
  • Strong analytical skills as well as a structured, solution-oriented and self-responsible working method.
  • High communication and stakeholder management competence as well as the ability to convey complex regulatory requirements in an understandable way.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Other language skills, such as Spanish or Italian, are beneficial.

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
