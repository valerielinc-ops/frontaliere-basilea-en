Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 01.09.2025

Bethesda Spital combines cutting-edge medicine with first-class service and a unique ambience. With the five focus areas musculoskeletal system, women’s rehabilitation, pregnancy & childbirth and palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients a year benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

Clinic Rheumatology & Pain Medicine is the largest rheumatology clinic in northwestern Switzerland. From now on or by agreement we are looking for an assistant doctor. We are a continuing education clinic for rheumatology (cat B two years), pain medicine (SSIPM, 1 year), and internal medicine (cat D, 6 months). There is a close interdisciplinary cooperation with the university orthopaedics in-house, spinal surgery and the internal clinics for rehabilitation, internal medicine and neurology. The Clinic of Rheumatology and Pain Medicine actively participates in doctor-patient (AP) teaching at the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Basel.

Their tasks

  • Admission and care of inpatients of the clinic
  • Assistance and infiltration in sono- and C-arc-controlled musculoskeletal interventions
  • Treatment of outpatients in the ambulatory Rheumatism Pain (depending on the level of training and experience in the inpatient area)
  • Day, night, weekend and emergency services
  • Participation in the further education and training program of the clinic

They bring with them

  • Completed studies
  • At least 2 years of experience at a Swiss clinic (internal medicine and/or rheumatology)
  • Verified and recognized diploma by MEBEKO
  • Knowledge of German at least level C1 (Goethe Institute)
  • Enthusiasm and motivation for the profession and the joy of learning
  • Teamwork, flexibility and agility

We offer

  • Learning to diagnose and manage a wide range of inflammatory and degenerative rheumatoid pain problems, including imaging anatomy (X-rays, MRI, CT, sonography) and image-guided interventions on joints, soft tissues and spine
  • Mentoring and curriculum planning (cooperation/network of our clinic with the Rheumatological University Clinics Basel and Bern)
  • Multifaceted training opportunities
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with different disciplines

Are you interested in this exciting challenge? We look forward to your online application.
