Full description

To strengthen our team in the production area, we are looking for a dedicated and structured Training Leader (a). In this key role, you ensure a uniformly high level of training for our production employees and actively contribute to the sustainable development of competence in the cluster. They develop and coordinate training programs, accompany new employees when they join and support existing teams – always with a focus on quality, compliance and sustainability.
