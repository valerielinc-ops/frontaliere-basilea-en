The position Synthetic Molecules Technical Development (PTDC) is responsible for the development and production of Drug Substance (DS), Drug Product (DP), as well as the analytical development and quality control of our portfolio of synthetic molecules. PTDC works closely with important partners in research and early development (pRED / gRED) and global technical development (PTD). In PTDC, drugs are produced from synthetic molecules. We develop robust, cost-effective manufacturing processes and create knowledge through innovation and continuous improvement in a cooperative environment. The possibility The team of SHE Support & Compliance (PTDC-T) is responsible for safety, health and environmental protection in the areas of DS and DP. This role is crucial to promote and strengthen a robust, proactive safety culture in the production areas. The tasks are as follows:

  • Support of the SGU Chemist in compliance with safety arrangements and the control of general order and cleanliness in production and laboratory buildings.
  • implementation of various safety rounds and control in compliance with regulations and requirements.
  • Issue work permits and management of safety materials.
  • Support for event clarifications and active assistance in the event of accidents and events.
  • Implementation of safety training for employees.
  • Instruction, care and control of foreign traders on site.
