Profile

Due to developments in healthcare, we have become an indispensable part of healthcare provision. With our team of over 140 employees, we care for and support people of all age groups with a wide range of clinical conditions. One of our most important tasks is to enable the residents of the affiliated municipalities to live a dignified life in their own homes, whether in the case of a temporary acute need for support or in stable care situations. Meaningful work that offers variety and brings joy!
