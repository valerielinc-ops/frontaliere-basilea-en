Full description

Tasks

  • You will lead the research department strategically and operationally and take responsibility for the professional and human leadership of your team.
  • You will develop a forward-looking research and publication strategy and effective research management in close cooperation with universities and many other network partners.
  • Together with the head of the department, you will contribute to the establishment and establishment of Baselland Archaeology as a competence centre and research hub.
  • You lead and accompany research projects on findings and findings of all eras in the entire cantonal area as well as in the perimeter of the Roman city of Augusta Raurica.
  • You develop, implement and guarantee scientific standards within the Archaeology Baselland.
  • You actively supervise and promote young scientists, for example by accompanying final theses and supervising internships.
  • You coordinate and participate yourself in the communication of our research results through lectures, scientific publications or the preparation for publication via our communication channels.
  • You actively support the ongoing change process in the Department of Archaeology Baselland and contribute your professional research perspective to the further development of the organization and processes and structures.

Requirements

  • You have a completed doctorate in archaeology with proven research activity and a well-founded publication portfolio (including peer-reviewed articles).
  • You have already demonstrated your strong competence in the evaluation of findings and findings as well as the safe use of digital tools, especially GIS and databases, in your previous tasks.
  • You bring leadership experience in leading teams and projects, including budget and resource responsibility, as well as strong change expertise.
  • You have experience in the care and promotion of young scientists.
  • You have experience in communicating research results to both a scientific audience and a broad public.
  • You have the ability to prepare up-to-date research content for the benefit of specialists from museum education, and a strong willingness to support the Römerstadt Augusta Raurica in communicating archaeological topics to a broad population.
  • You are familiar with the Swiss archaeological research landscape as well as the local archaeological and historical sources.
  • You have the ability to actively and systematically develop and maintain cooperation and network relationships with external partners and institutions.

The "Archaeology Baselland" is a newly formed department of the Office for Culture of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. As a cantonal specialist, it is responsible for the protection and research of archaeological sites, zones and movable objects on the cantonal territory. The department is created within the framework of the organizational development of the Office of Culture: The archaeological departments and collections of the previous main departments "Archaeology and Museum Baselland" and "Augusta Raurica" will be included therein. organizationally combined.
