Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position Group Communications (GC) is responsible for creating a lasting positive image of Roche and represents the company's voice on a Group level: We bring clarity to complexity, strengthening Roche’s reputation, advancing business outcomes, and inspiring our people to deliver innovation for patients. The External Engagement team works across the entire Roche Group, including the Pharmaceuticals and Diagnostics Divisions.

As the Head of Executive Communications, you will report to and work closely with the Global Head of External Engagement, collaborate with the Head of Group Communications and members of the Communications Leadership Team (CLT) to help co-create the communication strategies for Roche’s executives, and to deliver an integrated Executive Communications Strategy for Roche as a whole. You will lead a team of Executive Communicators to develop and execute executive communication strategies, ensuring that our executives have an aligned and impactful voice to external and internal audiences around the globe.

The Head of Executive Communications leads and oversees the team responsible for the development and implementation of internal as well as external communications for Roche’s Executives worldwide. Executives at Roche are defined as the members of the Roche family represented on the Roche Board of Directors, the Chairman of the Board of Directors, members of the Corporate Executive Committee, the Head of Global Product Strategy and the Head of Pharma International.

The Opportunity In this role, you will make critical contributions to the overall vision, strategy and infrastructure of the team and are a strong team player and collaborator. Your key responsibilities and accountabilities will be to:

  • Provide direction and leadership to the team of executive communicators, including defining and setting the current and long-term vision, strategy, plans and objective. Recruit, coach and develop your team members, ensuring they fully utilise their potential and stay up to date with key industry trends, such as the usage of digital and AI technologies. Retain talent and set up long term succession plans as well as short and medium term capability and resource plans.
  • Represent External Engagement on various cross-functional teams and forums; providing communications subject-matter expertise and ongoing consultation on appropriate strategies and tactics.
  • Help break down silos across business areas and transfer/ leverage topics and learning across Roche.
  • Work closely with the CLT, especially the communication business partner teams to drive outcomes and vision, and to ensure robust development of industry-leading executive communications plans.
  • Act as a focal point for executive communications requests. Co-create executive communication strategies, including setting the strategic agenda for speaking engagements for Roche's executives.
  • Draft speeches, talking points, briefings, video scripts, letters, and internal messages for Roche's executives. Provide rehearsals for speeches and communications training, including Q&As and performance feedback.
  • Support the Global Head of External Engagement in drafting talking points for executive media interviews.
  • Contribute to quarterly business results reporting.
