Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for an intern in digitalization, optimization & standardization in work preparation special product production (m/f/d) in order to complement our team and to actively shape the digitalization and the continuous improvement of our processes in special product production. The aim of the internship is to analyze and optimize complex technical processes and to further develop them in the long term through digital solutions and automation.

What is this point about?

Do you want to actively promote digitalization in an industrial environment? Are you excited about analysing processes, evaluating data and deriving concrete improvements from them? Does it appeal to you to work together with different specialist areas on solutions that make everyday work more efficient?

In this role, you will have the opportunity to gain practical experience at the interface of production, data management and process optimization and directly contribute to the future viability of our special product production. You work closely with experts from work preparation, production and other departments and are part of a dedicated team.

What tasks will you take on?

You will

  • contribute to the standardization and optimization of master data processes and further develop uniform standards for data maintenance
  • Develop and improve methods and concepts for sustainable assurance of the quality and consistency of SAP production master data
  • analyse, document and identify potential for increasing efficiency
  • Evaluate and analyze data in order to check the quality of manual data maintenance and make potential for improvement visible
  • contribute to the further development of the process for handling special product production orders and actively shape future solutions
  • Develop and implement automations to simplify recurring tasks and make workflows more efficient

What do we expect from you?

You

  • complete a degree in the field of industrial engineering, computer science, data science, information technology, software engineering or in a comparable degree program
  • are interested in process optimization, data analysis and digital and automated solutions
  • work analytically and in a structured way and quickly acquire new topics
  • have a basic understanding of structured software development and common development processes
  • Practical experience is beneficial
  • communicate very well in German and ideally bring knowledge in SAP R/3
  • convinces through a sense of responsibility, a high willingness to learn, a quick understanding and an independent working method

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications that reach us by e-mail cannot be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
