Full description

As an automation engineer: in Pharma Engineering (m/f/d) you take care of existing and new automation systems, which are or will be integrated into the infrastructure of our customer. The process plants to be serviced have a very high degree of automation and are strongly networked with each other or with higher-level IT systems.

Start date: Dec 2026 or Jan 2027 End date: 12-month contract (with high chances of renewal) Workload: 100%, focus on on-site presence (min. 60% on-site) Place of work: Basel Work model: Staff rental

Your tasks

  • Operational engineering with the aim of ensuring the maintenance of production at all times
  • Continuous optimization of process plants and evaluation, testing and recommending new technologies
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary teams with internal and external employees and companies
  • Independent cooperation in automation projects in all project phases with responsibility for costs, deadlines, resources and technical solutions
  • Support for audits and inspections by internal bodies and authorities in the area of GMP and SGU
  • Ensure 24/7 support as part of a 24/7 support team

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training or studies as a technician / engineer in the field of automation, electrical engineering, technical computer science or similar
  • Mind. 3 years of professional experience in the automation environment, preferably with a pharmaceutical background
  • Very good user knowledge in the field of automation software from Siemens PCS7, S7, TIA, WinCC
  • Sound experience in the GMP and GAMP environment
  • Independence, initiative and integrity as well as very high willingness to provide services with a high sense of duty
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing are required, English skills are an advantage

Nice to have

  • High social skills, team skills and very good communication and coordination skills also in dealing with stakeholders and at all levels
  • Enthusiasm and self-responsible, customer-oriented working method
  • Questioning conventional thinking patterns and openness to new, creative ideas – even outside of the usual fields (“out of the box thinking”)
  • Ability to break down complex issues and communicate them to customers and stakeholders in adapted language
