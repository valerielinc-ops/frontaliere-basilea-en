Full description

Your remit

Tasks in the main area of Packaging & Labeling

  • Laser marking of implants and instruments (setting up, programming, labelling)
  • Conduct of optical checks
  • Independent final washing, packaging and labelling of Medartis products in the ISO7 cleanroom
  • Cleaning and disintegrating the cleanroom and furniture according to cleaning plan
  • Independent attachment of secondary packaging in controlled room
  • Maintenance, cleaning and maintenance work at the machining centres
  • Compliance with all process processes in and around the cleanroom to ensure product quality
  • Standby for piket and/or shift operations

Flexible support in other areas

  • Flexible support in other areas such as plate production, quality control, warehouses, etc., depending on current needs and operational priorities
  • Compliance with the specific safety, quality and GMP requirements, regardless of the specific sector of activity
  • Occasional, daily operations at the Umkirch site are also possible

Your qualifications

Requirements

  • Experience in the production environment, ideally in medical technology or in the quality environment
  • Careful, precise working methods and high quality awareness
  • High flexibility and willingness to work in different areas of responsibility
  • Teamwork skills and joy in cross-cutting cooperation
  • Reliability and a good understanding of specifications and documentation requirements
  • Flexibility in working time (e.g. shift or use times depending on the area) is required
  • Very good German in word and writing (B2), English is an advantage
