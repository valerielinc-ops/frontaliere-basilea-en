Full description

Intern / Internship Social Pedagogy FH (m/f/d) 60-80 % Child and adolescent psychiatry, acute and discharge station, Liestal On the Station for Eating Disorders and Crisis (AEK) with twelve beds we treat children and adolescents up to the completion of 17. Age of life is treated from the full spectrum of psychiatric diagnoses. Shooting is possible around the clock every day. The AEK is located in the child and adolescent psychiatry building on the Liestal campus. It is aimed at young patients for whom an outpatient therapy is insufficient or not possible, because their psychosocial life situation does not allow it or the symptoms are particularly severe, for example in the case of self- or external danger or if affected persons suffer from other diseases.

Their tasks

  • You will learn about inpatient child and adolescent psychiatry as well as the socio-pedagogical field of work of the Department of Eating Disorders and Crisis Intervention
  • They accompany children and adolescents in everyday life and support them according to the milieutherapeutic treatment concept
  • They take on initial reference personnel tasks and participate in the planning, implementation and evaluation of socio-pedagogical interventions
  • You will participate in interprofessional rapports, case meetings and core team meetings
  • You will get to know the cooperation with relatives as well as internal and external specialist bodies
  • You will gain insights into the tasks of social work, in particular networking, exit planning and cooperation with schools, authorities and social insurance companies
  • During the internship, you will be accompanied by a qualified practical trainee. Regular practical training sessions support your professional and personal development

Your profile

  • You complete the bachelor’s degree program Social Work at a university of applied sciences and are looking for an internship as part of your practical module
  • You are interested in working with children and adolescents in a psychiatric context
  • They meet people with appreciation, openness and empathy
  • They have initiative, a sense of responsibility and a strong willingness to learn and reflect
  • You enjoy working in the interprofessional team and are actively involved in the learning process
  • Ideally, you will have completed initial training or initial professional experience

Job opening: The job is to be filled on 1 February 2027.
