Full description

In order to support our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified specialists. To strengthen our mass spectrometry service team, we are looking for a dedicated Lab Technician in our analytical laboratory.

We are looking for

As a committed and motivated personality, you are part of our agile QC service unit. You can expect a variety of tasks in a dynamic and exciting environment. In this important position, you will perform GMP-compliant analyses of starting materials, intermediates and peptide and oligonucleotide active ingredients. An additional focus is on the independent review of analytical evaluations and results in the context of double checks.

Your responsibility

  • Analysis for release of starting materials, intermediate and end products by mass spectrometry
  • Conscious GMP-compliant documentation and evaluation of the analyses carried out
  • Perform independent double checks of raw analytical data, evaluations, calculations and results for completeness, plausibility and GMP conformity
  • Clear documentation of detected inconsistencies and coordination of necessary corrections with the responsible employees
  • Participation in the continuous improvement of analytical questions and work processes in the routine environment

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a chemical laboratory technician, specialisation in analytics or synthesis, or comparable training
  • Professional experience in an industrial laboratory
  • Knowledge of common IT applications (e.g. LIMS) as well as general software affinity
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • You are a team player and appreciate the cooperation in the team.
  • Reliable personality that contributes to a positive work environment
  • Enthusiasm for responsibility and initiative
  • Strong quality awareness and a very precise, structured and comprehensible working method
  • Professional experience in analytical chemistry and knowledge in instrumental analytics, such as chromatography (HPLC) or mass spectrometry (MS)
  • Experience with analytical data verification or with a formalized double check process in the GMP environment

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements and option to receive additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG and the possibility of a super-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners as well as discounts for public transport and Reka money
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
