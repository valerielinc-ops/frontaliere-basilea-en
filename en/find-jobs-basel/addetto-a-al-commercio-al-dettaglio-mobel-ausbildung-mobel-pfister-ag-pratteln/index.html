At Pfister, we have one vision: we want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 17 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at Pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • During your 3 years of training you will learn the courteous and competent advice of our customers in the furniture sector
  • In varied departments, trained specialists teach you target-oriented techniques in contact with our customers
  • You also acquire sound knowledge about our furniture range, as these form the basis for successful advice
  • You attend secondary or district school and write good grades
  • In addition, you are an open and winning person who likes to communicate with their fellow human beings.
  • You enjoy working in a team and are ready to learn from your more experienced colleagues
  • You use your flair for shapes and colors with passion to furnish rooms in style and mood.
