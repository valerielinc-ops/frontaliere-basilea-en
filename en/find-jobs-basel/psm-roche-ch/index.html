The Position Job Summary The Patient Solution Manager will play a crucial role in planning, executing, and closing projects, ensuring they are completed on time, within scope, and within budget. This role requires exceptional organizational, communication, and leadership skills.

Key Responsibilities

Define nation-wide and local (by different ecosystem circle) projects scope, goals, and deliverables. Develop comprehensive project plans, budgets, and timelines.

  • Build and lead cross-functional project teams. Set clear expectations, delegate tasks, and ensure team members are motivated and productive.
  • Allocate and manage project resources efficiently. Monitor resource utilization and adjust as needed.
  • Identify project risks and develop mitigation plans. Proactively address issues to keep projects on track.
  • Ensure that project deliverables meet quality standards. Conduct regular quality checks and reviews.
  • Maintain clear and open communication with stakeholders, team members, and senior management. Provide regular project status updates and reports.
  • Monitor project budgets and expenses. Control costs and seek opportunities for cost savings.
  • Manage changes in project scope, schedule, and costs. Assess the impact and communicate changes effectively.
  • Documentation: Maintain accurate project documentation, including project plans, schedules, and status reports.
  • Build strong relationships with KOLs or other key stakeholders. Ensure their needs and expectations are met throughout the project lifecycle.
