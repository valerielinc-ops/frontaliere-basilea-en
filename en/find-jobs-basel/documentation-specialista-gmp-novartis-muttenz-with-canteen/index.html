Job Description Summary #LI-onsite Location: Muttenz, Switzerland

Do you have an eye for detail, like to work in a structured way and would like to support production documentation in a GMP-regulated environment? Then you are exactly right with us.

As a Documentation Specialist, you are responsible for managing and handing over production documentation, coordinating document updates and the associated inputs and printouts from SAP. Care of the document life cycle and archiving are also part of your task.

Become part of a highly motivated team of experts working together in a dynamic, cross-functional environment. If you have a strong quality orientation, team spirit and experience in regulated organizations, we look forward to receiving your application.

The term of office shall be 18 months.

Job description

Main tasks

  • Preparation, printing and compilation of production documentation as well as handover to production staff in accordance with the specifications of the production plan
  • Track and coordinate the update of documents in production
  • Testing and compilation of batch documentation after production for timely delivery to quality assurance in the required quality
  • Implementation and verification of entries and transactions in the ERP system
  • Archiving of production documents

Minimum requirements

  • Administrative training
  • Experience in a production plant is an advantage
  • Excellent knowledge of Office Software
  • SAP skills
  • High degree of conscientiousness and care (documentation, order and cleanliness in the workplace)
  • team skills and strong team spirit
  • Resilience and ability to work under pressure
  • Knowledge of GMP, good documentation practice
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of English is desirable
