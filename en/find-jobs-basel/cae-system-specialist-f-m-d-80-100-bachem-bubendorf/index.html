Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our engineering services team, we are looking for a dedicated CAD & CAE Engineer (a) (80 -100%). In this position, you will be responsible for the planning and engineering of production and infrastructure facilities, coordinate the creation of 3D models and planning documents, accompany projects until successful implementation. In addition, we offer you the opportunity to develop into an AVEVA super-user and to play a central role in the long-term support and further development of our engineering platforms.

Their tasks

CAD / CAE Plant Planning

  • Planning and creation of 3D models for production, chemical and pharmaceutical plants as part of conversion and new construction projects
  • Creation and maintenance of planning and engineering documents such as R&I schemes, isometrics, parts lists and mounting drawings
  • Coordination and integration of all trades involved, in particular buildings, electrical, EMSR, ventilation and cleanroom technology
  • Support of projects across all phases, from planning to successful commissioning and implementation
  • Participation in the introduction, application and further development of BIM methods and digital planning processes

Perspective development to AVEVA Super-User

  • Support in the support and administration of the AVEVA engineering platforms as well as their continuous development
  • Contact for users in first- and second-level support as well as participation in training and training of new users
  • Maintenance and further development of templates, standards and databases as well as close cooperation with IT, engineering and external partners

Your profile

  • Completed training e.g. as a constructor in plant or building planning or comparable
  • Several years of professional experience in plant planning, process and/or building technology (preferably in the chemical / pharmaceutical environment)
  • In-depth knowledge of AVEVA CAD/CAE tools, experience in the role as key user, CAD administrator or super user of advantage
  • Several years of experience in CAD design (2D/3D)
  • High load capacity and a weakness for digital planning
  • Flowing knowledge of German, very good knowledge of English in word and writing

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
