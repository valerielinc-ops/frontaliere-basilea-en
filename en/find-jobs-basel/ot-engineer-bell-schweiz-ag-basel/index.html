Full description

Are you passionate about automation, industrial IT systems and modern production technologies? Then you have come to the right place! For our Operational Technology Engineering Team in Basel, we are looking for a committed professional to manage and further develop our production systems and actively contribute to the digitalisation of our production processes. In this role, you will work closely with the Technical, IT and Production departments and make an important contribution to the further development of our production sites.

We can only consider applicants who are legally entitled to work in Switzerland (EU/EFTA). We only consider direct applications.

  • Contract type: Permanent
  • Workload: 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible working hours
  • Start date: Immediately
  • Language: German
  • Work location: Bell Schweiz AG, Elsässerstrasse 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • Ensure the reliable operation of our OT systems in the production environment, particularly in the area of SCADA as well as MES and WMS systems
  • Configure, commission and further develop automation solutions, as well as provide support for technical faults in 3rd-level support
  • Develop automation concepts and feasibility studies to optimise and digitalise our production processes
  • Integrate new automation systems into the existing system landscape and conduct functional and integration tests
  • Participate in and assume responsibility for projects relating to Operational Technology, automation and Smart Factory
  • Evaluate suitable hardware, control systems and software solutions, and work closely with the Technical, IT and Production departments

Requirements

  • Completed technical vocational training, for example as an automation technician or in a comparable technical profession, with further training at HF/FH level, for example in automation, systems engineering or a related field, as well as a strong understanding of IT
  • Experience in automation or industrial IT, ideally in food production, logistics, process or industrial automation
  • Good knowledge of managing IT systems, particularly Windows environments, servers and networks
  • Experience in developing automation concepts and an understanding of the integration and communication of different systems
  • Knowledge of PLC programming and visualisation, for example with Siemens S7, TIA Portal or WinCC V7/V8, is an advantage
  • Very good German and good English skills, both spoken and written
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working, as well as enjoyment of collaborating with different specialist departments

What we offer

  • Work-life balance: A balanced work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 vacation days and transparent, electronic time recording. This allows you to flexibly compensate for any overtime with additional days off.
  • Catering: We place great importance on culinary offerings: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu including soup and salad, water & tea and free fruit, as well as high-quality products at heavily reduced prices, await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient access to public transport, attractive subsidies for tickets and ample parking in the car park.
  • Opportunities for professional development: Expand your knowledge through extensive continuing education opportunities, high-quality training at the Coop Bildungszentrum and diverse personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, an attendance bonus, discounted Reka-Checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness memberships – for health and fun in your free time!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our department event and strengthen team spirit at the company run – celebrating, getting active and making connections together!
  • Market-competitive salaries and social benefits: Secure your future with our above-average pension provision, receive anniversary bonuses, a Christmas bonus and attractive bonuses for employee referrals. You will also receive the Coop staff discount card, and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Arrange your working hours flexibly with part-time arrangements and experience an inclusive working environment in which diversity is valued and promoted!
