Full description

UKBB’s neurodevelopmental paediatrics accompanies children and adolescents with neurological, developmental and complex issues. Our interdisciplinary team works closely with medical, psychological and therapeutic professionals to ensure the best possible diagnosis and care. To support our dedicated team, we are looking for a reliable and experienced personality for the secretariat.

Entry date: as of January 1, 2027 or by arrangement

Their tasks

  • Comprehensive administrative support for medical and psychological specialists, including reporting, capacity and deployment planning, agenda management, correspondence, file publication and billing
  • Organization of courses, further education and symposia of senior doctors
  • Processing of insurance and IV inquiries as well as obtaining cost estimates / credit languages
  • Ensuring and continuously optimizing efficient administrative processes
  • Coordination of internal and external communication with specialist centres, institutions, parents and patients
  • General administrative activities of the Secretariat

Your profile

  • Completed commercial training, training as medical secretary or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in the administrative or medical environment
  • High organisational and communicative competence, initiative and a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Team-capable, resilient personality with strong social skills
  • Very good IT knowledge (MS Office); Experience with hospital software is advantageous

Our offer

  • A responsible, varied activity in a dynamic working environment
  • A collaborative, committed team with appreciative cooperation
  • A modern workplace in the border triangle
  • A supportive, participatory leadership culture
