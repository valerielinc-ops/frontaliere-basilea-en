Full description

Do you like to actively shape production and take responsibility for modern, automated plants? Then you are exactly right with us! Become part of a dedicated team focused on quality, precision and continuous improvement. You can expect varied tasks at automated and manual workstations.

Converter Casting mill (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Teaching, set-up, operator maintenance, repairing minor faults and producing automated punching, grinding and band sawing systems with ABB robots, including logging in and out of orders
  • Installing and optimizing new parts on the automated systems and adapting simple ISO programs based on existing templates
  • Setting up hand punching systems including logging on and off orders
  • Cooperation and support at hand plastering stations such as belt grinders and cutting grinders
  • Supporting shift leaders and ensuring a smooth production process

Your profile

  • Craftsmanship and ideally a basic mechanical training
  • Technical understanding and good understanding of program flows
  • Experience in handling automated systems as well as knowledge of ABB robots are beneficial
  • Secure handling of MS Office and knowledge in operational data acquisition
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Readiness for 2-shift work absolutely necessary
