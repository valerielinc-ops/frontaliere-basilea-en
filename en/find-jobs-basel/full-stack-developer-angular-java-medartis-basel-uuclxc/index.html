Full description

We are looking for a Full Stack Developer to join our Digital Solutions team in Basel and help develop and evolve a modern web platform for innovative applications in medical technology, with AI coding agents as a natural part of our development workflow. You will work across the entire software development lifecycle, from translating business and clinical requirements into technical solutions to architecture, full-stack development, testing and continuous improvement. Working closely with interdisciplinary teams, you will combine modern web technologies with sophisticated 3D applications.

Ready to shape the future of software development in medical technology? Then apply now as

Full Stack Developer Angular/Java

Your responsibilities

  • Design and develop secure, scalable and stable Java web APIs using Spring Boot.
  • Develop modern web applications using Angular, TypeScript and WebGL/3D technologies.
  • Translate business and clinical requirements into clear and practical technical solutions.
  • Make AI-assisted development work in practice: use AI coding agents in your daily work, review and validate what they produce, and build the guardrails, tests and review practices that keep AI-generated code secure and maintainable.
  • Contribute to system architecture and technical direction across complex distributed systems.
  • Ensure high standards of quality and security through unit testing, automated end-to-end testing and code reviews.
  • Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams and contribute to continuous improvement of our products and engineering practices.

Your qualifications

  • University degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science or comparable education.
  • Solid experience in Java, Spring Boot, JavaScript/TypeScript and modern web technologies such as HTML and CSS/SCSS.
  • Experience with Angular and ideally WebGL or other 3D technologies.
  • Hands-on experience with AI coding agents and LLM-based development tools is an advantage.
  • Experience with agile software development, e.g. Scrum, Test-Driven Development and Continuous Integration.
  • Experience in 3D voxel modelling or rendering algorithms is a plus.
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented mindset with the ability to translate clinical and business needs into practical technical solutions.
  • Strong Communication and Collaboration Skills in an Interdisciplinary Environment.
  • Fluent in English or German, both spoken and written.

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.

We look forward to your complete online application. Medartis
