Full description

Klinik Arlesheim is a leading centre for integrative medicine. For more than 100 years we have successfully combined conventional medicine with anthroposophical medicine. Medicine is complemented by comprehensive therapies and individual care. Inpatients serve around 90 patients in the fields of oncology, internal medicine and psychiatry. Our offer is complemented by an oncological day clinic and other outpatient offers such as GP medicine and nine specialist outpatient clinics. We are there for our patients around the clock, whether in our 24/7 emergency with walk-in and intermediate care (IMC/monitoring station) or through acute care at home (hospital at home). Connected to our pharmacies (both hospital and public pharmacies), we produce medicines according to GMP-small quantities guidelines in our own therapeutic laboratory. To complement our care team for inpatient oncology and specialized palliative care, we are now looking for a Dipl. Care professional 40-80%.

Their tasks

  • Professional and person-centered care and care of our patients in the field of specialized palliative care and integrative oncology
  • Organisation and implementation of diagnostic and therapeutic prescriptions, including administration of chemo- and immunotherapies, extended by external applications such as wrappers and rhythmic rubs according to Wegman/Hauschka
  • Cooperation in the care of learners and students in the department
  • Goal- and solution-oriented cooperation in an interdisciplinary treatment team
  • Use in 3-shift operation

They bring with them

You have completed vocational training as a dipl. Nurse HF and are open to our integrative treatment and therapy concept. In this sense, you are interested in Anthroposophical care and medicine. They have sensitive and appreciative treatment of patients and their relatives. You also attach importance to a team-oriented and interprofessional way of working. They are aware of their own role and have the ability to act and behave appropriately. Ideally, you will have experience in oncology care or palliative care.

We offer

We offer you an interesting, responsible and varied activity with exciting development and further education opportunities. We attach great importance to a pleasant working atmosphere. We live a family-friendly personnel policy and offer attractive employment conditions with at least five weeks holidays and an easy-to-reach place with excellent public transport connections. We look forward to meeting you. Only direct applications shall be considered for this call.
