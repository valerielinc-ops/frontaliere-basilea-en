Full description

Migros Take Away Liestal offers fresh and seasonal food every day. From a large selection, our guests can cater throughout the day. For our motivated team, we are looking for a service-oriented personality with pleasure in sales.

Mansioni

  • Customer service, debt collection, advice and active sales
  • Management of buffets and display cases as well as attractive presentation of goods
  • Preparation of coffee specialities, as well as other cold and hot drinks
  • Cleaning and cleaning work according to HACCP

Requirements

  • Professional experience desirable: in gastronomy / retail food / barista
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EBA): Gastronomy / Food Retailing
  • Flexible operational readiness from Monday to Sunday and on public holidays in different shifts from early morning to evening (05:00 - 21:30)
  • High team orientation and pleasure in sales
  • Fresh and exact working method
  • Know-how in the preparation of coffee specialities (barista or in service)
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Leisure and cultural activities: various discounted or free cultural activities
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost share of CHF 500.- to a fitness subscription per year
  • Food: Rich selection and discounted prices, food discount in M-Restaurants and TakeAways
