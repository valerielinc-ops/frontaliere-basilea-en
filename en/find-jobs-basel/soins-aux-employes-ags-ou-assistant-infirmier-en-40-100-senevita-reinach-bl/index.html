Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. In January 2026, the Senevita Birsgarten opened in Reinach /BL. We offer a total of 88 care places (22 protected department), 52 apartments for assisted living and a restaurant in an idyllic location with very good public transport connections. We are looking for agreement a motivated and team-oriented personalities as Employee care and care AGS or nursing assistant / in SRK 40-100 % For inpatient care with Inhouse Spitex Actively shape our new company and your team. You contribute significantly to introducing the processes to establish the services and quality at the highest level. You can expect a new and modern working environment, as well as the opportunity to shape and shape your team and the home for our residents in a sustainable way. Your tasks

  • Support for the implementation of nursing-care measures
  • Assistance in recording the maintenance effort / documentation using interRAI LTCF and RAI HC
  • Contact with relatives
  • Professional and individual implementation of care and primary care

  • Flexibility and teamwork
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (min. level B1)
  • Understanding and empathy for older people
