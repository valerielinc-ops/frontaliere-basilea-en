Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated scientist QC(a) (80 - 100%).

Their tasks

  • Independent analysis of starting materials for GMP synthesis and peptide- and oligonucleotide-based active ingredients
  • Evaluation, interpretation and GMP-compliant documentation of analysis data
  • Use of spectroscopic methods for quantification, characterisation and stability testing of samples
  • Development, optimization and implementation of analysis methods
  • Critical evaluation and plausibility checking of analytical results
  • Perform routine analyses in compliance with GMP guidelines
  • Self-responsible processing of project tasks and support of analytical devices
  • Ensuring a smooth measurement service and coordination of analysis orders
  • Preparation of development reports, SOPs and analysis instructions
  • Active support for analysis and development projects as well as continuous development of the working environment and processes

Your profile

  • Completed studies or doctorates in chemistry, biochemistry, analytical chemistry or a comparable discipline
  • Experience in the analytical investigation of peptides, proteins, oligonucleotides or related biomolecules
  • In-depth knowledge in NMR spectroscopy for the analysis and characterization of biomolecules
  • Ideally, additional expertise in further spectroscopic, biophysical or analytical methods
  • First professional or project experience in pharmaceutical analytics, biophysical characterization or method development of advantage
  • Experience in the GMP-regulated environment as well as in dealing with quality-relevant processes is desirable
  • Practical knowledge in techniques such as SEC-MALS, DLS, ThT assay, CD, SEM or rheometry is advantageous
  • Experience in the evaluation of analytical data as well as in dealing with corresponding specialist software
  • Structured, quality-conscious and independent working method combined with analytical thinking skills and pleasure in complex questions
  • Open, communication-strong and team-oriented personality with an interest in interdisciplinary cooperation and the active participation of a growing field

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Option to receive additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
