Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for an ICT System Engineer (f/m/d) to complement our team and actively shape our IT and OT landscape with innovative solutions and cross-divisional projects.

What is at stake here?

Do you love solving complex technical challenges? Do you enjoy not only operating systems, but actively developing them? Do you want to make a real contribution to the digital transformation with your work? Then you are exactly right with us! In this role, you actively shape the future of our industrial IT and OT environments and contribute your expertise to exciting projects that sustainably improve our production and processes.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Operation, maintenance and continuous development of OT environments.
  • Design and implementation of client engineering solutions with a focus on industry and production.
  • manage and implement cross-divisional projects for the optimization of IT and OT processes.
  • Plan and implement IT and OT security projects in accordance with current standards and compliance requirements.
  • Advise and support the departments at the 2nd and 3rd levels – including analysis and finding solutions for complex technical issues.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed training as a computer scientist or equivalent with at least 5 years of professional experience in the field of work.
  • bring several years of professional experience as a system administrator in the Microsoft environment.
  • have ITIL experience in a global service organization.
  • Work structured, analytical and solution-oriented.
  • communicate clearly and like to work in a team.
  • speak and write very well German and English.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support your work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We live family-friendly policies – including childcare and holiday care.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
