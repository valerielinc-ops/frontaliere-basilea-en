Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – that means more to us than simply completing tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards and have the courage to break new ground. With us you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and where your ideas, your initiative and your contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • You work in an agile team and drive the expansion of our central CRM solution (Salesforce) for Helvetia's sales network (field sales, outbound and direct sales).
  • You determine, structure and prioritize requirements from customers as well as internal stakeholders from sales and IT, define the product backlog based on this and develop the corresponding user stories, epics and concepts
  • As a product owner, you form the interface to the product management team, our sales organization and other product teams (e.g. offer routes)
  • You control the further development of the CRM platform along the sales processes and the CRM target vision, ensuring the quality of implementation and promoting the anchoring of the transformation in the organization
  • You will find the sweet spot between customer requests, internal interests, technical feasibility and innovation
  • Through sparring and coaching with other teammates in an interdisciplinary team, you promote internal knowledge development
  • You work collaboratively and across teams

Requirements

  • Study or higher education in business administration, business informatics or computer science
  • Several years of experience as a product owner in the CRM area or other applications is mandatory
  • Many years of professional experience in business engineering or business analysis/requirements engineering in an agile environment
  • Knowledge of the financial industry (especially in sales processes) and modern web applications is an advantage
  • You communicate convincingly, show initiative, are solution-oriented and have an independent and structured way of working
  • Change processes and learning new skills stimulate you and give you joy
  • You speak German fluently and have a good knowledge of English; knowledge of French is an advantage

We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions - for our customers as well as for us as a team.
