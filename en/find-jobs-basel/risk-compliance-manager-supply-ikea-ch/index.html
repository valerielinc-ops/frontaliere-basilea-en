Company description Hej, Do you want to go to work and help create a better life at home? With us, you can feel at home in your work while sharing that feeling with millions of people around the world. We encourage you to stand up for yourself and others, creating an environment where everyone feels welcome. We invite you to open new doors, embrace mistakes, and grow from them. As our Risk and Compliance Manager, you’ll have many opportunities to try things you’ve never done before.

Job description Your new job As a Risk and Compliance Manager , you’ll play an important part in the team and help the business grow with confidence, while protecting our people, assets, reputation and long-term value. Your primary focus will be to create an environment where leaders consider risk in their decisions, compliance is part of how we work, and safety, security and integrity are never up for negotiation. You’ll report to the Chief Financial Officer Supply. The impact of our work will resonate in homes and hearts across the world.

On a typical working day, you will

  • Protect our financial performance, reputation, people and assets by supporting business leaders in spotting and managing strategic and operational risks before they happen
  • Give business leaders clear, objective risk insights that support decisions on growth, investments, sourcing and market expansion
  • Build and mature our risk management, governance and compliance frameworks, so they meet legal requirements and still keep daily business simple and efficient
  • Help business leaders strengthen the resilience of sourcing, manufacturing, logistics and supplier networks against disruptions such as geopolitical events, climate-related risks and supplier failures
  • Lead global safety and security programs, business continuity and crisis management, so we are ready to respond and recover quickly
  • Promote ethical leadership and a strong speak-up culture among co-workers, suppliers and franchise partners
  • Partner with and coach business leaders, so risk awareness and accountability become part of everyday management
  • Keep improving our governance, risk and compliance work through benchmarking, technology and lessons learned, and make sure it stands up to internal and external scrutiny

Qualification What we’d like you to bring to the table You are motivated by making a meaningful business impact, protecting people and reputation, and leaving the organisation stronger than you found it. The role as a Risk and Compliance Manager requires you to:

  • Have a university degree and a professional accreditation in finance, audit, legal or compliance
  • Bring senior experience in risk, compliance, ethics and integrity, safety and security, or internal audit within an international organisation
  • Understand finance (P&L, balance sheet and cash flow) and how our business creates value, so you can tell acceptable commercial risk from unacceptable exposure
  • Know how sourcing, manufacturing, logistics and franchise operations work together, and how governance structures and steering documents support them
  • Be at home in a complex, multi-dimensional organisation, with legal entities, processes, business units and enabling functions
  • Communicate clearly in writing and in person, from Board presentations to workshop facilitation, and translate complex risks into practical business decisions
  • Influence without direct authority, with sound judgement, analytical thinking, integrity and calm under pressure

It’s a plus if you also

  • Have a background in finance, business unit, country or supply chain leadership
  • Have led crisis response or business continuity in a complex, international supply chain
  • Have worked with franchise partners or in a consumer-facing business
