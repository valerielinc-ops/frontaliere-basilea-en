Main responsibilities

  • /What to Expect
  • As a Consultant Laboratory Design Architecture (m/f/d) , you will take independent responsibility for the management, capacity allocation, and budgeting of global laboratory planning projects, as well as the further development and scaling of global laboratory planning tools. Your main responsibilities include:
  • You lead stakeholder workshops for the precise gathering and analysis of future customer and workflow requirements.
  • You develop holistic 2D and 3D laboratory layout plans using state-of-the-art CAD and BIM software (especially Revit).
  • You consistently integrate LEAN principles and sustainability aspects into customized spatial concepts and ensure compliance with international standards.
  • You support the co-development and further development of global, internal tools and actively drive innovation in ongoing projects, developing sustainable standards from them and successfully scaling them across our international affiliates.
  • You manage the external partner network and ensure quality as well as the formal sign-off of project milestones.
  • You visually prepare complex technical matters and confidently present intermediate and final concepts to international clients.
  • You design and lead worldwide on-site training sessions to actively share your knowledge of sustainable laboratory planning within the Roche community.
  • You continuously monitor technology trends and evaluate innovative software solutions for the laboratory planning of the future.

Key requirements

  • / What You Bring to the Table
  • You have a successfully completed degree (Bachelor/Master) in architecture, civil engineering, or a comparable field of study.
  • The following points round out your profile:
  • You possess several years of solid professional experience in laboratory planning, technical facility management, or the design of complex building structures.
  • You have deep knowledge of Revit, experience in 3D visualization, as well as sound, practical knowledge of BIM and LEAN methodologies.
  • You stand out with strong intercultural communication skills, fluent English (B2 level), and a willingness for global on-site deployments (Spanish skills are a plus).
  • You are characterized by a basic understanding of business administration for budget monitoring and a strong awareness of sustainable planning processes.
