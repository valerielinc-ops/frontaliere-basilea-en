Technical Support Specialist ( Slovak & Czech language)

Locations: Warsaw, Prague or Bratislava (Your choice!)

Are you ready to transition from technical expertise to life-changing impact? Our Regional Customer Support Center (RCSC) is the intellectual engine behind laboratory excellence in Central Europe. We are looking for a Czech and/or Slovak-speaking talent who is hungry for knowledge, enjoys problem-solving, and is ready to master the world’s most advanced diagnostic technologies.

At Roche, we offer a hybrid work model that combines flexibility with in-person collaboration. For now, we require our employees to be in our offices on average two days per week. The specific office days may vary depending on business needs, such as workshops, conferences, town halls, team meetings, and other collaborative events.

The Opportunity

More Than Just Support This isn't a typical helpdesk role. This is an engineering and application consultancy position where you become the "guardian" of diagnostic accuracy.

  • Master the Complexity: You will work with high-end IVD (In-Vitro Diagnostics) systems that combine robotics, complex biochemistry, and advanced IT.
  • Continuous Growth: We provide an intensive, world-class training program. We don’t expect you to know everything on day one, but we expect you to be the one who never stops asking "why?".
  • Agile Impact: Join a team where your proactivity isn't just welcomed – it's required. You will prevent issues before they happen, using digital twins and remote diagnostic tools.

Who You Are

  • Education: You have a degree in Engineering, IT, Biochemistry, Lab Diagnostics or equivalent. More importantly, you have a "detective’s mind" for troubleshooting.
  • Language Power: You are a native or C1/C2 speaker of Czech and/or Slovak . Your English is strong enough to lead you through international trainings and global technical forums.
  • The "Lab-to-Office" Bridge: If you’ve worked in a clinical lab and feel you’ve outgrown your current walls, we want your diagnostic logic. We will teach you the engineering; you bring the passion for science.
  • Resilience & Ambition: You are someone who sees a complex technical error not as a problem, but as a challenge to be conquered.

What Roche Offers You

  • Autonomy: A hybrid work model in Warsaw, Prague, or Bratislava.
  • Unmatched Portfolio: Access to technologies that are setting the standard for the next decade of healthcare.

The role is an 18 - month contract via an external agency.
