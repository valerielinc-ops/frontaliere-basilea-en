Full description

Our client is seeking an experienced Media Intelligence Manager to transform media, social media, and reputation data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making.

In this role, you will monitor and analyze developments across traditional and digital media channels, identify emerging issues and reputational risks, and provide timely intelligence to key stakeholders. You will play a critical role in evaluating communication effectiveness, assessing stakeholder narratives, and supporting informed decision-making in a dynamic international environment.

This position is ideal for professionals with a strong background in media intelligence, reputation analysis, and communications analytics who are passionate about turning complex information into meaningful business insights.

Key Facts

  • Position: Media Intelligence Manager (m/f/d)
  • Location: Basel, Switzerland
  • Workload: 100%
  • Working Model: Hybrid (up to 50% remote)
  • Contract Duration: 12 months
  • Extension: High likelihood of extension
  • Start Date: December 2026 or January 2027
  • Travel: Occasional travel within neighboring countries

Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver and quality-assure daily media reviews, alerts, and recurring intelligence reports
  • Monitor traditional and digital media channels to identify emerging issues, reputational risks, and opportunities
  • Provide timely intelligence assessments and research during sensitive situations and potential crisis events
  • Conduct media resonance and reputation analyses to evaluate communication impact and stakeholder perceptions
  • Analyze complex media and social datasets and translate findings into actionable recommendations
  • Collaborate with external media-monitoring and analytics providers to ensure high-quality service delivery
  • Support the publication and distribution of communications across digital channels
  • Identify opportunities to optimize intelligence processes through AI-enabled tools and automation solutions

Required Qualifications

  • Degree in Communications, Journalism, Political Science, Data Analytics, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in Media Intelligence, Media Monitoring, Communications Analytics, Reputation Analysis, or a comparable field
  • Strong understanding of the Swiss and German-language media landscape
  • Experience with media monitoring and social listening platforms such as Brandwatch, Talkwalker, Sprinklr, Unicepta, Factiva, Swissdox, or comparable tools
  • Proven ability to analyze complex datasets and convert findings into concise and actionable insights
  • Practical experience using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Large Language Model (LLM) tools for research, analysis, and process enhancement
  • Strong analytical and communication skills
  • Fluency in German and English, both written and spoken

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience within highly regulated industries
  • Experience in Corporate Communications, Corporate Affairs, Public Affairs, or Reputation Management environments
  • Experience supporting crisis communications and issue management activities

Interested? If you have a passion for media intelligence, reputation analysis, and data-driven decision-making, we look forward to receiving your application.
