Full description

Tasks

  • Development and further development of a clear product vision and strategy for the M365 environment, taking into account organizational requirements, market and technology trends and technical dependencies
  • Collection, analysis, prioritization and documentation of requirements and translation into feasible, economical solutions with high acceptance
  • Collaboration in the technical and technical conception of modern M365 solutions
  • Planning and implementation of roll-outs, with initial focus on SharePoint Online, as well as step-by-step development of the entire platform
  • Ensuring the technical and organizational integration of M365 into the existing IT landscape with particular attention to data protection and the handling of sensitive data
  • Transparent, target-group-oriented communication with internal and external stakeholders over the entire product life cycle
  • Professional leadership and close cooperation in an interdisciplinary, cross-functional team Requirements
  • Completed degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education
  • Several years of experience as a product owner, ideally in complex organizations or public administration
  • In-depth knowledge of the Microsoft M365 Cloud Platform beyond pure user knowledge
  • Very good understanding of the interaction of people, processes and systems
  • Strong communication and moderation skills as well as a high service orientation
  • Very good German skills in word and writing, English skills are advantageous
  • Product ownership training (e.g. Scrum PSPO) and/or project management (e.g. HERMES) are beneficial; MS-900 certification desirable The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton.
