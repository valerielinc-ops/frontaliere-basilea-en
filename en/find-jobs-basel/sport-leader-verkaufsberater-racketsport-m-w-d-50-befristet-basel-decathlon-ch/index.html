Full description

We are looking for reinforcements for our water/winter team in Basel! Accompany our customers through late summer on the water and start the preparations for the coming winter season with us. Share your passion for stand-up paddling, kayaking, skiing, snowboarding and the like. Your tasks: In the middle of it instead of just being there

  • Expert advice on water and winter sports: You are the expert for SUPs, neoprene, as well as for the first skis, snowboards and winter clothing. Whether you are a last-minute summer vacationer or an early bird for the slopes - you will advise our customers competently and on an equal footing.
  • Season change & technology: You support the quick change from the water sports range to the winter sports highlights. You have an eye for detail and ideally have a technical understanding of sports equipment.
  • Showroom management: You ensure that your departments always look great, take care of merchandising and optimal product availability during the transition to the season.
  • Omnichannel & circular economy: You don't just sell, you offer solutions - from test rental to click & collect to our second-life service for used sports equipment.
  • Logistics power: Receiving goods, booking in new winter goods and efficiently managing the shelves are part of your daily warm-up.
