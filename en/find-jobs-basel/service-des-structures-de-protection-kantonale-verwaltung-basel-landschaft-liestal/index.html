Office for Military and Civil Protection (AMB)

The Office for Military and Civil Protection (AMB) is an operational, training and administrative organisation. The AMB is an office with around 50 employees within the Security Directorate of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. The AMB is responsible for the missions of the Einsatzverband Bevölkerungsschutz, the training in civil protection as well as the crew and cadre in civil protection. Administrative activities include, among other things, military tasks on behalf of the Federation or the operation of the Liestal Barracks. Furthermore, the AMB prepares operational and precautionary planning as well as logistics and administration services.
