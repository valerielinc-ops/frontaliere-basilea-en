Group Controller & Treasury Manager

Location: Basel, Switzerland On-site: Monday to Friday

About the Role

We are looking for a Group Controller & Treasury Manager to lead our Finance operations and ensure the smooth running of all controlling and treasury activities at group level. This is a key, hands-on leadership role that combines financial control, cash management, and business partnering.The ideal candidate is a highly analytical and business-minded finance professional with strong treasury knowledge and proven experience managing a small finance team in an international retail environment.

Your

  • Responsibilities

· - Oversee all financial controlling activities, including budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, and management reporting. · - Lead treasury operations, ensuring effective cash flow management, liquidity planning, and bank relationships. · - Prepare consolidated group financial statements and monthly reporting packages. · - Monitor KPIs and support management with data-driven insights to guide strategic decisions. · - Manage and coach a small team within the finance department. · - Partner with cross-functional teams to ensure financial accuracy, efficiency, and compliance. · - Develop and maintain internal controls and financial processes to safeguard company assets. · - Coordinate with external auditors, tax advisors, and financial institutions. · - Contribute to strategic planning by providing actionable financial analysis and business recommendations.

  • Profile

· - Proven experience in controlling and treasury within an international company (ideally retail or fashion). · - Strong understanding of financial reporting, liquidity management, and forecasting. · - Excellent command of accounting principles (Swiss GAAP / IFRS a plus). · - Strategic mindset with a hands-on approach. · - Strong leadership, communication, and influencing skills. · - Highly organized, analytical, and detail-oriented. · - Advanced Excel skills and experience with ERP systems (SAP) · - Fluent in English (German or French a plus).

What We Offer

· - A key leadership role in a dynamic, international fashion company. · - Direct impact on financial performance and strategic direction. · - An empowering and collaborative culture in our Basel headquarters. · - Competitive compensation and benefits package.
