The Position Welcome to the DOEA External Manufacturing Product & Project Leadership team, a vital part of our Operational Excellence organization. We operate as a dynamic network collaborating closely with associated product squads, MSAT, Portfolio Management, Procurement, Supply Chain, and Quality. Our department focuses on driving strategic alignment, elevating operational performance, and ensuring seamless external manufacturing and supply chain processes. By bridging the gap between strategic organizational goals and actionable product initiatives, we guarantee the highest standards of quality and efficiency across complex, high-level global manufacturing ecosystems. As the External Manufacturing - Technical Product Leadership Lead - Instruments & Devices (DC) ( Technical Product Leadership Lead), you will serve as the primary disciplinary and professional expert for our Product Leaders, directly managing a team of approximately seven subject matter experts. In this critical leadership role, you will guide portfolio alignment, standardized governance, and the continuous optimization of product management practices. You will drive organizational change initiatives, support strategic investment decisions, and introduce innovative product management technologies. Ultimately, you are tasked with elevating quality and efficiency standards across the entire product portfolio while cultivating robust internal and external stakeholder relationships and fostering a culture of strategic accountability and cross-product collaboration. The Opportunity

  • Lead the harmonized execution of product strategy and governance, standardizing product lifecycle practices, defining portfolio-level KPIs, and ensuring operational and regulatory compliance across all products.
  • Translate organizational strategy into measurable, multi-product portfolio objectives, including rolling out the Network strategy for Product Leads to ensure squad prioritization fully aligns with overarching goals.
  • Direct the comprehensive talent lifecycle for a team of Product Leaders—including recruitment, performance management, coaching, mentoring, and addressing skill gaps—while fostering an engaging work environment that maximizes individual and collective potential.
  • Support resource allocation by analyzing Product Leader capacity and portfolio efficiency, ensuring the right talent and investments are applied to product initiatives across associated squads.
  • Translate high-level organizational requirements (such as CAPEX reductions, SMC cost reductions, and impairment reductions) into actionable items for MSAT and squads, while managing critical interfaces between the Network and supporting functions like OBPM and System Development.
  • Take end-to-end accountability for external manufacturing operations (including injection molding, automated assembly, high-level instrument assembly, reagent fill/finish, and packaging), defining processes for technical supplier evaluation, supply vulnerability analysis, and capacity utilization.
  • Monitor operational performance against standardized KPIs, set targets for technical savings and issue resolution, and provide robust technical oversight and escalation management in partnership with MSAT, Quality, and suppliers.

Who You Are

  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree in a relevant technical or business field (a Master’s or PhD in a technical discipline is highly preferred) and possess fluent English communication skills, along with the flexibility for global travel.
