  • We aim to activate data citizenship and digital mindset, revolutionize our FAIR data ecosystem and systems landscape, re-design excellent processes, and generate transformative insights. The Opportunity
  • You will be responsible for driving the digital technology strategy through the delivery of key digital initiatives and use cases in the Quality domain and you take the role of an Agile Product Owner (PO) to partner with customers across PT functions to architect the planning (e.g. diagnose), execution (e.g. design & build), and sustainment of digital solutions to achieve the PT Digital Aspirations.
  • Strategic Digital Transformation: Architect key initiatives and act as a digital translator between business needs and technical roles by navigating across both domains seamlessly.

  • Enable significant improvements in quality management processes through innovative digital solutions, confirmed by measuring key business metrics.
  • Requirements Analysis: Utilize your broad end-to-end Quality business understanding to perform detailed requirement analyses with the team, with a strong focus on balancing process requirements, stakeholder requirements with digital product simplicity, network scalability and sustainability.
  • Prioritization and Team Setup: Conduct use case prioritization and assume the Product Owner role to catalyze the implementation of key digital use cases.
  • Coach teams that are developing and deploying digital use cases, e.g. share best practices, agile ways of working, backlog management, sprint planning and ensure full impact capture & coordination between different initiatives.
