Events Manager — Medartis

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

To strengthen our Global Events Team in Basel, we are looking for an organizationally strong and committed personality as an Events Manager. In this role, you plan and organize our appearances at international congresses, Medartis courses as well as IBRA symposia and workshops - from conception to implementation on site to follow-up. As a contact person for your regions, you work closely with sales, marketing, product management and education as well as with external service providers and speakers and ensure that our events are implemented professionally, efficiently and compliant.

Events Manager

Your responsibilities

  • You plan and organize our congress appearances – from the stand concept, schedule and budget to stand equipment and logistics to follow-up, including success monitoring.
  • You collect offers, coordinate external service providers such as trade fair builders, technicians and logistics partners and negotiate sponsorship packages with conference organisers (PCOs) and clinics.
  • You organize Medartis courses as well as IBRA symposia, courses and workshops, including premises, infrastructure, catering, supporting program and course materials.
  • You are responsible for participant and speaker management – from registration to travel and accommodation to billing and certificates.
  • You are the central contact person on site, coordinate the processes and find pragmatic solutions for short-term changes.
  • You will organize hybrid and virtual formats such as webinars, live streams and live surgery broadcasts as well as other events such as HQ visits and sales events.
  • You keep an eye on budgets and dates, check invoices and maintain the event data in our systems as well as the event agenda on the website and intranet.
  • You manage the regions assigned to you (subsidiaries and distributors) and ensure compliance with our quality and compliance requirements.

Your qualifications

  • Basic commercial training completed
  • Further training in event or congress management is an advantage.
  • Proven experience in the organization and implementation of events, ideally in congress organization.
  • Experience with hybrid and virtual event formats as well as hands-on workshops or wet labs is an advantage.
  • Experience in contract negotiations with hotels, locations and congress organisers as well as in risk and emergency management on site.
  • Experience in the medical technology or healthcare industry as well as knowledge of compliance requirements in dealing with healthcare professionals are beneficial.
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Experience with Cvent, SharePoint and CRM systems such as Salesforce is beneficial.
  • Organizational, service- and solution-oriented personality with negotiating skills, confident appearance also under time pressure and enjoyment of cooperation in an international environment.
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing, French is an advantage, as well as willingness to travel

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.

We look forward to your complete online application. Medartis
