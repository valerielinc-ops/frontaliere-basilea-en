Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the migration of existing individual tariffs into a new inventory and management system and ensure professional correctness and tariff consistency
  • You will analyze existing tariff landscapes, identify potential for optimization and develop sustainable tariff architectures
  • You are actively involved in the design, development and introduction of new life insurance products
  • You ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and take into account relevant legal and supervisory requirements in product design and tariff development
  • You take on co-responsibility in the management of a sub-project and support in planning, coordination, control and progress monitoring

Requirements

  • You have a completed degree in actuarial mathematics, mathematics, mathematical statistics or a comparable quantitative discipline
  • You are an actuary SAV or have an equivalent recognition of a foreign actuary association or an equivalent level of knowledge
  • You bring several years of professional experience in pricing individual products
  • You have in-depth knowledge of product design and tariff calculation of life insurance products
  • You have experience in stochastic modeling as well as in the development and application of pricing models and are familiar with tariff architectures and their implementation in management systems
  • Ideally, you will bring practical experience in the migration of tariffs and stocks
  • You have experience in the management or co-responsibility of sub-projects and convince through structured working methods and high implementation strength
  • You preferably have programming skills in Python
  • Knowledge of R and SQL is also welcome
  • You communicate stylishly in German (native language or comparable level), have good English skills in words and writing and in the best case bring Spanish skills

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
