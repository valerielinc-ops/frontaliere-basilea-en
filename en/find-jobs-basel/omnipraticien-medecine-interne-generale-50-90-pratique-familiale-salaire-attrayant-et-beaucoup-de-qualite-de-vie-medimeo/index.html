You would like to work independently as a family doctor, but at the same time appreciate the exchange with experienced colleagues? Are you looking for a family practice where you can personally accompany your patients instead of working in a large anonymous structure? And you prefer a quieter residential region or would like to continue living in Germany and commute to Switzerland? Then this established group practice in the Fricktal could be just right for you! Also for specialists from Germany, the border location offers an interesting perspective. In the heart of the Fricktal, not far from the German border, you can expect an owner-managed family practice with a well-established team and a long-standing, colourfully mixed patient base. Currently, three doctors work together in different part-time periods. They are supported by six medical practice assistants and one learner. The practice has three consultation rooms, an additional treatment room, its own laboratory and an X-ray system. The purchase of an ultrasound device is planned. Medical care is broadly based. In addition to classical family medicine, manual medicine and complementary treatment methods are also on offer. There is enough room for personal focuses and an independent working method. The region also has a lot to offer: Instead of big city hustle and bustle, you expect a natural environment, manageable structures and a high quality of life. Thanks to the proximity to the German border, the practice is also interesting for cross-border workers.

Their tasks

  • Independent family care of a versatile, regional patient base
  • Diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic diseases
  • Long-term support of patients in different life situations
  • Close cooperation and professional exchange with medical colleagues
  • Possibility to contribute your own medical interests and additional qualifications
  • If interested in participating in the further development of the practical offer

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Enjoyment of a broad, patient-oriented family medicine
  • Self-employed, responsible and collegial working methods
  • Interested in long-term work in a family practice environment
  • Openness to different medical approaches
  • Applications from Germany are also welcome if the requirements for an outpatient activity in Switzerland are met

Their advantages

  • Attractive remuneration: basic salary from around CHF 160'000 at 100% with additional revenue share; depending on experience and performance, a higher income is possible
  • Benefit-based wage model: 40-45% turnover share on medical services
  • Flexible workload: Between 50 and 90%, adapted to your personal life situation
  • Five weeks of holidays: With uncomplicated and flexible holiday planning
  • Supported training: Practice covers training costs by agreement
  • Family working environment: Owner-managed practice with short decision-making paths and personal cooperation
  • Good infrastructure: own laboratory, X-rays and planned extension of ultrasound diagnostics
  • Close to the border: interesting for doctors who want to continue living in Germany
  • High quality of life: Natural region with diverse leisure opportunities away from the big city
