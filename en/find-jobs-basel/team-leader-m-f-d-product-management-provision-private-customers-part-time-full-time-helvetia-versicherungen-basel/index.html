Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Tasks

  • Personnel and technical management of the product management team private customers.
  • Further develop the product strategy in line with the corporate strategy.
  • Overall responsibility for the entire product and service portfolio.
  • Control of the complete value chain: product structure, features, pricing, processes, documents, fund offerings, AVBs.
  • Design of new product features incl. Integration of behavioral economic elements.
  • Ensuring annual product requirements (e.g. tranches), naming, USP definition, internal and external communication as well as content management on- and offline.
  • Preparation of volume and premium planning, implementation of product-related analyses and market comparisons, preparation for management and operating units.
  • Ensuring professional compliance across all product levels.
  • Support for special offers, participation in market development measures.
  • Active role in transformation and product projects such as LEAP or tranches.

Requirements

  • In-depth experience in leading and developing teams.
  • Several years of expertise in product management individual life as well as in-depth knowledge of the Swiss individual life market and private provision.
  • Deep understanding of the end-to-end product development process
  • Ability to analyze complex issues in a structured and strategically develop them.
  • Strong understanding of customer needs, market mechanisms and sales requirements.
  • Secure action in a broad network of provision, actuary, sales, IT, marketing, asset management and other areas.
  • High implementation orientation, prioritization competence and proactive action.
  • Flowing knowledge of German, other languages are an advantage
