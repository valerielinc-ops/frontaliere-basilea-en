Full description

Tasks

  • They take over the ISF and DaZ lessons in our 1st/2nd multi-year class.
  • In close cooperation with the class teacher, you accompany and support students with an increased need for support.
  • You fulfill your tasks with joy and responsibility. Challenges are met with serenity and humor.
  • In addition to your teaching activities, you show interest in actively shaping school development in the area of special support.

Requirements

  • They have a teaching diploma for the primary level and can preferably have the master's degree in curative education or a comparable curative education training or are in training for it.
  • For them, pedagogical cooperation and an appreciative treatment of colleagues is important.
  • They maintain an appreciative cooperation with the students and the guardians.
  • In the field of media and computer science, you bring good knowledge or are ready to acquire it.

We are a small, manageable village school with a kindergarten and three primary classes. Our school is characterized by a dedicated team that works together successfully and appreciatively. We attach particular importance to cross-level events, the pedagogical further development in the field of self-organized learning and learning coaching as well as a supportive cooperation of all participants.
