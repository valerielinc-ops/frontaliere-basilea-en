Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the department Global IT, we are looking for a dedicated Data Platform Owner Databricks (a), 80-100%

In this role, you will own and further develop Bachem's Databricks platform as the foundation for data, analytics, and AI initiatives. You will ensure a secure, scalable, compliant, and future-ready data platform that supports business-critical processes across manufacturing, quality, supply chain, and corporate functions in a highly regulated environment.

Your tasks

  • Own the Databricks platform end-to-end, including strategy, architecture, operations, governance, security, compliance, cost management, and roadmap development
  • Lead the transition and continuous evolution of a production-grade, GxP-qualified lakehouse platform on AWS
  • Define and maintain platform standards for Unity Catalog, workspaces, compute resources, networking, data architecture, Infrastructure as Code, and CI/CD frameworks
  • Establish scalable data ingestion and integration patterns for enterprise systems such as SAP, MES, LIMS, eQMS, and ELN
  • Ensure platform compliance with GxP requirements, data integrity principles (ALCOA+), audit readiness, access management, change control, and backup and recovery processes
  • Manage day-to-day platform operations, including monitoring, incident, problem, release, security, and access management
  • Drive FinOps activities and vendor management to ensure cost transparency, optimization, licensing governance, and effective partner collaboration
  • Enable self-service data capabilities through platform onboarding, documentation, best practices, and user support
  • Coordinate internal and external platform resources to ensure reliable platform delivery and continuous improvement

Your profile

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Data Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related field
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience in data platform engineering, platform ownership, or a comparable role
  • At least 3 years of hands-on experience managing Databricks platforms in production environments
  • Experience working in pharmaceutical, biotechnology, CDMO, or other regulated industries is an advantage
  • Strong expertise in Databricks, including Unity Catalog, Delta Lake, workspace administration, cluster and job management, and Databricks SQL
  • Experience operating Databricks on AWS, including IAM, VPC, S3, and KMS
  • Good understanding of GxP requirements, GAMP 5, ALCOA+, CSV, 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, and change control processes
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code and CI/CD tools such as Terraform, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, or Databricks Asset Bundles
  • English: fluent
  • German language skills are an advantage
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to explain technical concepts to business, quality, and leadership stakeholders
  • Familiarity with data integration, data governance, OT/IT data flows, analytics, and AI/ML workloads
  • Databricks certifications are considered a plus
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Collaborative, proactive, and solution-oriented mindset
  • Ability to work effectively with cross-functional and international teams

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal opportunities with internal development opportunities and continuing education
  • Additional days off (bridge days), which are determined each fall by Bachem
  • On-site daycare center and staff restaurant at the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem, as well as option for extra-mandatory retirement savings through our optional Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to benefits platforms offering discounts from external partners
