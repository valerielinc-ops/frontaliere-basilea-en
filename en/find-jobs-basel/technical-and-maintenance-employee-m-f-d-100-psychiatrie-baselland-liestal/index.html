Operation I Technology and maintenance

In order for our buildings and technical facilities to function reliably at all times, we need people who tackle, think and find solutions. As a result of the retirement of the previous job owner, we are looking for a committed and versatile personality for our Technology and Maintenance department.

Their tasks

  • Ensuring the operational readiness of buildings, facilities and modern building technology
  • Carrying out maintenance, repairs, troubleshooting and plant optimisation work
  • Preventive maintenance to ensure reliable and trouble-free operation
  • Coordination, repair and testing of equipment in compliance with legal requirements and appropriate training
  • Support for external service providers and support for technical work within the framework of internal projects
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with different disciplines as well as assuming responsibility in the Pikettdienst
  • Processing of orders, fault reports and requests about the maintenance programme (IH)
  • Operation and support of building control, alarm and locking systems

  • Experience in business maintenance desirable
  • Openness for further training (e.g. follow-up permit by type). 15 NIV
  • Category B driving licence
  • Experience with building control systems and fire alarm systems is advantageous

About us

The Psychiatry Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, daily clinical, home treatment and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. She supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of living, working and day-to-day planning (www.inclusioplus.ch). She also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Center for Work and Mental Health. At several locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, PBL employs around 1,300 people and treats, supervises and advises more than 15,000 patients per year (www.pbl.ch).
