The Position Are you ready to bridge the gap between traditional scientific publishing and the future of AI-driven research? We are looking for a forward-thinking Scientific Content Data Expert to join our agile team of Content Experts, Data Engineers and Product Owners.

In this highly impactful role, you will go beyond library science. As the publishing landscape rapidly evolves, you will secure access not only to essential STM (Science, Technology, and Medicine) literature but also to complex, AI-ready data packages. You will be at the forefront of navigating the complex legal terrain of content licensing and emerging AI-usage rights.

If you are a strategic negotiator with a systems-thinking mindset—someone who understands both the legal constraints of data compliance and the technical needs of a data-driven corporate environment—we want you to help enable our strategy and shaping our content portfolio.

The Opportunity

  • Portfolio Management: Manage and optimize the STM vendor portfolio, negotiating access to both traditional literature derived platforms and complex text and data packages.
  • Procurement & Negotiation: Drive the end-to-end acquisition workflow in collaboration with procurement, navigating the fragmented legal landscape of complex licenses and emerging AI-usage rights.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work within a diverse team of data engineers, product owners and learning experts to ensure smooth technical integration of acquired data and optimize the visibility and use.
  • Compliance Tracking: Monitor internal data usage to ensure strict alignment with vendor contracts, copyright laws, and specific AI-training restrictions.
  • Data-Driven Development: Analyze usage data and market trends to facilitate effective, evidence-based portfolio development.
  • Stakeholder Management: Act as the primary strategic liaison between external scientific publishers and internal business partners.

Who You Are

  • University degree in Information Science, Library Science, Data Management, or equivalent professional experience, with ~3-5 years of experience in related fields.
  • Proven track record working in a corporate environment (preferably Pharma, Healthcare, or Life Sciences).
  • Extensive experience in evaluating and negotiating complex electronic content agreements with data providers.
  • Solid understanding of data management concepts, AI-ready data products, and compliance regarding published STM content (especially navigating AI-rights).
  • Fluent in business English (written and verbal).
  • Ability to connect the dots between legal constraints, technical requirements (Data Engineering), and business needs.
  • Strong interpersonal and conflict-resolution skills with a talent for finding workable compromises between publishers and internal stakeholders.
  • A continuous learning mindset with the ability to adapt to a rapidly changing legal and technological landscape.
  • Advanced organizational, analytical, and time-management skills.
