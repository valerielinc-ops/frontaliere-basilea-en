What you do

  • You calculate technical provisions and analyze the results in the context of financial statement reporting in accordance with IFRS, OR and regulatory reports.
  • Develop creative solutions to simplify, optimize and further automate closing processes.
  • Contribute your expertise in special analyses as well as in matters relating to the management of insurance industries and the introduction of new insurance products.
  • Work in cross-divisional projects and assume responsibility for sub-projects or project management.
  • Analyze complex data sets and derive sound insights for different stakeholders.
  • Creates a bridge between actuarial, IT and business and helps to develop sustainable solutions.

What you bring

  • University degree with a scientific or economic orientation, for example in actuary sciences, mathematics, data science, business informatics, business administration or business administration.
  • Knowledge in determining technical provisions as well as accounting standards (OR and IFRS17) and actuarial processes is an advantage.
  • In-depth experience in data analysis as well as in using tools such as R, SQL, SAS, VBA or Python
  • Knowledge in the use of AI solutions for analysis and programming is welcome.
  • High affinity for IT systems, databases and process landscapes as well as interest in the further development and integration of data-driven and AI-supported solutions.
  • Analytical thinking, a high degree of curiosity and pleasure in penetrating complex questions in a structured way and developing pragmatic solutions.
  • Structured, goal-oriented working method with high quality standards as well as the ability to process complex issues appropriately.
  • Strong team and communication skills as well as the willingness to contribute ideas and create added value together.
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English in word and writing.
