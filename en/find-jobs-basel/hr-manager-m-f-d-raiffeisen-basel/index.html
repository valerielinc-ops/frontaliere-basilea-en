Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Basel, a locally anchored and successful bank, we are looking for a committed and independent personality for personnel responsibility.

What awaits you?

  • Responsibility for HR processes along the entire employee lifecycle, from recruitment to exit
  • Ensure high-quality personnel administration and the proper execution of all administrative HR activities
  • Advice and support of employees and managers in all HR issues
  • Cooperation with Raiffeisen Switzerland’s HR departments as well as active participation and further development of the bank’s HR processes

What do you bring?

  • HR certificate or comparable training in human resources
  • Several years of experience in an operational HR generalist function is a mandatory requirement. Experience in the financial sector or in the banking environment is an advantage.
  • High digital affinity and well-versed handling of HR systems
  • Workday knowledge is beneficial.
  • Enjoying a varied activity between consulting, administration and organization.
  • High service orientation and an exact and discreet working method.
