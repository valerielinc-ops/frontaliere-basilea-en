The Position

The Senior Data Advisory & Insights Specialist is responsible for leading, translating business challenges into analytical problems, and delivering data-driven insights and actionable recommendations to stakeholders. This role involves independently conducting comprehensive data analysis, developing compelling visualizations, preparing detailed reports, and presenting findings to various business units to inform strategic and operational decisions. In this role, you will act as a key liaison between data teams and business functions, ensuring that insights are clearly understood and leveraged for maximum impact.

Job Responsibilities

  • Scope / (Content Leadership): Leads data analysis projects and delivers high-value insights. Works independently on specific projects from inception to completion.
  • Accountability/Problem Solving : Solves complex business problems using data. Validates insights and ensures their accuracy with no supervision required.
  • Stakeholder Management : Manages direct communication with key business stakeholders regarding analytical findings. Engages proactively with broader business units to thoroughly understand their data needs.
  • Impact/Strategy: Drives medium- to large-sized projects that directly influence business decisions. Ensures that analysis projects are closely aligned with broader business strategy and goals.
  • Complexity / (Product Size): Handles more complex datasets and multifaceted analysis tasks. Develops comprehensive reports and interactive dashboards to support business needs.
  • Business / Technical ability: Applies strong analytical skills and the ability to communicate complex insights effectively. Demonstrates proficiency in data visualization tools and advanced data analysis techniques.

Qualifications Education / Experience

  • Demonstrated experience independently conducting comprehensive data analysis and managing medium- to large-sized projects that influence business decisions.
  • Proven track record of translating business challenges into analytical problems and delivering actionable, data-driven recommendations.
  • Experience acting as a key liaison between data teams and business functions, ensuring insights inform strategic and operational decisions.

Technical Skills

  • Proficient in utilizing data visualization tools to develop compelling visualizations and comprehensive dashboards.
  • Strong expertise in advanced data analysis techniques and handling more complex datasets.
  • Ability to independently validate insights and ensure high data accuracy.

Additional Qualifications

  • Exceptional communication and presentation skills, with the ability to manage direct communication with key business stakeholders and present findings to various business units.
  • Strong analytical mindset with a focus on ensuring complex insights are understood by non-technical audiences and leveraged for maximum impact.
