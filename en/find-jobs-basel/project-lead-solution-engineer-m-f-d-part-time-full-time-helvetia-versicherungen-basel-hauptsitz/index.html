Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

We are not looking for a classic project manager or a pure solution engineer or business analyst. We are looking for a technical generalist who understands business requirements, develops pragmatic solutions and drives their implementation together with specialist departments, IT and external partners. Tasks: • You analyze business requirements together with the specialist departments and develop pragmatic, feasible solution concepts from them • You design and realize digitalization and automation initiatives along the corporate strategy • You lead smaller to medium-sized transformation and digitalization projects and are responsible for budget, scope, quality and implementation success (incl. business case) • You coordinate and control external partners, service providers and internal stakeholders from business and IT (also external tenders if required) • You will conduct workshops, requirements analysis and decision-making processes • You evaluate new technologies, cloud services, AI applications and digital platforms for their added value for the company • You develop into a central contact person for the IT landscape and support the team with deputies and operational tasks Requirements • You work independently, solution-oriented and pragmatic and implement projects with high implementation strength • You convince through analytical thinking, networked action and strong stakeholder management competence. This will bring business and IT together successfully • You have several years of experience in Solution Engineering, Business Analysis, Digitalization and Project Management • You bring a broad understanding of modern IT architectures as well as their interaction in the corporate environment • You have experience with APIs, interfaces, integrations and (AWS) cloud and SaaS solutions • You have knowledge of data management, data models and analytics; Power BI is an advantage • You know current AI technologies and recognize their potential for business processes and automation • You master methods such as requirements engineering, process management and project management • You communicate sovereignly in German and English and convince by your appearance and your joy of change We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
