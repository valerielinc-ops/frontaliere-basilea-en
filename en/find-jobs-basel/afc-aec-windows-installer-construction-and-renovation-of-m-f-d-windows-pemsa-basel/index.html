  • indicates mandatory fields

Do you know that every millimeter counts in the window installation? Do you like to work with precision, have manual skills and know the challenges related to the installation of different windows systems? PEMSA Basel looks for, on behalf of one of our customers, an experienced window editor with EFZ / EBA (m/f/d) qualification for projects in Basel and the canton of Basel Country. You're expected to work in new buildings, renovations and refurbishments, where your specialist skills and your meticulous approach to work will be required. Shipments

  • Adjustable art assembly of wooden windows, wood-aluminum, PVC and aluminium
  • Disassembly and replace existing windows during renovation work
  • Preparation and control of window openings and installation quotas
  • Aligning, fixing and sealing of windows elements
  • Assembly and adjustment of hardware and other components of windows
  • Execution of fitting, adaptation and finishing
  • Control of operation, sealing and assembly quality
  • Execution of works according to mounting patterns and technical specifications

Profile requested

  • Complete vocational training (AFC / AFC), preferably as carpenter, or equivalent qualification
  • Multiannual practical experience in the installation of windows is preferential title
  • Good knowledge of the different systems of windows and mounting techniques
  • Mastery in the use of tools and assembly machinery
  • Technical skills and precision in work
  • Good knowledge of German written and spoken is indispensable
  • Class B licence is indispensable
  • Autonomy, reliability and teamwork
