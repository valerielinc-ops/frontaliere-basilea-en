Full description

Tasks

  • They are expected to work in two of our fourteen kindergartens. Your workload is 21 hours per week and you work in the morning.
  • In addition, you can work for a 10% time in the lunch table of the school supplementary care (SEB) (each on Monday, Tuesday and Friday from 12.00 to 1.30 pm).
  • The employment conditions are based on municipal requirements. The remuneration at 50% is CHF 500.00 per month and at a 60% fee CHF 600.00 per month, less social benefits.

Requirements

  • Do you need an internship for your further professional training? Want to find out if an educational profession is right for you?
  • Do you feel motivated to accompany children in kindergarten age and enjoy working in different classes and teams?
  • Are you preferably already 18 years old?
  • We offer good care by the teachers and the school management, an insight into working with children in school.

The municipality of Münchenstein is a natural agglomeration community in the canton of Baselland. Although today the municipality passes almost seamlessly into the city of Basel, it has preserved its independence and its own charm and character. The primary level Münchenstein consists of 14 kindergartens and 47 school classes. The more than 1,000 students are taught by 150 teachers at five school locations. The school is supported by three school social workers, interns in the kindergarten and four civil service providers.
