Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offers merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita triad in Lausen we are looking for a dipl immediately or by appointment. Care professional 60-80% with additional function RAI-HC Your tasks

  • You contribute greatly to ensuring that our residents enjoy professional and individual care and support at all times
  • You care for our residents professionally, individually and according to established care guidelines.
  • You take responsibility for the day
  • You carry out RAI assessments and BMF on schedule and create the care documentation as well as individual care planning.
  • You carry out needs assessments with RAI-HC for customers.
  • You are actively involved in rapports, team meetings and training.
  • You recognize emergency situations and master them

You bring that with you

  • Training as a Dipl. Care professional HF or FH
  • Ideally Spitex experience
  • You are motivated and are happy to contribute your strong professional and social skills
  • Ideally, you will bring knowledge in RAI HC/RAI LTCF and careCoach
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German (B2)
  • With your cheerful manner, you actively contribute to a company culture in which residents and employees feel comfortable.

We offer you

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
