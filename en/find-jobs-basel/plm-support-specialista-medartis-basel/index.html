Full description

To strengthen our product development team in Basel, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as a PLM Support Specialist.

In this role, you will support our development teams along the product lifecycle management (PLM) process and ensure that documentation, data, deadlines and open tasks are reliably coordinated and tracked. As an important interface, you work closely with product development, quality management, regulatory affairs and other specialist areas and contribute to efficient, structured and high-quality development processes.

PLM Support Specialist

Your responsibilities

  • Support and coordination of administrative and organizational tasks along the product lifecycle management (PLM) process.
  • Creation, maintenance and management of PLM-relevant documents, templates and information in the intended systems as well as ensuring their completeness and timeliness.
  • Coordination and consistent follow-up of amendments, open tasks, releases and deadlines as well as early detection of deviations and need for action.
  • Support development teams in PLM-related tasks and ensure compliance with defined processes, specifications and quality requirements.
  • Ensure high quality, consistency and traceability of PLM-relevant data and documentation.
  • Creation and maintenance of status overviews, evaluations and reports to create transparency about ongoing activities and open points.
  • Coordination of the information flow and close cooperation with product development, quality management, regulatory affairs and other specialist areas involved in the PLM process.
  • Active participation in the standardization, digitalization and continuous optimization of PLM processes, documentation, templates and tools.

Your qualifications

  • Completed commercial or technical training
  • Further training in administration, project assistance, process management or a comparable discipline is advantageous.
  • Several years of professional experience in a demanding administrative, coordinative or project support function, ideally in the field of product development or PLM.
  • Experience in document and data management and ideally with PLM, PDM or document management systems.
  • Good process understanding and the ability to quickly capture complex processes, interfaces and dependencies and to consistently track open topics.
  • Strong quality awareness and a structured, precise and reliable way of working
  • Experience in a regulated environment such as medical technology, pharmaceuticals or life sciences is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office 365 and a secure handling of structured data, documentation and digital workflows.
  • Proactive, service- and solution-oriented personality with strong communication and coordination skills and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing.

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.
