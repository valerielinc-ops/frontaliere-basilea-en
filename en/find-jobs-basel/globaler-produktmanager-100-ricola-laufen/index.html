Global Product Manager (100%) - Ausschreibung •

Do you want to make a difference, not just turn up to work? Then you’ve come to the right place. Interested, as

Global Product Manager (100%)

to be a part of Ricola?

That's what you can achieve with us.

Do you want to help create products that delight consumers around the world? As part of our Global Product Management team, you will actively shape the future of the Ricola product portfolio. Together, we develop new products and line extensions, optimize existing recipes, and bring innovations to life – from the initial idea to successful market launch – with consumers always at the heart of everything we do. Your mission:

  • Lead global product and innovation projects and provide cross-functional leadership to project teams
  • Develop and implement new product innovations, including creating compelling product concepts and further develop existing products in line with our innovation strategy
  • Develop robust business cases to support decision-making for new product initiatives
  • Analyze market, consumer, and competitive trends to identify new growth opportunities
  • Manage innovation projects throughout the Stage-Gate process, ensuring successful delivery in terms of quality, timing, and budget
  • Collaborate closely with global stakeholders and our local market organizations while effectively managing diverse interests and priorities
  • Develop consumer-centric solutions that create sustainable value for consumers and our markets worldwide

How well-versed are you in these areas?

  • At least three years of experience in Product or Brand Management, ideally within the FMCG/CPG industry
  • Proven experience in global project management with a demonstrated ability to successfully lead complex cross-functional projects
  • Strong analytical skills and a solid understanding of business cases and market dynamics
  • A strong consumer focus and a service-oriented mindset towards our markets
  • Enjoy working in cross-functional and international teams
  • Strong influencing skills and proven experience in stakeholder management
  • A positive entrepreneurial mindset with the initiative and courage to drive ideas forward, identify opportunities, and actively shape innovation
  • Comfortable navigating change, ambiguity, and new challenges
  • Business fluent German and English, both written and spoken (minimum C1)

That’s something you can look forward to.
