Full description

This is what awaits you

As a KV learner:r you are from your first day a part of the team of the general agency miles. You will get to know the insurance industry first hand and support us in our daily work.

This is what you do with us

At the center of the apprenticeship is your training as a commercial employee in the private insurance industry.

In the various departments, you are in direct interaction with our customers and take care of their needs.

We accompany you in your development and your trainer helps you to get to know the different facets of the insurance business. You bring your youthful freshness into our team and take the responsibility given to you.

This is what you bring with you

  • School leaving with good performance
  • Reliable work and willingness to learn new things
  • Enjoyment of people as well as open and friendly treatment of our customers
  • Motivated, curious and enthusiastic personality
  • Interest in the Insurance World

Working with us

A three-year basic education in the insurance industry awaits you with a varied training program and individual support. Learn more about the apprenticeship as a merchant/merchant EFZ at Mobiliar.

The general agency Meilen not only distinguishes the optimal location directly at the train station. No! A performance-oriented, happy team is waiting for you, who can hardly wait to accompany you during your education.

Your place of work is miles and your apprenticeship starts in August 2027

This is what we offer

Care

We personally accompany you in your education. During your 3-year apprenticeship, a vocational trainer will assist you.

Training

We will cover your training costs. In addition, we support you with examination simulations and training-specific further education.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate.
