Full description

You keep an overview when others only see material shortages?

In this key role, you ensure that our production is supplied with the right components at all times. You control suppliers, coordinate appointments and bring purchasing, production and logistics together. You not only have delivery times and inventories in mind, but also actively contribute to making our supply chain more efficient and efficient.

Operational/r PurchaserIn components – plant Sissach (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Scheduling of the allocated shopping items and ensuring timely availability of materials
  • Support and management of suppliers for external contract processing and maintenance of sustainable supplier relationships
  • Monitoring delivery dates as well as proactively clarifying deadline deviations and delivery problems
  • Implementation of offer comparisons and order processing with national and international suppliers
  • Maintenance of relevant item and supplier master data as well as evaluation of supply chain key figures
  • Close cooperation with production, production planning, logistics, quality management and global purchasing to ensure smooth production processes

Your profile

  • Completed technical or commercial training, further training in purchasing (e.g. as a purchasing specialist with a federal certificate, technical merchant or comparable) is an advantage but not a prerequisite
  • At least three years of professional experience in operational purchasing of an industrial environment, ideally in a production company would be desirable
  • Experience in scheduling and dealing with national and international suppliers
  • Good ERP knowledge (e.g. SAP, ABACUS or Dynamics) as well as secure handling of MS Office
  • Independent, resilient and assertive personality with a strong team and service orientation
  • Stylistic communication in German and English
