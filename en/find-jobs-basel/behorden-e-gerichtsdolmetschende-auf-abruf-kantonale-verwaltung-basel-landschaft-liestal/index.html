Translation Coordination Office

Working as an administrative and court interpreter is demanding in various respects. Depending on the job, our interpreters must also be able to record very complex issues and translate them exactly into the other language. It is precisely when interpreters are used in the field of law enforcement and criminal justice that they must also be able to deal with stressful situations and distinguish themselves accordingly. All information that authorities and court interpreters receive in the context of their work is subject to official secrecy and may therefore not be shared with third parties. This also applies to family members or friends. It goes without saying that in addition to all these qualities, (very) good language skills - both in the working language and in the German standard language - must be available.

  • Possibility of accreditation in other partner cantons after passing an admission course
