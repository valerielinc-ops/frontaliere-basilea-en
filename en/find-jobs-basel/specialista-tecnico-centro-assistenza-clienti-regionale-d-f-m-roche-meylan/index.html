The Position Within the French Hub of the Regional Customer Support Center, the RCSC Technical Specialist provides Roche Diagnostics customers and employees with the support necessary for the optimal functioning of Roche systems and thus contributes to customer satisfaction.

Your main responsibilities

  • Provide first-level support for French, Belgian and Swiss (French-speaking) customers on Roche Diagnostics systems in the Chemistry, Immunology and Pre/Post analytical ranges as well as second-level support for employees in the Export Division countries.
  • Receive client requests and document them quantitatively and qualitatively in real time in the tools made available (CRM) in accordance with current rules.
  • Makes the diagnosis of re-runs in collaboration with the client and proposes the first corrective actions using all the documentation and tools made available.
  • Identifies and escalates quality issues by contacting subsidiaries, RCSC and global teams.
  • Possibility of hosting internal training sessions
  • Provide on-site support related to the use of Roche systems
  • Possibility of client site intervention with or without field presence, as part of training or expertise.
  • Contributing to information sharing, optimisation of practices and the advancement of its own skills
  • Communicates with colleagues at the Regional Customer Support Center and/or the field on a client's situation in case of a critical or complex problem
  • Feeds the different shared knowledge bases (OneNote, G site, RDK).
  • Identify actions to increase its autonomy by being proactive and active in its development.
  • Contributes to the launch of new products and/or modifications of existing products
  • Ensures compliance with standards and rules related to its work area

Who are you?

  • Bac + 2/Bac +3 training in the fields of automation, instrumentation, electronics and computer science or medical biology analysis with specialization in biomedical instrumentation
  • Professional experience in a medical environment, in the fields of diagnosis, customer service or in the laboratory of medical analysis
  • Technical knowledge and maintenance methodologies (diagnosis and problem solving)
  • Network knowledge would be appreciated
  • Current French and a good level of English essential (B2)
