Cantonal Social Office

The Cantonal Social Office (KSA) monitors and checks the work of the municipalities with regard to the proper implementation of social assistance, among other things in the form of audits. It also advises, trains and supports the 86 Basel bidder municipalities in the implementation of social assistance. Furthermore, the Cantonal Social Office is the cantonal office for jurisdictional issues and for medical emergencies. In the complaint procedure, the Cantonal Social Office initiates complaints against decision-makers of municipal welfare authorities. In the area of asylum and refugees, the Cantonal Social Office cooperates with the Confederation and assigns the persons assigned by the Confederation to the municipalities. It also supports communities in promoting the integration of people from the asylum and refugee sectors. Furthermore, the Cantonal Social Office advances child support contributions and supports persons in the case of separation in the demand of legally determined maintenance contributions. The Cantonal Social Office also assumes cantonal tasks regarding rental interest contributions and home permits in the canton. In addition, the Cantonal Social Office is involved in poverty prevention and combating, addresses overarching issues of poverty policy as well as political initiatives and formulates socio-political strategies and concepts.
