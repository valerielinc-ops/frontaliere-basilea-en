Full description

With the banking internship "Bank Entry after Matura (BEM)" at Raiffeisenbank Aesch-Pfeffingen, you will start your banking training in a motivated team - the perfect introduction to your professional future.

Tasks

During your internship

  • You will gain exciting insights into all areas of our bank, from private, credit, investment and corporate customer advice to back office, loan processing and credit office
  • Get to know how to advise customers step by step and develop your skills through direct exchange
  • Do you work with modern, digital tools and systems
  • Do you support the processing of banking transactions in the background
  • Do you actively participate in projects and help organize events
  • As part of your training, attend CYP's face-to-face courses, in which banking specialist knowledge and operational skills are acquired
  • Duration of the internship 18-24 months depending on previous training

During the training you will be accompanied and supported by competent and experienced practical trainers.

Your benefits

麟 6 weeks vacation  Own laptop for face-to-face courses & work  Regular team events and exchanges with other trainees ❤️ Dynamic team and appreciative, family working atmosphere  Many opportunities for further training within the Raiffeisen Group  Various preferential conditions, e.g. on banking products or insurance

Requirements

School-related

  • You completed your Matura at a full-time middle school
  • You are a graduate of an HMS/WMS with a vocational baccalaureate in another industry
  • You have a federal diploma. EFZ as a businesswoman/businessman and you did not acquire your professional qualification in the banking sector
  • You have a specialist baccalaureate
  • You are in a long-term internship at a commercial or business school (after completing at least 3 years of school)

Personal

  • You are sociable and communicative and are not afraid to approach people
  • You are a team player
  • Enjoy learning
  • Are reliable, complete tasks accurately and are willing to invest time in your training
  • You are open to learning new things and are interested in banking
  • You are interested in applying information sources and using new technologies
  • It is important to you to develop yourself personally
