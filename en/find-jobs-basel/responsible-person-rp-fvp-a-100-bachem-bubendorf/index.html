Full description

Bachem is seeking an experienced Responsible Person (RP / Fachtechnisch verantwortliche Person, FvP) to provide independent technical and GMP oversight for activities covered by the Swiss establishment licence. The role combines regulatory accountability, sound quality judgement and senior interaction with authorities, customers and internal stakeholders.

Your responsibilities

  • Act as the designated RP/FvP for the assigned activities under Bachem’s Swiss establishment licence and ensure effective technical supervision.
  • Provide independent oversight of applicable manufacturing, testing, batch disposition, storage and distribution activities in accordance with Swiss GMP/GDP requirements and Bachem’s Pharmaceutical Quality System.
  • Make or support independent quality and regulatory decisions for significant deviations, investigations, CAPAs, changes, product-quality defects and other critical quality events.
  • Ensure appropriate escalation and measures where product quality, patient safety or regulatory compliance may be affected, including quarantine, supply restrictions, withdrawal or recall where required.
  • Support inspections, establishment-licence activities and required communication with stakeholder and other health authorities
  • Represent Quality in customer audits, quality meetings and critical product or supply discussions while maintaining the independence of the RP/FvP function.
  • Contribute to key quality governance processes and promote a strong, riskbased quality culture.

Your profile

  • University degree in pharmacy, chemistry or another relevant scientific discipline, with education, technical knowledge and relevant professional experience and suitability for the authorised activities
  • Substantial experience in pharmaceutical, biotechnology or API manufacturing and strong knowledge of GMP/GDP, Swiss pharmaceutical legislation and relevant EU GMP and ICH expectations.
  • Demonstrated experience in batch disposition, deviations, investigations, CAPA, change control, complaints, product-quality defects and recalls.
  • Strong scientific judgement, integrity, trustworthiness and the ability to take independent, well-founded decisions.
  • Ability to ensure direct technical supervision through an appropriate level of availability, on-site presence and workload for the approved scope.
  • Clear communication and confidence in interactions with authorities, customers and senior stakeholders.
  • Trustworthiness and independence in exercising the RP/FvP function
  • Strong German and English language skills are essential

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
