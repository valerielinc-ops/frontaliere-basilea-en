As a diverse real estate service provider, we combine retail know-how with real estate expertise in center and mixed use site management. Mixed-use properties with over 400 residential properties now complement our portfolio in Basel and Pratteln. Therefore, we are looking for a committed, open-hearted and reliable personality as property management / rental specialist (m/f/d) St. Jakob-Park, Basel, 50% This is what you do in your role as a rental specialist (m/f/d):

  • You control the digital re-letting process from the receipt of notice to the transfer of the property to the new tenants, communicate with tenants and interested parties, check applications and create lease contracts
  • You reduce vacancy through effective marketing measures – from error-free online advertisements to the coordination of photos and 360° tours
  • You independently carry out visits, transfers and acceptances of apartments. If necessary, also outside the usual working hours. This is what you do in your role as (m/f/d) clerk:
  • You support the management team with administrative tasks and ensure smooth processes
  • You coordinate cooperation with FM providers, craft businesses and agencies – including offers, contracting and work control
  • Final statements, custodial settlements and the processing of creditors, debtors and dunning procedures are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • You bring a basic commercial education; a further education in real estate management is advantageous or you are ready to complete it
  • Ideally, you have already gained initial experience in the real estate industry or in an administrative function with customer contact
  • You work in a structured way, keep an overview even in hectic situations and communicate safely in German
  • English is a plus
  • You are resilient, customer-oriented, capable of teamwork and mobile with a valid driver’s license
  • Personal contact with tenants and prospective tenants makes you happy and the service orientation is your top priority Here are some of our benefits:
