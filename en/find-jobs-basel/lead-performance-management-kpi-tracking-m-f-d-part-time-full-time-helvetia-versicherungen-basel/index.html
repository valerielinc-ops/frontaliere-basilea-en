Full description

Data Analysis / Data Science Busy: 50-100% Place of work: Level of experience: FlexOffice manager: on site In the area of customer services, we are further developing data-based process control. For this purpose, we are expanding the existing non-financial KPI set, creating reliable data and establishing a binding management dialogue for effective management of customer processes. We are not looking for a person who only processes numbers. In this role, you have the chance to make a tangible contribution. We are looking for a leading professional who combines process understanding, KPI governance, project work and technology competence. Someone who creates common, management-relevant key figures from data and drives reporting solutions that have a sustainable effect.

What you do

  • You take over the leadership and further development of the team operational performance management & KPI tracking in the area of customer services
  • You develop, harmonize and anchor as product owner non-financial KPIs resp. Process key figures and ensure that they are uniform, comparable, traceable and relevant for control across all areas
  • You establish and maintain binding technical definitions, calculation logics, data requirements, responsibilities and standards for key figures and reports
  • You control reporting projects and initiatives from conception to prioritization and implementation to operationalization and continuous development
  • Translate professional, functional and non-functional requirements into clear specifications for data products, data models and reporting solutions
  • You are responsible for the further development of data products and reporting solutions (mainly Power BI) and ensure that the reports are actively used in management dialogues to derive concrete measures and thus support continuous process improvements.
  • You accompany managers in the application of common performance indicators

What you bring

  • Several years of experience in operational performance management, business analytics, process management, reporting or data-driven control, ideally in the insurance or service environment
  • Leadership experience and experience in the management or professional management of projects as well as in cooperation with different stakeholders and hierarchical levels
  • Sound process understanding of service processes and experience in the development, harmonization and governance of KPI definitions, calculation logics, reporting standards and responsibilities
  • Very good knowledge of Power BI; good SQL knowledge and understanding of modern data platforms and data architectures
  • Ideally, you develop and optimize data models and dashboards yourself. Experience with Databricks or comparable environments is an advantage
  • Ability to structure, prioritize and transform professional requirements into viable solutions together with departments, IT and data teams
  • Analytical thinking, initiative, assertiveness as well as a winning and convincing personality, which with its teamwork and communication strength ensures a positive cooperation with the operational areas
  • Leadership with the ability to accompany specialist areas in the change to data and process-oriented thinking
  • stylish knowledge of German, good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge in Python is desirable
