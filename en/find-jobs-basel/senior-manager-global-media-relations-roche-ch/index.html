Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position As a Media Spokesperson for the Roche Group, you will join a dynamic team dedicated to shaping the global narrative of a leading healthcare company. You will make sure our message is clear, impactful, and aligned with our corporate values across international markets.

In this position, you will serve as a primary voice for Roche, managing high-stakes communications and building vital relationships with the global press. You will play a pivotal part in protecting and enhancing our corporate reputation while supporting our senior leadership in their media engagements.

The Opportunity To excel in this position, you will take on a diverse range of responsibilities aimed at elevating Roche’s media presence:

  • Serve as the official media spokesperson for the Roche Group to represent the organization to external stakeholders.
  • Proactively shape the media narrative on key strategic, business, and reputational priority topics in collaboration with global communication teams.
  • Coordinate news flow and strategic planning across various internal communication departments.
  • Manage issues proactively by networking with content owners and utilizing media monitoring for early detection and timely response.
  • Provide comprehensive media support, including briefings and media training, for Corporate Executive Committee members and subject matter specialists.
  • Cultivate and maintain strong relationships with key journalists across international, Swiss, financial, and business media.
  • Install and configure communication strategies per guidelines while assisting with the evolution of our digital and social media presence.

Who you a re

  • A University degree in science, economics, or PR/Communications, paired with a minimum of seven years of professional experience in Corporate Communications or Media Relations within a multinational setting.
  • Outstanding interpersonal and communication skills, with a proven ability to influence and work effectively with senior management.
  • An excellent team player with high integrity, strong listening skills, and the ability to build trust across diverse teams.
  • The resilience to work effectively in a high-pressure environment while managing complex, sensitive topics.
  • Proficient written and verbal communication skills in both German and English; additional experience in social media or the healthcare industry is a plus.
