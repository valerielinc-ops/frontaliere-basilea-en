Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our drug production team, we are looking for a dedicated Lab Technician R&D (a), 100%. In our production plant, you produce pharmaceutical peptides under GMP operations. Through practical training, you will gain the necessary security to work independently and independently on our production facilities. You are happy to take action and enjoy varied tasks.

Their tasks

  • Preparation, implementation and evaluation of laboratory tests
  • Development and optimization of the manufacturing process from synthesis to purification to the final active ingredient as well as the production of peptides, partly under GMP
  • Responsibility for the analysis of experiments and interpretation of results
  • Active participation in improving our equipment and workflows as well as maintaining the laboratory infrastructure
  • Involvement in scale-up projects and optimisation of the manufacturing process as well as evaluation of new technologies and reagents
  • Ensuring clean documentation

Your profile

  • Completed training as a chemical laboratory (EFZ) / Bachelor in Life Science or comparable training or professional experience
  • Enjoying preparative organic chemistry in an innovative environment
  • Flair for handling technical equipment and software
  • A structured, clean and independent way of working
  • Flexibility, communication and willingness to learn
  • Very good knowledge of German (minimum B2 level), knowledge of English is an advantage

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements and option to receive additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners, as well as discounts for public transport and discount on Reka money
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
