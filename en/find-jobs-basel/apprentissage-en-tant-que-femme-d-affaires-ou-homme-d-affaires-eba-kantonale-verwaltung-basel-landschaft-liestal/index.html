Tasks

Are you facing the decision on how to continue after school?

  • The Swiss education system is among the best in the world. Vocational apprenticeship in Switzerland is excellent and leads to very good future opportunities.
  • We offer a commercial apprenticeship for interested school leavers at the Financial and Church Directorate of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. What does that mean to you?
  • In public administration, you go through two departments in rotation and get to know the various tasks of a public administration.
  • The teaching is varied; the tasks range from postal processing to customer advice at the counter or tasks to support the teams.
  • Our professional, dedicated vocational training specialists support you in achieving the learning goals so that you can successfully complete the apprenticeship after two years.

Requirements

  • Enjoying contact with customers and employees
  • Interest in digital tasks and economic events
  • Responsible, independent and team-capable personality with good manners
  • Great willingness to learn
  • Completed secondary school level A
  • Good to very good grading in German, English and Mathematics

We need the following documents from you

  • Complete application with letter of motivation, CV with photo
  • Copies of certificates from the last three school years as well as existing diplomas (keyboard writing, computer science, languages)

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and fields, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
