Full description

In order to drive our continued organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified specialists.

To strengthen our team in the Global Engineering department, we are looking for a dedicated Director Project Large CAPEX (a) 100%.

Your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for complex large-scale and investment projects (capex) across all project phases - from the feasibility study to handover to operations.
  • Management and control of feasibility, variant and concept studies including technical, economic and regulatory assessment as well as decision preparation for management committees.
  • Selection and control of suitable project management models (EPCm, EPC, AE&CM) from the client/owner perspective.
  • Ensuring production readiness as well as GMP, safety and legal compliance of systems, buildings and infrastructures.
  • Conception, planning and implementation of new buildings and conversions including integration of process, automation and building topics.
  • Leading interdisciplinary project teams and managing external partners and service providers.
  • Planning, monitoring and implementation of commissioning and qualification of new systems and buildings.
  • Budget responsibility including planning, consolidation and monitoring of investment and operating costs.

Your profile

  • Completed technical university degree (ETH/FH/TU) or comparable qualification.
  • Several years of experience in managing complex large-scale and investment projects in an industrial environment, ideally GMP-regulated.
  • Profound experience with project delivery models such as EPCm, EPC and AE&CM.
  • Experience in early project phases (feasibility and concept studies) as well as in governance and decision-making processes.
  • Good overall technical understanding of process, automation and building topics.
  • Experience in budget and investment management (Capex/Opex).
  • Several years of leadership experience as well as strong communication and coordination skills.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good English.

Our range

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further training opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option to work from home and the option to take additional vacation days with a reduced workload
  • In-house daycare center on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as the option for extra-mandatory provision with our optional savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • A variety of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
