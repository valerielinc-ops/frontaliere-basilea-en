Full description

For a renowned private clinic with a public service mandate in the canton of Basel-Land, we are looking for a specialist in radiology in the amount of 50-100%. The Radiology Institute specializes in musculoskeletal diagnostics on the musculoskeletal locomotor system and has diagnostic devices of the most modern generation (MRI 3T, Multislice-CT/Dual Energy, DVT, Weight-bearing-CT, digital radiology, fluoroscopy, US). What awaits you in this challenging task

  • They independently and responsibly supervise the implementation of imaging examinations from the indication test to the mediation of findings.
  • They guarantee a high professional quality standard within a team- and solution-oriented process
  • You work with the most modern diagnostic infrastructure with a focus on musculoskeletal system

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title radiology (CH or MEBEKO-approved)
  • Willingness to delve deep into musculoskeletal diagnostics and interventional techniques
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration and communication with internal and external referents
  • High degree of commitment, teamwork and social competence both internally and externally
  • Solution-oriented, careful working method with high professional quality standards

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Interesting, specialized and responsible work in an innovative, dynamic environment with collegial, team-oriented working structures
  • Opportunity to acquire an MSK continuing education diploma as well as the expert status of a recognised society for musculoskeletal radiology within the framework of a fellowship
  • Attractive remuneration and personal and professional development opportunities
  • Flexible working time models with up to seven weeks of holidays possible, family-friendly framework conditions
  • Above-average pension benefits, free flu and hepatitis B vaccinations and confidential, external employee advice
