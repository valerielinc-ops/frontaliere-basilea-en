Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters. The Position Welcome to a highly dedicated team where you will have the unique opportunity to gain deep insight into all aspects of investor relations at a world-leading, science-driven pharmaceutical company. Our team focuses on maintaining strong relations with the capital market while offering a dynamic and fast-paced environment with ample opportunities for professional development. As Investor Relations Officer, your basic responsibilities are to ensure the correct flow of relevant information and company messages, analyze financial community expectations and sentiments, develop the financial community’s understanding and appreciation of Roche’s pharmaceutical business with focus on the development pipeline. You will gain experience related to internal and external communication to senior management and the financial community. The Opportunity :

  • Liaise with franchise heads, product teams, business units, competitive intelligence teams, business development, communication and finance to keep up to date with the Pharma pipeline and ongoing business developments
  • Contribute to quarterly results presentations for the management team with focus on the preparation and review of slides decks and Q&A
  • Organize IR events, develop story lines and contribute to external presentations delivered by senior management at medical conferences and IR company days (e.g. Pharma Day, Diagnostics Day, ESG Day, Digitalization Day)
  • Review and provide feedback to achieve alignment between IR and communication teams for Media & IR releases in assigned areas
  • Prepare supporting materials and draft Q&A documents for senior management prior to investor conferences/visits
  • Compile analyst notes to summarize financial community sentiment, expectations and projections
  • You will be acting as a key point of contact for analysts and investors to process all sustainability (ESG: Environmental, Social, Governance) related requests
  • You will liaise with Sustainability, Access, Safety Health & Environment, Legal, and Communications on sustainability and governance topics
  • Manage all ESG content on the IR homepage Who you are: You have a strong life science/medical background with working experience either in the pharmaceutical industry, pharma consulting or insights businesses, or financial industry (Pharma/Biotech financial analyst). You enjoy working in a highly dedicated team with the opportunity to gain deep insights in all aspects of investor relations at a world-leading, science-driven pharmaceutical company with great opportunities for professional development.
  • Minimum 5 years of working experience in the pharmaceutical industry (e.g. business development, scientific/medical communication, competitive intelligence, M&D, R&D etc.) or in an associated field (e.g. Pharma consultancy, insights business, financial analyst in investment banking)
  • Strong interest in the trends shaping the pharmaceutical and biotech industry and in capital markets (e.g. understanding company valuations)
  • Ability to network and liaise with a wide variety of groups within the company (e.g. R&D, product teams, communication, finance) to obtain and share relevant information
  • Excellent narrative development skills with an ability to explain scientific and business concepts alike
  • You have developed presentations on scientific, industry or business related topics and have excellent proficiency in Excel, Power Point / Google slides
  • Excellent English language skills (written and spoken), German
