Full description

Do you have a strong experience in the analytical development of synthetic drug substances with a specialization for peptides? Then you might be the team member who we are looking for!

Your responsibilities

  • You serve as the Analytical SME/analytical project lead (APL), driving the development of state-of-the-art analytical control packages for synthetic molecules, including direct materials, IPCs, intermediates, and crude APIs.
  • You actively contribute to portfolio delivery by ensuring efficient and effective process development analytics, including the adoption of modern techniques like modeling and simulation.
  • You coach technicians and junior scientists on project work and serving as a central point of contact for project-related information
  • You supervise internal laboratory activities and / or testing activities at CLOs/CDMOs
  • You collaborate cross-functionally with key partners in research and early development, Regulatory, and Quality to ensure seamless project execution and alignment.
  • You author, review, and approve critical documents and regulatory dossiers (e.g., QIMPD, NDA) while ensuring compliance with quality standards and data integrity.
  • You act as a Chemistry Project Lead (CPL), leading external testing activities at CDMOs or CLOs.

Your background

  • A Master's or Ph.D. in chemistry, pharmacy, or a related field.
  • Min. 5 years of professional experience within the analytical development of synthetic peptides.
  • You have a robust foundation in liquid chromatography (HPLC and UHPLC) with proficiency in various detection methods, including UV, Mass Spectrometry (MS), fluorescence, and charged aerosol detection.
  • You possess a solid understanding of analytical chemistry, particularly chromatographic techniques (HPLC, GC), titrations, and various physicochemical methods.
  • You demonstrate excellent collaboration, technical supervision, and project management skills, with a structured and rigorous approach
  • Digital acumen to support digital transformation initiatives; familiarity with statistical analysis of analytical data is also advantageous
