Full description

  • Independent conduct of a general internistic walk-in consultation for clarification and treatment of acute medical complaints
  • Participation in the establishment and continuous development of the walk-in clinic
  • Family medical care for patients with a longer-term connection to the Pain Clinic Basel
  • Consultant internist care for outpatients
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with the departments of the Pain Clinic Basel
  • Specialist doctor Allg. Internal Medicine (FMH or equivalent)
  • Additional ability card Manual medicine, sports medicine, abdominal sonography, neural therapy or psychosomatics beneficial but not condition
  • Enjoyment of working in an interdisciplinary team
  • Very good knowledge of German, foreign language skills are a plus
  • Strong team skills and high quality awareness in patient care
  • In-house laboratory and X-ray, CT, ultrasound, stress ECG and DEXA
  • Multimodal therapy possible with psychologists, physiotherapy and nutritional counselling
  • A competent, motivated and helpful team
  • Independent work with a high level of personal responsibility, co-design of treatment processes
  • Development opportunities in an expanding working environment
  • Very good connection to public transport
  • 5-week holidays and regulated working hours
  • Various discount offers from the Pain Clinic Basel and the Swiss Medical Network Group
  • Discounts via Swibeco (Coop, Zalando, Digitec, Ikea etc.)
  • Free mineral water, coffee and other warm drinks

Become part of a new care offer of the Pain Clinic Basel. The Pain Clinic Basel is consistently developing its outpatient range of services. In addition to our established interdisciplinary range of treatments, a walk-in clinic is currently being developed that offers patients uncomplicated access to internistic acute care. For the development of this new area, we are looking for a committed personality with pleasure in design, innovation and interdisciplinary cooperation.
