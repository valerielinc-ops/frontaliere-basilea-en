Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our Global Accounting Team in Bubendorf/BL, we are looking for a Group Treasury & Corporate Finance Manager (a), 90-100%.

They support the implementation of the Bachem Group’s global liquidity and financing strategy. They ensure access to capital, manage financial risks and support management in strategic decisions regarding financing, capital structure and financial management of the Group. In addition, you will take on selected tasks in the areas of corporate finance and group accounting and support the preparation and analysis of the Group’s consolidated financial reporting. They also support the responsible insurance department and act as the central contact person for insurance issues, policy management and claims within the group.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for short- and long-term Group-wide liquidity planning
  • Management of the Group’s cash management, further development of cash forecasts and treasury processes
  • Monitoring worldwide banking relationships and bank accounts
  • Introduction of a Treasury Management System (TMS)
  • Analysis of foreign exchange and interest rate risks and monitoring of Treasury policy
  • Supervision of the syndicated credit agreement
  • Monitoring of covenants and financing indicators and support for future financing transactions
  • Support for Group consolidation according to Swiss GAAP FER
  • Cooperation in cash flow, financing and balance sheet analyses
  • Support and further development of the global insurance portfolio
  • Coordination of claims cases
  • Cooperation with brokers and insurers
  • Support for international insurance issues

Your profile

  • Further studies in finance, accounting, economics, auditing or comparable training
  • Treasury certification or training beneficial
  • Several years of experience in the corporate treasury of an international company
  • Experience in cash management, financing, relationship management with banks, risk analysis
  • Good knowledge of group accounting and consolidation processes
  • SAP and Treasury System Experience Benefits
  • Insurance knowledge is beneficial
  • High willingness to take responsibility, flexibility and team spirit
  • Motivation to actively shape processes and drive change
  • Structured, independent and service-oriented working methods
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing, French is an advantage

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
