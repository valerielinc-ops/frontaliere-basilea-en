The Position The Opportunity

We are looking for a highly motivated, organized, and people-centric Talent Acquisition Partner Intern to join our Human Resources team. In this role, you will gain hands-on experience in full-cycle recruiting. You will work closely with our Senior Talent Acquisition Partners to attract, engage, and hire top talent while ensuring a world-class candidate experience.

This internship is designed to give you a comprehensive understanding of how a modern corporate recruiting engine operates, making it the perfect launchpad for a career in HR or Talent Acquisition.

Key responsibilities include

  • Candidate Sourcing: Assist in identifying and engaging top talent using various platforms, including LinkedIn Recruiter, job boards, and university alumni networks.
  • Job Postings: Help draft, format, and publish engaging job descriptions across multiple channels.
  • Resume Screening: Review inbound applications and resumes to identify candidates who meet the basic qualifications for open roles.
  • Interview Coordination: Schedule phone, video, and in-person interviews, acting as the primary point of contact for candidates and ensuring a seamless, positive experience.
  • ATS Management: Help maintain our Applicant Tracking System (i.e. Workday) by keeping candidate profiles updated and ensuring data accuracy.
  • Reporting & Analytics: Assist the TA team in tracking key recruitment metrics (e.g., time-to-fill, source of hire) to optimize our hiring strategies.

Who You Are

We are committed to your professional development, offering 1:1 mentorship from seasoned Talent Acquisition professionals alongside a hybrid work environment with flexible hours designed to work around your class schedule.

We are looking for someone who…

  • Is currently enrolled in, or recently (in the last 12 months) graduated from, a Master’s program in Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, Communications, Life Sciences or a related field.
  • Possesses strong verbal and written communication skills in English; conversational German is strongly preferred.
  • Has exceptional organizational skills and high attention to detail.
  • Demonstrates a proactive, self-starter mentality with a strong eagerness to learn.
  • Displays a high level of empathy and a genuine passion for connecting with people.
  • Navigates Google Workspace and Microsoft Office applications with foundational proficiency.
  • Bonus: Gained previous experience in a customer-focused environment.
