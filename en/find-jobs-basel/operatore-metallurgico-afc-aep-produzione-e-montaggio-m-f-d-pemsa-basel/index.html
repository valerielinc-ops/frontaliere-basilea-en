Do you like working with metal, do you have a good dexterity and want to exploit your skills in varied projects? PEMSA Basel seeks, on behalf of one of our clients, a motivated metal mechanic with EFZ / EBA (m/f/d) qualification for assignments in Basel and in the canton of Basel Country. Whether it's in the workshop or on the construction site, you will work on different metal structures and, together with your team, you will ensure high quality results. Shipments

  • Construction and assembly of metal and steel structures
  • Production and processing of railings, stairs, doors and other metal components
  • Cutting, drilling, sanding and adapting metal profiles
  • Welding and assembly work according to your qualifications
  • Assembly of metal structures in the yards
  • Work according to technical drawings and mounting patterns
  • Control of dimensional accuracy and performance quality

Profile requested

  • Training completed as AFC metallurgical worker, AFC metallurgical technician or equivalent qualification
  • Vocational experience in the metallurgical sector
  • Manual skills and technical understanding
  • Knowledge in metal processing and assembly
  • Ability to make technical drawings based on received training
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Approach to reliable, accurate and team-oriented work
  • Category B licence is preferential title

  • Activities throughout Switzerland
  • Expert and competent staff consultants
