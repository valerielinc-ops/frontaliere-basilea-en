Full description

Together with our customers, we connect the real and the digital world. As one of the leading technology companies, Siemens employs around 6,000 people in Switzerland. With our groundbreaking solutions in the areas of building technology, energy, mobility, health and industry, we are shaping the future. In this leadership role, you take responsibility for a dedicated customer service team in the area of security and actively shape the further development of our service organization. If you want to lead people, ensure operational excellence and combine customer proximity with entrepreneurial thinking, you are at the right place with us. Apply as Team Leader: in Security 80-100% and start immediately or by appointment – your team is looking forward to you. Your new tasks

  • You will lead and develop a customer service team in the area of security in the Reinach region in terms of expertise, organization and personnel.
  • You ensure reliable, customer-oriented and economical order and service processing – from planning to coordination to successful implementation.
  • You are responsible for the professional, scheduled and commercial achievement of your team’s goals and ensure transparency in quality, costs, deadlines and resources.
  • You plan and control the human and material resources and ensure optimal utilization and forward-looking assignment planning.
  • You promote the development of your employees, strengthen cooperation in the team and create an environment in which personal responsibility and continuous improvement are lived.
  • You review existing processes, identify potential for optimization and implement improvements together with your team and across all areas.
  • You work closely with internal interfaces, sales, project organization and customers and thus contribute significantly to customer satisfaction and the success of our security organization. Your talents and experiences
  • You have completed an apprenticeship in electrical, security or building technology or an IT education with relevant further education at the level of HF/FH/NDS/CAS – or bring a comparable qualification.
  • You have several years of experience in the service or project environment, ideally in security, building or electrical engineering.
  • You bring leadership experience or are ready to take the next development step into a leadership role.
  • You understand business contexts, work in a structured way and make decisions with regard to quality, customer benefit and profitability.
  • You are a resilient, committed and communicative personality who motivates people, takes responsibility and shapes change constructively.
  • You work safely with MS Office and use digital tools efficiently in everyday management and work.
  • You communicate very well in German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of English and/or another national language are beneficial. Your advantages
  • Your own electric car (which you can also use privately)
  • Independent, flexible working
  • Many further development and development opportunities
  • Access to employee stock plans
