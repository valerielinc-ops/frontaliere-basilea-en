Full description

Maternity replacement: Wed and Fri(full day) 10.6L ISF Buus-Maisprach district school Buus Fixed-term contract from 4.1.2027 to 3.7.2027

Your responsibility

  • As a teacher of special support, you are responsible for pupils in the 2. Cycle and work in the 5th and 6. Class closely with the class teacher.
  • They usually teach in team teaching with the class teacher or in a half-class setting.
  • They define goals, forms of work and content together with the class teachers.
  • Close cooperation with the class teachers, the staff and the school management is very important to us at our rather small school.

Your Background

  • You have training for the primary level / 2nd cycle.
  • It is important to us that you teach with enthusiasm, that you care about the well-being of the children and that you appreciate working in a team.

Your advantages with us

Employees at the center

Appreciation, respect and reliability characterize our cooperation. Because our employees are important to us, we protect and promote their health and ensure safety in the workplace.

Shaping the future for the general public

We create added value for society. Working for the canton means being close to the population and doing something for them. Political processes often play an important role in this.

Flexible working hours

The model of annual working hours allows personal coordination of professional and non-professional activities. Flexible working hours and part-time work support individual life models. There are also interesting options for early or partial retirement.

Mobile working

In some cases, work can be done from home or from another location. This increases the flexibility of work organization and your work-life balance.

Generous for holidays and holidays

All employees are entitled to at least five weeks of vacation. In addition, there is the option of converting the 13th month's salary into additional vacation or taking unpaid leave.

Numerous professions in an exciting environment

We are the largest employer in the Basel region and have been employing specialists in a wide variety of professions since 1833. In doing so, we keep up with the times and also stay on the ball when it comes to digital development.

Occupational and social security

The canton offers secure jobs and is responsible for its employees. It is also characterised by solid social benefits.

Centrally located and well connected

The place of work is located in the middle of the Basel region and has convenient public transport connections. There are many opportunities to eat a variety of meals here, as well as great leisure and cultural activities to relax after work.

Fair and transparent wages

The canton lives its role model function and is clearly committed to a fair wage policy. In doing so, we use a transparent and comprehensible wage system. Good performance and committed work have a positive effect on personal wage development.

Work fully remunerated

Overtime is to be compensated. For this purpose, we record working hours.

Start of career

The high-quality apprenticeships in many different areas impart in-depth knowledge and enable a wide range of options for later careers. During the training to become a professional, we support language stays and the attendance of optional subjects. The annual apprentice camp is a special highlight.

Moving forward thanks to a long-term perspective

Our personnel planning is sustainable and designed for the long term. This includes in particular the promotion of the personal and professional development of employees, specialists and managers. Learn more about our terms and conditions of employment

Buus-Maisprach district school

The Buus-Maisprach district school has 9 classes with around 155 pupils, who are taught by 20 teachers. We offer a responsible and varied job in a school building in a beautiful location and in a motivated and committed team.
