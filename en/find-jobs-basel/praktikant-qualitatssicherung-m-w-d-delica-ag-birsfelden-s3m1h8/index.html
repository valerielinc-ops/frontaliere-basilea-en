This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you not only want to control quality, but also actively shape it? In this internship, you are in the middle of everyday production instead of just in the office. Together with production, technology and logistics, you ensure that Swiss quality products meet the highest standards. You take on responsibility, gain valuable practical experience and develop both professionally and personally.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Birsfelden, Hafenstrasse 120, 4127 Birsfelden

Important information

It is a one-year internship. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You support quality assurance in the production environment and work closely with production, technology and logistics.
  • You participate in quality projects and take on your own topics and projects depending on the level of training.
  • You carry out quality and hygiene tours as well as monitoring and document your findings in a comprehensible way.
  • You will assist with training, revise documents and help prepare quality assurance activities such as Product Safety Week.
  • You will work on quality deviations together with the specialist departments and help to develop sustainable and preventive solutions.

Requirements

  • First practical experience of advantage: In the food industry
  • Student (Uni/FH/HF): Bachelor's degree in food science or teaching as a food technologist
  • You work flexibly, reliably and accurately and keep track of changing priorities.
  • You think networked, openly approach people and find pragmatic solutions together with various specialist areas.
  • in German (negotiation certain)

  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items

Contatto

Application and contact Severine Jegge Recruitment Business Partners

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time afterwards to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 100 %
