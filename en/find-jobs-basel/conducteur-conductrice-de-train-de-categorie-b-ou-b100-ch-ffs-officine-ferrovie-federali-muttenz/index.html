Full description

SBB | SBB Cargo International: Driver in category B or B100 (CH) - - - SBB Cargo International is the market leader in European rail freight transport on the north-south axis. Thanks to our more than 1,000 employees in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Italy and France, we travel around 550 trains per week from the North Sea ports to Italy and back. In doing so, we are shifting thousands of net tonnes from road to rail and making an active contribution to more climate protection. Do you want to set the course for your future and take responsibility for European freight transport? Become part of our team as Drivers in category B or B100 (CH) In Muttenz

Their tasks

As a driver in Category B, you drive freight trains primarily on the Swiss network and, depending on your qualifications, also on the German and French networks. With your professional and responsible commitment, you make a significant contribution to a safe, reliable and punctual railway operation. You carry out brake tests, create the necessary brake documents and ensure a safe and energy-efficient driving style. In addition, you will recognize and correct irregularities and disturbances on the train vehicles used within the framework of your competences.

Your profile

You have a valid category B or B100 railway driving licence. With a category B100 qualification, you are ready to complete the category B training. In addition, you are willing to qualify for at least one other network (Germany and/or France). You work independently, reliably as well as solution- and performance-oriented. Even in demanding situations, you keep an overview and act responsibly. You have good reaction and coordination skills and have very good knowledge of German. Knowledge of Italian and French is an advantage. We presuppose the willingness to work shifts and assignments on weekends and public holidays.
