The Position What does our team look like? You start with us at Pharma Biotech in Penzberg. We manufacture drug active ingredients in the indication areas oncology (e.g. for the treatment of breast cancer) and cardiovascular diseases. In the functional unit Microbiology & Environmental Monitoring & Cleaning of quality control, the biotechnologically produced active ingredients and the process environment are tested for compliance with high hygiene standards and microbiological purity. The task of the “Quality Control Microbiology & Environmental Monitoring & Cleaning” unit is, among other things, to collect samples and carry out microbiological tests to check the active ingredients and the process environment.

In the function as laboratory worker Quality Control, you carry out the following activities:

  • Sampling in production laboratories and operations as part of environmental monitoring
  • GMP-compliant documentation of implementation
  • Organisation of sampling in the monitoring team
  • Performing hygiene plan work
  • Support for the nursery kitchen when needed

Who you are

You have completed vocational training and basic knowledge in microbiology and hygiene. (GMP skills and/or aseptic work are an advantage.) We are also open to non-specialist vocational training backgrounds. Technical understanding also distinguishes you.

In addition, the following aspects apply to you

  • Reliable and dutiful work
  • Good ability to integrate into a team and get involved
  • Helpful and open personality with an interest in working with various internal interfaces
  • Flexibility to adapt to changing circumstances and new, short-term orders
  • Confident use of digital tools and willingness to get to know new programs
  • Fluent oral and written knowledge of German
