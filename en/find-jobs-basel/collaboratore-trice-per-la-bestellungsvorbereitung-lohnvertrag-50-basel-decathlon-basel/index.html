Full description

Mission

Your future playing field

You will become part of the store in Basel, which opened in 2018 and consists of a team of 45 employees.

And now about your job: Are you looking for a dynamic job where no two days are alike? Would you like to share your passion for sport and offer every customer who enters the store an unforgettable experience? Do you love action, working on the sales floor and teamwork? Then the position as a sports advisor in sales is exactly right for you.

What you will do every day: Your tasks

  • Orders: you prepare orders in the store as quickly and efficiently as possible.
  • Availability and logistics: You pitch in! From receiving the delivery to stocking the shelves, you ensure the accuracy of your inventory and the quick availability of your products. You also prepare online orders.

This job is NOT for you if...

  • You would rather sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • You don't like spontaneously approaching others.
  • You think that unpacking pallets, stocking shelves and handling goods are "not your problem".
  • You are not prepared to see 10'000 steps on your watch at the end of the day.
  • You are looking for fixed, routine working hours: Here, we live to the rhythm of sport and our work schedules adapt to the activity.

Profile

What we expect from you

  • You have excellent interpersonal skills. Customer satisfaction is your compass.
  • You are bold, curious and always eager to learn.
  • You are organized, efficient and know how to set priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • You are a sports enthusiast and an active person: That is the company's DNA!
  • You have a perfect command of German. Good English/French skills are a real plus.

What we offer you

  • A flexible and secure hourly wage contract: As the name suggests, you will be paid by the hour. In other words: The more you work, the higher your salary! To give you security, we guarantee you a monthly minimum in any case (a workload agreed in the contract that varies between 20 % and 50 %). During peak periods, we may offer you the opportunity to work additional hours. Don't panic about your schedule: We will always let you know at least 48 hours in advance, and we only do so if your availability allows it!
  • Hourly wage: The wage is 25 CHF gross per hour. Compensation for public holidays and vacation, as well as the 13. Monthly salary (if you have an open-ended hourly wage contract). On each 15. of the month, you will be based on the hours worked in the previous month (between the 16. of the previous month and the 15. of the current month) paid.
  • Employee shares : Once a year, you can become a shareholder in the company. This is an excellent investment, as the value of the stock has never been negative since Decathlon was founded 50 years ago!
  • Passion for sport: We support your sporting activity! After 1 month of employment, benefit from a 25 % discount on your Decathlon purchases.
  • Further training: You take part in training courses to improve your technical knowledge about products, your sales techniques or your interpersonal communication.

Your career: With us, career development is no myth

You start with us in the store? You start as a salesperson? You can progress to department manager, take on a leadership role as an Operations Manager and even become a Store Manager! We have dozens of stories like his.

The application process: How do we get to know each other?

  • Your application: CV + answers to the short questionnaire (no cover letter required!).
  • The interview: A direct conversation with your future manager to see whether it’s a "match".
