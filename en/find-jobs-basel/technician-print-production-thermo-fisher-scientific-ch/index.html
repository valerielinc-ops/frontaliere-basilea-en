Technician, Print Production

Job Summary

The Technician, Print Production coordinates the execution of the label print process to ensure technical accuracy and manufacturing integrity. He identifies and ensures that the correct sizing, design and materials are utilized to create labels and booklets which meet end user specifications and packaging application needs.

The technician provides technical expertise on the label requirements. He verifies that job information, artwork, specifications and materials are complete and technically suitable for the intern manufacturing steps. Working closely with Project Management, Label Design and Operations.

The role works independently on standard and complex assignments and escalates quality, timing or technical risks when needed.

Responsibilities

  • Check sizing and layout to suit the container and application
  • Create and design new templates
  • Work with operation to design new tooling
  • Check materials and print set-ups
  • Responsible for creating and maintaining information database
  • Support intern teams with technical matters, join client meetings
  • Resolve routine issues; escalate risks to compliance or delivery
  • Support procedure updates, process improvements and colleague training
  • Apply Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) in all areas
  • Actively participate in PPl/4Is activities by identifying improvement opportunities and contributing to solutions

Minimum Qualifications

  • Knowledge of print processes or similar technical field
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office, InDesign and Illustrator
  • Ability to work independently on complex projects
  • Advanced knowledge of SOPs and quality standards

Education / Experience

  • Experience in artwork or technical drawings
  • Experience in print production, prepress, printshop or packaging is an asset
  • Requires professional degree, preferably in a print shop or technical field
  • Working knowledge in German and English
  • Experience in conducting client meetings
  • Requires good communication, organizational skills and project management skills
