Full description

Tasks

  • A well-founded three-year basic commercial education with a high practical relevance at the Office for Business and Bankruptcy in Liestal. Optimally complemented by the theory in the Commercial Vocational School and intercompany courses.
  • Develop your personality together so that you can take on responsibility in the workplace and gain self-confidence in everyday work.
  • Best possible career opportunities for your further career at the Cantonal Administration Basel-Landschaft or outside.
  • A varied apprenticeship: the tasks range from postal processing to customer advice at the counter to your own case handling and tasks to support the teams.
  • Our professional, dedicated vocational training specialists support you in achieving the learning goals so that you can successfully complete the apprenticeship after three years.

Requirements

  • Joy of contact with customers and other employees
  • Very good manners and expression in German
  • Interest in digital tasks and economic events
  • Motivation, willingness to learn and a positive basic attitude
  • Completed secondary school level P or good degree level E
  • Good to very good grading in German, English, French and Mathematics

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and fields, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
