This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You ensure that visitors, employees and partners feel welcome with us right from the start. With organizational talent, an overview and a pronounced service idea, you are the central point of contact at the reception and contribute to a positive experience every day.

Mansioni

  • You receive visitors, coordinate registrations and unsubscriptions and provide a professional first impression of our location.
  • You manage the telephone exchange, provide information and reliably forward requests to the right places.
  • You are responsible for bathroom management for internal and external employees and are the first point of contact for questions.
  • You take on a wide range of administrative tasks such as handling e-mails, mail and managing work equipment and orders.
  • You support the smooth process at the reception and are actively involved in organizational and administrative topics.

Requirements

  • Min. 5 years of professional experience: reception, customer service or administration beneficial
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): basic commercial education
  • Very good MS Office skills and good foreign language skills
  • You are a helpful, communicative and service-oriented personality with a well-groomed demeanor.
  • You work reliably, keep track even in hectic situations.
  • Pensum: 65% (50% regular assignments plus 15% holiday representation) - Wednesday: half day, Thursday and Friday: full day
  • Holiday representation in 100% allowance for about 7 weeks per year (outside of school holidays)
  • German (flowing)
  • English (very good knowledge)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Professional provision: Compared to the BVG, depending on age, 7% - 18% more is paid by Delica
  • Meals: Balanced and fine lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for only CHF 8.-
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Mobile subscriptions: Top prices on mobile subscriptions at Galaxus Mobile, Sunrise or Wingo – starting from CHF 10.- / month
  • Sports and fitness offers: Free use of the fitness studios integrated at the site in Buchs, Birsfelden and Meilen as well as CHF 200.- Refund on fitness annual subscriptions
  • Club School Migros: Up to 70% refund per course
  • Health insurance: Up to 25% discount at various insurance partner companies (CSS, Sanitas, AXA, Generali and many more)
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Over 50% discount on subscriptions in the Schinznach thermal bath, Sole Uno or Alpamare adventure park
