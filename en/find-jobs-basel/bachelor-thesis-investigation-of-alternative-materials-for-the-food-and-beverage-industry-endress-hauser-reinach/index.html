Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a dedicated personality to study alternative materials to complement our global team and make a significant contribution to more sustainable solutions in the food and beverage industry. With your work you help to evaluate materials and solutions that can be used in hygienic sensitive applications in the future, i.e. exactly where quality and safety play a central role for all of us in everyday life. What is the point about? Do you want to find out which materials are really sustainable in food production? Does it appeal to you to work with specialist areas and partners and translate their experiences into concrete recommendations? And do you want to make a real contribution to more sustainable industrial solutions with your thesis? An exciting topic awaits you, in which you combine theory and practice and can directly influence future applications. What tasks will you take on? You will:

  • Conduct surveys and evaluations of alternative materials based on stakeholder feedback (online and on site).
  • Capture current F&B applications in plants and processes in the food and beverage industry.
  • make sound recommendations. What do we expect from you? You:
  • studies industrial engineering, food technology, food science, process engineering, business administration or a comparable degree program.
  • are interested in topics related to sustainable materials, industrial applications or product management.
  • are communicative and willing to travel on a small scale to gain first-hand insight.
  • have good knowledge of German and English in words and writing.
  • safely deal with common MS Office applications.
  • work independently, are open to new things, learn quickly and bring a good communication skills. What can you expect from us?
  • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport. How do you apply? To apply, please upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons. Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding. Apply now.
