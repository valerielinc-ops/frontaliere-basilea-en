Full description

Automation ensures the reliable availability and further development of our technical plants and systems and thus forms an important basis for smooth production and logistics operations. You will oversee modern automation and server systems, support projects from optimization to the integration of new plants and work closely with production, technology and IT. If you are enthusiastic about technology, like to take on responsibility and are looking for a varied job in a committed team, you are a perfect fit for us.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: day shift
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Alsatian road 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • Support and assurance of the technical availability of automation systems at the Basel site
  • Support and further development of the superimposed server systems in the field of automation and operational technology (HMI, SCADA, PLS)
  • Interface function between user, IT, technology and suppliers
  • Implementation, support of projects in the area of automation (e.g. optimization, adaptation, extension, modernization) on the plants and systems
  • Integration of plants, systems and components into the structure of the Bell Group
  • Support for the commissioning and production support of highly complex plants
  • Contribute your know-how for the technical support of the various maintenance and project teams

Requirements

  • Completed training as a technician preferably with a preliminary training as an electrician, electronics engineer, automatist or computer scientist
  • Professional experience in the programming and commissioning of PLC and visualization systems, such as Siemens S7 (Step 7 Classic / TIA Portal) and WinCC, as well as in fieldbus systems such as ProfiNet etc.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good knowledge of English in word and writing, French is an advantage
  • Experience with IT systems (Windows Client, Server, Networks)
  • Experience in the implementation and coordination of automation projects
