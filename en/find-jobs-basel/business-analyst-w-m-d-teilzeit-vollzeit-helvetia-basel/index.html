Full description

Business Analysis / Requirements Engineering Busy: 50-100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • You are responsible for requirements management for the further development and maintenance of our core insurance systems, in particular Adcubum Syrius
  • You take on professional requirements, design solutions, prioritize requirements and accompany their implementation until successful introduction
  • You will analyze, model and document business requirements in the field of accident and health insurance
  • You translate professional needs into clear IT requirements and evaluate solution variants in terms of functionality, quality and cost-effectiveness
  • You coordinate test activities, create test concepts and test cases and accompany releases until successful commissioning
  • Through tests and quality assurance, you ensure that new requirements are implemented reliably and sustainably
  • You act as a central interface between departments, IT and external partners and ensure optimal coordination between all parties involved
  • You actively participate in projects and coordinate topics across different systems and stakeholders

What you bring

  • You have a degree or a higher education in business administration, business informatics, computer science or a comparable subject area
  • You bring at least five years of experience in business analysis, business engineering or requirements engineering in an agile environment
  • You know the operational processes of an insurance company, ideally with a focus on existing and financial processes as well as property, service or commission processes
  • You have experience with industry platforms and core insurance systems, ideally with Adcubum Syrius
  • You have knowledge of the accident and health insurance industry as well as its specific processes and requirements
  • You convince with a high IT affinity, profound knowledge in databases and database queries as well as experience in the creation of technical specifications
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and have the motivation to become involved in complex end-to-end processes and new topics.
  • You communicate stylishly in German, have good knowledge of English and understand how to coordinate complex requirements with different stakeholders.
