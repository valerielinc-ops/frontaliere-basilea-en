02

Your responsibilities

  • You actively support customers in using and optimizing software solutions that are used in public institutions as well as companies.
  • You handle inquiries and incidents in first- and second-level support, analyze technical problems, and systematically forward more complex cases to the development department.
  • You reproduce error cases in test environments, carry out configuration changes, and use your knowledge to create database queries and ensure data quality.
  • You develop and implement process improvements, deploy automation and thus ensure a continuous increase in efficiency in support.
  • You take on your own projects, work closely with other teams and support the introduction of new system functions and test runs.

04

Your profile

  • You have completed training in IT or an equivalent technical qualification and gained initial professional experience in supporting software applications.
  • You have a strong interest in combining technical and organizational challenges, and would like to further develop your knowledge of databases and automation.
  • You have an analytical mindset, can approach complex problems in a structured manner and find practical solutions.
  • You are communicative, customer-oriented and enjoy direct interaction with customers to resolve their concerns professionally.
  • You have very good German language skills as well as good French language skills.
  • You bring a positive, solution-oriented attitude and value collaboration in a dynamic team.
