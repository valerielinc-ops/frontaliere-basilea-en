Full description

Are you passionate about food and have a talent for sales? Then you've come to the right place. As a Food Sales Assistant, you are responsible for the assortment in the branch and shape our customers' shopping experience with your appealing product presentations.

Duties

  • Managing the food department
  • Product presentation and maintenance of assortments
  • Responsibility for order and cleanliness in the work area
  • Deployment in the warehouse

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in retail, food sector
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail
  • Customer-oriented and friendly demeanor
  • Independence and flexibility
  • Good specialist knowledge in the food sector
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Occupational pension: Pension fund with above-average benefits, employee contribution 8.5%, employer contribution 17%
  • Social benefits: In case of illness, salary continuation is 100% for two years; in case of accident, we are insured with SUVA
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounted or free cultural offers
