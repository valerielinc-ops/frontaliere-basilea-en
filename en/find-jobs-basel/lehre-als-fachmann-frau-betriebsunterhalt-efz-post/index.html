Apprenticeship as a Facility Maintenance Specialist EFZ — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you skilled with your hands, are you motivated by varied tasks and do you love being outdoors? Then an apprenticeship in facility maintenance is exactly right for you! During the three-year basic training, you will acquire the skills you need for a successful start in the world of work.

Your training

  • From the very beginning, you will work as part of the team and help with facility maintenance at Post and partner companies.
  • As an all-rounder, you will ensure that the infrastructure used by Post employees is functional, clean and orderly.
  • You maintain and clean buildings, machines and outdoor facilities, monitor building services, carry out repairs, rectify structural damage, maintain green spaces and deal with waste disposal.
  • During your training, you will complete, among other things, a placement at a horticultural business and acquire the knowledge needed to maintain green spaces.

What you bring

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, enjoyment and motivation who want to make a difference. You should meet the following requirements:

  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • You enjoy manual work.
  • You have a good understanding of practical and technical matters.
  • Reliability and the ability to work independently are among your strengths.
  • You are physically resilient and enjoy interacting with people.
