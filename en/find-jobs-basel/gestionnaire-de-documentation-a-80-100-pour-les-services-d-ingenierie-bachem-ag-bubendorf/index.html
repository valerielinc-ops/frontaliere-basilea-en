Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in Engineering Services, we are looking for a dedicated Documentation Manager (a) (80 -100%) Their tasks:

  • You are responsible for GMP-compliant and efficient filing and management of project and inventory documentation for production and infrastructure facilities as well as building documentation (in paper form and electronic filing).
  • You check documents for accuracy and completeness and where applicable to GMP compliance
  • You work in a project team (matrix organization) and support in the procurement / creation of documents and services (such as external calibrations)
  • For government and customer audits, you are responsible for the rapid provision of the documents of our engineering department.
  • Through continuous maintenance and updating, you ensure continuous improvement of documentation storage and processes.
  • Using an electronic documentation management system as a key user Your profile:
  • You have basic technical or scientific training
  • Ideally, you have experience in documentation management / storage and are used to handling technical documents and certificates (e.g. FDA / ATEX, etc.).
  • They have experience in the regulated environment, ideally GMP. The application of the MS-Office range (especially Excel) does not cause you any difficulties.
  • They can easily navigate interdisciplinary teams and have good knowledge of German and English.
  • You already have experience in using Documentation Management Systems Our offer:
  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
