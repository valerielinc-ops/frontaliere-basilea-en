The Position The organization “Site Basel/Kaiseraugst” as part of the global function “Corporate Strategy, Sustainability & Sites” provides the infrastructure for the entire site and continuously develops it further. We work with all stakeholders along the entire value chain – from early research to patient care. With us, you can expect a modern organisational model with a lot of room for manoeuvre, freedom of choice and personal responsibility.

In the area of "Business Solutions", we design tailor-made solutions together with our stakeholders. This also includes the operational and project engineering services of the “Pharma Engineering Drug Substance” group.

As a Senior Qualification Lead, you take over both the professional management of the strategically important sub-circle “Drug Substance Qualification” (Matrix Lead / approximately 30 members) and the operational responsibility for the services and services provided by this circle.

The services of the circle include the preparation of qualification / validation documentation taking into account all internal and official GMP requirements, the maintenance of established qualification processes, the presentation and representation of qualifications and CSV validations during audits and inspections as well as the management and implementation of qualification / validation activities for our various internal business partners in the field of drug substances at Site Basel.

In your role, you lead the circle professionally and act at the interfaces to peers and internal partners. You proactively contribute with your personal wealth of experience to clarify and present technical GMP topics in a sustainable way. The preparation and communication of concepts, issues and decisions in word and writing - in English and German - is one of your strengths.

The possibility

  • Professional management, strategic orientation and further development of the circle "Drug Substance Qualification" including resource and competence development.
  • Consulting and partnership-based support of internal business partners in all qualification and CSV issues.
  • Development of strategies and concepts for qualification, validation and review activities.
  • Management, implementation and support of qualification/validation activities in investment projects as well as representation in audits and inspections.

Who you are

You have completed technical studies (FH/TU) as well as in-depth experience (> 5 years) in the GxP/Qualification/CSV environment. In addition, you will bring several years of management experience and in-depth know-how in operational engineering and the handling of investment projects in the pharmaceutical industry.

You bring the following skills

  • Effective Leadership & Empowerment: Proven strength to lead and empower interdisciplinary teams of experts and securely steer decisions across all levels.
  • Strategic agility & process simplification: Pronounced strength in complexity reduction and a strong drive to see and use changing government requirements as an opportunity for a future-oriented realignment of processes and concepts.
  • Target-oriented communication: Quick understanding for precise communication of technical issues to stakeholders, internal partners and authorities (Swissmedic, FDA, EMA).
  • Collaboration & Agility: High social skills, team spirit and enthusiasm for flat, agile organizational structures.
  • Personal responsibility and focus on results: Pragmatic, reliable working methods with a clear focus on priorities and measurable added value.
  • Project Management & Compliance: Sound methodological knowledge and secure application of relevant regulations and guidelines.
  • Language Competence: Negotiable German and English in word and writing.
