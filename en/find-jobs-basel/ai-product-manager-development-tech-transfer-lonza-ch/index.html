AI Product Manager – Development & Tech Transfer Location: Basel, Switzerland

Leads AI products and opportunities across Development & Technology Transfer, combining strong understanding of biopharmaceutical process development and manufacturing with AI product leadership. Works across modalities and functions to identify where AI can improve process understanding, development, scale-up, technology transfer and manufacturing readiness, and orchestrates cross-functional Fusion Teams to deliver measurable outcomes in partnership with business product owners and scientific SMEs

What you will get

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • A variety of benefits tailored to your localality.
  • Access to global benefits via https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

What you will do

  • Understands the end-to-end development and technology transfer lifecycle, from process development and characterization through scale-up, transfer, validation and manufacturing readiness.
  • Works with scientists, engineers and manufacturing SMEs to identify where AI can improve process understanding, prediction and decision-making, rather than simply digitizing existing activities.
  • Translates scientific and process challenges into AI product requirements, working closely with AI Engineering, Data Science, Data and platform teams.
  • Plans and facilitates delivery rhythm of the Fusion Team assigned to an AI opportunity, including maintaining a prioritized backlog focused on high-value work
  • Drives the opportunity through defined lifecycle stages, ensuring required deliverables and evidence are ready for stage-gate decisions
  • Develops continuously own AI product & delivery expertise, staying current with relevant practices and sharing insights with other AI Product Managers
  • Supports the AI Product Lead with roadmap alignment & stakeholder reporting, keeping AI opportunity progress & priorities visible across the organization

What we are looking for

  • Experience in Process Development, MSAT, Technology Transfer, Manufacturing Science/Engineering or a closely related biopharma area
  • Experience with digital, data or AI-enabled products/solutions
  • Strong understanding of biopharmaceutical process development, scale-up and technology transfer
  • Able to work credibly with scientists, engineers, MSAT and manufacturing teams
  • Comfortable working across different modalities and identifying opportunities for reuse
  • Strong scientific and analytical mindset
  • Able to challenge assumptions and connect patterns across processes and modalities

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
