Role overview

Tasks

  • Participation in the organization and implementation of the annual IT-based application process for a bridge offer.
  • Administrative, individual examination of applications for a bridge offer of about 700 young people every year, partly in exchange with teachers and other participants, for example the disability insurance.
  • Providing telephone and written information about the bridge offers as well as conducting telephone consultations of young people, parents, teachers, training companies, authorities, institutions.
  • Cooperation with the various providers of bridge offers.

Requirements

  • Training Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ
  • Experiences dealing with young people in complex life situations
  • Knowledge in vocational studies and the educational landscape of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft

The Bridge Offers Coordination Office is part of the Main Vocational Training Department. Together with the other training contributions, vocational integration, occupational training, this department is responsible for vocational training and integration into vocational training.
