Do you want to take care of your patients in the long term and personally – and at the same time keep enough space for family and private life? With our client in the Basel area, you can expect an outpatient activity with a lot of room for manoeuvre and a workload that can be based on your living situation. Our client is a newly established and modernly furnished group practice in the Basel area, in which several outpatient disciplines work together under one roof. The team is consciously personal: Doctors, doctors and MPAs know each other well, support each other and maintain an uncomplicated approach at eye level. For the gynecological consultation, there is already a patient strain that has grown over many years. An experienced gynecologist accompanies the practice during a transition phase, so that a gradual takeover is well possible. At the same time, the demand in the region is high, which gives you the opportunity to further develop your own patient base according to the desired workload. Part-time as full-time is possible. Holiday planning is also handled in a family-friendly and flexible manner. The practice is located directly next to a train station and can be reached by fast train in just under 20 minutes from Basel. Entry is possible by early 2027 or by arrangement.

Their tasks

  • You independently conduct your outpatient gynecological and obstetric consultation
  • They provide long-term and personal care for patients across different life stages
  • You gradually take over an existing patient base and develop it according to your workload
  • Upon request, it is possible to work as a doctor with a hospital partner in the region
  • You are interested in the further development of the gynecological offer

Your Competences

  • You have a specialist title in gynecology and obstetrics or an appropriate recognised qualification
  • You have at least three years of professional experience at a recognised continuing education institution in Switzerland
  • Pregnancy ultrasound is not a requirement
  • You appreciate the independent outpatient work and long-term care of your patients
  • You work independently, empathetically and gladly in a family, interdisciplinary team

Their advantages

  • Part-time or full-time with individually designed workload
  • Family-Friendly Holiday Planning, Even With Longer Contiguous Holidays
  • Existing patient base with additional growth potential
  • Choice between sales-based compensation or fixed wage model
  • Evidence medical activity possible on request
  • New practice spaces, short decision paths and very good public transport connections
