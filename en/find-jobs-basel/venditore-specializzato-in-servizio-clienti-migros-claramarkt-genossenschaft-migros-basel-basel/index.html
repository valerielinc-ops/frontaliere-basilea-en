Full description

As a specialist salesperson* in customer service at Migros Claramarkt, you are the first point of contact for our customers. With your service-oriented and solution-strong nature, you handle issues competently and ensure a positive customer experience.

Mansioni

  • Care for customers in a friendly and competent manner
  • Processing requests, complaints and returns
  • Handle cash, exchange and refund processes correctly
  • Responsibility for presenting and ensuring availability of flowers

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in the retail trade

  • Customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales
  • Sound expertise in the field of flowers and floristics
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • Engagement in customer contact
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo
