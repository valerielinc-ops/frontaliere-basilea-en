As a Analytics & Intelligence - Senior Analyst ( Digital Marketing Analyst) , you will support the marketing team by building and maintaining reports and dashboards within Adobe Experience Platform and the Adobe Experience Cloud. You will focus on organizing data, tracking key metrics, and delivering clear insights that help teams monitor performance and make informed decisions across digital channels.

Your Opportunity

  • Build and maintain dashboards and reports within Adobe Experience Platform and Adobe Experience Cloud
  • Work with marketing teams to translate business requirements into clear reporting and visualization outputs
  • Collect, organize, and validate customer and campaign data across digital channels (web, email, paid media)
  • Support KPI definition and ensure proper tracking is implemented across campaigns
  • Analyze email campaign performance (open rate, CTR, bounce, conversions) and provide actionable recommendations to improve engagement and effectiveness
  • Analyze performance data to identify trends and provide actionable insights
  • Ensure data accuracy by validating tracking and resolving issues using debugging tools
  • Support A/B testing analysis and share performance insights with stakeholders
  • Collaborate with marketing, product, and tech teams to improve data capture and reporting
  • Mentor junior analysts and guide stakeholders on reporting best practices

Who you are

  • You have a Bachelor’s Degree (computer science, engineering or other technical disciplines) with 4–7 years of experience in digital analytics or marketing analytics. Preferably, you have a Master's degree
  • Strong hands-on experience with:
  • Adobe Experience Platform
  • Adobe Analytics
  • Adobe Experience Manager
  • Solid understanding of digital marketing channels and performance metrics
  • Experience analyzing email campaign performance (open rate, CTR, bounce rate, conversions) and deriving actionable insights
  • Experience analyzing campaign performance (CTR, conversion, engagement, etc.)
  • Familiarity with:
  • Google Tag Manager
  • Adobe Launch
  • Strong SQL skills and proficiency in Excel / Google Sheets
  • Strong attention to detail with a focus on data accuracy
  • Good communication skills and ability to work with cross-functional teams
  • Experience in healthcare/life sciences is a plus
