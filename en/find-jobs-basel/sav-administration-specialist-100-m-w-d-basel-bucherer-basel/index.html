Full description

Your role with us

  • They support our local and international customers at the reception of the watch service and offer a personal service at the highest level
  • They record the watches in our internal repair system and ensure correct processing of orders
  • You take over the administrative and logistical handling of the repair orders
  • You create cost estimates and invoices and track ongoing repairs daily
  • They coordinate the cooperation between the watch service, our suppliers and manufacturers as well as external partners and internal bodies
  • You order the required materials and accessories for the watch service
  • You process all orders in accordance with our values, guidelines and internal guidelines

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training in the retail trade with a focus on the watch industry
  • Sound expertise in the field of watches and several years of professional experience
  • Pronounced service and customer orientation as well as pleasure in personal advice
  • Very good communication skills and a high sense of responsibility
  • Independent, structured and precise working methods as well as strong organisational skills
  • Analytical thinking, initiative and flexibility
  • Experienced handling of ERP systems and the common Microsoft Office programs
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, other languages are an advantage
