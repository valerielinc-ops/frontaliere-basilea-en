Full description

Opening of the third living area Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. In January 2026, the Senevita Birsgarten opened in Reinach /BL. We offer a total of 88 care places (22 protected department), 52 apartments for assisted living and a restaurant in an idyllic location with very good public transport connections.We are looking for a motivated and team-oriented personality as residential management 80-100 %
