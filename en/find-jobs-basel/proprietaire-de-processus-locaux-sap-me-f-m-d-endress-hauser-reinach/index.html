Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Local Process Owner SAP-ME to complement our team and actively shape the digital development of our production processes. In this key role, you will contribute to the successful implementation of global standards in the manufacturing execution environment at the Reinach site and to shape the production of tomorrow together with various specialist areas. Flowtec AG develops and produces innovative flow meters for customers worldwide. At our site in Reinach, you will work in a modern production environment that combines technology, quality and continuous improvement.

What is this point about?

Do you want to actively promote the digitalization of modern production? Does it appeal to you to implement and further develop global SAP ME standards locally? Do you like working at the interface between production, quality, industrialization and IT? In this role, you actively shape the further development of our manufacturing execution landscape and ensure that digital solutions create tangible added value for our production. They are part of a local production function and work closely with global teams of experts and internal stakeholders.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Implement and further develop global standards for SAP Manufacturing Execution at the Reinach site and ensure their sustainable application in production.
  • Drive ME projects and digitalization initiatives on their own responsibility, from requirements analysis to tests to successful implementation at the site.
  • Analyze, optimize and standardize production processes in the SAP ME environment and develop practical solutions together with production, industrialization, quality assurance and IT.
  • Coordinate system changes, releases and updates, perform, document and release user acceptance tests.
  • Ensure compliance with quality and compliance requirements and enable 1st-level support and participate as 2nd-level support in the event of complex disruptions.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed studies in the field of industrial engineering, business informatics or a comparable qualification.
  • bring experience in production processes as well as in dealing with MES systems, ideally SAP ME, and ERP systems.
  • have knowledge of lean manufacturing, process analysis and continuous improvement.
  • work analytically, structured and solution-oriented and like to implement ideas pragmatically.
  • demonstrate strong IT affinity as well as good communication and project management skills.
  • communicate securely in German and English and work successfully with different stakeholders.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We value a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working hours and the opportunity for mobile work.
  • We offer you a modern working environment with innovative technologies and short decision-making paths.
  • We ensure that our employees work in ultra-modern premises with ergonomic workplaces.
  • We promote an open and trusting corporate culture that focuses on cooperation, responsibility and personal development.
