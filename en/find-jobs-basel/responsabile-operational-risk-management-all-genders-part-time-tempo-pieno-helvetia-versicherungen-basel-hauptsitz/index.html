Full description

Actuarial Services / Risk,IT / Computer Science Place of work: Level of experience: Management FlexOffice: on-site As Head of Operational Risk Management, you will lead the development and continuous enhancement of our Group-wide operational risk framework. In this senior leadership role directly reporting to the Group CRO, you will set the strategic direction for operational risk management and ensure effective governance and controls across the teams of qualitative risk management, ICT and third-party risks, information security, data protection, BCM and physical security. Working closely with senior management, Risk Committees, local Risk Management functions and other key stakeholders, you will help ensure that operational risks are identified, assessed and managed consistently across the Group. In this broad Group-level leadership role you will have the opportunity to set strategic direction, shape tour operational risk management and establish common standards across the organisation while working with stakeholders across functions and business units.

What you'll be doing

  • Lead the Group-wide Operational Risk Management function, including the design, implementation, assessment, assurance, reporting and continuous improvement of the operational risk framework
  • Define and further develop the operational risk strategy, risk appetite and limits, ensuring appropriate governance and consistent Group-wide application
  • Drive and strengthen risk culture across the Group by fostering risk awareness, diversity of thought, psychological safety, and a strong speak-up culture that encourages constructive challenge, accountability, and learning from mistakes, while embedding sound risk management into everyday decision-making
  • Oversee qualitative risk management, including the Internal Control System (ICS), operational risk processes and the related governance framework
  • Advance ICT and Third-Party Risk Management and strengthen ICT resilience
  • Contribute to safeguarding the Group's operational resilience by anticipating and assessing external trends and translating these insights into the continued development of operational risk frameworks
  • Provide governance and oversight for information security, cyber defense and security management, including the continuous development of relevant Group-wide processes and standards
  • Lead the Group-wide data protection strategy and governance framework, including the continuous development of the Data Protection Management System and the implementation of regulatory requirements, coordinate the management and reporting of significant data protection incidents and ensure appropriate reporting to relevant committees and supervisory authorities
  • Provide strategic and operational leadership for Business Continuity Management and Physical Security, ensuring a high level of organisational resilience
  • Act as a trusted partner to senior leadership, Risk Committees, local Risk Management functions and external regulators on operational risk and resilience topics

What you'll bring to the role

  • Master's degree, preferably in Risk Management, Finance, Business Administration or a related field
  • 10+ years of relevant experience in operational risk management in the financial industry with at least 5 years in a leadership position (incl. leading leaders)
  • Extensive experience in operational risk management, governance and framework implementation within a complex organisation
  • Strong expertise in internal control systems, business continuity and crisis management, ICT risk, third-party risk and security governance
  • Sound understanding of information security and AI, ideally including experience related to CISO/CSO responsibilities
  • Sound knowledge of data protection governance and regulatory requirements
  • Proven ability to work effectively with senior executives, governance and risk committees, and external regulators
  • Strong leadership capabilities combined with the ability to connect strategic objectives with pragmatic implementation
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to inspire and engage others, bringing people together and turning ambition into action
  • The ability to create an inclusive environment where diverse teams thrive, encouraging curiosity, open-minded collaboration and respect for different perspectives
  • Professional English language skills
  • German is a plus
