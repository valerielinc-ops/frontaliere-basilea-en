Full description

Admission by appointment · Bruderholz, Secretariat · Job reference 13210 · Permanent employment

Tasks

Leadership and organisation

  • Team management: Disciplinary and professional leadership of the team of clinic secretaries and MPAs (about 20 FTE) incl. Assignment planning, recruitment, targeted promotion as well as further training, employee interviews, holiday and deputy planning
  • Smooth operation: Ensuring efficient consultation and operational planning across seven teams and managing the daily huddle and team meetings
  • Central contact person: Competent interface for team-internal and cross-team topics with performance and patient management, insurance and legal, HR and center management
  • Team culture: Designing an appreciative team and feedback culture in which responsibility is taken and solutions are found together
  • Central coordination and handling of civil liability cases, complaints and opinions of the Centre
  • Process Management: Active further development and maintenance of internal processes incl. Map and update in the document control system
  • Project management: Active participation in projects
  • Deputy Assistant to Centre and Clinic Management

Requirements

Training and expertise

  • Sound basis: Completed commercial or medical basic education or studies
  • Leadership competence: demonstrable training in team leadership and development as well as multi-year job-relevant professional experience and leadership responsibility for a large team in the hospital or medical environment
  • Organizational skill: Strong skills in process optimization, coupled with a very structured, assertive and diplomatic personality
  • Expertise: Experience in liability, insurance and quality issues as well as safe handling of MS Office, in particular Word, PowerPoint and Excel Mindset & Soft Skills
  • Leadership: enthusiasm for employee motivation, team development and an interdisciplinary, interprofessional exchange
  • Strength of communication: Confident knowledge of German in words and writing for precise and winning communication at all levels
  • Service idea: Enthusiasm for a service-oriented team, where patients and referents are the focus and are looked after professionally and friendly even in the most hectic circumstances
  • Resilience with heart and mind: Keep calm even in hectic situations, keep an overview and give the team orientation

Benefits

Flexible working models: Flexible working time model, annual working hours, home office
