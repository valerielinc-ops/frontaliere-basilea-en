Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated Chemist (a), 100% .

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the timely production and delivery of a commercial peptide product
  • Ensuring smooth batch-through in production, quality control and quality assurance
  • Monitoring of product performance, quality, yield and delivery deadlines
  • Analysis of process and production data as well as identification of optimization potential
  • Assistance in handling deviations, OOS/OOE cases and CAPA measures
  • Cooperation in the continuous improvement of processes and production processes
  • Close cooperation with production, QA, QC, supply chain, TechOps and other interfaces
  • Support for audits, inspections and customer inquiries
  • Collaboration in product-related lifecycle and change management activities
  • Active participation in cross-sectoral projects to ensure efficient and GMP-compliant production

Your profile

  • Chemistry degree (university or university of applied sciences)
  • In-depth understanding of technical equipment and interdisciplinary interest
  • Knowledge in the formulation and automation of production processes (e.g. PCS7) is an advantage
  • Professional and project-related leadership skills
  • Practical, conceptual and analytical working methods
  • Strong teamwork and communication skills
  • Reliability, a high sense of responsibility and exact working methods
  • Excellent knowledge of German and good knowledge of English

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
