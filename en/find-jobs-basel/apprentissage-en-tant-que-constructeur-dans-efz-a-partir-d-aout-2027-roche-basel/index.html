Top Arguments for the Job

  • In cooperation with professionals, you will invent, develop and design products, tools or devices for machines from different business areas.
  • You process assignments independently or as a member of a project team, you conduct customer meetings and create specifications from the requirements and wishes.
  • With state-of-the-art work equipment, you sketch solutions, write concepts, evaluate variants and present the results to the client.
  • Using 3D CAD software, you model components and determine the suitable material, join them to form moving assemblies and create drawings.
  • You develop and describe test and assembly instructions, create documentation for the operation and maintenance of your devices
