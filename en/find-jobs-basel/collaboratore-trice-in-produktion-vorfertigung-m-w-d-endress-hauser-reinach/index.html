Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

What is the role about?

They produce Coriolis flow meters in a team of around ten and operate modern test, blast and welding systems. In addition, they assemble, wire and weld assemblies, ensure quality and actively contribute to 5S, TPM and continuous improvements.

What tasks will you take on?

  • They produce Coriolis flowmeters in a team of around ten and thus make an important contribution to our high-quality sensor production.
  • They load and operate test, blasting and welding systems and ensure their safe and efficient use.
  • They assess welds and surfaces in terms of quality, precision and professional design.
  • They assemble and wire assemblies precisely, carry out soldering work and weld components directly to the plants.
  • They work flexibly in two-shift operation, keep plants and workstations in optimal condition with 5S and TPM methods and actively participate in the continuous improvement process.

What do we expect from you?

  • They have completed basic education at EBA or EFZ level, ideally in a technical or craft environment.
  • They bring experience from an industrial production environment and know the requirements of modern production processes.
  • They have a strong quality awareness and assess surfaces and work results safely and accurately.
  • They work carefully, cleanly and accurately in assembly, cabling and soldering work and convince with their distinct craftsmanship.
  • They communicate safely in German and are characterized by flexibility, personal responsibility, willingness to learn and a strong team spirit.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.

Thank you for your understanding.
