We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today - will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • You are a sales talent and take the initiative on the sales floor and like to lead a team
  • Your tasks include, in addition to your own sales responsibility, the assortment maintenance in your specialist goods group boutique, the management of a small sales team and the achievement of all KPI to be successful
  • You are responsible for your product group and manage the DailyBuisness directly on the sales floor
  • You inspire our customers with an attractive product presentation and are an absolute role model for learners and your sales team
  • You are responsible for the proper preparation and processing of customer orders
  • You are a competent and solution-oriented contact person for the concerns and wishes of our customers
  • You regularly participate in assortment meetings and quickly put concepts into action
  • You are a team player, open-minded and customer-oriented
  • We also address all lateral entrants who have already managed a small team and would like to start in the interior segment.
  • You have a great affinity for sales and you enjoy leading the competition
  • You bring experience in trade or sales
  • You are a vocational trainer and enjoy supporting our learners during their training
