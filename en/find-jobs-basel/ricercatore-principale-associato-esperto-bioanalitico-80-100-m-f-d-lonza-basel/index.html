For our successful Drug Product (DP) focused branch located in Basel and Stein, Switzerland, we have exciting opportunities. Become part of a group that enables its customers to meet some of the greatest challenges in patient treatment and join us by applying for the Associate Principal Scientist-Bioanalytical Expert position.

What you will get

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance

What you will do

  • Hold responsibility for planning, review and approval of analytical laboratory work for biologics, including method transfer and validation of potency assays for clinical and commercial products under GMP
  • Responsible for analytical methods such as cell-based assays, ELISA/LBA, etc.
  • Act as point of contact for customers
  • Handle GMP-compliant documentation and processes
  • Contribute to scientific field or technique by solving scientific problems within the department including complex investigations under GMP
  • Presenting work at scientific conferences or in peer-reviewed publications

What we are looking for

  • Master Degree in Biotechnology, Analytical Science, Biochemistry, or related Life Science subject
  • Significant work experience in the biopharmaceutical industry
  • Min. 3 years of experience with cGMP work
  • Knowledge of several bioanalytical methodologies, including mammalian cell culture, preparation of primary cells, cell-based assays, ligand binding assays, etc
  • Prior experience supporting and presenting in audits is desirable
  • Ability to lead complex investigations under GMP
  • Familiarity of working with electronic documentation systems (laboratory notebook, document management, LIMS)
  • Excellent communication skills, team player, flexible and able to manage complexity
  • Proficient knowledge of written and spoken English. Any other languages are a plus
