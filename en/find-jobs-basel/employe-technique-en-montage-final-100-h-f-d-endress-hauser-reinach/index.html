Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusting relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a technical employee in final assembly to strengthen our team in final assembly and jointly produce high-quality flowmeters reliably and precisely.

What is at stake here?

Do you enjoy precise assembly work? Do you want to contribute to the quality and reliability of innovative measurement technology? Do you like working in a motivated team with varied tasks? Then you can expect a versatile role in which your technical understanding and quality awareness make an important contribution to the production of our flow meters.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • the assembly and calibration of flowmeters.
  • Pick individual parts and thus ensure a smooth production process.
  • Maintain and maintain equipment to ensure its reliable functioning.
  • actively live our lean philosophy and drive continuous improvement.
  • work flexibly in 2-shift operation and contribute to joint team success.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed basic technical training, ideally as an electromechanic.
  • bring first professional experience in the field of electromechanics.
  • communicate safely in German language in word and writing.
  • Work flexibly, agilely and gladly in a team.
  • are characterised by quality awareness, responsibility and craftsmanship.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We promote work-life balance and offer flexible working hours.
  • We support sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly offers, including childcare and holiday care.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
