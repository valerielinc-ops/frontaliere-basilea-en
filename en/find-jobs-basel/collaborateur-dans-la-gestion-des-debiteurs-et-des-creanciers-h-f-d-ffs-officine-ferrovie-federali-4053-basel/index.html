Benefits

As an employee of a subsidiary of SBB Cargo, you benefit from various SBB benefits. This includes a general subscription (GA) or half-tax subscription, discounts at many companies and online shops and an annual bonus for good business results.

Work-life balance

We offer you a flexible working time and part-time model. Our annual working time model allows you to combine private life and work. We work 260 days annually - holidays that fall on a weekend are paid to you in the form of overtime.

Education and training

In addition to a workplace in our modern office in Basel, directly at the SBB station, we offer our employees the opportunity to work in the HomeOffice.

ChemOil Logistics AG

ChemOil Logistics AG is a subsidiary of the Swiss Federal Railway SBB Cargo AG. With a turnover of around CHF 140 million, we are the market leader in the field of hazardous goods logistics on the railways in Switzerland and on an international expansion course – also in neighbouring industries such as agriculture, construction and raw materials. Our customers are among others the top players in the European chemical and mineral oil industry.
