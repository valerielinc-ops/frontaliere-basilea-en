Full description

Within the framework of a one-year temporary employment, you support the Customs & Transportation team in day-to-day business and during ongoing projects. In particular, you will assume the following tasks:

  • Export management - land, air and sea
  • Coordination of national and international transport activities
  • Master data maintenance in SAP ERP
  • Active monitoring of the transports of our shipping platforms (CH, DE)
  • Support in offering and issuing certificates of origin
  • Training as a forwarding agent
  • Experience in customs and export is required
  • Good SAP knowledge is essential
  • English fluent in word and writing (C1)
  • English good in word and writing (B2/C1), other languages beneficial
