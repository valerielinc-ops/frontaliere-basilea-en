YOUR TASKS

  • Correct offsetting of the services provided
  • Operation and maintenance of equipment and maintenance of operating tables and storage materials
  • Support for smooth cooperation between storage and surgical care teams

Your profile

  • Professional experience and knowledge in the medical field
  • Completed further training as an expert / surgical storage care specialist
  • Good physical condition and pleasure in dealing with people
  • Ability of targeted communication combined with very good knowledge of German
  • Readiness for shift and pikett inserts
  • Organizational skill, sense of responsibility, initiative and reliability
