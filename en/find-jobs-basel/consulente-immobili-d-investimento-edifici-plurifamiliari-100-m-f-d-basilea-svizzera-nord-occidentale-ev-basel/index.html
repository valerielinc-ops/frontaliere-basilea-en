Full description

Are you ready to actively shape the market for investment properties in the economic region of Basel? Do you bring acquisition strength, persistence and entrepreneurial drive to advise investors and owners at the highest level? Then start your success story with us and become part of a world leading network.

Engel & Völkers Commercial stands for the highest level of expertise in the mediation of residential and commercial buildings as well as investment properties. In Basel and northwestern Switzerland, we offer you a dynamic environment in which you can fully develop your passion for numbers and returns.

Your mission

As a consultant in the commercial sector, you are the strategic partner for our investors and owners and accompany them from the first contact to the conclusion of the contract:

  • Market Development & Acquisition: You acquire and broker investment properties in a defined target area in Basel and the surrounding area and win new mandates proactively and systematically. You use market data and data-based acquisition tools.
  • Network: You sustainably support owners, prospective buyers and investors. You will continuously expand your network with investors, banks, insurance companies and other key industries, including at industry-relevant networking events.
  • Analysis & Strategy: You create sound real estate analyses, prepare market data and develop tailor-made marketing strategies for investment properties and multi-family homes.
  • Object presentation: From shooting on site to creating high-quality marketing documents, the process is in your hands.
  • Transaction support: You create marketing documents, help design sales contract concepts and coordinate the negotiations until the conclusion.
  • Marketing Impulses: You initiate and run customer-related promotions to maximize the visibility of your objects.

Your profile We are looking for personalities who convince through professionalism and bite:

  • Education: You have completed a basic commercial education or training in real estate, banking or insurance. A further education in real estate brokerage or valuation (e.g. SVIT) or a study in economics, real estate or finance is a plus.
  • Background: You have several years of proven successful experience in sales or real estate brokerage and can demonstrate acquisition and closing success in the commercial segment. Alternatively, you bring in-depth experience from banking or insurance with a clear connection to the real estate market. Experience with investment properties, apartment buildings or investment properties is ideal.
  • Expertise: You are familiar with property valuation, return calculation and market analysis.
  • Mindset: You have a real passion for real estate and numbers, a strong interest in performance-driven distribution, and the bite to stick with it.
  • Competence: You communicate convincingly, negotiate skillfully and bring graduation strength. You work absolutely customer-oriented and have a friendly, committed and well-kept appearance.
  • Locality: You have good market knowledge in northwestern Switzerland. An existing network with investors and decision-makers in the region is a great advantage.
  • Languages: You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing. High-level French and English are beneficial.

This is what we offer you at Engel & Völkers

  • Future: A growth-oriented environment in a crisis-resistant industry.
  • Freedom: A task with high design and action competence as well as flat hierarchies.
  • Team culture: An open-minded, collegial team and an open corporate culture with regular events.
  • Flexibility: A contemporary IT platform that enables flexible and mobile work, including partial remote work alongside field service and acquisition.
  • Development: First-class training offers via the worldwide E&V Academy.
  • Benefits: Attractive employee discounts over corporate benefits and a performance- and success-oriented compensation system without caps.
