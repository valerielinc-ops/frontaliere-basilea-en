Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Team Lead Device Software Test to complement our team and actively shape the technical and strategic development of our software test area. In this role, you make a decisive contribution to the quality and reliability of our products and create the conditions for a sustainable further development of our test platforms and test processes. As part of our development organization, you will work in an innovation-driven environment where technical excellence and collaboration go hand in hand.

What is this point about?

Do you want to lead a committed team and actively promote its development? Are you enthusiastic about sustainably improving software quality through modern test strategies? Does it appeal to you to combine technical challenges with strategic responsibility?

In this key role, together with development, quality and product teams, you will shape the future of our device software tests. They take responsibility for the technical orientation of the team, drive the further development of the test platform and ensure that our high quality standards are also met in the future.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • lead the Device Software Test Team professionally and personnel, provide orientation and promote the further development of employees through regular feedback and individual development goals.
  • Establish clear responsibilities and reliable ways of working, remove obstacles and represent the interests of the team to relevant stakeholders.
  • define the strategic and technical direction of the team and derive goals, priorities and development initiatives from this.
  • Develop the testing strategy and control the testing activities in accordance with product and quality objectives.
  • Ensure test and process quality through standards, reviews and proven methods and promote reliable and long-term maintenance test solutions.
  • technically and conceptually further develop the test platform and drive the scaling and automation of the test landscape.

What do we expect from you?

Size

  • have a university degree (uni/ETH/FH) in electrical engineering, technical computer science, software engineering or a comparable field of expertise.
  • bring experience in personnel or lateral leadership and lead clearly, appreciatively and development-oriented.
  • have several years of experience in object-oriented software development with C# or C++ and like to work on technical topics themselves.
  • have a strong quality awareness and understand the requirements for reliable and long-term maintenance test solutions.
  • use agile methods appropriately and evaluate new technologies with regard to benefits, effort and risks.
  • work bindingly and solution-oriented, bring different perspectives together and communicate safely in German and English.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We offer you a responsible leadership role with high visibility and creative leeway in an innovative technology environment.
  • We value a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working models that are oriented towards the requirements of modern working environments.
  • We work in a collegial and respectful environment where cooperation, trust and mutual support are lived.
  • Together with dedicated teams, we enable you to develop technologies that contribute to the success of our customers worldwide.
