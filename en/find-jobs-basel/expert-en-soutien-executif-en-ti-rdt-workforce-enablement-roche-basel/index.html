The Opportunity

  • Act as a digital enabler and owner of the digital employee experience for our business colleagues and respective site operations.
  • Deliver personalized, high-touch IT support for senior leadership across on-site, off-site, and virtual communications to ensure uninterrupted operational continuity.

  • Partner with product teams to enable standard workplace infrastructure , executing both global and local deployment projects across the organization.
  • Support local Dawn Raid and Global Crisis Management teams , while participating in the 24/7 hotline support rotation (approximately 1 week every 6 weeks).

Who you are

  • Education: Higher education degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline (or equivalent practical experience).
  • Experience: 3+ years of hands-on experience in executive/VIP IT support, deskside operations, or IT service management within a global corporate environment.
  • Security & Compliance Awareness: Sound knowledge of current legal, technical, and organizational requirements for information security, with a track record of active adherence.
  • Technical Proficiency: Proven expertise in hardware lifecycle management, incident handling (SNOW/ServiceNow), digital analytical tools (e.g., Nexthink), and event/meeting infrastructure support.
  • Communication & Stakeholder Management: Outstanding interpersonal, communication, and influencing skills, with the ability to maintain executive intimacy, discretion, and composure under pressure.
  • Strategic & Agile Mindset: Highly organized problem-solver with strong decision-making abilities, strategic agility, and a passion for managing change and driving process improvements.
  • Collaborative Team Player: Proven success collaborating across cross-functional teams and external service providers to deliver an exceptional employee experience.
  • Language: Must be fluent in both English and German.
