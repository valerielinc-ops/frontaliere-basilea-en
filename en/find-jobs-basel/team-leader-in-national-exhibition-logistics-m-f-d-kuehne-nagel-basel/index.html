Full description

More than a job

Whether international trade fair or regional exhibition: As a team leader: in Basel-based National Exhibition Logistics, you are responsible for our activities at the Basel and Zurich locations. Together with your team, you ensure that complex trade fair projects are smoothly planned, controlled and implemented.

You bring people, goods and dates together and create the basis for convincing trade fair appearances. In this way, you help our customers to successfully present their brands and products.

How you get involved

  • Contact person for trade fair companies, customers and other stakeholders
  • Expert and disciplinary management of the commercial and technical team incl. Operation planning, training and quality assurance
  • responsibility for budget, costs and economic results of the area
  • Creation of offers and coordination of incoming orders
  • Independent handling of transports by air, sea and land freight
  • Planning, control and monitoring of trade fair projects as well as on-site support during events

What sets you apart

  • Completed training in the field of forwarding and logistics or a comparable qualification
  • Professional experience in the logistics sector, ideally in trade fair logistics
  • Extensive leadership skills as well as pleasure in motivating and developing employees
  • negotiation skills as well as a high degree of customer and cost orientation
  • Working method
  • Readiness for assignments on weekends, holidays and on business trips
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing
  • Driving license of class B of advantage
