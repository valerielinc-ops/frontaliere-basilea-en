Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the Materials Management department, we are looking for a dedicated Storage Technician area Tankfarm (a), 100%.

Their tasks

  • Secure product transfer from trucks to the tank warehouse (raw goods and waste) in compliance with all safety, quality and environmental requirements incl. Full logging
  • Monitoring and operation of tank depots
  • Detection, analysis and resolution of disturbances
  • Performance of maintenance, control and preventive maintenance work
  • Picetting service and readiness for Saturday work (teamed); Balance day in the following week
  • Compliance and implementation of occupational, environmental and explosion protection regulations (ATEX)
  • Documentation of all activities, deviations and reports in accordance with internal requirements
  • Responsible management and operation of all work and tools

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as logistician, polymechanic or plant manager or comparable technical training
  • Experience in tank storage operations
  • Knowledge of dangerous goods (ADR/RID) desirable
  • Advantageous forklift identification
  • Strong sense of safety, quality and responsibility
  • Physically resilient, reliable and stress resistant
  • Flexible, team-ready and ready for piketting and occasional Saturday work (rotation in the team)
  • in German: nehandlungssicher
  • Basic knowledge of cGMP and experience in dealing with SAP S/4HANA is an advantage

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Additional free days (bridge days), which are fixed in autumn by the Bachem
  • Company-internal KITA and staff restaurant on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of the pension fund contributions by Bachem as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our election savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
