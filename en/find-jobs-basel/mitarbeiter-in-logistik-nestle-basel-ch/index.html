Short overview Position

The Basel factory is a powerful production facility of Nestlé Suisse S.A. and produces THOMY culinary products for domestic and foreign markets.

For our production department we are looking for an engaged person as an employee:in logistics by immediately or by appointment.

A day in the life of one employee: in logistics

  • All work with forklift trucks
  • Provision of domestic and export orders
  • Loading of export goods
  • Unloading of raw materials
  • Assistance in the award of the pallets with SAP label and storage
  • Sample collection
  • In- and out-of-stock storage
  • Supply of production lines
  • Cleaning work according to cleaning plan
  • Cooperation with internal and external contact persons

This makes you successful

  • Completed apprenticeship in logistics or in the food industry
  • forklift driving licence (SUVA recognised)
  • Compulsory, exact, efficient and reliable working methods
  • SAP knowledge
  • Very good oral and written German knowledge
  • willingness to work flexibly in a shift operation
