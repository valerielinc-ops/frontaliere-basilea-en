Full description

Do you want to make a direct contribution to excellent patient care with your leadership work? Then you become part of our Central Patient and Assignment Management (PZM), the central hub for patients, families, assignments and internal departments at UKBB. In this versatile leadership role, you are one of four team leaders at the Patient Management Center (PZM). They take over the personnel and professional management of the inpatient admission and day clinic with four employees, the IV coordination with two employees and the emergency room with seven employees. You will be actively supported by a deputy. They actively shape administrative processes, further develop processes and ensure that patients and their families are accompanied professionally, efficiently and service-oriented from the first contact.

Entry date: by immediate or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Personnel, professional and organizational leadership of the teams in the inpatient admission and day clinic, IV coordination and emergency department
  • Responsibility for ensuring efficient and service-oriented administrative processes along the patient path
  • Central contact person for patient-related administrative processes and interfaces
  • Further development, optimization and documentation of processes and workflows
  • Promoting a customer-oriented, quality-conscious and constructive team culture
  • Assuming delegated tasks of department management, including evaluations, reporting and management of the personnel planning system (PEP Polypoint)
  • Close cooperation with internal and external stakeholders
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations in inpatient admission and day clinic

Your profile

  • They have completed commercial training or a comparable qualification and have experience in the medical-administrative field. Ideally, you have also completed further training in the health care or management area.
  • They have several years of professional and managerial experience, preferably in the healthcare sector, in a patient-related service organisation or in the field of health insurance.
  • They are well versed in handling the common Microsoft 365 applications and ideally have experience with SAP IS-H or other clinical information systems.
  • They have good oral English skills. Knowledge of French and/or Italian is an advantage.
  • They are characterized by high personal responsibility, resilience, flexibility and reliability.
  • Your working method is structured, solution-oriented and networked.
  • They convince through strong communication and leadership skills.
  • They value interdisciplinary cooperation and enjoy networking medical, nursing and administrative requirements.

Our offer

  • A varied and responsible leadership function with scope for design
  • The opportunity to actively develop central administrative patient processes
  • A modern and future-oriented working environment in a leading university children’s hospital
  • Collaboration with committed interprofessional teams
  • Contemporary employment conditions and attractive social benefits
  • Diverse opportunities for professional and personal development
  • A motivated team that looks forward to your support
