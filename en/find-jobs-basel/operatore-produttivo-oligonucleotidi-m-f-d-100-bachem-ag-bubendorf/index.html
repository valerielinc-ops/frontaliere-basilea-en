Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our drug pilot department, we are looking for a motivated laboratory assistant (a) 100% .

In our production plant, you produce pharmaceutically effective oligonucleotides and related substances. You work under GMP conditions and use modern production facilities from production to cleaning. Through practical training, you will gain the necessary security to work independently and independently on our production facilities and become part of our team. If it is also important for you to work with us on the continuous improvement of our production facilities and work processes and appreciate the opportunity to help shape your working conditions, then you are at the right place.

Their tasks

  • They control and monitor our manufacturing processes at the interface to R&D.
  • They document GMP and control the batch documentation.
  • They help to qualify the production equipment.
  • They prepare in-process controls and sample trains and partly carry them out themselves.
  • Together with the responsible group leaders, they make decisions for the further course of production on the basis of in-process controls, for example.
  • They carry out cleaning and maintenance as well as simple repair work on the production facilities.
  • They are preparing manufacturing campaigns. For this purpose, you provide equipment, solvents and starting materials in the respective production rooms and control the apparatus and plants.
  • You are actively and independently involved in the continuous improvement of our production facilities and work processes.

Your profile

  • Chemical laboratory, chemical or equivalent training or chemical and pharmatechnologist.
  • Flexibility, communication and willingness to learn are among your strengths.
  • Preference to work and documentation according to GMP specifications as well as experience in the GMP environment is advantageous.
  • They are characterized by a high degree of reliability, independence and teamwork.
  • They are interested in technical connections and automated processes.
  • A structured and clean and independent way of working that allows you to keep an overview at all times
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge in organic synthesis and chromatographic purification, as well as common analytical techniques such as HPLC/UPLC are advantageous
  • Experience with activities in the clean room is advantageous, but not a requirement
