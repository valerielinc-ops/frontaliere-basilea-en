Job Description Summary Basel BDSS Cell Bank Center is a manufacturing unit (MU) responsible for the production of GMP and analytical cell banks for Novartis Biologics. The CBC process specialist is responsible for the GMP-compliant processes of the cell banks managed by the Basel Cell Bank Center (production, storage, inventory, shipping). The process specialist is the first point of contact for questions and topics relating to CBC operations in Basel BDSS.

Job description

Main responsibilities

  • Implementation and support of all production processes in compliance with GMP, HSE, environmental and safety guidelines as well as assuming the tasks of a device operator.
  • Ensure a smooth production process by operating, cleaning, preparing, sterilising and monitoring equipment, premises and auxiliary facilities.
  • carrying out sampling, in-process controls, environmental monitoring and other quality-related activities to ensure product quality.
  • Responsibility for complete, timely and GMP-compliant documentation, including batch records, logbooks, SAP bookings and production-related records.
  • Ensure availability of consumables, resources and production resources, as well as support in production planning and resource management.
  • monitoring of installation performance, early detection of deviations and initiation and coordination of appropriate immediate measures in the event of technical failures or operational deviations.
  • Assistance in the investigation of deviations, in particular human error, as well as cooperation with experts and quality assurance for root cause analysis and problem solving.
  • Promoting a high GMP, quality, safety and 5S culture through consistent compliance and implementation of the applicable standards on the shop floor.
  • Ensure effective communication and coordination between production, quality, technology and other interfaces to support trouble-free operation.
  • Support team and operational performance by organizing operational activities, batch releases of intermediates, problem solving, and continuous optimization of processes and resources to achieve production goals.

Minimum requirements

  • technical degree
  • University degree (chemical engineering, pharmaceutical technology, biotechnology, biochemistry) for some technology platforms desired or equivalent experience.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience as an equipment specialist or 10 years in the chemical/food/medical/biotechnology/aseptic manufacturing or development sectors.
  • mastery of the local language of the site. intermediate knowledge of English.
