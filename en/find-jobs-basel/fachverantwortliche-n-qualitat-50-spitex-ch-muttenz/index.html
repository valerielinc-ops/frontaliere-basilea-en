Full description

SPITEX MUTTENZ AG – committed, well-founded and goal-oriented We provide high-quality outpatient care for the approximately 18’000 residents of the municipality of Muttenz. Our team of 70 employees supports people of all ages with professional advice, care and domestic services in living independently and contributes to their quality of life.

We are looking for a Quality Specialist 50% Shaping quality together We see quality as a joint development process. We want not only to meet standards, but also to learn together, shape change and create conditions in which our employees can work safely, professionally and with enthusiasm. You will become part of a committed team in which personal responsibility, collaboration and participation are central elements of our organisational culture. Thorough onboarding goes without saying for us. Your tasks

  • You will further develop our quality management together with the responsible employees and the co-management team
  • You lead and support quality- and care-related projects and bring evidence-based insights into practice
  • You further develop quality standards, processes and tools and support their implementation in day-to-day work
  • You plan internal quality reviews, support audits and evaluate quality metrics
  • You advise and support employees and the co-management team on quality issues and promote professional reflection and knowledge exchange
  • You identify potential for improvement and shape change together with those involved

What you bring

  • Diploma as a nursing professional HF/FH and at least 5 years of professional experience in an outpatient Spitex setting
  • Further training in healthcare quality management, e.g. Quality Manager in Healthcare Advanced (SAQ-QUALICON), or equivalent training
  • Experience in quality management as well as in professional leadership and advisory work
  • Good knowledge of the legal and professional quality requirements in the outpatient sector
  • Confidence in working with evidence-based specialist literature and digital tools
  • An independent, solution-oriented and communicative person who enjoys development and change

What we offer you

  • A responsible specialist role with plenty of scope for shaping things
  • The opportunity not only to monitor quality, but also to actively develop it further
  • Close collaboration with the co-management team and other key people in our organization
  • An innovative organization with short decision-making paths and room for your own ideas
  • Support and assistance with professional and personal development
  • Attractive employment conditions, loyalty bonuses, and an employee-friendly and sustainable pension fund
  • An organizational culture characterized by trust, dialogue, responsibility, appreciation, and learning

Profile

Spitex Muttenz AG supports people in living independently and safely in their familiar surroundings. On behalf of the Municipality of Muttenz, we provide professional services in the areas of nursing, care, housekeeping, and psychiatric nursing. Our approximately 70 employees support our clients individually, tailored to their needs and in close collaboration with relatives, family doctors, and other specialists. As a locally rooted, nonprofit-oriented organization, we combine high quality standards with personal and trusting care. At the same time, we are committed to training future professionals and continuously developing our services. Make a meaningful impact. Accompany people with humanity. Shape things together.
