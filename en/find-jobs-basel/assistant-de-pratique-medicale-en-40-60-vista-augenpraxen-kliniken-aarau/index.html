To strengthen our Vista team at the Binningen site, we are looking for a qualified and ready-to-performance personality as Vista Eye Practices & Clinics, with its approximately 35 locations and approximately 500 employees, are the largest provider of eye medicine in Switzerland. We offer first-class medical services to our patients and set standards in the disciplines of ophthalmology, eye surgery and laser eye medicine. In this role, you are a central contact person for patients, internal and referring physicians, the non-medical staff of our practice and other Vista locations in the region. Their tasks

  • Patient reception and care in a lively environment
  • Preoperative preparation, predrips and postoperative care of our patients
  • The careful and competent preparation of the consultation hours
  • Provision of patient records
  • Assistance during the consultation and minor surgical procedures
  • Delivery of medicines by order of the doctor
  • After thorough training and familiarization, you carry out preliminary examinations such as autorefractometers and pressure measurements and assist with intravitreal injections.
