The Position User Experience (UX) Designer Join our Roche Pharma R&D product environment where you will be working on digital tools that support complex scientific, clinical, or operational workflows. The work requires structured thinking, attention to detail, collaboration with product and engineering teams, and the ability to design for users working with complex information, data, and regulated processes. The UX Professional is a developing User Experience Designer who contributes to product teams by supporting research, design exploration, prototyping, and validation activities. In this role, you will work on defined product areas, translate user and stakeholder needs into clear design artifacts, and gradually take ownership of smaller design problems under guidance from more experienced UX professionals. You will contribute to wireframes, prototypes, user flows, journey maps, and usability testing activities, while learning how to explain design decisions, evaluate alternatives, and use evidence to improve the user experience. This role is suited for someone with foundational UX skills, curiosity about complex digital products, and the ability to learn quickly in a multidisciplinary environment. Responsibilities

  • Conduct user research, usability testing, design discovery, and design execution activities under guidance.

  • Use the Roche / R Design System to build consistent, accessible, and scalable interface solutions.
  • Support design documentation by capturing key decisions, assumptions, open questions, and user needs. Accountability / Problem Solving
