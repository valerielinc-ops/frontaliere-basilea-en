Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new solutions or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed.

As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the environment.

In order to further develop our business area in the field of industrial wastewater treatment and wastewater pretreatment plants, we are looking for an Outside Sales Engineer / Project Manager (m/f/d) with strong sales strength and in-depth process engineering expertise. In terms of content, this is a business development role with a focus on market development, new customer acquisition and the development of sustainable customer and project relationships.

What is this point about?

Are you enthusiastic about technical solutions with direct customer benefits? Do you enjoy entering new markets, gaining customers and actively promoting innovative industrial wastewater treatment concepts? Do you like working at the interface between sales, process engineering and project implementation?

In this role in the field of sales, you strategically develop the industrial wastewater technology business field and actively promote new acquisition (Hunter mentality). They accompany projects from the first idea through test measurements and concept definition to handover to project management – and take on project responsibility themselves if necessary. Your work and field of application can extend throughout Switzerland.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • actively promote market and business development in the field of industrial wastewater treatment and wastewater pretreatment plants
  • Generate leads and build a sustainable, promising project pipeline
  • Consulting customers technically and commercially, with a clear focus on new acquisition
  • Carry out test measurements at the customer and derive procedural concepts for wastewater treatment from them
  • Develop customized solutions in close cooperation with internal specialist departments
  • hand over the developed concepts to responsible project managers for further preparation and project implementation
  • Training for employees and external contacts on process engineering issues relating to industrial wastewater treatment and pretreatment plants
  • If necessary, take over project management functions and actively accompany projects

What do we expect from you?

  • Attractive occupational social and pension benefits.
