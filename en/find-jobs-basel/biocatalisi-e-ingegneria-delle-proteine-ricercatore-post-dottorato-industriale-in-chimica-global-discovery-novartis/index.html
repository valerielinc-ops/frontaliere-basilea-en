Job Description

Your Responsibilities

Your responsibilities include, but are not limited to

  • Design and develop innovative technology platforms in biocatalysis and protein engineering as part of the Genesis flagship program, advancing Novartis's discovery capabilities at speed.
  • Conduct enzyme discovery and engineering campaigns — leveraging latest computational tools and high-throughput automated screening — to identify and optimise novel biocatalysts for pharmaceutical synthesis applications.
  • Develop novel biocatalytic synthetic solutions that enable efficient, scalable, and sustainable preparation of drug candidates across novel modalities, from early discovery through to clinical and commercial supply.
  • Get exposure to peptide chemistry and display technologies to expand the scope and diversity of biocatalytic solutions available to the Novartis portfolio.
  • Apply cutting-edge automation platforms and advanced automation for high-throughput enzyme screening, data-rich experimentation, and rapid iteration across engineering campaigns.
  • Contribute to defining scientific strategy on Genesis program projects — identifying bold opportunities, designing novel experimental approaches, and helping shape the direction of the platform.
  • Collaborate with world-class experts in Molecular Biology, Biochemistry, Bioinformatics, Process Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, and Analytical Chemistry across Novartis and with external academic and industry partners.
  • Communicate your science — present findings internally, contribute to patents, and make complex science accessible to diverse audiences.

What You'll Bring to the Role

  • A completed PhD at the interface of Chemistry and Biology with a focus on biocatalysis, synthetic biology, enzyme engineering, or a closely related discipline.
  • Hands on experience in development of peptide-, or protein-modifying enzymatic transformations is preferred.
  • Strong track record of original research and the ability to develop innovative (bio)catalytic transformations.
  • Scientific curiosity and team spirit to effectively work at the interface of multiple scientific disciplines in a fast-paced, multicultural environment.
  • Effective interpersonal and communication skills are essential.
  • Effective knowledge of English, both oral and written, is required.
  • Experience with state-of-the-art high throughput, computation or display technologies as well as previous post-doctoral experience is a further advantage but not a requirement.
