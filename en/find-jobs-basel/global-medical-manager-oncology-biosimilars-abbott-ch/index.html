Global Medical Manager Oncology – Biosimilars

Location: Basel, Switzerland | Division: Established Pharmaceuticals (EPD)

At Abbott , we believe people can live fuller lives through better health. Every day, our medicines and technologies help millions of people around the world manage health conditions, overcome barriers to care, and achieve better outcomes.

Biologics have transformed cancer care—and biosimilars are making this breakthrough accessible to more people worldwide. By delivering high-quality, affordable alternatives, biosimilars open the door to earlier treatment, reduce disease burden, and promote equity in healthcare.

About the Opportunity

Reporting to the Global Medical Director and working closely with the Oncology Biologics Core Team, this strategic global role is responsible for driving the medical affairs strategy across oncology biosimilar assets and multiple tumor indications. The position plays a key role in supporting global launches, evidence generation, scientific engagement, and medical excellence, with a particular focus on enabling success across Emerging Markets.

Main Responsibilities

Global Medical Strategy & Launch Leadership

· Owns the global medical strategy for assigned oncology biologics across multiple tumor indications · Leads medical launch readiness and life cycle planning, with specific accountability for Emerging Markets · Serves as senior medical representative on global cross functional and brand teams Scientific Leadership & Strategic Insight · Acts as a global scientific leader for the portfolio, identifying unmet needs, data gaps, and long‑term medical opportunities · Provides senior medical input into asset strategy, indication sequencing, and scientific differentiation Evidence Generation & Medical Governance · Owns the Integrated Evidence Generation Strategy, including IIS, RWE, post‑marketing and observational studies · Chairs or represents Medical Affairs in IIS, publication, and governance forums · Ensures regional medical activities are aligned with global strategy and scientific priorities Scientific Communication & External Engagement · Leads global publication planning and congress strategy for assigned assets · Engages and manages global and regional KOLs, scientific societies, and expert panels · Acts as a senior scientific ambassador for the portfolio Regional & Affiliate Medical Enablement (Emerging Markets Focus) · Serves as the primary global medical interface for Emerging Markets · Provides strategic guidance, scientific materials, and training to regional and affiliate medical teams · Ensures consistency of medical messaging across diverse healthcare systems

Training, Compliance & Regulatory Support

· Oversees development of global medical training materials and scientific platforms · Provides senior medical review of promotional and non‑promotional materials · Supports regulatory and safety documentation from a medical and scientific perspective Portfolio Expansion & Business Support · Provides medical expertise to support licensing, acquisition, and in‑licensing evaluations · Contributes to due diligence and asset assessment from a senior medical standpoint

The profile

  • Medical Doctor (MD) degree or an advanced scientific degree (PhD or M.Sc.) in a relevant life sciences discipline.
  • 5–8 years of Medical Affairs experience within the pharmaceutical industry.
  • Strong experience as medical manager or medical lead of complex therapeutic areas as oncology, haematology, rheumatology, immunology, rare diseases, ideally with biologics or biosimilars.
  • Proven regional or global Medical Affairs experience and demonstrated success in launch and post-launch environments.
  • Experience supporting Emerging Markets and working across diverse healthcare systems is highly preferred.
  • Solid understanding of clinical research, evidence generation, and medical governance processes.
  • Proven expertise in managing key opinion leaders and collaborating with scientific societies and external experts.
  • Demonstrated business acumen and strategic insight into the pharmaceutical industry.
  • Excellent communication, presentation, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Fluent English language skills, both written and spoken; additional languages are an advantage.
  • Ability to operate successfully in a highly matrixed, international environment and influence without direct authority.
