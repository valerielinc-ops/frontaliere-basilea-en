Full description

Shaping the future together – your teaching at Endress+Hauser At Endress+Hauser, you don’t just work anywhere – you are part of a team working on exciting technologies for industry worldwide. Our approximately 18,000 employees develop, build and improve measuring instruments that are used all over the world. What is important to us? Collaboration, trust and team spirit. Because together we achieve more – and every idea counts. As a family-run company, we think long-term and invest in your future. With us you not only get a top education, but also many opportunities to develop and shape yourself.

What is the point about? Are you interested in technology and electronics? Do you enjoy wiring, checking and getting things working? And do you want to actively participate in ensuring that modern systems work reliably? Then training as an automatic technician* in EFZ is just right for you! In this training, you will learn how electrical controls are constructed, wired and tested. You will work on our calibration systems, assist with commissioning and help to analyze and correct errors. You are an important part of our technical team and get exciting insights into the world of automation.

What tasks will you take on?

You will

  • Switchgear combinations for our calibration systems or Produce customer systems and wire them to a complete system
  • Cooperation in putting plants into service
  • Finding errors, troubleshooting and performing maintenance work
  • Participation in technical documentation

What do we expect from you?

You

  • are in middle or upper school
  • Shows good performance in mathematics, technical design and STEM subjects
  • have craftsmanship skills and interest in technology
  • work carefully and have good logical and analytical thinking skills
  • are team-oriented and engaged

What can you expect from us?

  • Security and stability of an internationally operating family business
  • Very good takeover opportunities after training
  • A motivated and dedicated training team that will accompany you
  • Costs of the U-Subscription (Public)
  • Delicious and cheap lunch menus from 5.50 CHF in the company's restaurant

For your application we need

  • Letter of motivation
  • CVC
  • All upper secondary education certificates
  • S2-Check with evaluation in the professional field and/or multicheck
