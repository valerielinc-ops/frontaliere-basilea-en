25.09.2026 Reinach BL

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Reinach BL, a successful and locally oriented bank in the village center of Reinach, we are looking for a committed and service-oriented personality as a customer consultant.

What do you expect?

  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous customer portfolio support
  • Competent and comprehensive advice to private customers in the basic products Saving, paying, investing and provisioning
  • Reception and service of customers in the customer zone
  • Active potential recognition and forwarding of customers to the responsible specialists
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the customer’s living situation as well as within the customer-specific product range
  • Sporadic support of our cash desk
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular in contact campaigns

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training or similar training
  • Experience in the financial services sector
  • Regional anchorage
  • Enthusiastic and customer-oriented personality with a winning appearance
  • Independent, exact and responsible working method with pleasure in direct customer contact
