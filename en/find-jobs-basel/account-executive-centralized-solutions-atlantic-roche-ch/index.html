As a Account Executive you will be responsible for promoting and driving the sales cycle of (Diagnostic sub specialty) products within hospital-based laboratories, independent reference laboratories and defined medical research centers. You will work closely with your National Business Leader to execute on growth and retention plans across your assigned customer base as well as collaborate with your local Provincial Healthcare Leader and Customer Lead (s) to focus on specific regional trends. You are the technical product expert at the laboratory level and you use this knowledge to build trusting and credible relationships within the hospital lab network.

The Opportunity

  • Collaborate with the regional squad to execute on customer related goals (growth, retention, experience, etc)
  • Leverage your understanding of the customer, your regional squad and our internal network to provide the right support to your account(s) at the right time.
  • Co-own the stellar customer experience in the hospital lab network (Lab Staff, IT, Clinicians) with the Support Lead
  • Conducts regular check-INs with the senior laboratory stakeholders and clinicians to better understand the labs' priorities and challenges and how Roche can support them and provide value
  • Maintain a deep technical knowledge of your product portfolio and a profound interest and understanding of your customer’s working environment and personal and professional objectives. You will use this knowledge to bring true value and enable us to become the Partner of Choice
  • Maintain and grow a healthy sales opportunity funnel in REXIS. Focus and priority should be given to segmented target accounts and opportunities. You are responsible for customer and opportunity data integrity (accuracy and completeness).
  • Use Miller Heiman for strategic planning of your sales opportunities and the Challenger selling model to help customers navigate alternatives.

  • Mastery of two-way communication; communicates, actively listening, using silence to elicit more information and clearly conveying ideas once the customer understands their problem
  • Ability to assimilate and master scientific and clinical information
  • Passionate and empathetic about our customer and patients and their environment
  • Demonstrated financial and business acumen
  • Strong presentation skills
