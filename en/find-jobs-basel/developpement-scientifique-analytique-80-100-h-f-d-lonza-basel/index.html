What you will do

  • Perform pharmaceutical development and / or analytical development studies for parenteral drug products of biologics (e.g. monoclonal antibodies, novel formats, ADCs) according to study protocols or other work instructions (including HPLC and CE purity methods, content by UV, identity, color, clarity and opalescence, pH, osmolality, extractable volume, CCI, visible particles, sub-visible particles and related DP analytics)
  • Follow applicable SOPs and current documentation practices
  • Main departmental/organizational responsibilities are to ensure proper information flow related to assigned IRP tasks and to contribute to departmental meetings and other departmental area, as needed
  • Maintains training status by completing required assigned trainings in a timely manner and attending on the job trainings (OJTs) and classroom trainings
  • Contributes to keep up of laboratory facilities, digital databases by engaging in periodic activities like cleaning and updates
  • Ensure proper maintenance of assigned equipment as IRP and other instruments in the laboratories. Adheres to relevant safety and health protection following lab and safety instructions
  • Main laboratory responsibilities are IRP (Instrument Responsible Person) including associated training tasks for assigned simple instrument(s), to operate complex instruments with guidance. And to manufacture / prepare and / or test non-GMP drug product samples for supportive stability studies and preclinical use (e.g. GLP tox studies), applying aseptic working techniques, as applicable

What we are looking for

  • Training on the job as lab technician (CTA, BTA or PTA)
  • Working experience as a research/ technical assistant in the pharmaceutical or chemical industry, preferably in pharmaceutical development Experience working in a cGMP-regulated environment is advantageous.
  • Strong working knowledge in relevant analytical techniques in protein/peptide analytics. Experience with laboratory specific software (e.g. Empower, Chromeleon, LIMS-system)
  • Experience in reporting, interpretation and documentation of scientific results
  • Good communication and inter-personal skills
  • Ability to work independently in a team
  • Excellent communication, reporting and scientific skills in English
