Full description

Actively design the meat and charcuterie range You know the meat market, recognize trends and have a sense of quality, products and customer needs. In this role, you will actively develop our product range and work closely with suppliers, branches and national departments.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, BZM GMBS, Ruchfeldstrasse 15, 4142 Munichstein

Mansioni

  • Further development of the meat and charcuterie range
  • Cooperation with suppliers, branches, logistics and national specialist bodies
  • Analysis of market and sales figures and derivation of measures
  • Share responsibility for availability, quality and margin
  • Cooperation in projects and development of regional value-added products
  • Preparation and implementation of regional and national action and promotion plans
  • Maintaining and ensuring a correct master database
  • Conduct of tastings and quality assessments

Requirements

  • 2 years: as a marketing professional, product manager or comparable training advantageous
  • Completed basic or further training: as a meat specialist, butcher, cook or comparable training in the food sector
  • You enjoy product range design, market observation and product development
  • You are an independent, willing-to-learn personality and will gladly take on responsibility
  • You are a team player with excellent communication skills
  • You think and act entrepreneurially, take initiative and take responsibility
  • You are enthusiastic about food and quality and convince with communication and negotiating skills
  • You move safely in digital systems and are well-versed in using Office 365
  • German (flowing)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Modern Infrastructure: Inviting and functional office world, modern IT infrastructure
  • Central place of work: Well accessible by public and private transport.
  • Employee offers: Various offers, discounts and preferential conditions
  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance: 8.5%, AG share 17%
