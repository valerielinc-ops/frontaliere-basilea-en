Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our approval lab of the QC Early Phase development department, we are looking for dedicated Lab Technician Quality Control.

We are looking for

As a committed and motivated personality, you are part of our agile development department. You can expect a wide range of tasks and questions in a dynamic and exciting environment. You are responsible for analyses of oligonucleotide active ingredients under GMP as well as their stability tests.

Their tasks

  • Using chromatographic methods to test the release and stability of our active ingredients under GMP
  • Conscious documentation, evaluation and review of analyses
  • Cooperation in the maintenance and qualification of analysis equipment
  • Participation in optimization and development projects
  • Opportunity for professional development by participating in the software-supported implementation and documentation of GMP analyses
  • Development of sustainable improvements for all analytical questions in the method life cycle
  • Close cooperation in a motivated and dynamic team, including working with our method developers

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a chemical laboratory expert, specialisation in analytics or synthesis, or comparable training courses
  • Professional experience or training focus in instrumental analytics with focus on chromatography (HPLC)
  • Experience in the analysis of oligonucleotides
  • Knowledge of common IT applications (e.g. Chromeleon, LIMS, SAP) as well as general software affinity
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • You are a team player and appreciate the cooperation in the team.
  • Reliable personality with enthusiasm for responsibility and initiative
  • Quality-conscious, exact and service-oriented working method
  • Openness to innovation and change and an eye for process contexts

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as the possibility for a super-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
