Introduction

The Technical Product Owner possesses in-depth knowledge of the business domain, relevant technologies, and organizational strategy to provide holistic, enterprise-wide consulting advice. In this role, you will apply systems thinking to navigate the complexity of interconnected systems, overseeing initiatives with full autonomy and leading analysis efforts across highly ambiguous spaces. Acting as a trusted advisor to leadership, you will actively shape long-term roadmaps, drive consensus through persuasive communication, and recommend optimal technology solutions for sustainable business transformation.

The responsibility of RDT Site, SHE & Sustainability spans across the Business Functions Sustainability, Group SHE (Safety, Security, Health and Environmental Protection) and the Sites Network, consisting of the large multi-functional sites Mannheim, Penzberg and Ludwigsburg in Germany

  • South San Francisco, Indianapolis, Pleasanton and Santa Clara in the US; Rotkreuz, Basel/Kaiseraugst and Roche Forum Buonas in Switzerland as well as the global functions Global Capital Investment, Real Estate & Facility Management, Global Data & Digital Solutions and Site Network Strategy.

The technology stack of Product Sites supports the solutions of following capabilities:

  • General Site Services & IoT
  • Fixed Asset Strategy, Portfolio & Project Engineering
  • Facility Management & Real Estate
  • Site Logistics, Supply and Disposal

With the mission that employees and business functions work in safe, attractive, and sustainable sites with the same experience, this role serves defining product vision and direction, maintaining full end-to-end lifecycle accountability for technology solutions across a key subset of capabilities. Success requires a sophisticated ability to reconcile global strategy with localized business priorities, ensuring precision in execution without sacrificing high-level vision.
