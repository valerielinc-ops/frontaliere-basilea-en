Full description

Tasks

  • Together with the medical and business management, you assume strategic, organizational and nursing responsibility for the Universitary Heart Center.
  • They actively shape the further development of the heart center and provide impulses for the highest quality, cost-effectiveness and consistent patient-centered care.
  • They lead the nursing management level with five direct and approximately 110 indirect employees and promote a high-performance, appreciative leadership culture.
  • They ensure high-quality nursing care and actively promote the further development of an evidence-based nursing practice
  • You are responsible for the care budget of the heart center and optimize the resource planning and the use of resources in close cooperation with the care management.
  • They establish, evaluate and optimize processes and structures continuously with regard to quality, efficiency and cost-effectiveness. Requirements
  • Diploma completed as a nurse HF/FH with a relevant master’s degree (e.g. in nursing science, management in healthcare or advanced practice nursing)
  • Several years of leadership experience in a complex, highly specialized hospital organization, ideally in the field of cardiology or cardiac surgery
  • Strong leadership, communication and change skills as well as the ability to inspire people for common goals
  • Decision-making strength, entrepreneurial thinking and a high implementation orientation
  • Enjoying interprofessional collaboration and the ability to make relationships sustainable at eye level
  • Strategic thinking skills, innovation and a high quality awareness in the university environment We offer:
  • Attractive wage policy and excellent social benefits
  • Advantages for shopping, sports and leisure
  • Assistance in moving and finding accommodation
  • 25 holiday days plus 5 also free flexitage
  • Support for further education and career planning
  • Campus in the heart of Basel and the Trinational Corner
  • Comprehensive childcare facilities
  • Free Sports and Exercise Program
