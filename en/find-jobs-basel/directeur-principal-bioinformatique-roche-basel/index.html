The Position Roche is working to improve DNA sequencing data analysis. Roche is innovating new approaches that fundamentally change when and where DNA sequencing gets processed. Our goal is to analyze data as it's generated, where it’s generated.

We are seeking a visionary and deeply technical leader to champion the customer-facing bioinformatics and software strategy for our revolutionary Axelios sequencing platform. As we launch XOOS —our cutting-edge ecosystem of bioinformatics tools—you will be the bridge between our pioneering new customers and our internal development teams.

This is not a traditional role. You will lead an elite team of bioinformatics scientists and software engineers to create bespoke, high-impact solutions, resolve sophisticated technical roadblocks, and even engage in collaborative research with early adopters. Because the Axelios platform operates on a completely different paradigm than traditional Sequencing-by-Synthesis (SBS), you will be instrumental in educating the market, ensuring seamless migration to our tools, and translating complex customer feedback into actionable development pipelines.

The Opportunity

  • Customer Advocacy & Technical Enablement: Serve as the primary executive-level technical contact for new customers, driving deep adoption of the XOOS bioinformatics ecosystem across major clinical and research applications.
  • Team Leadership: Build, inspire, and manage a high-performing team of customer-facing bioinformatics scientists and solutions architects.
  • Bespoke Solution Engineering: Lead the creation of customized workflows, data analysis pipelines, and software integrations to solve unique customer pain points and accelerate Axelios platform adoption.
  • Collaborative Research: Partner with strategic early-adopter customers on collaborative research projects, co-authoring whitepapers, and demonstrating the unique capabilities of Axelios non-SBS data.
  • Cross-Functional Feedback Loop: Act as a critical strategic partner to internal Bioinformatics and Software R&D teams, synthesizing market requirements, feature requests, and technical friction points into the product roadmap.
  • Commercial & Technical Strategy: Collaborate with Sales and Product Management to support high-value customer engagements, technical evaluations, and complex contract closures.

This position is based on-site in Santa Clara, CA or San Diego, CA. (This is a hybrid position.) Relocation benefits are not being offered for this role.
