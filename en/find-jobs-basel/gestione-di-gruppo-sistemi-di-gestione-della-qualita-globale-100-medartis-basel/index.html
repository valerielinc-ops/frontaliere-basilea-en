Group Manager Global Quality Management Systems 100%

Your remit

  • Professional and disciplinary leadership and targeted further development of the QMS team
  • Strategic orientation and continuous optimization of local and global quality management systems
  • Ensure compliance with relevant regulatory and normative requirements, in particular ISO 13485 and FDA QMSR
  • Initiation, coordination and implementation of local and group-wide QMS and improvement projects
  • Harmonization and standardization of quality processes, SOPs, templates and tools across the organization
  • Control of eQMS optimizations and

System changes in close cooperation with Quality Operations, IT and other stakeholders

  • Technical responsibility for central QMS processes such as NN/CAPA, Change Control, Document Management and Management Review
  • Establishment and analysis of relevant quality indicators and derivation of effective improvement measures
  • Monitoring internal and external audits and regulatory inspections
  • Strengthening a sustainable quality culture and promoting the continuous approach to improvement

Your qualifications

  • Completed studies in the field of life sciences, engineering, pharmacy or comparable
  • multi-year professional experience in the field of quality management in the medical technology or pharmaceutical industry
  • Management experience and demonstrable experience in the professional management of projects/teams
  • Safe handling of MS Office as well as experience with PLM or document management systems
  • Extensive organizational skills and a high quality awareness
  • Strength of communication and joy in cooperation with various stakeholders
  • Structured, solution-oriented and

self-responsible working method - Very good German and English knowledge in word and writing

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of osteosynthesis products in the area of facial skull and extremities. Medartis has committed itself to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and offering the best service for the benefit of the patient.
