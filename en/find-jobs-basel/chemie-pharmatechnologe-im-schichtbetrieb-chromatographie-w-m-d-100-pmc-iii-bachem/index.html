Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in chromatography, we are looking for a dedicated chemical & pharmacologist in shift operation (a), full-time.

Their tasks

  • Perform chemical process steps in the production environment (e.g. cyclization, decarboxylation, folding, distillation) according to production instructions
  • Preparation and handling of buffers, solutions, solvents, active ingredients and suspensions incl. Presentation/inclusion of process solutions according to HVO
  • Filtration of process and product solutions and packing/unpacking of chromatography columns incl. Preparation of column suspensions
  • Process control incl. Scanning, weighing, calibration of pH meters, pH adjustment, sampling and registration for analysis
  • Operation of automation systems (SIMATIC PCS7 / PAX X, WinCC, SIMATIC Batch) as well as basic understanding of displacement and centrifugal pumps

Your profile

  • Completed training as a chemical and pharmatechnologist/login EFZ (or equivalent) or at least 3 years of professional experience in a chemical or pharmaceutical production environment
  • In-depth understanding of Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) and a strong awareness of safety and quality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Readiness for multi-shift model: 3-3-3 (3 Early-3 Late 3 Free) and 6-4 shift operation (6 days of work – 4 days off)
  • Ideally, experience in the GMP-regulated environment as well as in dealing with production plants and reactors
  • Knowledge of HPLC, ultrafiltration (UF/DF) or electronic batch records (e.g. PAS-X) is advantageous
  • Interest in continuous improvement (5S) and further development of processes

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
