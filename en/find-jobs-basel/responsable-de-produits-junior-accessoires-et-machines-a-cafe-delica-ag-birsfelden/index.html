Full description

Do you love reliably coordinating products, processes and documents and successfully advancing them? At Delica you will take on a versatile role related to Accessories & Coffee Machines. You can expect an appreciative team that is proud of its customer-oriented products, and an environment in which you can develop further and help shape shared successes. Mansioni

  • You manage and optimize our accessory range of the brands CoffeeB, Café Royal, Delizio & Cremesso independently
  • You implement projects in the Accessories & Coffee Machines area operationally and coordinate tasks, appointments and coordination with internal departments, especially with marketing and purchasing, as well as external partners
  • You are responsible for the coordination of print data and operating instructions for the sales packaging of Accessories & Coffee Machines in collaboration with marketing and external agencies
  • You support with administrative tasks in the coffee area with a focus in the area of coffee machines
  • You carry out market and trend analyzes in relation to the relevant projects
  • Requirements
  • 2 to 3 years of professional experience: in a comparable function, ideally in product management or marketing
  • Completed studies (university/ETH, FH, HF): focus on marketing / business administration or commercial training with appropriate further training
  • Enjoyment of coordinative, administrative and cross-departmental tasks
  • You are no stranger to initiative: you work in a structured, precise manner and independent, keep track of multiple parallel topics and complete tasks on time
  • You think in a solution-oriented manner, act pragmatically and impress with clear, cooperative communication with different stakeholders
