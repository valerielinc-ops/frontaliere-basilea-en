The Position The International Product Manager acts as the ultimate owner of their specific product area, holding accountability for delivering measurable business outcomes beyond feature outputs. This role leads continuous product discovery and validation to ensure solutions are valuable to customers, viable for the business, and technically feasible. By providing strategic context and driving cross-functional collaboration, the International Product Manager aligns engineering efforts with broader business goals while serving as the deep subject matter expert on the product domain.

The Opportunity

Main tasks

  • Product Outcome Accountability: Holds overall accountability for the product's outcomes, responsible for delivering measurable business results rather than just feature outputs.
  • Continuous Discovery & Validation: Leads continuous product discovery to ensure solutions are valuable to customers, viable for the business, usable by users, and technically feasible to build.
  • Strategic Context & Alignment: Provides strategic context to the team, ensuring that stable core teams are focused on work that is genuinely worth building and delivers necessary results.
  • Cross-Functional Leadership: Drives cross-functional collaboration, balancing the needs of customers, the business, and technical feasibility.
  • Customer & Domain Expertise: Maintains comprehensive and well informed knowledge of customers/users (qualitative and quantitative), product data, and the broader market and industry.
  • Risk Ownership: Owns the customer value and viability risk, ensuring the product meets business needs while solving customer problems.

Additional tasks

  • Data-Driven Learning: Constantly learns from customers and data to inform product decisions.
  • Business Acumen: Applies deep business knowledge, including finance, sales, and legal aspects, to product decision-making.
  • Owner's Mindset: Maintains an owner's mindset for the specific product or problem area, acting as the critical element for the success of the empowered product team.
