Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the Global Project Management department, we are looking for a dedicated “Associate CMC Project Manager” (a), 100%

Your Tasks

  • Coordinate and manage customer and internal R&D projects with cross-functional teams of 3-10 members.
  • Ensure project deliverables are achieved on time, within scope, and in line with customer agreements and contracts.
  • Act as the primary point of contact for project-related questions, issues, and coordination activities.
  • Drive ownership and accountability for all project deliverables across the project team.
  • Structure, coordinate, and monitor project activities using established tools, systems, and processes.
  • Prepare and maintain accurate project documentation, including meeting minutes, action lists, and project plans.
  • Facilitate effective communication with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Proactively identify risks, challenges, and mitigation measures to support project success.
  • Support continuous process improvements and contribute to project management best practices.
  • Develop towards a leading project management role with increasing responsibility.

Your Profile

  • BSc or equivalent with at least 3 years of experience in the biopharmaceutical, pharmaceutical, CDMO, or chemical industry; or MSc or equivalent with at least 1 year of relevant industry experience.
  • Customer-facing project management experience is preferred.
  • Exposure to a GMP-regulated environment is an advantage.
  • Strong organizational, planning, and coordination skills.
  • High level of ownership, accountability, and reliability.
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities effectively.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Fluent in English (minimum C1 level).
  • German language skills are an advantage (Bubendorf site).
  • Team-oriented personality with a proactive and solution-focused mindset.

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal development and training opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with home office days and an option for obtaining additional vacation days through workload reduction
  • Company‑run daycare center on the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as option for extra-mandatory pension provision with our Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to benefits platforms with discounts from external partners
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
