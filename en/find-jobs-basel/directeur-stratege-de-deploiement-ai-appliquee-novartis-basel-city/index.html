Job Description

Main responsibilities include, but are not limited to

  • Independently drive strategic tech/AI projects for Novartis Group, the CEO and the Executive Committee
  • Drive value by defining cross-cutting solutions (including cross-functional working groups, tech products) that will enable AI to fully scale in the business

  • Develop network of tech/AI experts and leaders across Novartis

What you'll bring to the role

  • Bachelor’s degree, advanced degree preferred (MS, MBA)
  • Proficiency in English required (spoken and written) with proficiency in other languages a plus
  • Hands on experience (e.g., 2 years) in digital/AI related fields e.g., product management, digital analytics, building software products
  • Established record of driving strategic AI/digital transformation and topics with value creation in mind
  • Significant experience gained in a top tier consultancy (manager level)
  • Project and program management experience in strategic projects, ideally in pharma/healthcare with a track record of strong business impact
  • Effective and persuasive communicator with strong presentation, communication (both written and verbal), influencing and negotiation skills.
  • Ability to work with and lead multi-disciplinary teams including engineers, architects· Experience in executive level communications.
  • Ability to travel domestically and internationally, to be determined based on business needs.
