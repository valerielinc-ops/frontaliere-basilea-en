Full description

About us REHAB Basel, Clinic for Neurorehabilitation and Paraplegiology, treats and rehabilitates patients with paraplegia, brain injury and related clinical pictures after an accident or illness. Our ultimate goal is, together with the people entrusted to us, to achieve the greatest possible independence and an optimal quality of life. We treat successfully in the area of initial and subsequent rehabilitation. The clinic has 110 beds and an outpatient operation. Around 660 employees implement the vision and values of REHAB Basel in their daily work. At REHAB Basel you will work in a unique building in the attractive border triangle of Switzerland, France and Germany. For our team in the Psychological Service we are looking for a pregnancy and maternity representation from 15. Oct. 2026 or by arrangement for a limited period until the end of April 2027 a psychotherapist with a 60% - 80% workload with an option for permanent employment.

Job title Psychotherapist 60% - 80% m/f/d

Your responsibilities

  • Psychotherapy of people with paraplegia and/or brain injury
  • Accompanying relatives
  • Support for treatment teams
  • Representation of the Psychological Service in the interprofessional team

Your profile

  • Advanced or completed psychotherapy training
  • Psychotherapy in German and ideally also in French
  • High degree of independence, transparent communication, resilience and ability for interprofessional cooperation
  • Interested in working with physically ill people
  • Welcome (but not provided) are previous experiences and interests in the fields of psycho-oncology, palliative psychology, ethics

You will find with us

  • A dedicated team and interprofessional cooperation
  • Contact with diverse tasks and clinical pictures
  • Good support for familiarization in the field of activity
  • A highly specialized, modern rehabilitation center

In addition, you will find diverse, team-promoting and employee-friendly offers such as staff trips, personnel events, free sports for employees and much more.

Our team is looking forward to you.
