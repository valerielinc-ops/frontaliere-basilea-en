Full description

Klinik Arlesheim is an acute hospital with 90 inpatient beds in the fields of oncology, internal medicine and psychiatry/psychosomatics as well as an oncology daily clinic with 24 beds. Outpatient primary care services include gynecology, dermatology, gastroenterology, family medicine, cardiology, neurology, pulmonology and sleep medicine. The emergency is open around the clock. Medical diagnosis and therapy is based on the modern possibilities of scientific medicine. As the first anthroposophical clinic in the world, Klinik Arlesheim has more than 100 years of experience with the integrative concept of anthroposophic medicine. To strengthen our medical diagnostics team, we are looking for a trained dipl immediately or by agreement. day care professional (Monday-Friday).

Task profile

  • Professional assistance in endoscopic examinations: You support our medical team in diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopies (e.g. gastroscopy, colonoscopy) and take over the comprehensive preparation of the patients.
  • Care and supervision: After endoscopic procedures, you accompany our inpatient and outpatient patients: inside, monitor the sedation and take care of the follow-up care.
  • Ensuring equipment and hygiene quality: They ensure that our endoscopic devices comply with internal hygienic requirements at all times.
  • Organization and day planning: You support the efficient planning and organization of the daily process and thus contribute to smooth processes.
  • Quality and concept development: You implement innovative care and support concepts and contribute to the continuous improvement of care quality.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work closely with our medical team and the secretariat, as well as other employees of the clinic.
  • Partial cooperation in the diagnostics of pneumology and cardiology: They support the departments of pneumology and cardiology in diagnostic examinations, take over the professional preparation, monitoring and follow-up of the patients.

They bring with them

  • Diploma as nurse HF or equivalent with SRK recognition
  • Experience in endoscopy, gastroenterology and/or functional diagnostics
  • Efficient and clean working methods and enjoyment of contact with people
  • Motivation and willingness to actively participate in new processes
  • Joy and interest to learn and pass on what has been learned
  • Flexibility, reliability, strength and independence
  • solution-oriented and communicative personality

We offer

A varied and responsible activity with a lot of creative leeway and a well-founded introduction. You can expect a good working atmosphere and attractive working conditions in a value-oriented hospital, a good public transport connection and our TNW job ticket, free use of pick-e-bike and regular working hours. For further information please contact Simone Heller, Head of Medical Diagnostics: Tel. +41 (0)61 705 70 33. Only direct applications will be considered. We look forward to meeting you.
