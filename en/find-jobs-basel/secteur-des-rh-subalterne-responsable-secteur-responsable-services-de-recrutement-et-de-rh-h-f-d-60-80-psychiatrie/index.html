Junior HR Area Manager / Area Manager - Recruiting & HR Services (m/f/d) 60 - 80% Psychiatry Baselland Human Resources

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the recruitment process of the assigned professional groups from the creation and publication of the job advertisement to entry
  • Application management and coordination of interviews
  • Preparation of the wage classification in coordination with the responsible managers
  • Clarifications and administrative support in special employment relationships, including social security issues and work permits
  • Contact person for managers and employees of the assigned professional groups
  • Advice on questions of maternity, unpaid leave, sickness and working hours as well as assistance in the event of withdrawals and personnel law issues
  • Support of managers in questions of performance and behavior as well as participation in handling more complex personnel situations
  • Processing of employment contracts and contract changes such as extensions, terminations and breakdown changes as well as organisational changes within the assigned occupational groups
  • Cooperation in the organisation and implementation of professional fairs and graduation ceremonies for learners
  • Gradually assuming additional consulting and responsibilities according to your strengths and professional development

Your profile

  • Commercial training and in-depth training in the field of human resources (at least at the level of HR-related processing); further HR training is advantageous or is sought
  • Professional experience in HR, preferably in recruiting and personnel administration
  • Experience in the clinic, hospital or pharmaceutical environment is an advantage
  • good knowledge of social security schemes, in particular in relation to different types of employment
  • Knowledge of hiring people from EU/EFTA and third countries is an advantage
  • Basic knowledge of employment law and interest in further deepening it
  • Structured, careful and independent working methods
  • High service orientation and enjoyment of cooperation within a versatile HR team

Job opening: The job is to be filled by agreement.
