Welcome to Medbase Basel St. Elisabethen

Our modern medical center is located in the heart of the cultural city of Basel and is well connected to public transport. We look after our patients interdisciplinary and quality-oriented. The centre’s main focus is on general internal medicine, physiotherapy and complementary therapies acupuncture/tuina and massage. Our team, consisting of almost 50 employees, consists of specialists, therapists from various specialist areas, MPAs and other employees. We maintain a cross-professional and mutually enriching cooperation. At Medbase Basel St. Elisabethen, we also appreciate the international flair in the border triangle. Our flexible working models and deputy solutions enable a good work-life balance.

Maternity representation 40-50% temporary until 31.8.2027

At Medbase Basel St. Elisabethen, you take care of the smooth running of the administration. You support the employees of our interdisciplinary team. For our customers you will find solutions in the administrative area. They feel comfortable and perceived by your expertise and friendly nature.

Your tasks include

  • Support of the interdisciplinary practice team in administrative tasks, e.g. processing of insurance reports
  • Invoice/warrant runs, rejection
  • Implementation of invoice and dunning runs and processing and follow-up of rejections and further clarifications resulting therefrom
  • Processing of patient requests by telephone and e-mail
  • Management and maintenance of the address base
  • Support of the head of physiotherapy in the organizational area
  • Further administrative work

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
