The Position The Employee Lifecycle Management team focuses on P&C lifecycle support and provides customer service and advisory support to P&C and business partners on global P&C systems and processes and ensuring end-to-end process completion in line with customer focused principles to ensure the Roche People Practices work for the business.

As an Employee Lifecycle Management Specialist , you will join our collaborative People and Culture team to provide vital advisory support, ensuring seamless and efficient end-to-end processes for our global workforce. This is a unique opportunity to directly influence our organizational culture by applying customer-centric principles that enhance the daily lives of our employees and support the overall success of the business.

The Opportunity

Operational Excellence & Data Integrity

  • Maintain accurate People and Culture system data to ensure high-quality service delivery and compliance.
  • Manage administrative activities for complex termination processes.

Advisory & Stakeholder Management

  • Resolve complex inquiries, acting as a primary point of contact to provide proactive advisory support to business partners.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with regional and global peers to drive global process consistency.

Continuous Improvement & Impact

  • Contribute to continuous improvement by analyzing service interactions to identify and implement process enhancements.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to streamline workflows and improve the overall end-to-end user experience.
  • Apply customer-centric principles to actively enhance daily employee interactions and support broader business success.

Who you are

  • 2-5 years of experience in People and Culture service delivery or customer care is required, with a proven ability to manage high-volume transactional processes effectively.
  • A strong ability to handle multiple priorities and adapt seamlessly to organizational changes thrives in a fast-paced, matrix-structured environment.
  • Excellent English and fluent German communication skills enable the clear conveyance of complex information and the maintenance of positive relationships with various stakeholders.
  • Proficiency in standard IT applications, specifically Google Suite and Workday, is essential, alongside a diligent approach to maintaining high levels of data accuracy.
  • A deep commitment to customer centricity allows for the proactive application of an "end-to-end" mindset to improve service delivery and enhance the user experience.
  • Growth-oriented thinking drives the continuous identification of process enhancements, workflow simplification, and the learning of new methodologies.
  • Certification as an HR Specialist (e.g., Federal Diploma of Higher Education or an equivalent relevant HR qualification) is preferred .
