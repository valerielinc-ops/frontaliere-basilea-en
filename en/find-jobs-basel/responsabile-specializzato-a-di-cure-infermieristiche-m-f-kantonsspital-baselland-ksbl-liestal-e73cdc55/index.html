Tasks

Do you want to not only live care, but actively develop it? Then bring your professional expertise and shape the future of care together with us.

  • Ensure professional, patient-centred and evidence-based care and care for our patients and their relatives
  • Share responsibility for securing and continuously developing the quality of care as well as implementing and sustainably anchoring LeanHospital
  • Advice and coaching of employees in complex patient situations or nursing issues as well as support of new employees
  • Introduction, training and evaluation of care guidelines, instructions and concepts on the station
  • Participation in the implementation of innovations and changes in care and in the interprofessional context
  • Contribute expertise in care-specific working groups and projects and help shape the further development of care
  • Member of the Station Management Committee within the framework of shared governance with joint responsibility for the strategic station development

Requirements

  • Base: Dipl. Nursing specialist FH (Bachelor of Science in Nursing)
  • Glad: At least 2 years of professional experience as a Dipl. Nursing professional, preferably in surgery and/or internal medicine
  • Very happy: In-depth competences in patient education, discussion and clinical decision-making
  • In addition: Quality-conscious, critical and reflective thinking as well as self-responsible and reliable action
  • Absolutely: cordial, appreciative and customer-oriented attitude towards patients, relatives and employees
  • And also: Empathy, flexibility, focus on results and assertiveness Do you share our values – savvy, warm, hands-on and reliable? Then you are a great fit for us!
