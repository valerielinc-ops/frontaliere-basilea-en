Full description

The Director, Medical Excellence Team Leader provides day-to-day leadership and line management to the Medical Excellence Managers who deliver Sobi’s Investigator Sponsored Studies (ISS), Managed Access Programmes (MAP), and Post-Trial Access (PTA) programmes. Reporting to the Head of Medical Excellence and Operations, the role sits between the Head of function and the Medical Excellence Managers, and oversees robust, compliant and efficient operational delivery across all relevant assets, therapeutic areas and regions. The Team Leader ensures the team is well resourced and applies aligned ways of working, whilst acting as a senior operational point of contact and decision-maker for ISS, MAP and PTA. They should also drive continuous improvement, governance, audit readiness and cross-functional collaboration, and deputise for the Head of Medical Excellence and Operations on ISS/MAP/PTA matters as required.

Key Responsibilities Team leadership and line management

  • Provides direct line management to Medical Excellence Managers, including objective setting, development, coaching, and performance management.
  • Ensures fair, balanced and transparent allocation of work across the team,
  • Onboards new team members effectively and quickly, builds in back-up and resilience across the team, and encourages a supportive, collaborative culture.
  • Identifies resourcing needs, supports recruitment, and scales capacity flexibly (including through vendors) to meet fluctuating demand; contributes to succession planning for the team.

ISS management

  • Oversees the ISS review and approval process across assets, ensuring consistent, aligned and compliant ways of working, and timely execution of ISS Evaluation Committee (EC) meetings and contracts according to agreed priorities.
  • Oversees the maintenance and improvement of a complete and audit-ready ISS database.
  • Ensures ISS Areas of Interest remain updated, focused and clearly communicated for all assets, working with Medical Program Leads and cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Oversees ISS budget tracking and the budget approval process, and supports development of guidance on budget funding / fair market value (FMV) in collaboration with Compliance and Finance.
  • Drives training and communication on ISS process, prioritisation and roles (including affiliates and regional/local teams).
  • Contributes to the ISS Steering Committee, as needed, in order to guide strategy and prioritization across the ISS programme.

MAP and PTA management

  • Oversees the MAP/PTA review and approval process, ensuring timely, compliant management of cases, documentation of decisions and communication to affiliates, external requestors and third parties.
  • Oversees the maintenance and improvement of a complete and audit-ready MAP/PTA database.
  • Operationalises MAP and PTA programmes for new assets, indications and regions.
  • Oversees vendor set-up and management for MAP/PTA programmes, and assesses new vendors as required.
  • Contributes to the MAP committee, supporting strategic decisions on new MAPs and transitions to post-trial access programmes.

Governance, process and continuous improvement

  • Ensures a robust governance framework for decision-making and strategic prioritisation of programmes and budgets across ISS, MAP and PTA.
  • Ensures audit readiness and supports system audits, including ownership and execution of CAPA plans and associated SOP updates.
  • Fosters continuous development of processes, platforms (e.g. the request portal), templates, checklists and guidance documents, including driving efficiencies including through the use of AI.
  • Develops reporting and dashboards to communicate valued information and team KPIs to leadership, MACD and cross-functional stakeholders; demonstrate the value and impact of the function.
  • Maintains and grows collaborative relationships with other functions, including Supply Chain, Pharmacovigilance, Quality, Legal, Compliance and Regulatory.
