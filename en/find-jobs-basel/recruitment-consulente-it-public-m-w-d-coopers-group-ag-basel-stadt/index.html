Are you a Cooper?

Are you a communicative and sales-driven personality who feels at home in sales? Is it important to you not only to build customer relationships, but also to maintain them sustainably and in partnership? Then you are exactly right with us! We offer you a great working environment, great colleagues and the opportunity to grow both personally and professionally.

Your tasks

  • You will gain new customers in the public administration environment and develop existing customer relationships into long-term key accounts.
  • You will support public administrations and public-law organisations in German-speaking Switzerland in successfully implementing their IT projects with the appropriate external IT specialists.
  • You are responsible for the entire process, from tendering to coordinating the right specialists to successful project implementation and customer support. You will be supported by our Talent Acquisition Team.
  • You support your team in the evaluation of public tenders and the preparation of corresponding offers.
  • You maintain a close, cooperative relationship with your customers and build sustainable, trusting business relationships.

You bring with you

  • A completed commercial or business education as well as experience in account management in personnel consulting, ideally in the environment of public administrations.
  • A sales-strong, communicative personality as well as a secure and professional appearance
  • A high quality standard for your daily work
  • A high degree of responsibility and self-motivation
  • Desire to work in a humorous, competent and appreciative team
  • Flowing German and good English skills

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
