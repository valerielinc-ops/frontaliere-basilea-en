At SPIRIT, we accompany people on their way to greater mobility, health and quality of life. We combine physiotherapy with modern training and create a smooth transition from treatment to sustainable movement. The human being is our focus. Personal, holistic and focused on the essentials. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated physiotherapist for a workload of 40-80%. This makes the job special We combine physiotherapy with exercise and use the possibilities of our generous training area with Pilates devices such as reformers, trapeze table and chair. Plenty of space for functional training and many other training tools. You work closely with experienced trainers and movement specialists. You design your treatments independently and accompany people individually on their way – from individual therapy to active training. And all this in the middle of Basel in our bright rooms in Gundeldingerfeld, in the middle of Basel. This is what awaits you

  • Professional qualifications

You have completed training as a physiotherapist and have an SRK recognition or the prerequisite to achieve it. You like to actively accompany people on their way to more mobility, resilience and quality of life and see training as an important part of a successful rehabilitation. Ideally, you have already trained in sports physiotherapy or would like to develop this focus. Experience in Pilates is a plus. But above all, you bring curiosity, openness and joy to accompany people holistically and continuously develop yourself professionally. Personality You enjoy working with people and meeting them with interest, appreciation and empathy. You take responsibility for your work, act independently and bring the ability to actively shape everyday practice. At the same time, you appreciate the exchange in the team and an open, uncomplicated communication. It motivates you not only to treat people in the short term, but also to accompany and strengthen them in the long term on their way to greater mobility, resilience and quality of life. Working principle You think solution-oriented and look at people in their entirety. You are responsible for your own work as well as working in a committed team. The combination of physical therapy, training and exercise corresponds to your understanding of sustainable rehabilitation. You bring in ideas, take responsibility and actively shape your work area. Who we are For more than 20 years, the SPIRIT STUDIO has combined movement, health and training. According to our motto “teach people, not poses”, we focus on people. Our aim is to accompany people professionally, personally and sustainably. We look forward to seeing you
