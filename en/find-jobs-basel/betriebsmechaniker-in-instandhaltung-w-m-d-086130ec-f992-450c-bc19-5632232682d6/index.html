Pensum: 100% | Augst

Make a contribution to the energy future. You are skilled in craftsmanship, versatile and want to work in an exciting environment where sustainability and energy production go hand in hand? Then you are exactly right with us! As a mechanic in our team, you ensure that our hydropower plants run smoothly and thus make a valuable contribution to the energy supply.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Performing inspection, maintenance and repair work
  • Participation in renewals of power plants
  • Operation and monitoring of the power plant control technology
  • General maintenance work on outdoor installations
  • Fault detection and repair of mechanical equipment
  • Readiness to perform Picettdienst (about 10 weeks per year)

Profile & Requirements

  • Completed basic mechanical training
  • Several years of professional experience in the maintenance of mechanical equipment beneficial
  • Good organisational and administrative skills
  • Readiness to use digital tools in everyday work
  • Efficient, independent and reliable work and pleasant manners
  • Willingness to learn something new in a hydroelectric power plant
  • Location within 30 minutes of Augst
