Sometimes you need a place where not everything has to go faster - but more consciously. We focus on people: our patients as well as our employees. We believe that good care is created where expertise, responsibility and humanity work together.

For our hospital in Basel, we are looking for a personality who would like to follow exactly this path with us: Dipl. Nurse HF 80 - 100% | Location Basel Your contribution

  • You will reconcile the quality of care with the individual needs of our patients
  • You will be responsible for the nursing diagnostic process (according to NANDA) and ensure structured, professionally sound care
  • You accompany and support trainees FaGe and HF students in their learning process
  • You actively represent the nursing service in the interprofessional team What sets you apart
  • Degree as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH or equivalent training with SRC recognition
  • Experience in acute care hospitals or rehabilitation
  • An open, solution-oriented and proactive way of working
  • High social skills, reliability and sense of responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German and strong IT user skills You can look forward to a varied and responsible job in a renowned Basel institution. With us you will work in a committed team that lives humanity, mutual appreciation and high quality care every day. If you value contact with older people and would like to contribute your experience in a specialized geriatric environment, we look forward to receiving your online application with a complete application dossier. The Adullam Foundation is a leading institution for geriatric medicine, therapy and nursing in northwestern Switzerland. It operates a hospital and an affiliated care centre in Basel and Riehen. The approximately 800 employees offer elderly people all-round care that is unique in the region, from acute medicine and rehabilitation to transitional and long-term care. Holistic care at Adullam includes high quality standards, lived humanity and great respect for old age.
