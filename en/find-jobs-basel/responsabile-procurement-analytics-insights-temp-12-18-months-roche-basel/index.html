Company and context

  • In Roche's Global Procurement organization, we support Roche's goal of bringing innovative solutions and medicines to patients through business and supplier partnerships.
  • We act as the bridge between Roche's business functions and our suppliers to understand their needs, find opportunities, and add value far beyond cost savings.
  • Within Global Procurement, the Analytics & Insights function integrates internal procurement, financial, and operational data with external market and supplier intelligence to generate actionable insights that drive data-informed decision-making across sourcing, category management, supplier management, and business strategy.
  • As the Head of Procurement Analytics & Insights (Fixed-Term), you will provide strategic leadership and operational management of our analytics function during a 12-18 month leave coverage period.
  • Your role is crucial in executing our current analytics roadmap while driving the organizational evolution toward a more insights-driven and decision-support-oriented team.
  • You will inherit an established team and strategy, but this is also an opportunity to accelerate transformation - evolving the team's capabilities, deepening stakeholder engagement, and shifting the organization's mindset toward proactive insights and data-informed decision-making.
  • You will guide the data-driven approach to procurement, driving insights, forecasting, and strategy to enhance the organization's sourcing, contracting, category management, and supplier management activities.
  • You will oversee the business insights internal support service, develop and implement procurement analytics solutions, ensure data quality and governance, and provide actionable insights that support decision-making, cost savings, and efficiency improvements.
  • You bring technical skills, strategic thinking, and expertise in procurement processes to influence and shape business outcomes. The Opportunity:
  • Leadership & Transformation: Provide strategic and operational leadership for the analytics team during a 12–18 month coverage, evolving it from operational reporting into a proactive, insights-driven unit.
