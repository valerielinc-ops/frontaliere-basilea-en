Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

What is the role about?

You will lead the IT systems and software engineering team with seven employees and strategically develop the team and system landscape of electronics production. In addition, you coordinate cooperation with central IT, production and other specialist areas and ensure competent 2nd and 3rd level support.

What tasks will you take on?

  • You lead the newly established IT system and software engineering team with seven employees in terms of expertise and personnel and develop the organization further.
  • They are responsible for the recruitment, introduction and sustainable development of new team members and create optimal conditions for efficient cooperation.
  • They shape the strategic development of the team and the system landscape for electronics production at the Reinach site and align them consistently with future requirements.
  • They ensure close cooperation with central IT, industrialization, production and project management and ensure competent 2nd and 3rd level support for complex technical issues.
  • They identify future requirements for systems and software, prioritize them together with the relevant stakeholders and coordinate their successful implementation.

What do we expect from you?

  • You have completed a technical degree in computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable field of study.
  • They bring several years of professional experience in the industrial environment as well as profound leadership experience and understand complex technical contexts.
  • They have proven experience in project management and software development as well as knowledge of SAP (R3 and S4Hana), ERP and MES systems (iTAC beneficial).
  • They communicate stylishly in German and English and move confidently in an international environment.
  • They impress with an independent, creative and solution-oriented working method as well as strong communication and team skills.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
