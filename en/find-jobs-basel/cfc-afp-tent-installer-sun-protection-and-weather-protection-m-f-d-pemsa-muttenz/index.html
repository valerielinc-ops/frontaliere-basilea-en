Pemsa is a nationally-leading staff selection agency, specialized in recruiting and providing qualified personnel in building, civil engineering, electronics, plant and industry technology. With over 30 years of industry experience, Pemsa offers both customers and candidates a comprehensive and professional range of services. Thanks to our capillary presence on the territory, we guarantee an optimal proximity to the yards of our customers – regardless of their location – thus creating efficient solutions for all parties involved.

Mansions

  • Mounting and repair of sun protection systems and weather protection, such as sun curtains, arm curtains and roller shutters, in compliance with safety and quality standards.
  • Independent installation of electrical drives and control systems on different plants in the field of solar protection.
  • Performing on-site maintenance and servicing works with customers, including fault diagnosis and troubleshooting.
  • Customer advice on technical possibilities and product characteristics, as well as custom solutions.
  • Organization of your vehicle park and materials management to ensure the smooth running of the activities.
  • Art-based documentation of all projects and activities, as well as collaboration with administrative staff and project managers.
  • Compliance with current safety standards and participation in continuing training for the continuous improvement of qualifications.

Profile required

  • Vocational training completed as a sun curtain installer with federal capacity certificate (AFC); AFC is an indispensable requirement.
  • At least 3 years of working experience in Switzerland in the installation of sun protection systems and weather.
  • Excellent German mastery at native language level to communicate safely with customers and team.
  • Availability of a vehicle park, including cars and tools, to be able to work independently and efficiently.
  • Good knowledge of electrical drive systems and control systems is an advantage.
  • High service orientation, self-organized working and reliability.
  • Availability for occasional interventions outside the usual home area; mobility and flexibility are required.
  • Essential category B driving licence.
