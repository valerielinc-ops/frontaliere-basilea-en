Full description

About us Learning to live again and again. The REHAB Basel, Clinic for Neurorehabilitation and Paraplegiology, treats and rehabilitates patients with brain injury, paraplegia and related clinical pictures after an accident or illness. We offer an exceptionally broad range of therapy in order to successfully help the people entrusted to us to achieve the greatest possible independence and an optimal quality of life. The clinic has 110 beds and an outpatient operation. Around 660 employees implement the visions and values of REHAB Basel in their everyday work. They work in a unique building in the attractive border triangle of Switzerland, Germany and France.

To complement our team, from January 2027 onwards, we will be looking for a speech therapist in the function of deputy head of speech therapy part-time or full-time (60-100%).

Job title Stv. Head of Speech Medicine 60-100% m/f/d

Your responsibilities

  • Close cooperation with the management of speech therapy and taking over the deputy during their absence with responsibility for the operational interests of the area and around 20 employees
  • Participation in the recruitment and training of new employees as well as in the scheduling and design of efficient internal workflows in close cooperation with the management
  • Active participation in the promotion, development and support of employees as well as their contact person for personnel and team-related concerns
  • Co-responsibility for the further development of team structures, processes and team cooperation
  • Diagnosis, therapy and counselling of patients with neurogenic speech, speech, voice, and swallowing disorders as well as facial paralysis
  • Documentation of the course of therapy and close interprofessional cooperation with therapy, doctors and nursing teams

Your profile

  • Completed study of speech therapy with EDK recognition
  • Several years of experience in the field of neurorehabilitation/clinical speech therapy
  • Joy of developing professionally and personally in a management function
  • High sense of responsibility, willingness to reflect and high social and communicative competence
  • Strong organisational skills, networked thinking and innovation
  • Commitment to quality assurance, further development and evidence-based therapy
  • Secure handling of documentation and clinical IT systems
  • Enjoy independent and patient-oriented work in the interprofessional team as well as a high ethical attitude
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French and/or Italian is advantageous

You will find with us

  • The entire spectrum of clinical speech therapy - a wide range of activities with diverse diagnostic and everyday as well as innovative therapeutic possibilities (e.g. therapy kitchens, workshop, therapy garden, tablets, possibilities of neuromodulatory stimulation - PES, FES, tDCS, TMS)
  • A targeted familiarization with the management function as well as a diverse range of internal and external training and further education supported by the employer
  • Expertise of certified instructors for the therapy concepts F.O.T.T., Bobath and Affolter
  • A close cooperation with other professional groups, such as music therapy or animal-assisted therapy with the animals from our therapy animal garden
  • Work-life balance through flexible, individual working time models and an appreciative working environment

In addition, you will find a wide range of team-promoting and employee-friendly offers such as staff trips, free sports for employees and much more. For information, Nina Clobes, Head of Speech Medicine, n.clobes@rehab.ch, is at your disposal. Our team is looking forward to you.

Position to be filled 01.01.2027 or by agreement
