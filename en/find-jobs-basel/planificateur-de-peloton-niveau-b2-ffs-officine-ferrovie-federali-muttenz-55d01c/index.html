Full description

SBB | SBB Cargo International: Train preparer/in Level B2 - - - SBB Cargo International is the market leader in European rail freight transport on the north-south axis. Thanks to our more than 1,000 employees in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Italy and France, we travel around 550 trains per week from the North Sea ports to Italy and back. In doing so, we are shifting thousands of net tons from road to rail and making an active contribution to more climate protection. Do you want to set the course for your future and take responsibility for European freight transport? Be part of our team as Train preparer/in Level B2 In Muttenz

Their tasks

As a member of the train preparation team, you make a significant contribution to operational safety and carry out technical departure or roadside inspections. You check freight wagons and cargoes and repair any damage and defects, as far as possible, independently. You carry out quality controls in accordance with AVV / UIC 471-3 (RID) and work closely with train drivers, the control centre, external partners and the employees of the shunting team. In addition, you carry out interventions on freight trains in German-speaking Switzerland.

Your profile

We are addressing a committed and team-capable personality with a high degree of solution orientation and an independent working method. Ideally, you have an apprenticeship as a visitor/car master or a comparable technical training in the railway environment. Several years of professional experience and knowledge in international freight transport are desired. You communicate safely in German, both verbally and in writing. Knowledge of Italian and French is an advantage. The readiness for shift work (24/7) and a category B driving licence are required.
