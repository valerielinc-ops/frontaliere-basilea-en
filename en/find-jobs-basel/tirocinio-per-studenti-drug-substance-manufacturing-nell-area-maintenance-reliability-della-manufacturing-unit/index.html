The Position At Roche, 94,000 employees in 100 countries are working to redefine the term healthcare. At the Basel Drug Substance Manufacturing site of F. Hoffmann-La Roche AG, you will get a unique insight into the production of chemical and biological active pharmaceutical ingredients for commercial and clinical care. Our site consists of three production units (so-called manufacturing units) and four support units (so-called units), in which a total of 650 employees focus on providing patients with medicines.

At the Basel Drug Substance site, we produce in three manufacturing units (Monoclonal Antibody (MAB), Antibody Drug Conjugate (ADC) and Single Use Technology (SUT). The three manufacturing units are supported by four units plus quality (MSAT, Materials Management, Facilities Management & SHE, Digital+OE, Quality). For the Monoclonal Antibody (MAB), Antibody Drug Conjugate (ADC) and Single Use Technology (SUT), we are looking for you! There are varied and challenging tasks waiting for you in a biological production environment. You will learn how GMP production works from start to finish.

The possibility

  • Reliable support in the area of GMP support and the independent creation of GMP change notifications (changes) to ensure regulatory compliance.
  • Maintenance, processing and adaptation of master data for process engineering plants as well as the implementation of targeted maintenance recordings and reviews.
  • Technical review of technical drawings (R&I flow diagrams) supplemented by structured data preparation and sound data analysis.
  • Active co-creation of initiatives for digitalization and visualization in order to map workflows and plant data transparently and efficiently.
  • Engaged in continuous improvement processes for the sustainable optimization of technical and operational processes.

Who you are

  • You are currently enrolled in a Master’s programme, preferably in the field of science or engineering, or you have completed your studies within 12 months of the start date.
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing round off your profile.

Application process

  • Please upload your CV and a matriculation certificate (if you are currently studying) or your diploma (if you have already completed your studies) with your application.
  • Due to regulations, non-EU/EFTA citizens must, as part of the application documents, present a certificate from the university stating that an internship is mandatory, as well as the duration of the mandatory internship.

Additional information

  • Please note that this position is designed purely as a practical internship. The writing of an academic thesis in parallel is therefore unfortunately not possible.
  • Duration: 12 months
