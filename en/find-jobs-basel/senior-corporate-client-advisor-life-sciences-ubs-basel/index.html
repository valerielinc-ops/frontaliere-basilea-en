Full description

Key responsibilities Are you sociable and appreciate a lively dialogue with innovative entrepreneurs in the life sciences environment? Do you want to support corporate customers from the life sciences industry in their various development phases – from young biotech and medtech companies to established life sciences companies? Then you are in the right place with us. We are looking for a committed personality to support complex corporate clients with a focus on life sciences in Basel.

And these are your core tasks

  • You are responsible for a portfolio of complex corporate customers with a focus on life sciences and develop existing customer relationships.
  • You support companies in different development phases – from young biotech and medtech companies to established life sciences companies – and tailor your advice to their specific needs.
  • You actively represent UBS in the life sciences ecosystem in Basel, expand your network and use relevant events to attract new customers and further establish UBS in the market.
  • You deal intensively with the business models, strategic priorities as well as financial needs, opportunities and risks of your customers and derive suitable solutions.
  • They recognize business opportunities at an early stage and develop them further with a structured and entrepreneurial approach.
  • Together with our specialists, you will develop needs-based solutions, including in the areas of financing, cash management, foreign exchange, trade finance and capital markets.
  • You work closely with colleagues from different areas of UBS and bring the right contacts to the table for your customers.

The team You will become part of our corporate client team in Basel and work in our Life Sciences Sector Team. Basel is one of the most important life sciences locations worldwide. Our aim is to accompany the companies in this ecosystem over different phases of their development and to build long-term, trusting customer relationships. You will work with experienced customer advisors and a broad network of specialists within UBS. At the same time, you will have the opportunity to continuously develop your know-how in corporate banking and the life sciences industry.

Your skills and experience

  • Experience in supporting corporate clients, corporate banking, relationship management or in a comparable customer-oriented function
  • Enjoyment in the acquisition and development of customer relationships
  • great interest in the life sciences industry; existing experience with biotech, pharmaceutical, medtech or healthcare companies is a plus
  • Extensive curiosity and joy in truly understanding business models and the strategic challenges of companies
  • high customer orientation combined with entrepreneurial thinking and a strong sense of business opportunities
  • Convincing and secure interaction with entrepreneurs, CFOs and management
  • good understanding of financing and credit topics as well as the motivation to continuously develop your professional knowledge
  • an autonomous, structured and responsible way of working and a strong risk awareness
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Commercial or higher education, ideally supplemented by further training in banking or finance

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
