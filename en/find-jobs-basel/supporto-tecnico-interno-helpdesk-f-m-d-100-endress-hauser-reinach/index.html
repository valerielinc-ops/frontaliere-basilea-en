Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a technically savvy and service-oriented personality as a product + application specialist / support technician.

What is this point about?

Are you excited about analysing technical issues and finding sustainable solutions together with customers and internal specialists? Does your strength lie in explaining technical connections in a comprehensible way and providing competent support to customers in the application of our products? Do you like working at the interface between customer, service organization and our international product centers?

Then you could be exactly the person we are looking for. In this varied function, you support our customers and internal contacts in technical issues relating to our measurement and automation solutions and make an important contribution to a high level of customer satisfaction.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Advise customers and internal contacts on technical issues related to products, applications, commissioning, faults and complaints.
  • analyse technical problems and develop sustainable solutions together with internal and external bodies.
  • Process, document and track service cases and technical inquiries as part of case management.
  • support our service organization as well as the service technicians in the indoor and outdoor service.
  • contribute to the maintenance and development of knowledge management and share technical know-how within the organisation.
  • work closely with customers, product centers, sales and other internal departments.

What do we expect from you?

See also

  • have completed technical vocational training in the field of electrical engineering, automation, mechatronics, mechanical engineering or a comparable discipline.
  • have ideally completed technical training (HF, TS or comparable).
  • bring several years of professional experience in a technical environment, preferably in measuring, control and control technology or industrial automation.
  • have strong skills for technical problem analysis and solution finding.
  • Convincing thanks to your communication strength, service orientation and structured working method.
  • work independently, responsibly and keep track of complex issues.
  • have very good knowledge of German and English, French is an advantage

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We value work-life balance and offer flexible working hours and the ability to work remotely to help our employees maintain a healthy balance.
  • We place great emphasis on the well-being of our employees by providing them with comprehensive occupational pensions and access to health services that promote the overall well-being of our employees.
  • We support family-friendly concepts, including daycare and holiday childcare, to help our employees reconcile their professional and family commitments.

How do you apply?
