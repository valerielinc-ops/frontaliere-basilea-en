Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a development engineer to join our team at Endress+Hauser Flow and jointly develop innovative flow measurement solutions that make a measurable difference in energy- and resource-intensive industries worldwide. What is this role about? Do you want to steer product development from idea to market readiness? Does it appeal to you not only to plan technology, but also to actively shape it? Does it motivate you if your work is used in industrial applications worldwide? Then this role is just right for you: you combine project management with technical expertise and drive innovations that are noticeable in practice. What tasks will you take on? They shall:

  • Leading product development projects for Coriolis flow sensors – from concept phase to successful implementation
  • Actively develop in projects and continuously deepen your technical expertise
  • Product improvements are responsible for including extensive investigations for error-cause analyses
  • Carry out measurement campaigns, in cooperation with institutes as well as in field tests with customers
  • Define test requirements and ensure experimental reviews
  • Ensure compliance with relevant international standards (compliance, metrological standards, print approval) What do we expect from you? They:
  • have completed studies in engineering (ETH, TU, FH) in mechanical engineering, mechatronics or similar
  • bring at least 2 years of experience in project management and preferably have an appropriate certification
  • communicate fluently in German and well in English
  • Ideally have professional experience in industrial metrology
  • distinguished by a quick comprehension, structured and independent working method, secure judgment and strong social skills
  • have a clear and effective communication ability, enjoy working in a team and are enthusiastic about a wide range of tasks in an environment that offers space for creativity and initiative What can you expect from us?
  • Exciting projects with global impact in a technology-leading company with over 18,000 employees worldwide
  • Attractive social benefits and fair compensation in a stable, family-run company
  • A collaborative, appreciative environment in which cooperation is lived at eye level
  • Individual development and career opportunities, tailored to your strengths and interests
  • We are a family business where friendliness and commitment are important
  • We promote lifelong learning through versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning How do you apply? To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons. Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding. Apply now.
