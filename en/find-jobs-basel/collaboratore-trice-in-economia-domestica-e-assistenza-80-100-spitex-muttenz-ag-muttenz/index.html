Full description

SPITEX MUTTENZ AG – committed, knowledgeable and goal-oriented We provide high-quality care outside hospitals for the approximately 18’000 residents of the municipality of Muttenz. Our team of 70 employees supports people of all ages in living independently through professional advice, care and household services and contributes to their quality of life.

We are looking for a Housekeeping & Care Assistant 80–100 % You have

  • Training as a housekeeping practitioner EBA
  • Training as an SRK nursing assistant
  • Experience and enthusiasm for housekeeping, care and supporting people
  • Independent, reliable and responsible working style
  • Empathy, an appreciative manner and good powers of observation
  • Very good German language skills and enjoyment of working independently in the field

Your tasks

  • Cleaning and upkeep of the household, taking individual needs into account
  • Support with household management, laundry care, shopping and preparing simple meals
  • Taking on care and respite assignments
  • Recognizing and communicating relevant changes and needs

What we offer you

  • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday between 8 and 17
  • Consideration of your personal needs when scheduling working hours
  • Independent and meaningful work directly in people's homes
  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working environment

You will become part of a committed team in which housekeeping, care and nursing work closely together. The focus is not only on a clean household: through your work, you help ensure that our clients can live at home in a self-determined and safe manner, with a good quality of life, for as long as possible. We look forward to receiving your application at personal@spitexmuttenz.ch or here via the Apply button. Our co-management team will be happy to provide information at 061 465 73 33 More information about our organization is available on our homepage www.spitexmuttenz.ch

Profile

Spitex Muttenz AG supports people in living independently and safely in their familiar home. On behalf of the municipality of Muttenz, we provide professional services in the areas of care, support, household services and psychiatric care. Our approximately 70 employees support our clients individually according to their needs and in close cooperation with relatives, family doctors and other specialists. As a locally rooted, non-profit-oriented organization, we combine high quality standards with personal and trusting support. At the same time, we are committed to training future specialists and continuously developing our services. Making a meaningful contribution. Providing human support. Shaping things together.
