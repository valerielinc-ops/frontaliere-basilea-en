Full description

Klinik Arlesheim is a leading centre for integrative medicine. For more than 100 years we have successfully combined conventional medicine with anthroposophical medicine. Medicine is complemented by comprehensive therapies and individual care. Inpatients serve around 90 patients in the fields of oncology, internal medicine and psychiatry. Our offer is complemented by an oncological day clinic and other outpatient offers such as GP medicine and nine specialist outpatient clinics. We are there for our patients around the clock, whether in our 24/7 emergency with walk-in and intermediate care (IMC/monitoring station) or through acute care at home (hospital at home). Connected to our pharmacies (both hospital and public pharmacies), we produce medicines according to GMP-small quantities guidelines in our own therapeutic laboratory. We are looking for a nurse at home 40% immediately or by appointment. With the innovative offer of hospital at home, we enable sick people to receive hospital-level treatment at home and increase employee satisfaction through high self-efficacy.

Their tasks

  • Assumption of treatment care for acutely ill patients: inside at home
  • Co-development and design of the treatment process in the interprofessional team
  • Ensuring the care process
  • Goal- and solution-oriented cooperation in an interprofessional treatment team
  • Implementation and guidance of integrated medical care measures

They bring with them

  • Training for/m dipl. nursing specialist HF (HF, FH, DNII, AKP)
  • Openness for an integrative treatment concept, focus on anthroposophical care and medicine
  • Team-oriented, process-focused working method
  • Interest in modern forms of work organization (New Work)

We offer

  • A collegial working atmosphere with a good and lived culture of encounter in a value-oriented hospital
  • Easily accessible place of work with excellent public transport connection (5 min to the tram or S-Bahn)
  • TNW Job Ticket and Free Use of Pick-e-bike
  • Careful training in a supportive team
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
