Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the GSCM Data & Applications department, we are looking for a dedicated Project Manager Supply Chain Applications – Cold Chain Monitorning & Visibility (CCMV) (a), 80-100 %

Their tasks

  • Management and operational implementation of a global cold chain monitoring and visibility solution for transport conditions and time out of refrigeration
  • Preparation, moderation and follow-up of project workshops, fit-gap/design sessions, training, key-user voting and steering committees
  • Ensuring procedural and regulatory requirements in a GMP/GxP relevant environment. Definition, prioritization and tracking of business and system requirements, milestones, risks and decision-making needs
  • Close cooperation with internal stakeholders from supply chain, materials management, QA, QE, IT, customer service, procurement and the global Bachem locations as well as with the implementation partners
  • Planning and implementation of the global rollout including piloting, change management, communication measures, hypercare, lessons learned and transfer to a sustainable operating model
  • Assumption of tasks within the supply chain application team for further applications and topics, representation in the NiceLabel (label management system)

Your profile

  • Completed studies in business administration, supply chain management, industrial engineering, chemistry or a comparable discipline as well as ideally a further education in supply chain management
  • Several years of professional experience (minimum 5 years) in project management, supply chain management, logistics, quality or IT applications, preferably in a GMP/GxP regulated environment of the pharmaceutical industry
  • Experience in the implementation, validation and further development of digital supply chain, logistics or SAP-based applications
  • In-depth understanding of end-to-end supply chain processes along the entire value chain from goods incoming to production and storage to distribution and transport logistics
  • Strong project management skills
  • Certifications such as IPMA, PMI or PRINCE2 are beneficial
  • Very good analytical, conceptual and communication skills as well as experience in the addressable preparation of complex professional, technical and regulatory content
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as secure application of MS Office, data analysis and reporting tools; ideally complemented by knowledge of SAP (MM/WM), system integration and validation-relevant documentation

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
