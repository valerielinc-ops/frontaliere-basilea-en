The Position Join Roche's Safety, Health, and Environmental (SHE) Department as a Senior SHE Specialist and make a meaningful impact on workplace safety and environmental stewardship. In this pivotal role, you'll identify and evaluate risks to employee safety and the environment while establishing robust controls to mitigate those hazards. Working collaboratively across the R&D department and throughout the business, you'll leverage detailed analysis, expert problem-solving skills, and extensive SHE expertise to protect our employees, our environment, and Roche's right to operate. Through strategic risk identification, comprehensive incident investigation and management, accident prevention, compliance management, and innovative program development, you'll help build and maintain a safe, compliant, and resilient workplace culture.

The Opportunity

As an expert leader with strong business acumen and a proactive, solutions-oriented mindset, you'll demonstrate mastery in executing and evaluating SHE risk assessments—including job hazard assessments, equipment safety evaluations, PPE assessments, and ergonomic risk assessments. Your proficiency in interpreting regulatory and technical documents, combined with your extensive knowledge of industrial environmental management and emergency response procedures, will be instrumental in driving compliance and protecting our workforce. With a background in chemistry, electrochemistry, or microbiology, along with your demonstrated expertise in biosafety, bloodborne pathogens, and chemical hygiene, you'll bring the technical depth needed to excel in our science-driven medical device R&D environment.

  • Provide SHE business partner support for the R&D department of a dynamic medical diagnostic device company
  • Lead or facilitate investigation of SHE incidents to identify root causes and implement corrective and preventative actions to reduce risk of recurrence
  • Develop, review, and revise SHE programs through technical writing to ensure compliance with applicable federal, state, and local regulations
  • Manage action plans to ensure incident follow-up is complete, effective, and timely
  • Prepare, coordinate, and conduct regular SHE-related meetings, inspections, and training sessions
  • Maintain comprehensive SHE documentation, incident databases, injury and illness records, and required permits in accordance with compliance standards
  • Develop metrics, summaries, and communications to provide accurate and timely reporting of SHE-related data and trend analysis
