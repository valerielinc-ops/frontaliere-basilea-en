05.10.2026 Liestal

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Liestal-Oberbaselbiet, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Gelterkinden and Sissach, we are looking for a personality as an assistant: in corporate client consulting.

What do you expect?

  • Support of team customers and handling of basic and general day-to-day business
  • Solution-oriented, physical and telephone advice for corporate clients in the areas of basic products, payment transactions, liquidity and financing
  • Active support of consultants in administrative activities: preparation and opening of new customers, product openings (payments, savings, financing, investments)
  • Accompaniment to customer visits incl. Preparation and post-processing
  • Independent processing and answering of written and telephone customer inquiries
  • Demand recognition and forwarding of customers to the relevant specialist consultants
  • Exercise of representative tasks for corporate client events and market development measures

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • First experience in corporate banking is an advantage
  • Team-capable, flexible and entrepreneurial personality with a strong customer orientation, negotiating skills and a secure appearance
  • Analytical skills and an exact and reliable way of working
  • High willingness to learn in a dynamic working environment
