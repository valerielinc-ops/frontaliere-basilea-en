Finance / Controlling / Audit Workload: 50-100% Location: Level of experience: With work experience FlexOffice: on-site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than just working through tasks. Here you will work with people who take responsibility, have high standards and have the courage to break new ground. This gives you the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • As part of our team, you will ensure that financial information is correctly recorded, processed and reported
  • You will be involved in the preparation of IFRS and OR financial statements, regulatory reporting and management reports
  • You will make a significant contribution to the quality and reliability of our monthly, quarterly and annual financial statements
  • You will support the finance integration and accompany the introduction of a modern group-wide financial platform
  • With your eye for efficiency, you will make suggestions for improvement and drive the automation and further development of our closing processes

What you bring to the table

  • Completed studies (HF, FH, university or ETH) with a focus on accounting, controlling or finance or an equivalent education or training in finance and accounting
  • Experience in accounting as well as in-depth knowledge of closing and reporting processes
  • Experience in the insurance or financial services industry is a plus
  • Handling SAP financial applications, preferably SAP R/3, S/4HANA and BW/4HANA
  • Very good written and spoken German skills as well as good English skills are an advantage
