Full description

You work at Helvetia Consulting AG – Helvetia’s internal consulting team. We have experienced project managers and young minds, rich expertise and fresh ideas at home. Together we support the transformation of our customers, develop future-oriented solutions and create sustainable added value for our customers and our organization.

Tasks

  • You take over the management of projects and transformations within Helvetia Baloise and accompany them from order clearance to successful implementation. You create transparency about progress, risks and dependencies and ensure that the agreed goals are achieved.
  • You connect different departments, IT units and stakeholders, lead project teams through ambitious changes and ensure sustainable decisions and effective cooperation across organizational boundaries.
  • You bring structure to complex projects, control resources, deadlines, costs and risks and create the basis for sound management decisions with user-friendly reporting.
  • You combine proven project management methods with new approaches and support our customers in successfully anchoring sustainable changes in the organization.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in managing projects or transformation projects, ideally in an insurance, financial services or consulting environment.
  • You move confidently between business and IT, think in a networked way and understand how to combine different interests into a common goal.
  • You communicate at eye level with specialists, managers and decision-makers and convince through your structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented approach.
  • You bring a high degree of personal responsibility, implementation strength and customer orientation and enjoy successfully leading demanding challenges to the goal.

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
