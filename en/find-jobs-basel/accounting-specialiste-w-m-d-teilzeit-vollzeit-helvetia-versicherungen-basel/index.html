What you do

What you bring to the table

  • Completed studies (HF, FH, university or ETH) with a focus on accounting, controlling or finance or an equivalent education or training in finance and accounting
  • Experience in accounting as well as in-depth knowledge of closing and reporting processes
  • Experience in the insurance or financial services industry is a plus
  • Handling SAP financial applications, preferably SAP R/3, S/4HANA and BW/4HANA
  • Very good written and spoken German skills as well as good English skills are an advantage
