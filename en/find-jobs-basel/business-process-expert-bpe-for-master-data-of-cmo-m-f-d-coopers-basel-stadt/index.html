Role overview

For our client, we are looking for an experienced Business Process Expert who will act as a key bridge between global process standards and local operational execution, drive process alignment and harmonization at site level, and bring strong expertise in SAP S/4HANA, master data, and end-to-end supply chain or manufacturing processes.
