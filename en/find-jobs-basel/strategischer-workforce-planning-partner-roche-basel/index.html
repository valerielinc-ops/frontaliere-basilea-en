The Position Are you ready to architect the future of work at one of the world's leading healthcare innovators? Roche’s Workforce Optimization (WFO) Area of Practice is a high-impact global team dedicated to revolutionizing how we ensure the future readiness of our most valuable asset: our people. By bringing together strategic People & Culture (P&C) insights with cutting-edge data science, we create a future-ready workforce combining internal talent and external expertise. Here, your work in Strategic Workforce Planning (SWP) isn't just about numbers—it's about ensuring we have the vision and the workforce that is doing now what patients need next . As an SWP/WFO Partner , you are a catalyst for a future-ready workforce. You will collaborate directly with business leaders and cross-functional partners to translate ambitious goals into actionable workforce strategies. This isn't a back-office role; you will be at the forefront, supporting SWP projects and while supporting the enablement and scaling of our frameworks to shape the Roche of tomorrow. If you are passionate about data-driven innovation and eager to lead the charge in navigating a rapidly evolving business landscape, this is your opportunity to make a lasting impact.
