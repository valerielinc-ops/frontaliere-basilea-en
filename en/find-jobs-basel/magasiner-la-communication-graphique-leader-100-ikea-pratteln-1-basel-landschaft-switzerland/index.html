Full description

Together as a team

Where others see furniture, we see clever solutions for a better everyday life. With a lot of passion and imagination, we work together to transform colours, fabrics and furnishings into an environment that inspires, captivates and motivates our customers to take a better life home with them.

Responsibilities

  • You will take a leading role in overseeing all communication with customers in the store.
  • You will apply your knowledge and understanding of people's lives at home and their buying behaviour to improve graphic communication inside and outside the IKEA store and simplify the buying process.
  • Using your graphic design skills and based on the IKEA concept and IKEA's visual identity, you will create and implement graphic solutions that exude clarity, consistency and simplicity and convey clear messages.

Requirements

  • You like to take on responsibility and are an inspiring and motivating leader.
  • You have a sound education in the field of graphic communication design, media and/or typography and have already led a team.
  • You enjoy working in a lively company and continue to educate yourself in order to always be up to date.
  • You are proactive and always actively looking for new and improved methods that keep the way of working simple and cost-effective.
  • You communicate fluently in German and English, both written and spoken.
