Full description

CordenPharma is one of the leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CDMO) and develops and produces pharmaceutical active ingredients, drugs and related packaging services on behalf of its customers as a “full service” service provider. The group employs around 3,500 people. Our network in Europe, Asia and the US offers flexible and specialized solutions across six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules and Oligonucleotides. We strive for excellence in supporting this network and are committed to providing the highest quality products for the benefit of patients. Our People Vision: We strive for excellence. We share our passion. Together, we make a difference in patients’ lives. Senior Expert Analytical Development & Team Leader Analytical Support Process Development, 100% (m/f/d)

Your exciting task area We are looking for a strong leader with strong analytical expertise and a pragmatic hands-on mentality. Subordinated directly to the Head of Development, you will take over the technical and disciplinary leadership of our Analytical Support Process Development Team with up to two employees. As a member of the Development Leadership Team, you are actively involved in the technological and strategic development of the Development department. You will combine scientific depth and strategic thinking with the ability to handle complex analytical questions hands-on if necessary. Your responsibilities with us:

  • Practical support in the laboratory, especially for complex analytical questions
  • Professional and disciplinary leadership as well as targeted further development of the team in development
  • Responsibility for resource planning, prioritization and efficient organization of analytical activities within the Development Department
  • Development and provision of analytical methods for development and catalogue products as well as professional support of method transfers and implementations
  • Support for the analytical characterization of organic substances as well as clarification of impurities in the context of process development and production troubleshooting
  • Ensuring continuous professional training and qualification of employees

Your profile

  • In-depth experience in method development, optimization and application of analytical methods for the characterization of organic substances
  • At least five years of practical professional experience in the chemical or pharmaceutical industry
  • In-depth practical knowledge in several of the following technologies: LC-MS, GC-MS, GC-HS, (U)HPLC, NMR, Titration, NIR, PAT and React-IR
  • Strong understanding of analytical devices, including troubleshooting and investment planning
  • Strategic and entrepreneurial thinking, combined with a pragmatic and solution-oriented working method
  • Willingness and competence to work practically in the laboratory for challenging questions
  • High degree of initiative, sense of responsibility and decision-making ability
  • Strong communication and leadership skills as well as the ability to communicate complex technical issues
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

Attractive and performance-oriented compensation package Dynamic and exciting working environment Appreciative working environment An attractive workplace with a high level of responsibility Corden Pharma offers you interesting and versatile tasks in a highly motivated team. Through practice-oriented training, we help you quickly gain professional security in order to work independently with a high level of personal responsibility.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
