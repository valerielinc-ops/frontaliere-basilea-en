Full description

Admission immediately or by appointment · Liestal, Pharmacy · Job reference 13068 · Permanent employment

Tasks

In order to strengthen our pharmacist team as a result of extended clinical-pharmaceutical tasks, we are looking for a dedicated specialist.

  • Ensuring the supply of medicines and the correct use of medicines
  • Support for the Pharmaceutical Clinical Care (KISIM) team: validation of regulations, advice to the medical profession and nurses
  • Supporting the team in the production of cytostatics (CATO): validation of regulations, release of cytostatics produced
  • On-call time (telephone accessibility)

Requirements

  • Eidg. dipl. Pharmacist or an equivalent foreign degree recognised by MEBEKO with good pharmaceutical expertise and a professional qualification (BAB)
  • Interest in drug management with master data maintenance, assortment adjustments, inventory corrections and handling of supply bottlenecks
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation with other hospital areas
  • Very good knowledge of German and routine handling of the PC (MS Office)
  • Independent, structured, exact and solution-oriented working method
  • Initiative, flexibility and good teamwork
  • First experience in hospital care is beneficial

Benefits

On and off: mind. 5 weeks holidays, loyalty holidays, 11 paid holidays and bridge days
