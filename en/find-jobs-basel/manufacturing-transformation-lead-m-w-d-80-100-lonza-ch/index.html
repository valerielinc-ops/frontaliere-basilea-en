The Manufacturing Transformation Lead (Director level) translates the evolving scientific and process roadmaps of Integrated Biologics, Advanced Synthesis and Specialized Modalities into future manufacturing concepts that support the Advanced Industry unit-cost ambition.

The role works closely with Platforms, R&D and Strategic Innovation to understand expected process evolution and then determines how those processes should be manufactured and operated in the future. This includes the application of advanced technologies, process intensification, PAT, process equipment concepts, automation, reduced manual intervention and increasingly touchless operations.

The role leads the Manufacturing Transformation Lead pilots, starting with Mammalian DS and Bioconjugation DS, using unit-cost diagnostics and future-state design to identify where changes to the manufacturing process, equipment and way of operating can create step-change value.

The Manufacturing Transformation Lead works closely with the Program Integration & Scale-Up Lead to translate pilot and platform requirements into coordinated program increments and to integrate relevant solutions from the other Advanced Industry workstreams back into the future manufacturing concepts.

As a core member of the Advanced Industry LT, this role connects platform innovation with manufacturing transformation, ensuring that scientific progress is translated into scalable manufacturing concepts that improve unit cost, productivity, robustness and long-term competitiveness.

The role is based in Basel, Switzerland.

  • Translate scientific and process evolution into manufacturing concepts, operating requirements and cost implications

  • Challenge how future processes can be manufactured more simply, efficiently and autonomously

Pilot Leadership

Manufacturing Requirements & Integration

  • Translate future manufacturing concepts into requirements for equipment, automation, data, facility and lab capabilities
  • Work with Advanced Manufacturing Technology to identify enabling technologies for automation, touchless operations and process control
  • Ensure solutions from other Advanced Industry workstreams are integrated into coherent manufacturing concepts

Value & Replication

  • Ensure future-state concepts are anchored in measurable unit-cost and productivity improvement
  • Translate pilot learnings into reusable manufacturing concepts and design principles
  • Support replication across all three platforms, sites and future builds
  • Contribute to prioritization of future Manufacturing Transformation Leadpilots and use cases
