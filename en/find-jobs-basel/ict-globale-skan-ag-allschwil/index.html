Full description

Founded in 1968, SKAN is a pioneer in cleanroom equipment and the development of isolators for the pharmaceutical industry. Innovative products, tailor-made solutions and an effective service organization have made SKAN a market leader and a key partner to industry and research laboratories. Overview Shape the technological future of the SKAN Group. SKAN is a pioneer in aseptic and aseptic-toxic manufacturing processes for the (bio)pharmaceutical industry and a global market and technology leader in process-critical isolator systems used to fill medications in compliance with the strictest sterility standards. Our portfolio is complemented by process support, software, services, and single-use systems for pharmaceutical and biotech companies, contract manufacturing organizations (CMOs), and research laboratories worldwide. Founded in 1968, SKAN has grown to approximately 1,700 employees. Around half of our workforce is based at our headquarters in Allschwil, in the Basel Life Sciences Hub, with the remainder working at our subsidiaries in Switzerland, Germany, Belgium, France, Slovenia, Croatia, Japan, the United States, and Brazil. Our international growth, including through acquisitions, presents a unique opportunity: Building on a solid ICT foundation, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the technological future of a globally operating corporate group. To lead this journey, we are looking for an entrepreneurial-minded leader with strong technological expertise, strategic vision, and a passion for inspiring people to embrace change. As Head of Global ICT, you will lead our global ICT organization of approximately 35 employees and transform it from a high-performing service provider into a proactive strategic business partner. Reporting directly to the Group CFO, you will personally present strategic ICT initiatives to the Executive Management Team and take significant ownership of technology, architecture, and investment decisions. You will connect cutting-edge technology with tangible business value – from business applications and secure, future-ready data and ICT platforms to the integration of acquired companies. Working closely with business functions and the Group AI Transformation Lead, you will establish the foundation for impactful digitalization, automation, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence.

Responsibilities

  • Lead Global ICT Strategy: Develop a clear technology and ICT roadmap, set priorities, and deliver transformation initiatives with measurable business value.
  • Drive Business Performance: Work closely with process owners and business functions to understand their needs and translate them into scalable, cost-effective solutions.
  • Modernize Applications and Architecture: Harmonize and further develop SAP, Salesforce, and engineering and CAD/PLM systems; shape enterprise, cloud/hybrid, and integration architectures.
  • Enable Data and AI: Own the group-wide data strategy and advance secure, scalable enterprise AI platforms in collaboration with the Group AI Transformation Lead.
  • Integrate Acquisitions: Incorporate acquired companies into a robust, unified ICT environment while balancing local requirements with global standards.
  • Anticipate Technological Developments: Monitor emerging technologies and evaluate them based on business value, security, cost-effectiveness, and scalability.
  • Ensure Security and Reliability: Safeguard cybersecurity, data privacy, governance, and business continuity across global ICT services.
  • Develop People and the Organization: Empower teams and leaders, actively drive change, and oversee the ICT portfolio, budgets, partners, and service quality.

Hardskills

  • You have several years of proven leadership experience as a Head of IT, CIO, or in a comparable ICT leadership role with international responsibilities. You have a demonstrated track record of translating transformation initiatives into tangible results.
  • You hold a university degree in computer science, business information systems, or a related field and have excellent German and English language skills.
  • You have strong, up-to-date knowledge of enterprise architecture, cloud and hybrid environments, integration, data platforms, and cybersecurity. You do not need to be a hands-on specialist in every area, but you must be able to challenge experts on technical matters and critically assess architectural decisions.
  • You have experience managing complex ERP and CRM environments, ideally including PLM/CAD systems. Data strategy, analytics, AI, and automation are concrete areas in which you are eager to drive progress – not just buzzwords. Experience with M&A integrations is a plus.
  • You understand governance, financial management, and the operation of business-critical systems. Experience in regulated or validated environments (e.g., GMP) is a valuable asset.

Softskills

  • You have an entrepreneurial mindset and confidently communicate business value, risks, investments, and priorities to the Executive Management Team.
  • You are a clear, inspiring, and decisive leader who develops talent and builds strong, trusted relationships across the business.
  • You are solution-oriented and highly effective at turning plans into action.
  • You demonstrate a confident professional presence and excellent communication skills.
  • You have a hands-on mentality and a strong sense of ownership and accountability.

We offer you

  • A key leadership position with a direct reporting line to the Group CFO and the opportunity to represent strategic ICT topics at the Executive Management Team level.
  • The freedom and responsibility to shape the technological, organizational, and cultural development of a global ICT organization.
  • An experienced team of approximately 35 employees, a solid foundation, and a strong partner in the Group AI Transformation Lead.
  • An internationally growing company with meaningful products that support the pharmaceutical industry.
  • A workplace at our headquarters in Allschwil, near Basel, six weeks of paid vacation, and the option to purchase additional vacation days.
