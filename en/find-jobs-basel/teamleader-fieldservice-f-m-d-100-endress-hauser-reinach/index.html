Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a dedicated leader for our service department who will lead our team of service technicians in the Sales Center Switzerland in a professional and human capacity and will work with us to drive the further development of our service business.

What is this point about?

Are you enthusiastic about leading, promoting and accompanying people in their development? Do you like working close to customers and also support your team on site during demanding assignments? Do you enjoy combining operational responsibility with customer orientation and entrepreneurial thinking?

Then you could be exactly the person we are looking for. In this role, you take responsibility for our team of service technicians and help to provide our customers with first-class service throughout the entire lifecycle of their facilities.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • manage, promote and further develop a team of around 13 service technicians professionally and in terms of personnel.
  • Support your employees in complex customer projects, commissioning and technical issues as well as competently advise customers.
  • support sales in the acquisition and sale of services such as commissioning, maintenance and calibration.
  • ensure the planning, coordination and execution of service orders and monitor their quality, adherence to deadlines and efficiency.
  • Create service offers, successfully complete projects and drive the continuous development of our service business.
  • They document and complete projects incl. Handover and final discussion with the customer

What do we expect from you?

  • have technical training with further training in management, management or management.
  • bring several years of experience in the management of employees and in the technical service environment.
  • think entrepreneurially and act customer- and solution-oriented.
  • manage to motivate, promote and successfully accompany employees through change.
  • convince through communication strength, organizational talent and a high level of initiative.
  • have very good knowledge of German and French
  • English skills round off your profile.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities as well as internal knowledge transfer.
  • We offer flexible working hours and support a balanced work-life balance.
  • We offer you a varied environment with a lot of personal responsibility and direct influence on the further development of our service business.
  • We support family-friendly concepts and create framework conditions for reconciling work and private life
