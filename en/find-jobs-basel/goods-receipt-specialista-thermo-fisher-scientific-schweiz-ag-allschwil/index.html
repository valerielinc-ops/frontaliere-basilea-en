Position Summary

The Goods Receipt Specialist in Allschwil is responsible for the timely receipt, booking, and storage of materials. The role records deviations in the TrackWise system and supports investigations. The incumbent ensures compliance with SOPs, GCP, cGMP, and safety requirements and monitors documentation practices within the eDMS.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure compliance with SOPs, GxP guidelines, and internal/external safety regulations
  • Receive goods, post goods receipts in GPMS, and hand over shipments to freight carriers
  • Perform high-bay warehouse picking activities and related WMS transactions
  • Physically store materials and perform GPMS/WMS postings
  • Execute required material movements, including transfers to external storage locations
  • Operate machinery and equipment used for material handling
  • Conduct inventory counts
  • Clean facilities, machinery, and equipment according to instructions
  • Support other departments as directed by supervisors

  • Ability to meet deadlines and prioritize tasks
  • Strong teamwork skills and resilience under stress
  • Reliable, accurate, and efficient working style
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications (Excel, Word, Outlook)

Preferred Qualifications

  • Forklift license
  • Class B driving license
  • English language skills, written and spoken
