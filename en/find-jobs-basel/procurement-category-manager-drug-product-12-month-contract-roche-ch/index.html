Main responsibilities

  • In this role, you will have the opportunity to drive strategic impact and manage end-to-end category performance through the following key responsibilities:
  • Define the DP & Finished Good Strategy and annual initiative pipeline by collating market, supplier, category, and internal data, while partnering with functions such as local and regional Procurement to gather input, endorsement, and validation.
  • Identify, manage, and execute strategic category initiatives to achieve top-down targets, executing projects in compliance with policies, guidelines, operating procedures, and 3rd Party Spend Policy directives while escalating issues and utilizing continuous improvement approaches like LPS and DPP’s portfolio planning (Cost Optimization Initiative).
  • Develop and maintain clear, executable category buying guides and content to translate category strategies and initiative outcomes into practical guidance for Procurement colleagues and business users.
  • Deliver Procurement Balanced Scorecard (BSC) inputs, manage accurate reporting of measures, metrics, and savings targets (liaising with operational sourcing hubs, Shared Service Centers, and the Business), and ensure spend data accuracy through data validation and governance remediation.
  • Lead supplier relationship management (SRM) and continuous improvement by identifying key suppliers, establishing and reporting value creation targets, mitigating risks, conducting regular business reviews, leading supplier innovation/development, and conducting thorough risk and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) reviews.
  • Build and maintain strong business partner relationships across Procurement, Finance, external manufacturing operation and quality, demand planning, global supply chain owners, Technical Product Leaders, and Legal to challenge demand, ensure contract compliance, and offer Procurement solutions for business priorities.
  • Support process and technology harmonization across global category teams, coach Procurement staff and project-related resources to foster professional development, and represent the Global Category Leader (GCL) in planning, stakeholder, people, and project management activities as needed.
