Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position PSD Operations supports Product Development’s (PD) mission to improve patients’ lives around the world through medicines that make a meaningful difference. As PD’s centralised administrative organisation, we operate as a prime service organisation that designs experiences for leaders and teams. This means anticipating what stakeholders need, bringing the right people together, and making it easier for teams to deliver on their priorities. As an Operations Partner , your primary responsibility will be to provide executive-level administrative support to a Senior Vice President who has recently joined PD. Your contribution will directly enable this leader and their team to operate effectively and execute on PD’s priorities. Alongside this core assignment, you will collaborate with other Operations Partners on shared initiatives and contribute your expertise across the wider organisation. Through our flow-to-work model, your responsibilities will evolve alongside business needs—giving you the opportunity to support diverse leaders, step beyond your comfort zone, broaden your capabilities, and make a lasting impact.

The Opportunity

In this role, you will

  • Provide comprehensive, executive-level administrative and operational support to an SVP and their leadership team.
  • Partner with business leaders as a proactive, trusted advisor to streamline daily operations, solve problems, and remove friction.
  • Orchestrate flawless meeting coordination, simplify workflows via internal systems, and support seamless recruitment and onboarding experiences.
  • Flow to high-value, prioritized work and programs across PD as business needs change, actively collaborating on cross-functional initiatives.
  • Drive operational excellence by continuously improving processes, automating tasks, and championing digital tools and AI.
  • Actively engage in our collaborative community, sharing best practices, taking ownership of wider team deliverables, and fostering an inclusive culture.

Who You Are

We are looking for candidates who demonstrate established or senior-level capabilities across administrative and operational excellence:

  • Education : You have an administrative qualification, a Bachelor’s degree, or equivalent, combined with proven administrative experience.
  • Professional Experience: Minimum of 5 years in an administrative/operational setting (excluding internships and any study-related work experience), with proven success supporting senior leaders (experience guiding or leading teams is an asset).
  • Enterprise Mindset & Functional Knowledge: Broad to in-depth understanding of functional and cross-functional processes, procedures, and enterprise systems, with the capability to navigate complex environments.
  • Communication & Storytelling: Strong active listener and transparent communicator who adapts smoothly to diverse cultures; skilled at conveying clear messages, utilizing confident presentation skills, and demonstrating high emotional intelligence.
  • Collaboration & Relationship Building: Exceptional interpersonal skills with a proven track record of creating psychological safety, resolving conflicts constructively, and building authentic, cross-functional networks.
  • Inspiring & Influencing Without Authority: A collaborative partner who steps forward with ideas, solves problems proactively, and can align and inspire teams toward shared goals.
  • Strategic Agility & Growth Mindset: A curious, continuous learner who welcomes feedback, proactively spots challenges and opportunities, and maintains a clear, customer-centric view of broader organisational goals.
  • Technical & Digital Fluency: Confident in leveraging operational systems, tools, and emerging AI/automation solutions to simplify workflows, build team capabilities, and drive efficiency.

Please note that direct experience supporting senior leaders as an Executive Assistant is a mandatory prerequisite for this role; applications that do not clearly reflect this background cannot be taken into consideration. Experience within a global pharmaceutical organization is an added advantage.
