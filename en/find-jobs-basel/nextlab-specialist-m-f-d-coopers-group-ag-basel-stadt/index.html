Full description

For a company from the pharmaceutical industry, we are looking for an experienced personality to support an ongoing digitization project in the field of Laboratory Systems. The focus is on the further development and local implementation of a globally used Lab Execution System (LES) based on the BIOVIA software. The aim is to efficiently map existing laboratory processes and workflows in the system, to optimize interfaces and to identify cross-site synergies.

In close cooperation with various internal specialist departments, you will support the continuation of the implementation and help to ensure sustainable digitalization and compliance in the laboratory environment.

Hard facts

  • Start date: 01.01.2027 (latest possible start: 01.03.2027)
  • Duration of use: 12 months, extension possible
  • Pensum: 100 %
  • Place of work: Basel
  • Home office: Predominant local presence required; punctual home office possible after familiarization and consultation
  • Travel activity: None
  • Working environment: Pharmaceutical industry, GxP regulated environment

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Recording and documentation of the current implementation status of the Lab Execution System (LES).
  • Analysis and comparison of previously implemented recipes and workflows with the procedures of other departments.
  • Identification of optimization potentials, suggestions for improvement and possible synergies between existing laboratory processes.
  • Support for the further implementation and local adaptation of the LES.
  • Conceptual and professional support in the implementation of the interface between LIMS and Nextlab.
  • Analysis and documentation of existing business processes, workflows and data flows.
  • Close cooperation with internal specialist departments, IT, engineering and quality assurance.
  • Ensure compliance with relevant GxP and compliance requirements as part of system implementation.

Qualifications

  • Completed vocational training or studies in the technical, pharmaceutical or scientific field, for example as a chemical or pharmatechnologist, pharmacist, chemist or automatician, each with a pronounced understanding of IT. Alternatively, a completed computer science training or comparable qualification.
  • At least 3–5 years of professional experience in the laboratory environment.
  • In-depth experience in the acquisition, analysis and documentation of business processes and data flows.
  • Experience in the GxP and non-GxP environment, ideally within the pharmaceutical industry.
  • Knowledge of laboratory information systems (LIMS) and lab execution systems (LES).
  • Ideally practical experience with Nextlab and singleLIMS.
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

Personal competences (advantageous)

  • Strong personality, self-confidently representing positions, openly addressing critical issues and constructively questioning different points of view.
  • Independent, initiative and solution-oriented working method with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • Strong social skills, flexibility and integrity.
  • Strong customer focus and fact-based and consistent decision-making.
