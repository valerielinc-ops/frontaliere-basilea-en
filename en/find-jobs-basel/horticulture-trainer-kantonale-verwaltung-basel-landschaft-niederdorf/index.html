Your mission

  • Training and individual support of instructed and external learners in the field of horticulture and landscaping at EFZ and EBA level
  • Planning, implementation and assessment of practical training
  • Cooperation in the planning and execution of horticultural and maintenance works
  • Reference work and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Training documentation, reporting and contract management

They bring with them

  • Completed vocational training as a gardener, preferably specialising in gardening and landscaping, as well as practical professional experience
  • Vocational trainer qualification or willingness to acquire it
  • Agogic training of advantage or willingness to develop in this field
  • Enjoying the education and support of young adults in a forced context
  • Independent, team capable, communicative and reflective personality with an appreciative and clear attitude
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good MS-Office skills
  • You are at least 26 years old, hold a category B driving licence and have an impeccable reputation
