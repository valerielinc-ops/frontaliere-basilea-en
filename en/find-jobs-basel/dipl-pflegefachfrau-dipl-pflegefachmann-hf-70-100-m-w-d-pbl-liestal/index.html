Together.

For what really counts Ambulatorium Reinach Center for Dependency Diseases Ambulatorium Reinach (SAE) offers a versatile and dynamic working environment. In a professional and appreciative environment, you accompany patients with dependency diseases as well as psychiatric and somatic concomitant diseases. The focus is on opioid agonist therapy (OAT), heroin-assisted treatment (HeGeBe) and other addictive medicine and psychosocial services.

Their tasks

  • Basic medical care with blood draws, ECGs, urine controls and wound care
  • Reference work and individual nursing care
  • Participation in individual treatment planning and follow-up
  • Accompaniment of drug-assisted treatment within the framework of OAT and HeGeBe
  • Promoting self-employment and supporting social and health stabilisation
  • Participation in therapeutic group services and harm reduction measures (Harm Reduction)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with medicine, psychology, social service and other professional groups
  • Nursing documentation and treatment-related organizational tasks

Your profile

  • Training as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH or equivalent degree recognised in Switzerland (SRC recognition)
  • Experience in psychiatric care, ideally in the addiction medical or outpatient field, beneficial
  • Interested in working with people with addiction diseases, psychiatric and somatic concomitant diseases and dual diagnoses
  • Enjoy independent work and interprofessional cooperation
  • Appreciative, resource-oriented and non-stigmatizing attitude

About us

The Psychiatry Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, daily clinical, home treatment and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. She supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of living, work and day-to-day planning (www.inclusioplus.ch). She also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Center for Work and Mental Health. At several locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, PBL employs around 1,300 people and treats, supports and advises more than 15,000 patients per year (www.pbl.ch).
