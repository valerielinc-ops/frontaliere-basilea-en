Cost of living vs Ticino

The median gross salary in Basel is about 25% higher than in Ticino (BFS LSE 2024 regional wage-level data). The median LAMal health-insurance premium is also lower: CHF 660/month vs CHF 677/month in Ticino (3% lower, BAG/UFSP data) — a real extra cost to factor into your net income.
