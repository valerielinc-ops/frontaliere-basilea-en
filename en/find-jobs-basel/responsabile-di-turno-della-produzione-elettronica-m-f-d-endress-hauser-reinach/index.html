Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

What is the role about?

In 2-shift operation, you lead a team of around ten employees and are responsible for efficient service planning and compliance with high quality and safety standards. In addition, you will promote team development, solve technical problems and optimize processes and plants with TPM, 5S and Kaizen.

What tasks will you take on?

  • In 2-shift operation, they lead a team of around ten employees, promote their professional and personal development, lead employee exchanges and actively participate in recruitment.
  • You are responsible for the operational planning and coordination of your shift, control the optimal use of personnel and resources and ensure a smooth production process.
  • They ensure compliance with high production, quality etc and safety standards and sustainably increase plant availability through the targeted use of TPM methods.
  • They systematically analyze technical problems, coordinate their efficient correction and drive the continuous improvement of processes and plants with 5S, Kaizen and KVP.
  • You promote knowledge transfer through targeted training of your employees and ensure successful cooperation as a central interface to internal specialist positions and experts.

What do we expect from you?

  • They have completed basic technical training, for example as a polymechanic, a production mechanic, a mechatronics technician and, ideally, a further training in the field of leadership.
  • They bring several years of professional and leadership experience in an automated, industrial production environment and understand how to lead and develop employees in a target-oriented manner.
  • They communicate very securely in German in word and writing, master the common MS Office applications and ideally have SAP knowledge.
  • They are convincing through excellent leadership skills, communication skills and team orientation as well as a high level of service and customer awareness and the willingness to continuously develop.
  • They work in a structured, analytical and solution-oriented manner, have excellent organizational talent and bring with them the necessary flexibility for 2-shift operation.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours - Even with changing shift times, you benefit from our flextime account.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.

How do you apply?
