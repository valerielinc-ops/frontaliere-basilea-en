Full description

To support the Legal and Compliance department at the Valora Group's headquarters in Muttenz, we are seeking a committed, competent and dynamic person for the role of Compliance Officer & Legal Counsel (m/f/d), starting January 1, 2027, or by agreement.

Tasks you will enjoy

  • You support our domestic and international group companies with legal, compliance-related, regulatory and governance matters.
  • You conduct legal and regulatory research and prepare facts, risk analyses, decision-making materials and initial proposed solutions in a structured manner.
  • You review, draft and revise contracts, policies, group directives, templates and other Legal and Compliance documents.
  • You contribute to contract negotiations, projects, compliance risk analyses, audits, internal investigations and fact-finding.
  • You support innovative projects of the Valora Group from legal and compliance perspectives
  • You support the confidential and transparent handling and documentation of reports submitted through the Valora Integrity Line.
  • You take on tasks in the areas of corporate housekeeping and corporate governance, for example relating to resolutions, powers of attorney, commercial register matters and the maintenance of corporate legal documentation.
  • You contribute to trademark matters.
  • You create and update training materials, knowledge repositories and internal communications materials, and support training sessions, workshops and awareness initiatives.
  • You work with internal departments, group companies and external advisers, and ensure complete, consistent and transparent documentation of the matters handled.

Requirements you bring

  • A completed degree in law or a comparable university degree with a focus on law, compliance or corporate governance. Admission to the bar and/or other legal qualifications are an advantage.
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in a legal or compliance department of an internationally active company and/or at an internationally active commercial law firm, ideally including experience in compliance, audit or risk management
  • Good knowledge of general civil law (e.g. employment law, distribution law, corporate law, contract law) and public law (e.g. competition law, administrative law), as well as criminal law
  • An interest in compliance management, corporate governance, regulatory requirements and economic contexts
  • Enjoyment of carefully analysing complex matters and presenting them clearly
  • A structured, reliable, proactive and independent approach to work
  • Strong assertiveness, resilience, negotiation skills and an enjoyment of team-oriented work
  • High integrity, trustworthiness and discretion when handling sensitive information
  • Team spirit, a service-oriented approach and audience-appropriate communication
  • Very good spoken and written German and English
  • French or other languages are an advantage
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office

Benefits we offer you

  • A dynamic, international working environment with plenty of room for ideas and creative input
  • A wide range of development and further training opportunities, including through our internal Valora Academy and live training sessions
  • A straightforward corporate culture with direct communication and an established first-name culture that promotes innovation and agility.
  • Exciting projects and varied tasks with considerable scope to shape things and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Enjoy attractive employee discounts on Valora formats and with selected partners in the areas of leisure, sport, health and mobility
  • Attractive employment conditions with our own pension fund and three flexibly selectable savings plans
  • Flexible working hours with flexitime and the option to work from home
