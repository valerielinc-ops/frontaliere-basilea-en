Full description

To support the legal and compliance department at the headquarters of the Valora Group in Muttenz, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a dedicated, competent and dynamic person in the function as Compliance Officer & Legal Counsel (m/f/d) tasks that you will like

  • You will support our domestic and foreign group companies in legal, compliance-relevant, regulatory and governance-related issues.
  • You will carry out legal and regulatory research and provide a structured list of issues, risk analyses, decision bases and initial solutions.
  • You review, create and revise contracts, policies, corporate instructions, templates and other legal and compliance documents.
  • You participate in contract negotiations, projects, compliance risk analyses, audits, internal investigations and factual clarifications.
  • You accompany innovative projects of the Valora Group under legal and compliance-relevant aspects
  • You support the confidential and traceable processing and documentation of messages from the Valora Integrity Line.
  • You take on tasks in the area of corporate housekeeping and corporate governance, for example in resolutions, powers of attorney, business register matters and the maintenance of company law documentation.
  • You are involved in trademark law matters.
  • You will create and update training materials, knowledge collections and internal communication materials and support training, workshops and awareness measures.
  • You work with internal departments, group companies and external consultants and ensure a complete, consistent and comprehensible documentation of the processes processed. Requirements that you bring
  • Completed law studies or a comparable university degree with a focus on law, compliance or corporate governance. Lawyer's patent and/or other legal training are an advantage.
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in a legal department or compliance department of an internationally operating company and/or in an internationally operating commercial law firm, ideally in the areas of compliance, audit or risk management
  • Good knowledge of general civil law (e.g. employment law, distribution law, company, contract law) and public law (e.g. competition law, administrative law) as well as criminal law
  • Interest in compliance management, corporate governance, regulatory requirements and economic contexts
  • Enjoy carefully analysing complex issues and comprehensible preparation
  • A structured, reliable and proactive, independent working method
  • Strong assertiveness, resilience, negotiating skills and enjoyment of team-oriented work
  • High integrity, trustworthiness and discretion in handling sensitive information
  • Team spirit, service orientation and addressee-oriented communication
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • French or other languages are beneficial
  • Secure handling of Microsoft Office benefits we offer you
  • A dynamic, international working environment with plenty of room for ideas and creative participation
  • Various development and further education opportunities, including through our internal Valora Academy and live training courses
  • An uncomplicated corporate culture with direct exchange and a lively du-culture that promotes innovation and agility.
  • Exciting projects and varied tasks with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Enjoy attractive employee discounts at Valora formats and selected partners in the fields of leisure, sports, health and mobility
  • Attractive employment conditions with own pension fund and three flexibly selectable savings plans
  • Flexible working hours with flexibility and the possibility to work in the home office
