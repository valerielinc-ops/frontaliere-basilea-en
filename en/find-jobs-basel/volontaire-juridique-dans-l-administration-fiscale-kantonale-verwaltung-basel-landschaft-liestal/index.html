Tax administration

The Basel-Landschaft Tax Administration is responsible for the correct collection of taxes from legal and natural persons. Your employees check tax returns, carry out the assessments and ensure the collection of state and direct federal tax. In addition, the municipality taxes are paid on behalf of the company. Other tasks include the assessment and collection of property profit taxes, change of hands taxes and inheritance and gift taxes, as well as the processing of withholding taxes. In addition, the tax administration ensures a uniform assessment practice in the canton by providing technical support to the municipal tax offices.
