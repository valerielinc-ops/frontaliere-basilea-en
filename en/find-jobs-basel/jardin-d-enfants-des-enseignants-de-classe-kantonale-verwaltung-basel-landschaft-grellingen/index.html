Primary level Grellingen

Primary level Grellingen is a small school with 2 kindergartens and 6 primary classes. We offer a responsible, versatile and varied activity in a lively environment. The Grellinger Schule is characterized by a committed and well-functioning team. Our school development topics are: cross-level reading promotion, socio-emotional learning and transitions.
