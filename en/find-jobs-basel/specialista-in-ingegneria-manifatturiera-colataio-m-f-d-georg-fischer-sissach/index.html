Full description

Do you love technical challenges and find fast, sustainable solutions when it comes down to it? In this versatile function, you play a key role in our production. They ensure that plants, machines and processes function optimally at all times and actively support production in technical issues and production disruptions. With your expertise in CNC machining, your hands-on mentality and your eye for optimization possibilities It contributes significantly to the productivity, quality and continuous development of our casting mill.

Specialist in manufacturing engineering casting mill (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Ensuring trouble-free production operations through rapid analysis and correction of plant and process faults
  • Technical support of production and advice and support of the head of department
  • Design of clamping and test equipment as well as creation and adaptation of CNC and robot programs
  • Preparation and maintenance of production-relevant documentation, working documents
  • Coordination of repairs, procurement of tools, spare parts and operating aids as well as cooperation with internal and external partners
  • Cooperation in projects, safety tasks of SiAs as well as the continuous optimization of products, processes and production processes

Your profile

  • Completed basic mechanical training
  • Further training as a technician HF/TS or comparable qualification is advantageous
  • Several years of professional experience in CNC machining (turning and milling with ISO code)
  • Good knowledge of CAD design, ideally with CATIA
  • Experience in dealing with PPS, PLM and ERP systems, preferably SAP
  • Knowledge of robotics and automation is advantageous
  • Experience in project work and enjoyment of learning new topics and technologies
  • Practical experience with lean methods such as 5S, SMED or OEE
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Team player with a high level of initiative and a sense of responsibility
