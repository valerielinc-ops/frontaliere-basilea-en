Office for Military and Civil Protection

The Office for Military and Civil Protection (AMB) is an operational, training and administrative organisation. The AMB is an office with around 50 employees within the Security Directorate of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. The AMB is responsible for the missions of the Einsatzverband Bevölkerungsschutz, the training in civil protection as well as the crew and cadre in civil protection. Administrative activities include, among other things, military tasks on behalf of the Federation or the operation of the Liestal Barracks. Furthermore, the AMB prepares operational and precautionary planning as well as logistics and administration services.

What else you should know

The field of operation and training logistics at the Office for Military and Civil Protection is versatile and wide-ranging. On the one hand, the 5-person department is responsible for the logistical matters in the office. This includes the warehouse management of army and civil protection materials, as well as the provision of logistical resources in case of deployment. On the other hand, maintenance work of materials, the spatial management of training facilities, as well as the administrative support of civil protection courses are part of the field of logistics in the AMB. This makes the work in the logistics department exciting, demanding and versatile. The mission and training logistics work at two locations. One location is located in the Liestal barracks, where the Liestal sub-camp manages the army equipment there. The second location is located at Oristalstrasse 100 in the Office for Military and Civil Protection. There, the civil protection material is managed by the Canton of Basel-Landschaft.
