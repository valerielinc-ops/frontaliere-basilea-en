Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Product Manager Level + Pressure (m/f/d) 100% to complement our marketing team in Reinach. In this role, you are responsible for the strategic and operational market development of your product portfolio and combine technical understanding with market, customer and sales orientation.

What is at stake here?

Are you enthusiastic about innovative measurement technology and would you like to actively shape markets? Do you recognize customer needs at an early stage and like to translate them into successful marketing and sales strategies? As a product manager for level and pressure measurement, you take care of your portfolio over the entire life cycle. They observe market changes, identify future requirements and support our sales and marketing colleagues in positioning our solutions convincingly with customers.

What tasks will you take on?

They

• are responsible for strategic and operational marketing activities in the field of level and pressure measurement • Identify and evaluate changing market and customer requirements as well as their impact on our product portfolio • are regularly present with our customers and internal contacts throughout Switzerland

  • The travel activity is about 30%

• develop marketing and sales strategies and implement them together with the relevant internal interfaces • observe the market, carry out market research and derive future requirements and potential from this • Maintain your product portfolio throughout the entire life cycle - from market launch to phase-out • support sales and marketing colleagues at seminars, lectures and customer visits

What do we expect from you?

  • have a degree from a technical college or university in the technical field, preferably in electrical engineering or information technology, or a comparable qualification
  • bring several years of professional experience as well as experience in direct dealing with customers
  • work independently, structured and consistently customer-oriented
  • are an open-minded and team-capable personality and convince through a communication-strong, secure appearance
  • are characterized by strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • speak fluent German and have very good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We attach great importance to your work-life balance – with flexible working hours and the possibility of home office.
  • We offer a modern working environment with amenities such as an in-house canteen.
  • We support you with family-friendly offers such as childcare during the holidays and day care places.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.
