The opportunity

As a laboratory assistant in in-process control at Basel DS, you contribute to providing patients with high-quality products. Their tasks include:

  • Performing GMP-compliant analyses in accordance with specific analysis regulations in the context of routine production, method and process validations and process transfers in cooperation with the market production of large molecules by
  • Creation and verification of GMP documents (analyses of raw data, qualification documents, test regulations, etc.) as well as approval of analysis results
  • Cooperation in the qualification and maintenance of equipment
  • Maintaining the quality system at Basel DS Manufacturing
  • Initiation of necessary measures in case of quality defects n
  • Readiness for piquetting in marginal times, weekends and public holidays

Who you are

  • You have successfully completed training as a laboratory assistant: in EFZ with experience in analytical field or comparable
  • You are familiar with the relevant regulations in the areas of occupational safety, environmental protection and quality assurance
  • They have an exceptional degree of independence, sense of responsibility and resilience
  • Good communication skills (German and English) and pleasure in working with other departments
  • Working in a GMP environment is familiar to you and gives you pleasure
  • Safe, orderly and clean working methods are important to them
  • Knowledge of laboratory systems (e.g. Empower, OpenLab, etc.) are advantageous
  • You are confident in dealing with the current computer systems, master MS-Office and have no reservations about using digital applications
  • You enjoy working together in a team, are motivated and approach your tasks with commitment and conscientiousness.
