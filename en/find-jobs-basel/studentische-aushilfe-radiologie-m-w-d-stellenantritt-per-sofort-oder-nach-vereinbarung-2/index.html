Full description

Appreciation. Security. Trust. Our modern radiology department covers the entire spectrum of imaging diagnostics. These include:

  • Conventional X-ray
  • Ultrasound
  • Computed tomography (CT)
  • Magnetic resonance imaging (MR)
  • Mammography
  • Interventional radiology and screening diagnostics

Our team consists of around 30 radiology specialists (RFP)/students as well as 2 medical practice assistants (MPA).

Their tasks

Support as a student assistant You join our team especially during marginal times, weekends and public holidays. Its tasks include, inter alia:

  • Assistance in patient transport between emergency department and radiology
  • Care and support of patients
  • Assistance in rearrangement and preparation for examinations
  • Control and verification of questionnaires and documents
  • Administrative activities and documentation
  • Re-establishing and filling materials
  • General support of the radiological team in day-to-day and emergency operations For medical students there is an additional possibility – depending on the level of training and competence – to carry out venous punctures and lay venous accesses under appropriate guidance.

Operational periods

The missions are flexible according to agreement

Weekends and public holidays

  • Assignments from about 12:00 to about 20:00 The exact assignment planning takes place individually and in mutual agreement.
