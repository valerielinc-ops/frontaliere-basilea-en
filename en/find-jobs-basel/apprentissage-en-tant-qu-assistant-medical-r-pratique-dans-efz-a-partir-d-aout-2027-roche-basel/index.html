Top Arguments for the Job

  • You support and advise patients in medical practices and other outpatient institutions.
  • You assist the doctor in surgical procedures such as punctures in abscesses or when sewing the wound and special examinations such as ergospirometry
  • You are responsible for the coordination and organization of various processes during the office hours
  • You create ECGs (electrocardiograms), lung function tests and hearing tests
  • You examine blood and urine by differentiating blood cells and evaluating the quality and plausibility of the test results
  • You work with microscopes, chemicals and equipment
  • You get to know the professional technology with the X-ray apparatus and learn how to deal with radiation protection
  • Administrative activities such as managing billing, handling payments, managing patient data, ordering medicines and consumables
  • In addition, you will get to know the field of occupational and emergency medicine at Roche in the medical service.
