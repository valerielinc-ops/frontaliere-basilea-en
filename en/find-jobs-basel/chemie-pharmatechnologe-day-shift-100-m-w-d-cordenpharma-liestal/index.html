Full description

CordenPharma is one of the leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CDMO) and develops and produces pharmaceutical active ingredients, drugs and related packaging services on behalf of its customers as a “full service” service provider. The group employs around 3,500 people. Our network in Europe, Asia and the US offers flexible and specialized solutions across six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules and Oligonucleotides. We strive for excellence in supporting this network and are committed to providing the highest quality products for the benefit of patients. Our People Vision: We strive for excellence. Do you already have experience in preparative HPLC in purification, nanofiltration or synthesis approaches of 1 - 250L? Then you are the right person with us. They are familiar with pharmaceutical production at multifunctional plants of various sizes (laboratory scale up to 250 L reactor size). In addition, you do not mind supporting the 2-shift team sporadically in case of real bottlenecks (Mo to early / late).

  • They do all the preparatory work necessary for the manufacturing process. This includes the provision or dispensing of starting materials, as well as the rinsing and control of apparatuses and plants.
  • They control and monitor the equipment and equipment necessary for the process of production of chemical/pharmaceutical products.
  • They monitor, observe and log the entire manufacturing process.
  • They carry out cleaning, maintenance and repair work of equipment, apparatus and equipment.
  • If necessary, the project teams support you in the preparation of reports or your supervisor in training on facilities, infrastructure projects and the evaluation of new devices.
