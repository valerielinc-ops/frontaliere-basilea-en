Full description

Tasks

  • Supporting and ensuring the operation of the technical infrastructure in the courtrooms
  • Preparation and installation of hall equipment according to the requirements of the respective negotiations
  • Professional and personnel management of Weibeldienst as well as promotion and further development of employees
  • Operation planning and ensuring a smooth operational process as well as cooperation in the daily tasks of the Weibel team
  • Support of judges and court clerks in the operation of audio, video, conference and presentation technology
  • Training of judges, court clerks and colleagues regarding the operation of hall equipment
  • Analysis and resolution of simple technical failures as well as coordination with central computer science for more complex problems
  • Payment and debt collection services

Requirements

  • Completed commercial or equivalent training with some years of professional experience and preferably first management experience
  • Resilient, responsible and assertive personality with high social and communication skills
  • Technical understanding and pleasure in dealing with modern media and conference technology
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Pronounced service orientation and secure contact with judges and parties to proceedings
  • Willingness to quickly familiarize yourself with new technical systems and apply them reliably even under time pressure
  • Numerical flair and good knowledge of German in word and writing

The courts of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft are responsible for the jurisdiction of the canton. At the level of the first instance, they consist of two civil district courts, the criminal court, juvenile court and coercive measures court, as well as the tax and expropriation court. The second instance is the cantonal court with four departments. The court administration is attached to the cantonal court.
