Full description

Providing numbers is good. Steering the future is better. You do not think in rigid tables, but in intelligent data streams and smart architectures? As part of the BI, Analytics & Digitalization team, you will become an architect of our decision-making foundation. Here, you do not simply build models—you create the foundation on which management and specialist departments make far-reaching decisions every day for FP&A, Supply Chain and sustainability (ESG). Make data speak and take our Jedox platform to the next level!

We can only consider applicants who are authorized to work in Switzerland (EU/EFTA). We consider direct applications only.

  • Contract type: Permanent
  • Workload: 100%
  • Working time model: Hybrid working arrangement by agreement
  • Start date: Immediately
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Elsässerstrasse 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • Architecture & Modelling: You design, implement and refine high-performance Jedox models (cubes, dimensions, business rules, subsets) and make complex planning logic intuitive to use.
  • Smart Data Integration: Using Jedox Integrator and lean Groovy scripts, you develop reliable ETL pipelines and ensure that source systems integrate seamlessly.
  • Platform responsibility: You keep the system fast, stable and secure—from permissions management and deployments to fine-tuning query performance and version upgrades.
  • Business Partnering on an equal footing: You translate operational controlling questions into smart forecast and rolling-forecast solutions and build interactive dashboards that deliver genuine aha moments.
  • Knowledge Transfer & Enablement: As a sought-after sparring partner, you empower key users to work with the platform, lead practical training sessions and make best practices visible across the team.

Requirements

  • Your foundation: A degree in business information systems, business administration with a strong IT focus or equivalent training—with a passion for working at the intersection of technology and business.
  • Jedox expertise: At least 1–2 years of solid practical experience in Jedox modelling, rule logic and ETL pipeline creation.
  • Code & Data Acumen: You have a sound understanding of relational and multidimensional data, are highly comfortable working in Excel/Power Query and have basic knowledge of Groovy, Java or similar scripting languages.
  • Controlling knowledge: Terms such as rolling forecast, contribution margin accounting or P&L are not foreign concepts to you, but exciting use cases.
  • Hands-on mentality: Curious, solution-oriented and communicative—you enjoy tackling things independently and actively simplifying processes.
  • Languages: Fluent German; good knowledge of English and/or French is a real plus.

What we offer

  • Work-life balance: A good work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 vacation days and transparent electronic time tracking. This allows you to flexibly compensate for any overtime with additional days off.
  • Food: We place great importance on culinary offerings: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu including soup and salad, water & tea, as well as free fruit and high-quality products at heavily reduced prices, await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient public transport connections, attractive ticket subsidies and sufficient parking spaces in the parking garage.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive continuing education opportunities, high-quality training at the Coop Education Center and diverse personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka-Checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness memberships – for health and leisure fun!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our department event and strengthen team spirit at the company run – celebrate, get active and make contacts together!
  • Market-rate salaries and benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement provision, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive bonuses for employee referrals. In addition, you will receive Coop's employee discount card and we will cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Arrange your working hours flexibly with part-time models and experience an inclusive working environment in which diversity is valued and promoted!
