The Position Who We Are At Roche, we believe every patient deserves a personalized care journey. Roche Information Solutions (RIS) is at the forefront of digital health, developing cutting-edge software solutions that transform patient care by turning complex data into actionable medical insights. We are looking for a visionary Clinician Centered Disease Management Lifecycle Leader to join our team. In this role, you will champion a critical portfolio of clinician-facing tools that empower healthcare providers to deliver precision medicine and make life-saving decisions every day.

The Position

As the Lifecycle Leader, you will hold end-to-end commercial accountability and full P&L ownership for our clinician-facing digital product portfolio. You will bridge the gap between long-term strategic vision and daily execution, shaping the product roadmap and leading a high-performing team of Product Managers to deliver meaningful outcomes for healthcare systems and patients worldwide. Your portfolio includes flagship solutions such as navify Clinical Hub (nCH) , navify Mutation Profiler (nMP) , and navify Cervical Cancer Screening (nCCS) . You will act as a key collaborator across the organization, partnering closely with RIS Go-to-Market (GTM), Foundation Medicine (FMI), and the RDS Molecular teams to scale our impact and seamlessly integrate software with our molecular diagnostics and sequencing hardware.

Your Key Responsibilities

Strategic Leadership & P&L Ownership

  • Drive the overarching commercial strategy and financial performance, holding complete accountability for portfolio P&L and business outcomes.
  • Formulate and implement joint value propositions and commercial entry strategies for the US market and global territories.
  • Translate complex strategic goals into clear, actionable objectives for product teams, fostering an autonomous environment focused on problem-solving.
  • Champion the central product vision and principles established by leadership, actively communicating the "why" to your team and the wider organization.

Product Excellence & Collaboration

  • Lead the lifecycle of clinician-facing tools, ensuring successful on-market management, with a specific focus on supporting and expanding APAC and global markets.
  • Partner closely with the RDS Molecular team to advance commercial and technological integration opportunities involving the Avenio portfolio and the Axelios sequencer.
  • Partner closely with Foundation Medicine on enhanced Precision Oncology offerings and collaboration
  • Monitor and report on core product performance metrics, market reception, and global affiliate feedback directly to executive leadership.
  • Continuously optimize organizational processes to elevate quality and efficiency standards across your lifecycle.

People & Lifecycle Coaching

  • Act as a dedicated leader-coach, prioritizing the recruitment, mentorship, and continuous upskilling of your Product Management team.
  • Guide teams in advanced product discovery practices, helping them make strategic, customer-centric trade-offs in highly complex clinical environments.
  • Structure cross-functional workflows and optimize team topology to drastically reduce organizational dependencies and move faster.
  • Resolve complex, cross-team conflicts and remove operational barriers in tandem with the Project Leader.
