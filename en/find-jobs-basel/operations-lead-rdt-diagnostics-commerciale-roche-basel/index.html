The opportunity

  • Your main focus will be holding end-to-end accountability for the operational health, infrastructure lifecycle, and business continuity of our critical global Quote Management ecosystem.
  • You are leading and governing our outsourced managed services partner, defining strategic operational KPIs, maintaining strict SLA compliance, and providing crucial business-domain mentorship to external team members.
  • You are driving deep cross-functional coordination across integration platforms (including SAP, Salesforce, and MuleSoft) and enterprise infrastructure teams, acting as a strategic liaison to break down technical silos.
  • You are managing long-term infrastructure planning, overseeing capacity management and budgeting, while actively identifying opportunities to implement innovative AI and automation solutions
  • You are fostering an ecosystem of extreme accountability and proactiveness, mentoring internal technical talent, and championing a "fix once" approach to eliminate recurring operational risks.

Who you are

  • Experience: 5+ years in IT operations, infrastructure, or business analysis with proven end-to-end support ownership; including 3+ years in the MedTech or Healthcare Life Sciences sector.
  • Vendor Management: A proven track record of managing and driving accountability with outsourced/managed services delivery partners.
  • Domain Expertise: Strong, demonstrable understanding of the Quote-to-Contract (Q2C), CPQ, and Commercial business domains.
  • Methodologies: Active ITIL, Agile (Scrum, SAFe)
