The Position The Opportunity In Roche’s PTD Biologics Pharmaceutical Development (PTDP), we are responsible for the technical development and preparation of sterile dosage forms of biotechnologically and chemically engineered molecules, including gene therapies.

We are now offering a unique opportunity to join our team in Basel and contribute to the formulation development of novel gene therapies by supporting protocol and sample preparation for laboratory activities, performing advanced analytics to assess the quality attributes of the prepared formulations, and a particular focus on setting up and executing a cell-based functional assay. If you are curious, willing to learn and work in a highly collaborative and supportive international team environment, we are looking forward to receiving your application.

As an intern in our team, your responsibilities will include

  • Working safely and responsibly in a BSL-2 laboratory environment, handling mammalian cells and recombinant adeno-associated viral vectors (rAAVs)
  • Carrying out day-to-day cell culture activities
  • Establishing a cell-based assay to assess the stability of rAAV formulations
  • Learning and performing relevant analytics such as dd/qPCR, flow cytometry, HPLC, DLS, etc and the associated preparatory procedures, such as RNA isolation
  • Planning your studies in line with the concurrent needs and timelines and preparing your protocols
  • Performing experiments, recording, analysing, and presenting your data to the local and broader teams
  • Opportunity to engage in departmental activities related to innovative formulations, processes, and technologies

Who You Are

  • You must either be currently enrolled at a university in pharmaceutical sciences, chemistry, biotechnology, chemical engineering, mechanical engineering, biology or a related field pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree; or have completed your studies (passed the 2nd state examination of your pharmacy studies) within the past 12 months prior to the starting date with a Bachelor's or Master's degree.
  • Cell culture experience and experience of working in the lab without direct supervision (e.g. on a research project) is necessary
  • Previous experience with molecular and/or biophysical analytical methods used in gene therapy formulation development is desirable
  • Demonstrate efficient time management, planning, and appropriate record-keeping, with previous experience using electronic lab books being desirable.
  • Understand data analysis and visualization.Previous experience analyzing data using MS Excel, Google Sheets, GraphPad Prism, R, JMP, or other analysis software would be an advantage.
  • Bring curiosity, adaptability, willingness to learn and support the team, collaborative spirit, openness to different working styles.
  • Work independently on small projects in the laboratory and/or support challenging aspects of ongoing projects.
  • Build technical documentation, present results in internal meetings, learn how current industrial requirements are implemented in everyday life, and gain insights into the multifaceted and diverse working environment of pharmaceutical development.
