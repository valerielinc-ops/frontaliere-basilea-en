Full description

Tasks

  • They accompany a girl with ASD in the standard lessons of the kindergarten (1st kindergarten year).
  • They are responsible for the social integration of the student and help her with the organization of work.
  • They maintain a good cooperation, both with the class teacher, the specialists and with the parents.
  • They are actively involved in the school team and participate in school events and projects (e.g. performances, festivals).
  • The teaching support of the child to be integrated usually takes place from Monday to Friday in the morning.

Requirements

  • They have completed training in social education.
  • They bring a warm, appreciative and at the same time clear attitude in dealing with students.
  • You are an open, resilient, flexible and reliable person with a solution-oriented attitude.
  • Experience in autism is beneficial.

Primary level Ettingen is a learning place where appreciative cooperation, learning in a variety of ways and a good interpersonal climate are lived. Here, children and teachers enjoy shaping their learning and work processes. The kindergarten and primary school Ettingen consist of 24 classes, 480 pupils and around 70 employees. We have an introductory class and an age-mixed small class.
