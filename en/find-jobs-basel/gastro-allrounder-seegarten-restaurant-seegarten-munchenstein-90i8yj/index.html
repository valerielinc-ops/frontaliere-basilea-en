Full description

The offer of Migros Gastronomy is diverse. Our guests can choose from a wide range of freshly and seasonally prepared dishes. For the Restaurant Seegarten, we are looking for a motivated and reliable Gastro Allrounder to support the existing team.

Responsibilities

  • Management of the buffet: Setup and breakdown, as well as regular refilling of food and drinks
  • Ensuring cleanliness and adherence to hygiene regulations in the buffet and work areas
  • Cash register operation: Independent handling of the cash register
  • Friendly and professional interaction with customers

Requirements

  • 1 -2 years of experience in gastronomy preferred
  • Completed basic or further training in system gastronomy
  • Customer-oriented, reliable, and team-capable personality
  • Independent and precise work style
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Migros Bank: Preferential conditions for employees
  • Employee benefits: Diverse offers, discounts, and preferential conditions
