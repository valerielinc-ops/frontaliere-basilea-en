Job Description

As part of the Thermo Fisher Scientific team, you’ll discover meaningful work that makes a positive impact on a global scale. Join our colleagues in bringing our Mission to life every single day to enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. We provide our global teams with the resources needed to achieve individual career goals while helping to take science a step beyond by developing solutions for some of the world’s toughest challenges, like protecting the environment, making sure our food is safe or helping find cures for cancer.

Description

  • Manage and enhance strategic sourcing initiatives for comparator drugs
  • Work with cross-functional teams to develop sourcing strategies that ensure the reliable and cost-effective supply of comparator products for clinical studies.
  • Negotiate pricing, support and develop supply agreements, and commercial terms with pharmaceutical manufacturers, wholesalers, and specialty suppliers.
  • Build and maintain strategic supplier relationships to support global clinical trial requirements.
  • Deliver value through cost savings, risk mitigation, and supply chain optimization.
  • Support customer bids, RFIs, RFQs, and feasibility assessments through sourcing expertise and market intelligence.
  • Monitor market trends, product availability, and supply risks, proactively identifying alternative sourcing strategies when required.
  • Collaborate with internal stakeholders to manage shortages, mitigate supply chain risks, and ensure continuity of supply.
  • Support our mission of enabling customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer.
  • Ensure a reliable, compliant, and cost-effective procurement of comparator drugs,

Requirements

  • Professional certifications (CPSM, CSCP, CPM, MCIPS) are desirable.

  • Demonstrated experience sourcing comparator drugs, ancillary supplies, or other clinical trial materials preferred.
  • Experience working in regulated industries such as life sciences, clinical research, pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, or medical devices preferred.
  • Knowledge of pharmaceutical supply chains, global sourcing markets, and supplier networks is highly desirable.
  • Experience working with pharmaceutical manufacturers, wholesalers, and specialty suppliers preferred.
  • Strong analytical and data-driven decision-making capabilities.
