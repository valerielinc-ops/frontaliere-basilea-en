Principal Expert embedded software (m/f/d)

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Principal Expert Embedded Software (m/f/d) to complement our team and help shape the strategic and technical development of our embedded software platform. In this key role, you will make a significant contribution to the development of innovative and reliable software solutions for our measuring instruments.

The office is located in Endress+Hauser Flow, a competence center for the development and production of flow measurement technology. Together, we develop technologies that ensure precise and safe processes in numerous industries worldwide.

What is this point about?

Do you want to shape the technical orientation of modern embedded software platforms? Are you excited about transforming sophisticated software architectures into robust real-time solutions? Would you like to share your knowledge and actively support the further development of teams?

This role then offers you the opportunity to bring your many years of experience into a central expert role and, together with interdisciplinary teams, to advance the next generation of our embedded software solutions. The position is part of our development organization and works closely with software developers, system architects and other specialist areas.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • assume functional and technical leadership in the context of our current and future software platforms.
  • Together with the team, develop software concepts, embedded software designs and architectural decisions for our platform.
  • Transform concepts into technical solutions, develop prototypes and support implementation with modern embedded C++ on real-time operating systems.
  • Further develop technical basics and best practices for robust, maintainable and testable embedded software and anchor suitable quality measures in the development process.
  • continuously improve our software development processes, identify optimization potential and implement sustainable improvements together with the participating teams.
  • Pass on knowledge, advise and coach colleagues and promote cooperation across teams and departments.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have a university degree (uni/ETH/FH) in electrical engineering, technical computer science, software engineering or a comparable field of expertise as well as many years of practical experience in embedded software development.
  • bring very good knowledge in modern embedded C++ and in the development for resource-restricted systems.
  • have in-depth practical experience with real-time operating systems such as FreeRTOS as well as with incidentality, timing and systematic debugging hardware-related software.
  • master the use of unit tests, static code analysis and code coverage methods to ensure high software quality.
  • ideally have experience in the industrial environment
  • Knowledge of functional safety or metrology is an advantage.
  • Communicate safely in German and English and convince with your strong team and communication skills.

What can you expect from us?

We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.

We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.

We value a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working hours and the opportunity to work mobile.

We support you with modern work equipment and an innovative working environment in which you can actively shape technical decisions.

We promote an open, team-oriented corporate culture in which cooperation, trust and mutual support are lived.

We offer you the opportunity to participate in future-oriented technologies and make a direct contribution to the success of our products used worldwide.
