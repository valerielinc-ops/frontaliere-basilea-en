Full description

For our client from the chemical industry, we are looking for an Executive Assistant Main tasks:

  • Provide overall administrative support to senior Legal leaders.
  • Manage calendars in MS Outlook and coordinate recurring meetings.
  • Prepare agendas, minutes, pre-reads and materials for team meetings.
  • Facilitate virtual meetings (Zoom, MS Teams).
  • Handle internal and external correspondence.
  • Manage documents, deadlines, travel and expenses.
  • Provide general administrative support to Legal teams.
  • Monitor contract/document workflows and ensure timely progression.
  • Support execution of contracts in line with internal policies.
  • Coordinate signatures via electronic signature tools or wet ink.
  • File documents electronically in the company’s repositories and CLM systems.
  • Manage physical archiving and support notarial/legalization tasks.
  • Assist in organizing and facilitating meetings and training sessions.
  • Support preparation of engagement materials, newsletters, surveys and other activities.
  • Maintain email distribution lists.
  • Support onboarding of Legal new joiners.
  • Act as point of contact for facility and IT-related queries.
  • Manage mail, courier shipments, meetings, events, travel and visas.
  • Draft and proofread business correspondence.
  • Locate/scan documents for reports, investigations, discovery, KYC, etc.
  • Document administrative processes and support process improvements.
  • Maintain Teams folders, SharePoint sites, org charts and related metadata.
  • Coordinate training sessions, including preparation, attendance tracking, and follow-up surveys.
  • Provide broad administrative support across Legal Operations processes. What you need to bring:
  • Several years’ experience in an administrative role; legal department or law firm experience is a plus.
  • Proven ability to work in local and international teams.
  • Experience in a multicultural environment.
  • Strong communication skills with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word), Teams, Zoom and SharePoint.
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills; ability to prioritize urgent tasks.
  • Enthusiastic, dynamic, ethical and positive approach.
  • High discretion and confidentiality.
  • Attention to detail, proactive mindset, ownership of tasks.
  • Service-oriented attitude.
  • Bachelor’s degree in business administration or related field (or equivalent).
  • Tech-savvy; comfortable learning new tools and digital processes.
  • Proficient in MS Office, SharePoint and virtual meeting tools (Zoom, Teams).
  • Experience with virtual facilitation tools (breakout rooms, Q&A, Menti) or willingness to learn.
  • Knowledge of contract-filing tools or legal systems is a plus.
  • Fluent in English; good German skills. Additional languages welcome.
  • Understanding of administrative, legal or corporate processes is an advantage.
