Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly seeking highly qualified professionals.

To strengthen our team in the Global Engineering department, we are looking for a dedicated Construction Management Lead (m/f/d), 100%.

Your tasks

  • Development, standardization, and enforcement of construction, HSE, and quality standards
  • Advising project teams on technical, organizational, and regulatory topics including feasibility assessments
  • Monitoring the entire project landscape, identifying risks, and escalating to management
  • Control of construction processes, trades, specialist site managers, and external partners in the GMP environment
  • Review of plans, construction scheduling, progress reports, and regulatory compliance
  • Conducting security inspections, risk analyses, and enforcement of security rules according to SIA 118
  • Ensuring GMP-compliant documentation including construction log, acceptances, inspection reports, and mechanical completion
  • Main interface to clients, planners, joint ventures, and suppliers, as well as moderation of coordination meetings and conflict resolution

Your profile

  • Completed technical studies in civil engineering, architecture, process engineering, or a comparable field
  • Several years of experience in construction management of large industrial or infrastructure projects
  • Experience in GMP environments from pharmaceuticals, biotech, or chemistry is an advantage
  • Knowledge of EPC/EPCM structures and complex stakeholder environments
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss building and safety regulations
  • Strong leadership and governance skills as well as diplomatic assertiveness
  • Structured, proactive, and solution-oriented approach with the ability to manage multiple project levels in parallel
  • Experience in managing external CM mandates or general contractors, as well as excellent German and English skills

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal opportunities for further development and training
  • In-house daycare center at the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as the possibility of non-mandatory pension provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to benefits platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy, and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A variety of free sports activities on the Bachem campus

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
