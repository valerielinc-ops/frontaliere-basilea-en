Full description

Meaningful activity in a multicultural working environment, flat hierarchies

What you do with us

  • You process the monthly wage runs together with our Payroll Manager and represent them in absences
  • You contribute to the year-end work, including payroll and withholding tax
  • You check working hours, mutations and wage payments in Abacus and maintain employee data
  • You are our social insurance specialist and are supervised by A–Z (AHV, BVG, UVG, KTG, EO)
  • You process insurance benefits in the event of illness, accident and maternity or paternity
  • You build a systematic absence management: You prepare cases and actively pass on the information to the responsible body
  • You are the first contact person for time recording
  • You create evaluations that make decisions easier
  • You work in HR projects and actively promote the optimization and digitization of our HR and payroll processes, including testing
  • You train your topics internally, briefly and without specialist Chinese
  • In addition, there are service anniversaries, the retired party and administrative special tasks

What you bring

  • Basic commercial training with further training in payroll, social security or human resources
  • Experience in contract processing – you know how a monthly ending feels when it gets tight
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss social security
  • IT affinity: You quickly work your way into systems. Knowledge of Abacus and Perigon is a plus, but not a must – as well as experience in healthcare
  • Exact work – and yet an eye for how things relate
  • Self-initiative: You do not wait until someone tells you what to do, but recognize for yourself what is pending
  • Service orientation: For you, the employees are not wage types, but people with concerns
  • Flexibility and enjoyment of a versatile all-rounder role
  • Humor. Really. We laugh even on the 25th of the month.

What you find with us

  • An HR team that grows together – short distances, open doors, real mutual support instead of responsibility ping-pong
  • The opportunity to be involved in building new structures and processes right from the start
  • A task with meaning: Your work keeps the company running, which serves people at home in Basel every day

What you find with us

Attractive training opportunities Velo check-days, all-weather suitable work and work clothes, free coffee, tea and fruit Annual health care contribution for your individual health needs

About Spitex Basel

SPITEX BASEL is a non-profit, non-profit-oriented foundation with a service mandate from the Canton of Basel-Stadt. As a leading Spitex organization with a public service mandate, SPITEX BASEL offers comprehensive services in the areas of outpatient care as well as housekeeping and care.
