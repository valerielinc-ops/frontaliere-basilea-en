Who we are

Psychiatrie Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, day-patient, outreach (home treatment) and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. It supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of housing, work and daily activities (www.inclusioplus.ch). It also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Centre for Work and Mental Health. PBL employs around 1,300 people at several locations in the canton of Basel-Landschaft and treats, cares for and advises over 15,000 patients each year (www.pbl.ch).
