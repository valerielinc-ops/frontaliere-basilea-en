This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you excited about solving technical challenges directly on site and ensuring smooth IT operations? You appreciate contact with different people and like to take responsibility?

Whether support request, installation or rollout: With your commitment, you ensure that our employees can count on a functioning IT at any time.

Then apply to us, because with you the IT runs around in our post offices!

You can do that.

  • As an ICT technician, it ensures that the IT workstations in our post offices and operating sites function reliably.
  • On site, you support employees with technical issues and ensure a high quality of service.
  • You process requests as well as move, add and change (MAC) orders in decentralized network operation.
  • You carry out rollouts, installations and smaller ICT projects independently.
  • Installing, replacing, reinforcing and uninstalling end devices is also part of your varied area of responsibility.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship, ideally in the ICT, computer science or technical environment.
  • For regional missions you bring the driver's license Cat. B and are happy to travel at our locations.
  • You work independently, reliably and customer-oriented.
  • Even in hectic situations, you keep an overview and enjoy working in a team.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing and are flexible for occasional assignments outside regular working hours.
