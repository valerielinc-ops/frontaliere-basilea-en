About the Role

Imagine helping bring the next generation of life-changing biologic medicines to patients around the world. As an Upstream Process (USP) Research Scientist II within the Biologics Research Center at Novartis, you will play a hands-on role in advancing our innovative biologics pipeline through cutting-edge mammalian protein expression technologies. Working alongside a collaborative team of scientists, you will contribute to the development and production of recombinant proteins and antibodies that support groundbreaking research programs. This is an exciting opportunity for a curious, driven scientist who enjoys solving complex scientific challenges, embracing new technologies, and making a meaningful impact on the future of medicine in a world-class research environment.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities

  • Execute mammalian cell culture processes to produce recombinant antibodies, antibody fragments, and therapeutic proteins.
  • Expand and maintain suspension cell cultures from laboratory to pilot scale manufacturing volumes.
  • Operate single-use bioreactor systems to support high-quality protein expression programs.
  • Design, perform, and analyze experiments to resolve technical challenges and optimize processes.
  • Evaluate scientific literature and apply innovative technologies to improve research outcomes.
  • Maintain laboratory readiness through equipment management and preparation of sterile production materials.
  • Document findings, communicate results, and actively contribute to multidisciplinary project discussions.

Essential Requirements

  • Master’s Degree in Life Sciences plus relevant industry experience of sterile mammalian cell culture or bioprocessing.
  • Strong hands-on experience with mammalian cell technologies, stable cell lines, and sterile laboratory techniques.
  • Hands on experience of Wave Systems
  • Knowledge of suspension cell culture systems and large-scale cell expansion workflows.
  • The ability to follow platform-based approaches with a curiosity and exploratory mindset to think about how improvements can be made.
  • Ability to rapidly learn and apply new scientific technologies, methodologies, and laboratory equipment.
  • Proven ability to prioritize workloads, manage timelines, and deliver high-quality results.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills with a proactive, quality-focused approach to teamwork. English, both written and spoken, is essential and German is advantageous

Desirable Requirements

  • Experience in Molecular Biology.
  • Experience in Downstream Processing
