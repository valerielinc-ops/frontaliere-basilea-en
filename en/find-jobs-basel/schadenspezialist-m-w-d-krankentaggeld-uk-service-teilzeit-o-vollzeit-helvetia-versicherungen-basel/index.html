Tasks

  • Independent and efficient processing of simple to medium personal injuries in the area of sickness allowance in French (Deputy in German)
  • Responsibility for the damage facility, including the cover test
  • Active case management in close cooperation with internal and external partners
  • Carrying out telephone initial examinations with insured persons and employers
  • Writing of correspondence in performance cases
  • Coordination with other social insurance schemes (IV, ALV, UVG, BVG etc.)
  • Cooperation in the further development of processes and automation

Requirements

  • Experience in the insurance industry, ideally in personal insurance
  • Commercial or basic medical training with further education in the insurance sector (e.g. social insurance specialist or insurance specialist with a federal certificate)
  • Strong service and customer orientation as well as a high sense of responsibility
  • Enjoy working with various internal and external contacts
  • Empathy, active listening and the ability to prioritize tasks
  • Very good knowledge in French and good knowledge in German are a must

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
