Key Responsibilities

The Study Statistician

  • is responsible for the overall quality and the timely delivery of the statistical analyses for assigned studies in compliance with Sobi SOPs and regulatory requirements/guidelines
  • provides leadership and oversight to in-house consultants and CRO statisticians working on the assigned studies.
  • supports the Program or Product Statistician in study design to meet project objectives and regulatory requirements
  • Develops or oversees the Statistical Analysis Plans (SAPs) and study-level statistical deliverables.
  • Provides statistical input to study protocols, CRFs TFL shells or prototypes.
  • Ensures that study result interpretation, presentations and CSR conclusions are scientifically justified by the study results.
  • Provides programming support for in-house reporting, simulations and exploratory analyses as appropriate.
  • Contributes to CSRs, regulatory question responses, publications and abstracts.
  • Stays informed of developments in new statistical methodology and statistically related guidelines and regulations through literature review and attendance at workshops and professional meetings.

The Program Statistician

  • supports the Product Statistician in study design for the program or indication to meet project objectives and regulatory requirements
  • is responsible for the overall quality and the timely delivery of the statistical analyses for the studies within the assigned indication or program in compliance with Sobi SOPs and regulatory requirements/guidelines
  • Provides statistical and strategic input into the design and preparation of Clinical Development Plan for the assigned program, ensuring alignment with TPPs and Target Label.
  • Contributes to regulatory submission documents for the assigned indications, including eCTD content, responses to regulatory questions and other relevant documents.
  • provides leadership and project coordination to the statisticians working in the program, including in-house consultants and statisticians at CROs

Scope of the Job

Study Statistician

  • provides leadership to in-house consultants and CRO statisticians working on the assigned studies.
  • Develops Statistical Analysis Plans and study-level statistical deliverables.
  • Provides statistical input to study protocols, Statistical Analysis Plans, CRFs and TFL shells or prototypes.
  • Contributes to CSRs, regulatory question responses, publications and abstracts as delegated.
  • Contributes to CRO evaluation, best-practice development, new initiatives and SOP development as relevant.

Program Statistician

  • Provide leadership and project coordination to the statisticians working in the program, including in-house consultants and statisticians at CRO
  • Provide statistical and strategic input into the design and preparation of the Clinical Development Plan (CDP) for the assigned program ensuring alignment with the TPPs and Target Label
  • Provide statistical and strategic input to relevant regulatory documents for the assigned program, e.g. eCTD responses to regulatory questions.
  • Provide statistical and strategic input into other regulatory documentation for the assigned program e.g., IB, PSUR and RMP
  • Take a pro-active role in designing innovative studies and programs, including Go/No Go criterion and associated risk levels, to meet project objectives
  • Contribute to the writing of publications and abstracts, in accordance with the publication strategy for the project
  • Stay informed of developments in new statistical methodology and statistically related guidelines and regulations through literature review and attendance at workshops and professional meetings.
  • Contribute to the development of SOPs as relevant

Education/Learning Experience Required: Bachelor of Science (BSc) in mathematical statistics, statistics or equivalent. Desired: Master of Science (MSc) or PhD in mathematical statistics, statistics or equivalent

Work Experience

Required

  • Minimum of 5 years of experience as a statistician in the pharmaceutical industry, CRO or related field.
  • Previous experience of working as a study statistician or equivalent
  • Experience working in more than one phase of clinical development
  • Experience of statistical programming in SAS; experience with R or other statistical software is desirable.

Desired

  • Experience of regulatory submission and defense activities
  • Experience of regulatory interactions
  • Experience of clinical program and study design and data analysis in using a variety of designs and statistical techniques
  • Experience of collaboration with statisticians and other functions at CROs in the area of out-sourced clinical studies

Skills/Knowledge

Required

  • Comprehensive knowledge of clinical and pharmaceutical drug development
  • Sound knowledge of theoretical and applied statistics
  • Ability to find innovative approaches to trial design and analysis
  • Sound understanding of the regulatory environment within clinical development
  • Ability to be the statistical representative from the company at regulatory interactions
  • Ability to coach less experienced statisticians in all aspects of their role.

Desired

  • Good understanding of CDISC, including SDTM and ADaM

Personal Attributes

Required

  • Excellent written and oral communication skills
  • Strategic thinking and influencing skills
  • Ability to communicate statistical information to non-statisticians
  • Strong ability to collaborate and contribute in global cross-functional teams
  • Leadership skills

Languages

Required

  • Excellent writing and oral communication in English
