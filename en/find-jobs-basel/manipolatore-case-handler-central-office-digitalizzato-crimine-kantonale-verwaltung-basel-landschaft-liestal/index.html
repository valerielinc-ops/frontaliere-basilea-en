Full description

Tasks

  • Further processing of complaints received by police departments and Suisse ePolice in the field of digitized crime
  • Obtaining, preparing and evaluating information
  • Easy research on social networks and publicly available data sources
  • Detection of case connections and possible series
  • Immediate action in the event of imminent danger
  • Preparation of reports and documentation based on the knowledge gained
  • Participation in the planning and implementation of education and training

Requirements

  • Police officer with federal qualification or completed commercial training (E- or M-profile) with several years of professional experience or equivalent training
  • Affinity and enthusiasm for digital technologies and law enforcement investigations
  • Structured, careful and independent working methods
  • High load capacity
  • Readiness for personal and subject-specific further development and further education
  • High social skills and initiative
  • Good communicative skills, stylish-confident German in words and writing, English skills are advantageous

The Police Basel-Landschaft is a modern police corps with a wide range of tasks. More than 700 employees in the canton ensure a high level of security for its residents. With advanced technologies, equipment and vehicles and in close cooperation with the population and partner organisations, crimes are prevented, combated and clarified and intervened in emergencies. Targeted and efficient, the department of the Security Directorate makes an important contribution to the sense of security and the quality of life in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft.
