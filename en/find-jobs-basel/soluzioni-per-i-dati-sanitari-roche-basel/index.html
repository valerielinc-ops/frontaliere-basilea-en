Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position In the Swiss branch of Roche Swiss Affiliate, we work at the interface between medical strategy, targeted data collection, evidence-based decision-making and the participation of the health ecosystem. Our division focuses on advancing patient-related data generation across the entire product lifecycle, as well as shaping the future of data-driven medicine in Switzerland.

As a Health Data Solutions Partner, you will assume a key strategic position with strong matrix leadership responsibility. In close cross-functional cooperation with Disease Area Squads, Market Access, Regulatory and Pipeline Strategy, you will be responsible for strategic integrated evidence support (Integrated Evidence Support) and Integrated Evidence Planning (iEP) throughout the entire product cycle, from the early pipeline to products already marketed. Through in-depth insights into the Swiss healthcare system, you will promote data-driven initiatives that optimize clinical practice, close critical evidence gaps for our products and deliver concrete results for patients.

For this challenging task, we are looking for a formative, activating personality at the interface of science, data analysis and strategy.

The possibility

In this role, you act as a motivating initiator:in, networker:in and impulse provider:in, bringing together multidisciplinary teams, breaking up silos and driving forward data-driven initiatives ("Friendly Activator").

  • Strategic Integrated Evidence Planning (iEP) & Life Cycle Management: Managing and orchestrating the iEP process for RPS for both pipeline assets and marketed products to support sustainable therapeutic value throughout the product lifecycle.
  • Identify Evidence Gaps: Proactively identify and address local and global evidence gaps to support our products across emerging and existing disease areas and indications, in close coordination with cross-functional squads and the pipeline strategy.
  • Advice on data projects: Strategic support and advice for internal teams through all phases of clinical data projects, ensuring alignment with product strategy, regulatory requirements and patient benefits.
  • Consulting & Upskilling: Training and consulting of internal teams and external stakeholders on diagnostic challenges, real-world data (RWD) and the integration of digital health technologies (digital health).
  • Matrix Leadership & Strategic Responsibility: Perceiving a driving role at the interface of medical strategy and targeted data generation for the entire Roche portfolio and the advancement of data-driven medicine.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: Close, proactive collaboration with Disease Area Squads, Market Access, Regulatory and Pipeline Strategy to optimize clinical practice and deliver tangible patient benefits.

Who you are

For this key location, we are looking for a motivating, scientifically-savvy, data-affine personality with a deep interest in the Swiss healthcare system and the ability to implement complex data projects.

  • Education & professional experience: Higher scientific degree (MD, PharmD or PhD) in life sciences or a related field, and at least 3+ years (or 5+ years for senior level) experience in the pharmaceutical or biotech industry.
  • Expertise: proven, in-depth expertise in Real-World Evidence (RWE)/Real-World Data (RWD), Medical Affairs, Investigator-Initiated Research or clinical research projects; A background in precision medicine, molecular biology and digital health is a strong advantage.
  • Swiss healthcare system: Deep structural understanding of the Swiss healthcare system, including clinical practice standards, compensation and reimbursement pathways, pre- and post-launch strategies and the regulatory environment.
  • Language skills: native language or safe German (mandatory) and safe English and French (very desired).
  • Mindset & leadership skills: Strong “Friendly Activator” mindset. Combined, proven matrix leadership capabilities to inspire multidisciplinary teams. Strategic agility to easily switch between the macro vision of the ecosystem and the micro details of concrete data projects. Strong customer orientation to build trusting partnerships.
