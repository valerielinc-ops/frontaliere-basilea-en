CordenPharma is one of the leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CDMO) and develops and produces pharmaceutical active ingredients, pharmaceuticals and related packaging services on behalf of its customers as a full-service service provider. The group employs around 3,000 people. Our network in Europe and the USA offers flexible and specialized solutions for six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules and Oligonucleotides. We strive for excellence in supporting this network and are committed to providing the highest quality products for the benefit of patients. Our People Vision: We strive for excellence.
