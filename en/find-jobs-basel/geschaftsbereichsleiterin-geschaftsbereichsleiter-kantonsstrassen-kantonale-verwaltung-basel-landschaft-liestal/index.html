Full description

Tasks

  • Professional, financial and personnel management of the Canton Roads business unit with the decentralized regional areas, signalling and maintenance management with approximately 90 employees
  • Active participation in the management of the civil engineering office and further development of medium- to long-term strategic goals in the civil engineering office and in the business area for reliable operation and maintenance including art buildings, traffic control systems, lighting and other ancillary systems
  • Ensure the necessary human resources and promote interdisciplinary cooperation in the business, department and directorate
  • Investment planning within the existing overarching processes and budget management
  • Overall management of maintenance and renewal measures as well as structural and operational maintenance and maintenance of technical road installations
  • Responsibility for service management through coordination of internal and external services within the business unit
  • Ensuring a timely and smooth event and winter service for the entire cantonal road network

Requirements

  • Successfully completed degree in engineering or comparable qualification with strong knowledge in civil engineering and road infrastructure
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in a comparable position with specialist and personnel management
  • Joy for innovation, ability to change, strong organizational skills and high problem-solving competence
  • Clear and addressable oral and written communication
  • Self-motivation and the ability to effectively delegate tasks

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and fields, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
