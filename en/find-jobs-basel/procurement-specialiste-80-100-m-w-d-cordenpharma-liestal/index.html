Full description

CordenPharma is one of the leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CDMO) and develops and produces pharmaceutical active ingredients, drugs and related packaging services on behalf of its customers as a “full service” service provider. The group employs around 3,500 people. Our network in Europe, Asia and the US offers flexible and specialized solutions across six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules and Oligonucleotides. We strive for excellence in supporting this network and are committed to delivering the highest quality products for the benefit of patients. Our People Vision: We strive for excellence. Your exciting task area

  • Self-responsible handling of operational purchasing processes in the ERP system – from demand recording to ordering to timely delivery
  • Obtaining, comparing and evaluating offers taking into account price, quality, delivery capability and regulatory requirements
  • Active monitoring of delivery dates, tracking of open orders and proactive coordination with internal suppliers and suppliers
  • Procurement of raw materials, chemicals, laboratory and consumables in the pharmaceutical and quality-relevant environment
  • Interface management between purchasing, quality assurance, production, laboratory, finance and suppliers to ensure smooth procurement processes
  • Service-oriented advice to internal customers on prices, delivery times, sources of supply and procurement options
  • Coordination and follow-up of relevant supplier documentation such as specifications, analysis certificates, BSE/TSE documents and other GMP relevant evidence
  • Support for change control processes and purchase-relevant projects to ensure documented and quality-compliant changes
  • Maintenance, harmonisation and continuous improvement of processes and purchasing master data
  • Support of the Head of Procurement in strategic purchasing initiatives (interface between Liestal and global purchasing)

Your profile

  • Commercial or technical basic training, ideally supplemented by further training in purchasing, supply chain management or a comparable area
  • Several years of experience in operational purchasing, preferably in the chemical, pharmaceutical or life science industry
  • Knowledge in the GMP-regulated environment or in quality-relevant procurement processes is an advantage
  • Experience in change, project or improvement initiatives, in particular in the areas of process standardization, supplier consolidation, compliance or cost optimization
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • Secure handling of ERP systems as well as very good user knowledge in MS Office
  • Communication strength, negotiation skills and convincing behavior in dealing with suppliers, global procurement and internal specialist areas
  • Team-oriented purchasing person with a high service orientation, business partner mentality and willingness to assume responsibility within the purchasing organization
  • Commercial judgment, data-based decision-making and the ability to translate global purchasing strategies into local action
