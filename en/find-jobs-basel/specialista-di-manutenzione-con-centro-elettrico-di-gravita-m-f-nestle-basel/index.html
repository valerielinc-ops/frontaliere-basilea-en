Summary of entity

Fabrik Basel is a high-performance production company of Nestlé Suisse S.A. and produces THOMY culinary products for domestic and international use. For our maintenance department, we are looking for an engaged person as a maintenance specialist immediately or by agreement.

A day in the life of a maintenance professional with an electrical center of gravity

  • Independent implementation of electrical maintenance, maintenance and repair work on our production facilities as well as on building technology
  • Ensuring the availability and operational safety of plants and machinery and correcting malfunctions and failures
  • Active support in the further development and optimization of preventive maintenance
  • Implementation of technical improvements, alterations and revisions
  • Cooperation in continuous improvement processes (CIP) to increase plant availability, quality and efficiency
  • Documentation of maintenance and maintenance work in the corresponding system
  • Support for the commissioning of new facilities and technical facilities

This makes them successful.

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automatic engineer EFZ, electronic engineer EFZ, electrical installer EFZ or comparable technical training
  • Further training as a technician HF (preferably system engineering, automation or electrical engineering) or a higher specialist examination (HFP) is advantageous
  • Several years of professional experience in electrical maintenance, ideally in an industrial production environment
  • Experience with PLC controls, preferably Siemens and/or Allen-Bradley
  • Knowledge of fault analysis and troubleshooting on automated equipment
  • NIV Art. 13 Advantageous approval or equivalent qualification
  • Good PC user knowledge (MS Office as well as maintenance or ERP systems)
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Readiness for flexible work assignments, piquet and occasional weekend assignments
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
