Full description

In order to strengthen our dedicated team in the neonatal intensive care unit (NEO), we are looking for a motivated and responsible nurse with expertise and heart for the care of critically ill newborns.

Entry date: by immediate or by agreement

Their tasks

  • They look after, care for and promote premature and newborns (level 3) in acute to critically unstable health conditions – always with a patient-oriented focus and a lot of empathy
  • They accompany parents and families during the often long hospital stay with empathy, expertise and open communication.
  • You take over the newborn primary care in the University Women’s Hospital and contribute your expertise and peace of mind in critical moments

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF/FH or a completed post-diploma (ÜWP) in neonatology or the willingness to complete it
  • You have experience in the care of premature and newborn
  • They enjoy caring for children and their relatives in the sense of a family-centered, development-promoting care
  • They have a high degree of professional and social competence
  • You are an independent, responsible, and resilient personality
  • You are a committed personality who lives team spirit and actively strengthens interprofessional cooperation

Our offer

  • A varied and meaningful activity in a professionally experienced, motivated and committed team
  • Comprehensive and structured familiarisation
  • Attractive and contemporary employment conditions, participatory service planning
  • Participation in internal and external training
  • Discover numerous other benefits under "our benefits" that we offer our employees
