The Position Your department

Roche Diagnostics Deutschland GmbH sells forward-looking diagnostics for blood analysis as well as laboratory concepts and solutions in clinics and laboratory institutes. In the coming years, a large number of systems will be replaced in the market. In order to meet this great challenge, we are looking for support in our new, forward-looking pre-configuration center. The new generation of devices (cobas pro & cobas pure) is pre-installed and prepared for implementation in the customer laboratory. We are proud of who we are, what we do and how we do it, with a clear goal in mind: Doing now what patients need next.

Your main tasks, after a detailed training, are

  • Results-oriented device preparation in the Pre-Configuration Center.
  • Installation, adjustment and commissioning (software, application & final testing) of complex Roche standard analysis devices e.g. cobas pure / cobas pro including cobas c 703 according to target agreements.
  • Timely and professional construction (applicative and technical) as well as if necessary. Repair of Roche systems with a simple degree of difficulty, including final test and routine running Documentation (in the CRM system)
  • Perform the electrotechnical safety inspection and final acceptance of the preconfigured systems before delivery.
  • Participation in data backup and the creation and maintenance of documentation (e.g. installation and commissioning plans) in accordance with requirements and internal specifications.
  • Independent troubleshooting and elimination of faults on analysis systems and electronically controlled systems and machines.
  • Timely reporting and immediate reporting of critical issues/complaints to the Roche Customer Support Center.
  • Compliance with the legal obligations of a medical device consultant acc. § 31 MPG (see Appendix) and ensuring compliant complaint processing.

You bring that with you

  • You have a recognised and completed at least two years of vocational training with specialisation in electrical engineering, electronics, mechanics, IT system electronics, or comparable
  • Ideally, You already have your first professional experience in the technical field
  • You have experience in dealing with technical systems (fluidics, electronics, metrology, metrology, IT)
  • Ideally, you can use control/operating software.
  • You speak and write fluent German
  • English skills in word and writing are desirable.

This work requires a background check for air cargo (ZÜP), which may be completed during the training period.

Location The office is located in Monsheim, near Worms.

This post shall be open-ended.
