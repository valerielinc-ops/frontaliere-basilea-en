Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Regio Muttenz, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Arlesheim, Münchenstein, Muttenz and Pratteln, we are looking for a personality as a customer advisor.

What awaits you?

  • Advice and support of customers in the customer zone
  • Demand recognition and forwarding of customers to the relevant specialist consultants
  • Cooperation in market development measures and performance of representative tasks
  • Ensuring the operation of all cash dispensers and the necessary stocks of notes and varieties
  • Support and relief of customer consultants in the administrative processing of all arising business cases

What do you bring?

  • Completed commercial training, experience in banking is advantageous
  • Knowledge of banking products in the areas of saving, paying, investing, provisioning
  • Enthusiastic and sales-oriented personality with a winning appearance
  • Self-employed, precise and responsible work
  • Strong communication capability

You enjoy direct, personal customer contact and wish for a diverse and exciting activity with design possibilities? Then you are exactly right with us!
