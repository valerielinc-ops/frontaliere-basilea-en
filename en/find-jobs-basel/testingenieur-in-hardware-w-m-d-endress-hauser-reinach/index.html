Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a test engineer (f/m/d) electronics manufacturing to complement our team and actively promote the further development of our test systems and the digitalization of our electronics manufacturing. In this role, you make an important contribution to the quality and reliability of our products and shape the future of our manufacturing processes.

What is this point about?

Would you like to help shape the test strategy of a modern electronics production? Does it appeal to you to automate and continuously improve complex inspection processes? Do you want to work closely with development, production and engineering to realize innovative solutions?

In this function, you combine technical know-how with innovative spirit and help ensure that our electronic assemblies are reliably tested and manufactured efficiently. The position is located in electronics manufacturing and works closely with research & development, industrial engineering and project management.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Create, validate, optimise and document complex optical, mechanical and electronic test programs for electronic assemblies
  • programming and maintaining industrial software solutions and other equipment for electronics production
  • co-develop the future test strategy as well as the digitalization of electronics production and evaluate new technologies and test methods
  • work closely with R&D, Industrial Engineering, Electronics Manufacturing and Project Management to implement efficient test solutions
  • Manufacturers of test facilities coordinate and provide technical support for the further development and stabilization of systems
  • Ensure 3rd level support for test facilities and contribute to continuous improvement of production processes

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have a technical vocational training as well as a completed degree programme (Uni/FH) in electrical engineering, mechatronics, automation technology or a comparable field of study
  • ideally bring professional and project experience in an industrial production environment
  • are characterized by a high quality awareness, a structured working method and very good knowledge of German and English
  • have in-depth knowledge in the programming and automation of plants and systems, ideally with NI TestStand and Vision Builder
  • know the development of Windows software with .NET and C# and move safely in the DevOps environment
  • preferably have knowledge of electronic circuitry and measurement technology as well as experience with databases, computer networks or programming in the NI environment

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We offer a flexible working time model without core time for a balanced work-life balance.
  • We invest in your health – with comprehensive health management.
  • We support you with a company pension and offers for the compatibility of family and work.
