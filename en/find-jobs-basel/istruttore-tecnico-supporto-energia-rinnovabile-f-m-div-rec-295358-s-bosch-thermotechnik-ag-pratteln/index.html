Full description

Your tasks

  • You will provide technical support for Buderus and Bosch wholesalers as well as group leaders and end customers.
  • You ensure the deputy of technical instructors in the fields of fossil and renewable energies.
  • You support Technical Customer Service with administrative tasks.
  • You train our partner companies in technical topics.
  • You manage the telecontrol systems of our product portfolio.
  • You set up the remote service area and ensure its ongoing support. You will establish and maintain international contacts within the Group and with suppliers, including participation in international technical training.
  • You coordinate and accompany all field and recall actions.
  • You carry out technical clarifications as well as warranty processing with suppliers, customers and the Group and ensure reporting to the responsible authorities.
  • You handle damage cases, record installations and prepare the corresponding reports.
  • As the top technical support authority, you handle complex cases when the group leader needs further support.
  • You develop and define technical courses with addressable course objectives, schedules and course content.
  • You create and develop technical training materials for the service.
  • You conduct technical training as a trainer.
  • You supervise and coordinate the training room in Pratteln.
  • You take on special tasks in customer service and work in professional associations.
  • You participate in marketing events such as trade shows and customer events.
  • You are ready to travel regularly at home and abroad.

Your profile

  • Training: Basic technical training in the field of heating, building or energy technology or a comparable training
  • personality and working method: Self-employed, self-initiated, responsible, structured, solution-oriented, analytical, strong in communication and sovereign in appearance. Joy of knowledge transfer and presentation as well as readiness for travel activities and continuous further education
  • experience: several years of professional experience in heat pump and heating technology, in-depth knowledge in heat pumps, hydraulics, control and plant technology as well as experience in carrying out technical training. Knowledge of our product portfolio is an advantage
  • languages: very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English is an advantage

Contact and additional information

You can expect a responsible and versatile job with a wide range of technical tasks and plenty of room for manoeuvre. You will actively contribute your expertise, develop technical support and our training offers and work closely with customers, partners and international Group offices.   • Business cars with private use in the EU
