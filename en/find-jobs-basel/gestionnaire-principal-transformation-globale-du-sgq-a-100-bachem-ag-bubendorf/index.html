Full description

We are seeking a highly capable Senior Manager – Global QMS Transformation to play a key role in delivering a global quality transformation program, including the implementation of a Veeva Vault-based enterprise quality platform.

In this role, you will act as the day-to-day execution lead and trusted partner to the Director, Global QMS Transformation, owning the execution of the implementation and ensuring it delivers measurable business value.

Your tasks

Drive Global Transformation

  • Manage day-to-day execution of a global QMS implementation program
  • Translate strategic objectives into structured, actionable plans
  • Ensure delivery across all phases, including design, build, testing, and deployment
  • Monitor progress, oversee risks, resolve issues, and maintain overall program momentum
  • Collaborate with global and local stakeholders to define and implement harmonized QMS processes
  • Balance business requirements with scalable and sustainable system solutions
  • Drive alignment across functions and sites while navigating complexity

Manage Partners and Delivery

  • Coordinate and oversee external vendors and system integrators
  • Ensure high standards of delivery quality, accountability, and collaboration
  • Maintain a pragmatic, results-oriented approach to execution

Ensure Compliance Integration and Business Readiness

  • Embed compliance requirements into program execution in partnership with internal stakeholders
  • Drive business readiness and successful deployment by leading and coordinating change management, communication, and training activities

Your Profile

  • 8–10+ years of experience in pharmaceutical or biotechnology industries
  • Proven experience supporting or leading system implementations and/or transformation programs, ideally in a global environment
  • Solid understanding of Quality Management Systems (QMS) and GMP frameworks
  • Experience working across cross-functional stakeholders
  • Experience implementing Veeva Vault (or other digital quality systems)
  • Strong execution focus with a demonstrated ability to deliver results
  • Ability to manage complexity and prioritize effectively in a dynamic environment
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
  • Certification such as PMP (Project Management Professional) and/or Lean Six Sigma nice to have
  • Familiarity with computer system validation (CSV) in regulated environments would be a plus

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with home office days and an option for obtaining additional vacation days through workload reduction
  • Employee development through numerous internal and external training opportunities
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as option for extra-mandatory pension provision with our Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to the Swibeco benefits platform with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
