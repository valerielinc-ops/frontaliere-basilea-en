Ombudsman Liestal 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Your responsibility

  • The body shall comprise, on the one hand, advisory work and, on the other hand, administrative tasks. They conduct initial contacts with the seekers, clarify their concerns and advise them on simpler matters.
  • They organise discussions between the ombudsmen and clients as well as with authorities and keep minutes of consultations.
  • The editing of correspondence or reports, the creation and evaluation of Excel tables and the creation of PowerPoint presentations is also part of your field of activity.
  • They are responsible for the entire administrative tasks of the Ombudsman's office, in particular for the management of the client database and the compilation of statistics.

Your background

Special authorities

The Canton of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the Canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and fields, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. The independent special authorities (ombudsman, supervisory body data protection and financial control) perform their task impartially and outside the authority of the Government Council. The heads are elected directly by the district council and supervised by it.

Ombudsman

The Ombudsman's office is at the disposal of the entire population in the event of differences with authorities of the canton and the municipalities. It can also be called by administrative employees in the event of personnel conflicts. The Ombudsman's Office advises those seeking advice confidentially and free of charge. It examines impartially whether the offices and services have acted lawfully, correctly and expediently. Their aim is to mediate and resolve the conflict. In addition, she is also responsible for whistleblowing cases in the canton.
