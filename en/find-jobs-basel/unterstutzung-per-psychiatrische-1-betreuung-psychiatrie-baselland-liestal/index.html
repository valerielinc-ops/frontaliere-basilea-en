Support for psychiatric 1:1 care Psychiatry Baselland You are looking for a meaningful part-time job with responsibility? Become part of our team! We are looking for dedicated employees for 1:1 care of patients in acute psychological crisis situations. The missions take place not only at night, but also in the afternoon – depending on clinical needs and your availability.

Their tasks

  • Care of patients in acute mental emergency situations
  • With your calm presence, you provide security, protection and stability
  • You build a trusting relationship and accompany the cared for empathetically
  • You observe attentively and are in close contact with the nursing team

Your profile

  • You are studying medicine, nursing, psychology, social work – or have an education in the nursing sector (e.g. nursing aid SRK)
  • You are interested in working with people in mental crisis situations
  • You are flexible, reliable and stay calm even in challenging moments
  • You have good knowledge of German

Job opening: The job is to be filled immediately or by agreement.
