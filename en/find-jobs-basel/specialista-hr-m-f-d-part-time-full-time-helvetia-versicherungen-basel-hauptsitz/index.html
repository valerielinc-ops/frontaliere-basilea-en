Full description

The HR world at Helvetia is in an exciting transformation. As part of the merger, two teams, processes and system landscapes are growing together to form a common future. This means change, room for manoeuvre and the opportunity to actively participate in the development of new structures. If you enjoy taking on responsibility in a dynamic environment, developing processes and shaping the future of HR, a varied and instructive task with a lot of development potential awaits you. >The job is limited until 31.12.2027, with an option for permanent employment.

Tasks

  • You are the first contact person for operational HR topics and system-related inquiries in your support area.
  • Along the entire Employee Life Cycle, you support and advise employees and managers competently and service-oriented – from contract creation to withdrawal.
  • Together with HR Business Partners, Recruiting, Payroll, Team Systems and various HR Competence Centers, you ensure smooth and efficient HR processes.
  • Through your ideas and commitment, you actively contribute to a positive employee experience and support the continuous digitalization of our HR landscape.
  • Holiday representatives within the team are flexible and thus contribute to a reliable cooperation.
  • Incoming ticketing requests are handled professionally, efficiently and solution-oriented.
  • In your role as a key user, you participate in the further development of various HR systems and engage in exciting HR projects during an important transformation phase.

Requirements

  • A completed commercial basic education or a comparable qualification as well as a further education in the field of human resources, ideally with a federal professional certificate.
  • Several years of experience in personnel administration
  • Knowledge of labour and social security law is an advantage.
  • An independent, structured and precise working method as well as a strong eye for detail.
  • High service orientation and pleasure in competently advising employees and managers.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing and good knowledge of French, English or Italian are advantageous.
  • Security in dealing with HR systems and digital solutions such as SAP, SuccessFactors, UKG or PeopleDoc as well as interest in new technologies and AI applications.
  • A team-oriented, resilient and solution-oriented personality with a positive attitude and joy of change.
  • Openness to new processes, different system worlds and the active participation in shaping a common HR future.

We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
