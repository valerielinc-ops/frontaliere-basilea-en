Are you looking for a break, between two stations or simply a temporary task out of a desire for flexibility? For six months, you will strengthen a manageable women’s clinic in northwestern Switzerland, work in the entire spectrum of gynecology and obstetrics and quickly find your way back into everyday hospital life in a family team. The women’s clinic of an established acute care hospital in northwestern Switzerland accompanies around 500 births every year. The task area covers the entire spectrum of gynecology and obstetrics, from the consultation hour to the operating room to the birth room. The team is committed, dynamic and family, working together interdisciplinary and interprofessional. For a temporary representation of six months from April 2027, the clinic is looking for a senior physician who would like to work with a clear time perspective, for example as a re-entry after a break or as a bridge before a next step.

Their tasks

  • Management of own consultation hours in the team as well as active participation in the surgical program and in the bearroom
  • Care of patients in the entire spectrum of gynecology and obstetrics
  • Participation in emergency, night and weekend services
  • Participation in the further training of assistant doctors
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in a team

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics
  • Professional experience in Switzerland, ideally as a senior physician
  • Experience in surgical gynecology and obstetrics
  • Flexibility and joy in quickly joining a new team
  • Very good knowledge of German

Their advantages

  • A clearly temporary task for six months, ideal for re-entry or as a bridge
  • A workload of 80-100%, with space for your personal planning
  • A family team that warmly welcomes you and supports you when you get started
  • Broad clinical activity with around 500 births per year to refresh or deepen routine
  • A hospital certified as family-friendly with holiday planning along the school holidays
  • Discount food, discounts with partners and free e-bikes in the region
  • If necessary, rooms, studios or apartments for employees for the duration of the assignment
