Gardens - Teamleiter/in Landschaftsgärtner/in Picture for garden care and landscape design (m/f/d) Candidate Apply for this job District for garden care and landscape design (m/f/d)

  • indicates mandatory fields

Do you feel comfortable in gardening and landscape, like taking responsibility and know how to drive a team on site? PEMSA Basel seeks, on behalf of one of our clients, an expert leader in the field of gardening and landscape (m/f/d) for diverse projects in Basel and Basel Country. You will coordinate everyday work, give a hand in person and you will make sure that the design translates into the realization of gardens and outdoor spaces of high quality.

Mansions

  • Management and coordination of a team in the gardening and landscape sector
  • Organization of daily activities and distribution of tasks among employees
  • Construction of gardening projects according to projects and specifications
  • Execution and supervision of flooring, coating and laying of natural stone
  • Coordination of drafting, planting and requalification
  • Ensure the availability of materials and the correct use of machinery
  • Check the quality of work, compliance with deadlines and safety standards
  • Coordinating work with the direction of work and other parties involved

Profile required

  • Complete training as AFC gardener, gardening address and landscape architecture, or equivalent qualification
  • Multiannual professional experience in gardening and landscape design
  • Experience as a team leader or in the management of small teams is preferential title
  • Good knowledge of flooring, earth movement and planting
  • Ability to safely interpret and implement gardening and construction projects
  • Compulsory category B licence
  • Good knowledge of German written and spoken mandatory
  • Approach to autonomous work, responsible and oriented to solutions
