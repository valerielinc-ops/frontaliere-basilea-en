Full description

Tasks

  • In your role, together with the head of department, you shape the strategic orientation and further development of the AIB and, as a member of the management board, bear joint responsibility for the successful implementation of the service mandate.
  • They lead the field of technology in strategic, professional and human terms and, with a clear attitude and targeted promotion, create an environment that enables ownership, professional excellence and strong cooperation.
  • A key focus Its function is to manage large complex and financially important infrastructure projects. They are responsible for this from strategic conception and fundraising to successful implementation.
  • As a driving force of technical and operational development, you recognize potential at an early stage and develop sustainable infrastructures, operational strategies and services with a particular focus on economic efficiency, resource optimization, circular economy and energy efficiency.
  • They represent the area of technology and the AIB in relevant committees and move sovereignly at the interfaces between technology, administration, politics and the public. Complex issues are communicated to communities, customers, political decision-makers and media in an understandable and convincing manner.
  • Different interests and challenging starting points lead you with strategic judgment, negotiating skills and decision-making strength to viable and sustainable solutions.

Requirements

  • They have a university degree at master level in civil engineering or environmental engineering or a comparable technical-scientific discipline. Scientific training in the municipal water management as well as research or development experience ideally complement your profile.
  • Many years of experience in the conception, planning and control of large and complex infrastructure projects combine with in-depth expertise in wastewater treatment and process technology.
  • They combine strong analytical and conceptual skills with entrepreneurial thinking and assess technical issues safely in the context of economic, ecological and regulatory framework conditions. Knowledge of water and environmental law complements your professional competence.
  • As an experienced and persuasive leader, you take responsibility, show attitude and understand how to lead, promote and challenge people with clarity and appreciation.
  • Even with high complexity and parallel projects, you are convincing with pronounced organizational ability, high reliability and a systematic and structured working method.
  • They communicate in a stylish and addressable manner and act sovereignly and credibly towards different stakeholders. Negotiation skills, conflict skills and excellent knowledge of German in words and writing round off your profile.

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and fields, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
