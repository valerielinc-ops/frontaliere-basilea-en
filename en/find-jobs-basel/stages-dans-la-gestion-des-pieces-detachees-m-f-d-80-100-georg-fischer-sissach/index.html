GF Building Flow Solutions (formerly Uponor) is a division of GF and a leading provider of intelligent and sustainable solutions for the safe and efficient use of water in buildings. Become part of a forward-thinking, people-centered company where your ideas and contributions really matter.

For our maintenance department, we are looking for a reliable, proactive and self-organized personality for 6 – 12 months as soon as possible or by agreement.

Internship in spare parts management (m/f/d) 80% - 100%

Their tasks

  • Independent responsibility for the entire project
  • Inventory of our various spare parts in the warehouse
  • Maintenance, input and optimisation of the master data in the maintenance software
  • Searches for the identification of article-specific features and the classification of spare parts

  • Preparation of photo documentation of spare parts

Your profile

  • During or shortly after completion of a bachelor's or master's degree in mechanical engineering or comparable
  • Basic technical understanding of mechanical, electrical and industrial components
  • Careful, structured and independent working methods as well as secure handling of MS-Office and databases
  • Enjoyment of practical and active activity, hands-on mentality and high affinity for exact data acquisition as well as structured documentation of spare parts
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
