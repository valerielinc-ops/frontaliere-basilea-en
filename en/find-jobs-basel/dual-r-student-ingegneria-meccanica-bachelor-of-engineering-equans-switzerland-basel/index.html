Full description

Technology inspires you, and you want to optimally combine theory and practice? Then a dual study at CALIQUA is exactly the right start into your professional future. As a leading company in plant engineering for heat, energy and process applications, we offer you the opportunity to combine your studies with exciting practical assignments and take on responsibility right from the start.

You can move that with us.

During the theoretical phases

  • Acquisition of sound mathematical, scientific and technical foundations
  • Learning to design, design, calculate and manufacture equipment and machinery
  • Deepening business knowledge
  • Linking theory and practice through practical study content

During the practical phases

  • Participation in exciting projects
  • Support of our specialist areas in day-to-day operations
  • Insights into various business areas and processes
  • Application and deepening of knowledge acquired in the course of study
  • Assumption of first responsibility in own tasks and subprojects

That's what makes you.

  • University entrance qualification (successfully completed Matura, University of Applied Sciences or Abitur)
  • Enthusiasm for technical and economic connections
  • Analytical thinking and good technical understanding
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Teamwork, commitment and perseverance
  • High willingness to learn and a sense of responsibility

That's what we offer you.

  • Three-year dual degree programme with recognised bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering
  • Attractive monthly allowance during the entire study period and 30 days of holidays
  • Practical training with varied assignments
  • Personal support by experienced specialists and managers
  • Modern work equipment and a collegial working environment
  • Very good acquisition and development opportunities after successful degree
  • Exciting prospects in a successful and future-oriented company
