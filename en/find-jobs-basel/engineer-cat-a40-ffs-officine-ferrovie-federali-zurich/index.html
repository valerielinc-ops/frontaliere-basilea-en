An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you move the SBB together with your teammates, as well as a varied and paid education. You also have various internal training opportunities. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us - e.g. with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get additional time surcharges between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. You can move that.

  • You prepare passenger trains for their journey, perform shunting movements on train compositions and drive various locomotives with a maximum speed of 30 km/h within a limited radius of the station to the defined starting point.
  • During the shunting movements, you monitor the road and thus ensure safety.
  • You create the driving readiness, enter data from the safety and information systems and perform brake tests.
  • Minor defects or faults on the vehicle can be corrected yourself if possible or reported with the intended instruments of the competent body.
  • Assignments as a shunting specialist: in (Ai40) in full swing are a matter of course for you. You couple and uncouple rail vehicles, direct individual shunting movements and reset train compositions.
