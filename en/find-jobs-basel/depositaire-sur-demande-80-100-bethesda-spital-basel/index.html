Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 11.09.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus topics of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system and rehabilitation as well as palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients annually benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

For our modernly equipped surgical department, we are looking for a surgical warehousing maintainer (80–100%)

Their tasks

  • Execution of the respective specific storage of the patients to be operated on
  • Co-responsibility in meeting the specific needs of patients during surgical care
  • Preparatory and follow-up measures and during the operation
  • On-call service
  • They operate medical devices and ensure their functionality

They bring with them

  • You have a specialist course in surgical storage care as well as several years of professional experience in an interdisciplinary surgical
  • High reliability and care
  • Joy of self-responsible work and a quick understanding
  • High physical load capacity
  • Manual skill and strong technical and hygienic understanding
  • Empathy and communication skills in dealing with people
