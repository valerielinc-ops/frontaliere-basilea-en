Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the Oligo Production department, we are looking for a dedicated (bio) chemist as product steward (a), 100% to ensure smooth project processes in the manufacturing sector.

Their tasks

  • They bear responsibility in customer projects for the (GMP) production of oligonucleotide-based active ingredients, reconciling the requirements and interests of customers and internal stakeholders with production capacities.
  • You will accompany projects in CMC management from initial orders to potentially commercial production and identify and manage necessary process adjustments.
  • Through their coordination and active participation in the interfaces to, among others, R&D, supply chain, technical authors, QC and QA Ensure smooth production according to timelines
  • You take over the communication to customers, provide updates on ongoing processes and process inquiries about production
  • You write final reports on production campaigns and prepare campaign data clearly
  • Furthermore, you process documents such as technical reports, risk analyses, specifications, SOPs, deviations or CAPAs in cooperation with other departments

Your profile

  • You have completed university studies as a (bio-) chemist, ideally with a doctorate, or a comparable education or training. Professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry
  • You have a quick understanding of complex chemical processes and technical relationships and understand how to discuss and present them in an understandable way.
  • They enjoy working in the interface area as well as sovereign management through structured working methods and high flexibility in changing priorities
  • Quick familiarization with new topics, pronounced adherence to deadlines and independent as well as team-oriented order processing
  • Communication and coordination are among your strengths
  • English and German are fluent.
  • Common MS-Office Programs Dominant You very well
  • They work tasks structured, accurate and responsible
  • A dynamic (production) environment brings your flexibility to light
  • Knowledge in the preparation of oligonucleotides (or peptides) and experience in customer contact are advantageous
  • It would be desirable to have first professional experience in production or in dealing with production plants as well as practice in the GMP environment.

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
