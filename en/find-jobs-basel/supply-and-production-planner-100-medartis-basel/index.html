Full description

As a Supply and Production Planner, you are responsible for the reliable and smooth supply of an assigned production portfolio on a global scale. They translate customer needs into actionable production plans and ensure that the required materials, capacities and resources are available on time. You play a key role in ensuring delivery capability and continuously develop planning processes and standards in line with the global supply chain strategy.

Is that true of you? Then apply, now to strengthen our team in Basel as

Supply & Production Planner 100%

Your responsibilities

  • Responsibility in the area of Plan-to-Produce for the creation, maintenance and continuous optimization of a complete and feasible rolling production schedule in SAP S/4HANA, taking into account needs, material availability and capacities.
  • Preparation, moderation and follow-up of regular coordination meetings with internal production units and external contract manufacturers to hand over and implement the production plan.
  • Ensure material availability for the assigned product portfolio in close cooperation with production, quality, purchasing, engineering, product management and suppliers.
  • Implementation of monthly rough-cut capacity planning in SAP S/4HANA as well as coordination with the production areas for early identification of bottlenecks, development of scenarios and definition of suitable countermeasures.
  • Preparation and support of the monthly cross-site S&OP Supply Review Meeting by providing meaningful analysis, reports and decision templates.
  • Analysis, monitoring and reporting of relevant supply chain indicators (e.g. OTIF, capacity utilisation, lead times) and derivation and implementation of continuous improvement measures.
  • Maintenance and continuous optimization of production-relevant master data as well as ensuring high data quality.
  • Participation in the further development of planning processes, methods and systems to increase efficiency and transparency along the supply chain.

Your qualifications

  • Completed Bachelor's degree in business administration, industrial engineering, supply chain management or a comparable qualification.
  • At least 3 to 5 years of professional experience in a comparable position within a regulated production environment, preferably in the pharmaceutical, medical or life science industry.
  • Very good knowledge (ideally key user) in SAP S/4HANA, especially in the modules PP (Production Planning) and MM (Materials Management).
  • Knowledge of SAP PP/DS is an advantage.
  • In-depth knowledge in production planning, material scheduling, capacity planning and supply chain processes.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing which enables effortless communication in daily work
  • Secure handling of MS Office, especially Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Strong customer, service and quality orientation as well as strong communication and team skills in dealing with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Analytical and networked thinking skills as well as a structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented working method with pronounced problem-solving competence.
  • Proactive and resilient personality with assertiveness, diplomatic skills and pleasure in continuous improvement, process optimization and change.

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.
