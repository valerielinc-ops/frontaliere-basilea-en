The Marketing Technologies (MarTech) Business Operations Manager is a critical role dedicated to ensuring that our technology investments translate into tangible business performance. Rather than focusing on technical maintenance, you will oversee a business readiness framework, designing the governance and operational structures that allow our teams to capitalize on new tools without disrupting core performance. You are the custodian of the "run state," responsible for ensuring that the business outcomes and growth are enhanced through the effective use of technology. A primary driver of success for this role is the continuous evolution of our business processes. Your objective is to ensure that our operational model is not just stable, but a competitive advantage, delivering measurable improvements in speed-to-market, cost-to-serve, and overall organisational productivity. The Opportunity As the Business Operations Manager, you are responsible for implementing changes and managing the service model to ensure the long-term operational success and sustainability of our business platforms. You will support the Business Operations Lead in managing the entire transition from project-led implementation to a stable, continuous, and high-performing "business as usual" (BAU) state.
