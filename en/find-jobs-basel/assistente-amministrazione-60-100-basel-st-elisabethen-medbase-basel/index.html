Welcome to Medbase Basel St. Elisabethen

Our modern medical center is located in the heart of the cultural city of Basel and is well connected to public transport. We look after our patients interdisciplinary and quality-oriented. The center’s main focus is on general internal medicine, physiotherapy and complementary therapies acupuncture/tuina, massage and osteopathy. Our team, consisting of almost 50 employees, consists of specialists, therapists from various specialist areas, MPAs and other employees. We maintain a cross-professional and mutually enriching cooperation. At Medbase Basel St. Elisabethen, we also appreciate the international flair in the border triangle. Our flexible working models and deputy solutions enable a good work-life balance.
