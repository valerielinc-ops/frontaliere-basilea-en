Full description

You are a logistics specialist and would like to contribute your experience in a technical environment in the food industry? Then become part of our team and take responsibility for managing spare parts, tools, materials and equipment needed for our technical activities. If you bring team spirit, strong organizational skills and the joy of a new challenge, this job is for you. Send us your application now! Tasks:

  • Material retrieval at contract suppliers according to specifications
  • Performing the goods incoming check (documents and goods)
  • Storage of goods after receipt of order
  • Setting up the goods according to order
  • Capturing material movements and ensuring traceability
  • Compliance with hygiene, cleaning and safety requirements
  • Material handling monitoring (FIFO)
  • Cooperation in inventory checks and inventories
  • Employees in the planning at the location Requirements:
  • Completed training as a logistician, warehouse operator or several years of professional experience in logistics
  • Good organisational capacity and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Teamwork, reliability and independent working methods
  • Knowledge of tools and spare parts
  • Good SAP and general computer skills
  • Knowledge of German and French
  • Advantageous forklift identification
