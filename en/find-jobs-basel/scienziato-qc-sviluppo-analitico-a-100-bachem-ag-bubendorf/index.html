Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our analytical development team in the early phase area, we are looking for a dedicated scientist – Analytical Development (a) 100% with in-depth expertise in the chromatography of oligonucleotides.

Their tasks

  • As a committed and motivated personality, you are part of our agile QC Early Phase Development Group. You can expect a wide range of tasks and questions in a dynamic and exciting environment. In this varied function, you will independently develop (U)HPLC methods for early and late phase projects in oligoanalysis.
  • Another focus is the development of mass spectrometry methods for quantifying impurities for oligonucleotides. You incorporate substance-specific information into method optimization and form robust methods for the release of GMP materials. Their methods form the basis for validation in the GMP environment.
  • You represent your work as an expert in interdisciplinary project teams as well as to external contacts.
  • In addition, you support us and your colleagues with your open-minded and friendly manner and are happy to actively contribute to the team.

Your profile

  • Completed studies in chemistry, pharmacy or a related scientific field
  • In-depth experience in instrumental analysis with a focus on chromatography
  • Hands-on experience in HR mass spectrometry of oligonucleotides
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Expertise in (U)HPLC method development and validation
  • Expertise in data evaluation with Chromeleon or other CDS
  • Hands-on experience in dealing with HR-MS systems
  • Knowledge in the analysis and/or synthesis of oligonucleotides
  • You think innovatively and look for optimization potential when changing
  • Reliable personality with enthusiasm for responsibility and initiative
  • Quality-conscious and exact working method
  • Appreciative and team-oriented personality

Our offer

  • A dynamis ches and a rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the possibility for home office
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as the possibility of a super-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Option to receive additional holidays through a reduced workload
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
