Das können Sie bewegen

  • Investigate endogenous anti-tumor T cell responses and epitope spreading following targeted cancer therapies
  • Identify and experimentally validate patient-specific tumor antigens and T cell epitopes using genomic, computational and functional approaches
  • Characterize tumor-reactive T cells longitudinally using functional assays, peptide–MHC tetramers, high-parameter flow cytometry and single-cell RNA/TCR sequencing
  • Integrate immunological, molecular and clinical data to understand determinants of durable tumor control
  • Take an active role in shaping the project, analyzing and presenting your data, and communicating the results through conferences and scientific publications

Das bringen Sie mit

  • Master's degree in biological sciences, immunology, molecular biology, biomedical sciences, medicine or a related field
  • Hands-on experience in experimental laboratory research and a strong interest in T cell immunology, cancer immunotherapy and translational human research
  • Interest in quantitative biology and willingness to work with complex genomic, single-cell and immunological datasets
  • Scientific curiosity, initiative and the ability to work independently while contributing effectively to an interdisciplinary team
  • Excellent English communication and writing skills; experience with primary human samples, flow cytometry, T cell assays, molecular biology, sequencing, bioinformatics, R or Python is an advantage

Benefits dieser Stelle

  • Access to state-of-the-art infrastructure for flow cytometry, DNA and RNA sequencing, cell culture and high-dimensional data analysis
  • Direct access to longitudinal clinical samples from patients undergoing advanced cancer therapies
  • A highly interdisciplinary environment connecting experimental immunology, translational oncology and clinical research
  • Close collaboration with basic and clinical research groups at the Department of Biomedicine, University Hospital Basel and external partner institutions
  • Cross-disciplinary training opportunities in human immunology, cancer biology, genomics and computational data analysis
  • The opportunity to contribute to a broader research program investigating endogenous anti-tumor immunity across cancer types and therapeutic modalities
