Role overview

Tasks

  • You take over as a substitute for a kindergarten teacher who has a longer holiday, the mixed-age kindergarten class.
  • They contribute constructively, committedly and with their own ideas to the class team.
  • Their working days are Monday and Tuesday (full day), Thursday and Friday mornings (=22.4 Lek). The Wednesday morning with the gymnastics lesson takes over another teacher.
  • They design a creative and versatile lesson and lead the class in a friendly and consistent manner.

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a primary teacher (kindergarten / lower level) or you are in training.
  • They are reliable and flexible and go through everyday work with motivation and serenity.

Sissach is a larger municipality in the upper part of the canton. Easy to reach by car and by train. A beautiful and well-equipped kindergarten awaits you. A curative school teacher and two experienced kindergarten teachers are open to exchange and offer support.
