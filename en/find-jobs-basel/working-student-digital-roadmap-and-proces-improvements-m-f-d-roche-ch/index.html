The Position We are a dynamic and global team responsible for shaping the digital roadmap and driving continuous process improvements for the global Clinical Manufacturing Network with Sites in Basel, Penzberg and San Francisco. We are looking for a highly motivated student to join us for a one-year internship / working student position.

The Opportunity

  • Organizational & Project Support: Assist the team with organizational tasks, project management activities and the tracking of Digital & Operational Excellence milestones.
  • Business Analysis: Analyze clinical manufacturing processes, identify business needs and define requirements to deliver data-driven solutions that drive organizational success.
  • Documentation: Support the preparation of presentations, reports, and training materials, and help research new digital tools or technology trends.
  • AI Use Case: We want you to have ownership! During your time with us, you will lead a dedicated mini-project: researching, conceptualizing, and potentially piloting a small Artificial Intelligence (AI) use case to solve a real internal challenge or streamline a specific process.
  • Global Communication: Support internal communication efforts to ensure our global stakeholders are aligned, engaged, and well-informed about ongoing initiatives.

Who you are

  • Currently enrolled in a university degree program (e.g., Business Informatics, Business Administration, Innovation Management, Computer Science, or similar).
  • Strong interest in digitalization, process optimization and emerging technologies (especially AI).
  • Excellent organizational skills and a proactive, hands-on mentality.
  • Strong communication skills with the ability to translate complex topics into easy-to-understand messages.
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken) is a must, as you will be working closely with a global team.
  • Experience with Google as well as MS Office (PowerPoint, Excel) and collaborative tools; first experiences with process mapping or project management tools are a plus.

Candidates must remain continuously enrolled at a university or higher education institution throughout the entire duration of their employment.
