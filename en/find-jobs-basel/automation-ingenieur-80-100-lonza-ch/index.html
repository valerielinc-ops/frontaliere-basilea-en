For our successful Drug Product focused branch located in Basel and Stein, Switzerland, we have exciting opportunities. Become part of a group that enables its customers to meet some of the greatest challenges in patient treatment and join us by applying for the Automation Engineer position.

What you will get

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance

What you will do

  • Routine sample preparation of drug product samples with an automated liquid handler as well as maintenance of automation systems
  • Design, develop, program, and implement automated solutions using various digital platforms (e.g. Microsoft 365 products)
  • Act as a subject matter expert (SME) in automation and digitalization to lead various workstreams
  • Participate in root cause analysis for automation-related issues and implement corrective and preventive actions
  • Ensure all automation activities comply with relevant GxP regulations, safety standards, and company policies
  • Engage directly with automation scientists and other stakeholders to propose and implement automated solutions accordingly

What we are looking for

  • Master Degree in Engineering, Computer Sciences, Life Sciences or a related field
  • Proficient programming skills in Python and other languages relevant to automation
  • Hands-on experience with liquid handlers is highly desired, e.g. experience with state-of-the-art liquid handler software/hardware such as Hamilton Vantage/STAR line or, Tecan Fluent/Evo
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively in a team environment
  • Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and troubleshooting skills
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken) is required. Any other languages are a plus
