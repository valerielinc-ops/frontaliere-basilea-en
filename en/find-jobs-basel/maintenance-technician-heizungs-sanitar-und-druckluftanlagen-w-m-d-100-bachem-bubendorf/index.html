Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in Technics & Maintenance, we are looking for a dedicated Maintenance Technician heating, sanitary and compressed air systems (m/f/d), 100%.

Their tasks

Operation & Responsibility

  • Active implementation of the role as asset owner
  • Operation and maintenance of heating and sanitary facilities (distribution systems, production facilities)
  • Ensuring plant availability and operational safety
  • Monitoring, control and documentation of installation operation

Maintenance & Disruption Management

  • Troubleshooting and troubleshooting of mechanical, electrical and control components
  • Implementation of maintenance and inspections according to the maintenance plan
  • Independent implementation of minor conversions and technical improvements
  • Remediation and documentation of faults at installations and individual equipment

Cooperation and projects

  • Cooperation with external service providers
  • Support for audits and official inspections
  • Cooperation in plant optimization and technical projects
  • Participation in the definition of maintenance measures

Organization and Security

  • Ensuring order and cleanliness in the allocated installation areas
  • Ensuring the availability of spare parts, materials and tools
  • Compliance with all relevant safety rules

Your profile

  • Completed training as maintenance specialist EFZ or equivalent training
  • Basic heating and/or sanitary engineering training or equivalent
  • Further training as a plant operator: in heating systems as well as further training in the field of maintenance is desirable
  • >4 years of professional experience in technical maintenance and experience in dealing with heating and sanitary facilities
  • Negotiable in German (verbal/written) and English orally desired
  • Secure MS-Office knowledge applied in the professional environment
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
