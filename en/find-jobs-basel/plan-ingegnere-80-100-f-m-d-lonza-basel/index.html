What you will do

  • Ensure the reliable operation and maintenance of laboratory equipment and facility systems through effective preventive maintenance programs.
  • Lead troubleshooting and root cause investigations for critical equipment failures.
  • Own and support Change Controls, CAPAs, Deviations, and Risk Assessments related to engineering systems

What we are looking for

  • Bachelor or Master Degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical, Biomedical, Industrial, or similar discipline)
  • Experience in engineering roles within the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, chemical, or other regulated industries
  • Knowledge of equipment lifecycle management, project execution, and maintenance practices
  • Experience working within GMP-regulated environments and quality systems
  • Familiarity with computerized systems, automation technologies, or laboratory equipment is an advantage
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
  • Excellent communication skills with excellent command of English and German, written and spoken
