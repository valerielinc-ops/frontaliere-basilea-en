What you'll be doing

  • Overall responsibility and center of competence for reinsurance treaty cessions in respect of the assigned business units and group protections.
  • Organizes, prepares and takes part in meetings with business units and reinsurance partners (broker and reinsurer)
  • Set up and manage reinsurance treaties, handle the reinsurance process with supporting systems and practices
  • Secure underwriting information (premiums, portfolio/risk profiles, claims, NatCat data in coordination with our Risk Assessment team)
  • Analyze and review the data and prepare the underwriting information for the applicable lines of business and programs
  • Develop and take care of relationships with business partners within and outside the Helvetia Group
  • Negotiate treaty terms and conditions with assigned business units and third party reinsurers
  • Account Handler for dedicated reinsurance contracts including administration and controlling of premium and claims accounting

What you'll bring to the role

  • University degree, preferable in economics or law or apprenticeship /trainee in (re)insurance or any other relevant qualification in (re)insurance
  • Experience in a similar position or as (Senior) Underwriter for reinsurance treaty business
  • Fluent in German and English, other languages are an asset. Ability to hold presentations to internal and external audience
  • Advanced analytical quantitative, as well as decision-making skills
  • Excellent negotiation and communication skills
  • Team player, able to cope with demanding situations and work with senior managers and key stakeholder within and outside Helvetia Baloise Group
