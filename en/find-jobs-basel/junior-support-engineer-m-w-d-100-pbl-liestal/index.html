ICT

In order to ensure the smooth operation of our clinical, nursing and administrative systems at all locations, we are looking for enthusiastic reinforcement for our well-established ICT support team!

Their tasks

  • You accept IT inquiries and disruption reports from our employees (doctors, nursing staff, administration) by telephone, e-mail and ticket system.
  • You analyze and fix hardware and software problems in first- and second-level support remotely or directly on the device.
  • You support our users directly on site at the various locations (e.g. hardware exchange, printer problems or the setup of workstations). They work closely with our external partners and ensure their optimal integration.
  • You are responsible for deploying, setting up and installing PCs, notebooks, medical peripherals and smartphones.
  • You manage accounts and permissions in the Active Directory and Microsoft 365 Admin Center (onboarding/offboarding of clinicians).

Your profile

  • Successfully completed vocational apprenticeship as a computer scientist/in EFZ (department of platform development or business informatics) or a comparable basic IT training.
  • High service orientation, patience and strong empathy in dealing with the various professional groups of a clinic company.
  • First practical experience in IT support as well as good knowledge of Windows clients and the Microsoft 365 product range.
  • You learn quickly, work exactly, keep an overview even in hectic situations and are a real team player.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing.

About us

The Psychiatry Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, daily clinical, home treatment and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. She supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of living, work and day-to-day planning (www.inclusioplus.ch). She also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Center for Work and Mental Health. At several locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, PBL employs around 1,300 people and treats, supervises and advises more than 15,000 patients per year (www.pbl.ch).
