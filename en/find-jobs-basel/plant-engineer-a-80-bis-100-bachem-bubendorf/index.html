Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are looking for a dedicated Plant Engineer (a), (80 to 100%) to strengthen our team in the Engineering department. The focus of this function is on the planning, coordination and tracking of maintenance, calibration and requalification work (WKR) as well as their GMP-compliant completion in SAP/CMMS. At the same time, you assume technical plant responsibility and help shape the sustainable further development of our production and process plants.

Their tasks

  • Focus WCR: Planning, coordination and timely tracking of WCR activities at production and process plants
  • Coordination of maintenance dates, plant availability and required resources with the participating bodies as well as organization and support of internal and external service providers in compliance with GMP, HSE, environmental and company specifications
  • Processing and tracking of WCR messages and maintenance orders in SAP/CMMS: agree priorities and processing statuses, track open points and ensure timely completion
  • Checking of WKR orders for complete feedback, test batches, usage decisions and documentation attachments as well as ensuring GMP-compliant documentation and proper order completion in SAP/CMMS
  • Maintenance and further development of SAP/CMMS master data and maintenance plans as well as participation in the optimization of maintenance strategies and intervals
  • Coordination of spare parts requirements and spare parts management and control of relevant operation and maintenance budgets
  • Technical responsibility for the integrity, availability and economic operational readiness of assigned assets throughout the lifecycle including lifecycle and obsolescence management
  • Analysis and correction of technical failures (troubleshooting) in cooperation with the participating specialist bodies as well as processing of technical deviations, change requests (MOC) and B2/B4 notifications in the CMMS
  • Planning and implementation of smaller engineering measures and plant adjustments from conception to required qualification (URS, IQ, OQ) to commissioning in compliance with budget, deadlines and technical requirements
  • Identification and implementation of technical optimizations and standardizations to increase plant availability, energy and cost efficiency and promote order and cleanliness (Good Housekeeping)

Your profile

  • Completed training with further training as a technician HF, Bachelor or Master in process engineering, chemical engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in the operation, maintenance or engineering of production and process plants, preferably in the pharmaceutical or chemical industry
  • Sound practical knowledge in maintenance and maintenance coordination as well as in technical operation and in the optimization of production and process plants
  • Technical understanding of process control technology and automation as well as experience with plant qualification (e.g. IQ/OQ) and smaller engineering measures
  • Experience in a GMP regulated environment and in the traceable testing and documentation of maintenance activities
  • Production experience is beneficial
  • Secure handling of SAP/CMMS, in particular in order processing, status tracking, master data maintenance and the documented completion of maintenance orders, as well as with MS Office
  • in German: fliessend

English: fluent

  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method with hands-on mentality as well as an understanding of plant availability, lifecycle and obsolescence topics
  • Strong organizational and coordination skills as well as clear communication in cooperation with different specialist offices and external service providers
  • Team-oriented, assertive personality with high personal responsibility and consistent follow-up of open tasks until complete completion
