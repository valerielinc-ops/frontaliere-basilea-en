Full description

To strengthen our team we are looking for a flexible, motivated personality for a versatile position as

Production Allrounder (plates)

Your remit

Tasks in the main area of plate production

  • deburring of implants according to current working instructions and quality specifications
  • Operation of plants and setting up of smaller process plants for the production of implants according to specifications
  • Complete manufacturing documentation according to valid documentation
  • Optical and mass checks on implants including documentation of results
  • Operating and supporting peripheral devices within the value chain
  • Ensuring the order, cleanliness and maintenance of the facilities and the work area
  • Active participation in KVP and 6S actions
  • Immediate reporting of product, process or quality deviations to the data center

Flexible support in other areas

  • Flexible support in other areas such as packaging and labelling, quality control, storage, etc., depending on current needs and operational priorities
  • Compliance with the area-specific safety, quality and GMP requirements, regardless of the respective field of application
  • Occasional, daily operations at the Umkirch site are also possible

Your qualifications

Requirements

  • Experience in the production environment, ideally in medical technology or in the quality environment
  • Careful, precise working methods and high quality awareness
  • High flexibility and willingness to work in different areas of responsibility
  • Teamwork skills and joy in cross-cutting cooperation
  • Reliability and a good understanding of specifications and documentation requirements
  • Flexibility in working time (e.g. shift or use times depending on the area) is required
  • Very good German in word and writing (B2), English is an advantage

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of osteosynthesis products in the area of facial skull and extremities. Medartis has committed itself to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and offering the best service for the benefit of the patient.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing international company with advanced social benefits.

We look forward to receiving your complete online application. Medartis
