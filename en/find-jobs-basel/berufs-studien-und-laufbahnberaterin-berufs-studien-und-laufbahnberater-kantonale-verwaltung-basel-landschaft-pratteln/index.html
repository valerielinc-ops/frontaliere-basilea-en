Department of Vocational Education, Secondary Schools and Higher Education / Career Center BL

The career centre (LBZ BL) in Pratteln, including mentoring and general further education, is a main department of the Department of Vocational Education, Secondary Schools and Higher Education (BMH). The LBZ BL advises and informs young people and adults on all questions of vocational, school and study choice as well as career planning and maintains a public information library. The career centre cooperates with schools, public training and partner institutions and conducts consultations and courses on behalf of third parties.
