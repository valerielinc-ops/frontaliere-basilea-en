We are looking for a detail-oriented and well-organized sales person with warehouse responsibilities to join our team in our Basel branch in Pratteln, Switzerland.   • Maintain a clean, organized and safe warehouse environment in compliance with all health and safety regulations
  • Assist customers in the sales area with product inquiries, recommendations and purchases
  • Accurately process transactions using point of sale (POS) systems
  • Monitor inventory levels and communicate replenishment needs to management
  • Collaborate with team members to achieve inventory and sales goals
  • Perform general warehouse maintenance and cleaning tasks
