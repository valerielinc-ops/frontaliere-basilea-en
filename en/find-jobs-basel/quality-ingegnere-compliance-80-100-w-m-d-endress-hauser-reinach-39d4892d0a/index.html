Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress happens by working together. As the global leader in measurement instrumentation, with over 17,000 employees, we shape the future in the field of process automation. Whether developing and realizing new technology as a team, collaborating to build instrumentation, or strengthening vital relationships with countless global industries, we work to create trusted relationships that help everyone thrive. As a family-owned company, we look beyond short-term moves and work to create a vision that is forward-thinking for our people, our clients, and the world at large.

We are looking for a Quality Engineer Compliance (f/m/d) to join us and play a key role in strengthening quality and regulatory compliance across our production environment at Endress+Hauser Flowtec AG. As part of Flowtec, one of our global production centers for flow measurement technology, you will work close to manufacturing and engineering, ensuring that our products meet international standards and can be trusted by customers worldwide.

What is the role about?

Do you enjoy working close to production while keeping quality and compliance at the center of your work? Are you motivated to translate complex norms and regulations into practical, value-adding solutions? Do you like sharing your expertise and collaborating with colleagues across functions and locations?

In this role, you combine technical expertise with a hands-on mindset to ensure that standards are applied effectively, audits are conducted professionally, and continuous improvement is embedded in our production processes.

Which tasks will you perform?

You will

  • plan, conduct, and support internal and external audits in the production environment.
  • analyze non-conformities, define appropriate corrective measures, and ensure their effective implementation.
  • apply your solid understanding of relevant norms and standards in a practical, production-oriented way.
  • actively share your know-how within the global network and support colleagues on regulatory and compliance topics.
  • collaborate closely with Production, R&D, Engineering, and Quality teams.
  • travel occasionally as part of your role, approximately 10%.

What do we expect from you?

You

  • hold a completed university or FH degree, for example in a technical feeld like Engineering or Quality Assurance, Compliance, or an other comparable field.
  • bring a strong technical background with several years of experience in Quality Assurance within an industrial setting, ideally in production and audit environments.
  • have very good knowledge of relevant norms and regulations, in particular pressure regulations, hygienic applications, Ex regulations, and ideally seal and marine approvals.
  • have experience implementing standards and norms in existing production processes and conducting assessments and audits.
  • work in a process-oriented, pragmatic way with a hands-on mindset and enjoy cross-functional collaboration.
  • have proven know-how in building, further developing, and closing quality and compliance gaps.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-owned, highly committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and development opportunities, internal knowledge transfer, and e-learning.
  • We support a healthy work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We provide support for sustainable mobility solutions such as public transportation.
  • We support family-friendly policies, including daycare places and childcare services during holidays.

How do you apply?

Agencies, HR service providers, or similar entities are kindly requested to refrain from unsolicited submission of profiles and inquiries regarding potential collaboration. Currently, we do not engage with external service providers for this role. Any unsolicited applications will be disregarded and deleted without further consideration. We appreciate your understanding.
