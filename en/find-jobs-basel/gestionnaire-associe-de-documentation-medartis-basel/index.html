Full description

To strengthen our Marketing & Medical Education team in Basel, we are looking for a committed personality as an Associate Documentation Manager. In this role, you make a significant contribution to ensuring that our product and marketing documentation meets the highest quality and compliance requirements. They accompany documents throughout their entire life cycle, ensure their timeliness and availability and work closely with marketing, medical affairs, regulatory affairs and other interdisciplinary teams. In addition, you support the continuous digitalization and optimization of our documentation processes. Do you want to make an important contribution to the success of innovative medical technology with your structured working method and your quality awareness? We look forward to your application.

Associate Documentation Manager

Your responsibilities

  • They support the management of global product and marketing documentation throughout the document lifecycle.
  • They maintain document management systems, databases and document repositories and ensure the completeness, traceability and timeliness of the documentation.
  • They review and revise documents in terms of comprehensibility, consistency, language, layout, and compliance with regulatory requirements and corporate design guidelines.
  • They work closely with marketing, medical affairs, regulatory affairs and other internal stakeholders to ensure high-quality and timely documentation.
  • They support the creation and maintenance of order information, product catalogues and the communication of documentation-relevant changes.
  • They coordinate the management of printed documentation, including print orders and stocks.
  • You actively participate in the digitization and continuous optimization of documentation processes, systems and workflows.
  • They make an important contribution to compliance with internal quality standards and regulatory requirements and ensure efficient documentation management.

Your qualifications

  • Completed technical or commercial training with further training in marketing, communication, technical documentation or a comparable specialisation.
  • Two to five years of professional experience in medical technology, pharmaceutical industry or a comparable regulated environment.
  • Experience in technical documentation, document management or regulatory documentation processes.
  • Strong quality awareness and a structured, precise and reliable way of working.
  • Experience working with document management systems as well as very good knowledge of Microsoft Office 365
  • Knowledge of Adobe Acrobat/InDesign as well as TYPO3 or PLM systems is an advantage.
  • Analytical thinking skills and an independent, solution-oriented and well-organized way of working.
  • Team-oriented personality with strong communication skills and enjoy working together in an international environment.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous.

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.
