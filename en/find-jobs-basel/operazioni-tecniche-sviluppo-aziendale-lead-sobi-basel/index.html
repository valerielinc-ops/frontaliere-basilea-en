Full description

About the role In this role, you will lead the Technical Operations contribution across Sobi’s business development lifecycle, from initial evaluation and due diligence through transaction execution to successful integration into Sobi. As part of the Technical Operations Strategy & Acceleration team, you will be the key link between Technical Operations, Search & Evaluation and Business Development. You will bring together experts across Quality, CMC, manufacturing, sourcing and supply chain, translate complex technical information into clear risks, opportunities and recommendations that support Sobi’s business decisions. You will work at the intersection of science, operations and business, collaborating closely with senior leaders, subject matter experts and external partners to evaluate opportunities, shape transactions and translate successful deals into executable operational plans. You will report to the Head of Technical Operations Strategy & Acceleration and be based at our Stockholm headquarters or global hub in Basel, with a hybrid working model that combines collaboration and flexibility. What you will do

  • Lead the Technical Operations contribution to business evaluations and due diligence, bringing together experts across quality, CMC, manufacturing, sourcing and supply chain
  • Turn technical, operational and financial insights into business recommendations, including COGS modelling, investment requirements and development or life-cycle management costs.
  • Represent Technical Operations in cross-functional deal teams and governance forums, providing clear recommendations to senior decision makers.
  • Shape the technical, manufacturing and supply aspects of transactions working with Business Development, Legal and Finance on transaction related agreements.
  • Lead Technical Operations integration planning and execution following a transaction, from pre-close planning through stabilisation andhandover to the line organisation
  • Own and continuously improve the Technical Operations business development and integration framework, tools and ways of working and lead selected strategic cross-functional initiatives

About you

You are a strategic and hands-on leader who performs well in a fast-paced environment and is comfortable making sound judgements with incomplete information. You are intellectually curious and build trust readily with specialists, senior leaders and external partners. You challenge assumptions constructively, communicate a clear position, remain composed under pressure and take full ownership of your work. You handle confidential information with integrity and discretion. What you bring

  • University degree in pharmacy, chemistry, biotechnology, engineering, business or a related field
  • Typically 10+ years in the pharmaceutical or biotech industry across development, CMC, manufacturing, quality, supply or commercialisation, with a strong understanding of cGMP manufacturing of biologics and/or small molecules
  • Proven experience leading complex business evaluations, due diligence, integrations or strategic initiatives in a global matrix organisation
  • Strong business acumen and financial understanding (COGS, CAPEX, P&L, NPV); experience with COGS models and transaction agreements is a plus
  • Excellent analytical, influencing and negotiation skills, clear and concise executive communication, and fluent English

Please note: the role may involve international travel and occasional work outside normal business hours, and requires the handling of commercially sensitive information in line with Sobi policies and governance.
