Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position The possibility At our location in Basel, we offer an exciting position as a shift worker in an innovative and ultra-modern production environment. In the Basel Drug Substance division, we are responsible for the production of life-saving drugs for cancer therapy in our highly automated production facilities for Single Use Technology (SUT) and Antibody Drug Conjugates (ADC). Our demanding manufacturing processes are carried out in strict compliance with Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP) and combine the latest technologies with the highest quality standards. The field of activity includes the production of culture media and buffers, the carrying out of cell cultures, the purification of biological active substances and the production and processing of ADC products in a highly regulated production environment. As an operator, you work across operations within the SUT and ADC operations and actively contribute to providing innovative therapies for patients. You will operate and monitor highly automated systems, ensure compliance with quality and safety requirements and work in a dynamic, interdisciplinary team.

Your tasks as a Process Technician include in particular

  • Carrying out biotechnological production processes (upstream, downstream, ADC) in compliance with GMP requirements
  • Production and management of media, buffers, samples and consumable and single-use materials
  • Operation, monitoring and maintenance of equipment including fault and alarm management
  • Ensuring GMP-compliant documentation and document control and closure
  • Monitoring of the qualifying asset status and material management support
  • Collection, documentation and reporting of discrepancies and specific occurrences
  • Training of employees and ensuring safety, order and cleanliness in the work area

Who you are

  • Completed vocational training as a chemical and pharmaceutical technologist or comparable training
  • Sound understanding of GMP, ideally with experience in pharmaceutical production or biotechnology
  • Good knowledge in MS Office (Word, Excel) and ideally in Veeva and MES
  • Experience in handling automated production scale equipment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • English skills are beneficial
  • Readiness for shift work
