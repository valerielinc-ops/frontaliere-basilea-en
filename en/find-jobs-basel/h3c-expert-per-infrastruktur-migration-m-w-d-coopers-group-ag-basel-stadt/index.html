Full description

For a demanding infrastructure transformation project for our customer in Basel, we are looking for an experienced:n H3C Expert. The project will replace an existing server, storage and virtualization landscape with modern H3C solutions. You play a central role in the planning, architecture and implementation of the new infrastructure and accompany the migration of existing systems into the new target environment.

Your tasks

  • Conception and design of the future H3C infrastructure
  • Planning and implementation of migration projects in the area of compute, storage and virtualization
  • Implementation and configuration of H3C UniServer, UniStor and CAS environments
  • Migration of virtual machines and data from existing platforms (e.g. VMware vSphere or Microsoft Hyper-V)
  • Building and optimizing high availability and disaster recovery solutions
  • Integration of backup, monitoring, active directory, network and SAN solutions
  • Support for sizing, performance optimization and capacity planning
  • Technical advice to project management and stakeholders
  • Documentation of architecture and operational processes

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as an Infrastructure Architect, Systems Engineer or Datacenter Consultant
  • Demonstrate practical experience with at least one of the following H3C products: H3C UniServer, H3C UniStor, H3C CAS, H3C UIS, H3C CloudOS
  • Experience in infrastructure migration projects and the transformation of existing IT landscapes
  • In-depth knowledge of server, storage and virtualization environments
  • Experience with VMware vSphere, Hyper-V or similar platforms
  • Good knowledge of storage protocols such as FC, iSCSI and NFS
  • Experience with clustering, high-availability and disaster recovery concepts
  • Knowledge in the integration of network, SAN, backup and monitoring solutions
  • H3C certifications are beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
