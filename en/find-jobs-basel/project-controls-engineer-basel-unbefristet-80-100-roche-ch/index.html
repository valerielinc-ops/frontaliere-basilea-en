The Position You are part of Project Delivery within Business Solutions. As a Project Cost Engineer (PCE), you support our project leaders in the financial management of investment projects. You take responsibility for cost control, forecasting and reporting, bring your technical know-how to our existing system landscape and actively promote the use of innovative, AI-supported tools for process optimization.

The possibility With this responsible task you support our team with the following main tasks:

  • Budget monitoring & cost control: Continuous monitoring of the released project budget, comparison of target/actual values for the early detection of deviations and management of contingencies.
  • Cost Forecasting & Cash Flow: Preparation and updating of rolling cost and cash outflow forecasts including preparation of decision-making bases for steering bodies (e.g. Steering Committees).
  • Cost Breakdown: Support for CAPEX cost estimation, cost breakdown structure and regular cost reviews in the project team.
  • Change Management: Operational management of the commercial project change process (change orders, follow-up review, maintenance of the change register and adjustment of budget baselines).
  • Project reporting & KPI determination: Timely preparation of standardized status and progress reports (e.g. monthly project management reports) as well as determination of relevant project KPIs.
  • Audit & Procurement Support: Review and correct accounting of invoices and supplements in close coordination with procurement and financial functions.
  • Management of portfolio systems: Secure operational use of existing project and portfolio management tools (e.g. CORA, CPPO, SAP, TMT, myBuy) as contact person in the project.
  • Impulses through AI tools & digitalization: Evaluation and practical implementation of modern, AI-supported solutions for automating routine analyses, data preparation and increasing efficiency in controlling.
  • Interface Coordination & Continuous Improvement: Active cooperation with project management, purchasing and finance as well as participation in lessons learned workshops for the continuous development of controlling standards.

Who you are

In order to succeed in this exciting role, you ideally bring the following qualifications:

  • Education: Successfully completed studies in the field of industrial engineering, business administration, construction/process engineering, natural sciences or a comparable qualification.
  • Professional experience: > 3 years of experience in Project Controls in small and medium-sized projects or Multi Project Controls / Portfolio Controlling of several small projects, ideally in the life sciences or pharmaceutical plant engineering environment.
  • System & Tool Competence: In-depth knowledge of MS Excel/Google Workspace
  • Experience with ERP and portfolio systems (such as SAP, CORA, CPPO or similar) is an advantage.
  • AI affinity: enthusiasm for digital trends and concrete interest in integrating AI-supported tools (e.g. for data analysis, reporting or process automation) into everyday controlling.
  • Working method: high affinity for numbers, very good analytical skills and a solution-oriented hands-on mentality as well as initiative and good communication skills
  • Languages: Very good German and fluent English skills in words and writing for work in interdisciplinary interfaces.
