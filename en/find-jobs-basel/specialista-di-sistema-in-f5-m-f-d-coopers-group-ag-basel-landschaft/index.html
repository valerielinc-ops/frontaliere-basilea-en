Full description

For our customer from the public administration in Liestal, we are looking for a:n experienced:n system specialist:in F5/IAM

Your tasks

  • Operation and further development of F5 BIG-IP with ASM / Advanced WAF, APM and LTM
  • Administration of WAF policies, access policies, SSO, MFA and SSL/TLS
  • Operation and further development of Keycloak and Nevis
  • Integration of applications using OIDC, OAuth 2.0 and SAML 2.0
  • Integration of Active Directory / LDAP as well as role and authorization concepts
  • Troubleshooting, security analysis and releases, updates and patches

Your profile

  • Several years of experience with F5 BIG-IP, in particular ASM / Advanced WAF, APM and LTM
  • Several years of experience in the IAM environment and practical experience with Keycloak
  • Sound knowledge of OIDC, OAuth 2.0, SAML 2.0, JWT and LDAP
  • Experience with SSL/TLS, TCP/IP, DNS, Reverse Proxies and Linux
  • Experience with highly available platforms as well as security and log management
  • Advantages: Experience with Nevis, iRules, iControl REST API, Ansible, SCIM and Docker / Kubernetes
  • Desirable: F5, IAM, security or network certifications (e.g. F5-CA, F5-CTS, CISSP, CISM, CCNA/CCNP)
  • Analytical, independent and solution-oriented working method as well as a strong security awareness
  • Readiness for piketting or standby
