Full description

The first impression counts! As a customer consultant: in Sales Zone you are responsible for the professional reception and the first-class advice of our customers. You convince in direct customer contact through your competent advice and your enthusiasm for our smart services and products. Whether in person or by phone: With your expertise and your customer orientation, you make complicated things easy for our customers.

You can do that.

  • You are responsible for expert advice and sales in the areas of numbers, savings/investment and simple pension advice
  • You recognize potential and develop solutions that are tailored to the individual wishes, goals and life situations of our customers.
  • You convince the customers of the benefit of a consultation and enthusiastically reconcile appointments with the customer consultants:innen (also via outbound telephony)

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic commercial training EFZ or comparable training
  • Experience in personal customer service in a sales zone
  • Completed SAQ certification or willingness to complete it
  • Enjoying the active telephone customer acquisition, motivated and collegial personality with Drive
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of French in words and writing. Further language skills are advantageous
