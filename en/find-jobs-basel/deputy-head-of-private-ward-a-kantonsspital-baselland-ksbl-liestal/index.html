Full description

Entrance by 1. March 2027 or by appointment · Liestal, Care · Job reference 13521 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Support of station management in planning, organisation and management tasks
  • Co-responsibility for implementing the performance targets on the station with around 18 beds
  • Co-responsibility for ensuring the quality of care as well as for the implementation and sustainable anchoring of the Lean- Hospital philosophy
  • Member of the Station Management Board in the sense of shared governance
  • Working in direct care in a motivated team with a total of 22 employees
  • Leading a zone with the assigned patient group

Requirements

  • Basis: Diploma as nursing specialist (HF, FH or equivalent)
  • Glad: Experience in an acute operation
  • Professional experience in a leadership position beneficial or willing to undergo leadership training
  • Very happy: desire to take on management tasks and to move forward with a positive basic attitude
  • And also: Empathy and interest in organizing, communicating and making a difference
  • Absolutely: Joy in a varied and lively hospital everyday life

All-round care: discounted day care on site, full parenting allowances, paid short absences in case of emergency care
