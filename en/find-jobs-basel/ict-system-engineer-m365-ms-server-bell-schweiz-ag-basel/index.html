Full description

Are you passionate about modern Microsoft cloud architectures, identity management and collaboration services? Then actively shape our central IT infrastructure! We are a dedicated IT team with a passion for reliable services and innovative cloud solutions. For the further expansion and operation of our infrastructure, we are looking for a professionally experienced personality who will take care of, develop and future-proof our central services.

We can only consider applicants who are entitled to work in Switzerland (EU/EFTA). We only consider direct applications.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working Time Model: Hybrid Working Time by Vote
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Alsace Road 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • Support, maintenance and development of the entire mail infrastructure (Exchange Online / Hybrid) as well as the Microsoft 365 services and collaboration tools.
  • Administration, monitoring and optimization of our Microsoft Azure Cloud Services and provisioning and maintenance of Azure VMs.
  • Reliable operation and further development of the Active Directory (AD / Entra ID) including the associated services (DNS, DHCP, GPO, certificates).
  • Manage the VMware environment and ensure smooth backups using Veeam.
  • Rapid analysis and resolution of complex fault images (2nd/3rd level) as well as support of the 1st level support in the event of escalations.
  • Management and cooperation in migration and rollout projects as well as standardization of processes using PowerShell scripting.
  • Occasional takeover of the piquetting service (about 1x per quarter) and punctual travel activities within Europe.

Requirements

  • Completed training in the IT sector (e.g. computer scientist EFZ / computer scientist system integration) or a comparable qualification.
  • In-depth practical experience in the operation and support of Microsoft 365 and mail services (Exchange).
  • Solid knowledge of supporting Azure online services.
  • Secure command of Active Directory and the associated network and security services.
  • Experience with Windows Servers, VMware virtualization, Veeam backup and PowerShell automation.
  • Current Microsoft certificates (e.g. MS-102: Microsoft 365 Administrator or AZ-104: Azure Administrator) are a big plus.
  • Independent, structured and service-oriented working methods, strong team spirit and readiness for emergency interventions within the framework of the Pikettdienst.
