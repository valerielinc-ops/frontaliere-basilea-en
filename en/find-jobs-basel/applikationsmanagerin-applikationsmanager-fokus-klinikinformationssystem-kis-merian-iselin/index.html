Full description

Role - Applications Manager (Focus on Clinical Information System (KIS)) - 80%-100%, starting July 1, 2026 or by agreement Description Merian Iselin Clinic is a modern private clinic with a public mandate in Basel. Our strengths lie in the fields of surgery, orthopedics, and urology.

  • We offer significant activity in a family atmosphere where people are at the center. To strengthen our ICT team, we are looking for an engaged and solution-oriented person as Applications Manager with a focus on supporting our clinical information system M-KIS (Meierhofer). Main responsibilities
  • Ensure the stable and user-oriented operation of our clinical information system (M-KIS, Meierhofer) and its development
  • Proactively manage processes close to KIS
  • Ensure smooth system use in daily clinical life
  • Analyze and resolve disturbances and anomalous behavior in KIS
  • Coordinate between internal departments and second and third-level support (suppliers)
  • Plan and implement releases, tests, and rollouts in close collaboration with departments
  • Collect, analyze, and specify functional requirements and implement them in collaboration with the producer
  • Formulate and follow up on change requests
  • Manage and moderate key user groups
  • Create and maintain system and process documentation (Confluence)
  • Create training materials and conduct user training sessions
  • Advise and support departments in optimal KIS use
  • Proactively inform users about news, maintenance, and outages
  • Requirements
  • Degree in computer science (FH/HF) or similar
  • Several years of experience in managing the applications of a KIS, preferably with M-KIS (Meierhofer)
  • Good understanding of clinical processes (e.g., patient administration, performance recording, documentation)
  • Experience in collaborating with software providers and release management
  • Analytical and structured working method and high service orientation
  • Enjoy working with medical and administrative departments
  • Excellent knowledge of German, knowledge of English would be an advantage
  • What we offer
  • Varied and responsible role in a collaborative ICT team with quick decisions and constructive collaboration
  • Lively, family-friendly, and stimulating work environment, modern infrastructure, training opportunities, and attractive working conditions
