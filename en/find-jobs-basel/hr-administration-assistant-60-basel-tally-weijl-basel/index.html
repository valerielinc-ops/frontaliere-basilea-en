HR Administration Assistant (60%) – Basel

We're here to make you feel sexy, empowered & UNSTOPPABLE. Founded in 1984, TALLY WEiJL has been on a crazy journey to celebrate fashion, independence, fun, love, & community for close to 40 years. Today we’re present in 35 markets with about 400 stores, online presence, and 3m+ fans across social channels.

About the Position

We are looking for an HR Administration Assistant to work 60%, starting on 1 December 2026. You will be based in Basel, supporting our team in Germany with HR administration in accordance with German employment law.

This is an entry-level position: no previous experience is required.

Your Responsibilities

• Support the preparation and administration of employment contracts. • Handle administrative tasks related to employee joiners and leavers. • Record and update sick leave and absence information. • Maintain accurate employee records and support day-to-day HR administration.

Your Profile • Fluent German is essential. • Strong attention to detail and good organisational skills. • A reliable approach to handling confidential information. • Willingness to learn and work as part of a team. • No previous HR experience required; applicants looking for their first job are welcome.

What We Offer

• A 60% position based in Basel. • A start date of 1 December 2026. • Practical experience across HR administration. • Guidance and support as you take your first steps in HR.
