Full description

Group leader Manufaktur (m/f/d) 80% inclusioplus work The inclusioplus business unit of the Psychiatry Baselland bundles the services of disability assistance. inclusioplus offers various forms of assisted work of different intensively supervised living arrangements in Liestal and in Niederdorf, ambulatory living support as well as meaningful daily arrangements for people with cognitive, psychological and multiple impairments. In order to strengthen our team in the Manufaktur integration company, we are looking for a committed and experienced personality with experience working with people with mental disabilities. In our manufactory, we produce cards and soaps, among other things, by careful handwork and also take on individual commissioned work. Production is embedded in an accompanying working environment that is geared towards participation, development and stabilization.

Your tasks of professional responsibility

  • Management and inclusive further development of the soap division
  • Planning, organisation and coordination of production processes
  • Self-employed and structured work instructions of 4-8 employees with IV pension
  • Quality control and participation in product development and design
  • Cooperation in the visibility of products (e.g. product distribution) Educational responsibility
  • Accompaniment and promotion of people with mental disabilities in everyday work
  • Reference work for around 12 employees: location determinations, funding planning and implementation
  • Case management in cooperation with internal and external specialist bodies
  • Documentation according to cantonal specifications
  • Cooperation in an interdisciplinary team

Your profile

  • Completed training as a worker/work teacher, social teacher/social teacher HF/FH or professional supervision EFZ
  • craftsmanship or technical interest and/or training, or Professional experience in production
  • Ideally, professional experience accompanying people with mental impairment
  • Resilient, empathetic and structured personality with high social competence and high quality awareness
  • Experience with disability insurance measures is advantageous
  • Dealing with MS Office
  • Very good written expressive capacity for reports and documentation
