Full description

Do you not just want to use machines, but actively contribute to creating real moments of enjoyment from high-quality milk? Then you are exactly right with us! In the Regio Molkerei of Basel, you will find a versatile working environment in which traditional craftsmanship meets modern production technology. Together with your team, you ensure that our dairy products are produced in the best quality every day. You can contribute your technical skills, take responsibility and actively shape the future of our dairy.

You can do that.

  • In the middle, instead of just doing so: You accompany the entire manufacturing process from the raw material to the finished product.
  • With your technical skills, you control and monitor our production facilities and ensure a smooth process.
  • Whether past milk, yogurt, cheese, cream, fondue or raclette: Quality comes first for you in the production of our diverse dairy products.
  • You ensure compliance with our high quality and hygiene standards at all times.
  • Your ideas are in demand! Together with the team, you will continuously develop our specialties.

You bring that with you

  • A completed training EFZ in the food sector (e.g. dairy technologist:in, food technologist:in or baker:in) forms your professional basis.
  • You have good knowledge of German and can communicate safely in everyday work.
  • Technical processes inspire you and the handling of production plants gives you pleasure.
  • Flexibility and the willingness to shift work round off your profile.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
