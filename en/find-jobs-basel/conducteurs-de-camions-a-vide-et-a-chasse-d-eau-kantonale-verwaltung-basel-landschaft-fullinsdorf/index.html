Full description

Tasks

  • Driving and operating the vehicles up to 40 tonnes including their special bodies (recycling and recycled) Suction vehicle
  • Responsible for the professional maintenance of special vehicles such as safety and functional checks, repairs, etc.
  • Operation, maintenance and cleaning of our mixed water basins, sewage pumps and our sewer network for optimal and safe operation
  • Process control system monitoring of installations
  • Perform periodic checks, detect faults and take the necessary measures to correct them
  • Maintenance and repair work as well as support for major revision and conversion work
  • Logging of all work and events in the maintenance system
  • Regular provision of cooking services

Requirements

  • Completed craftsmanship training with appropriate professional experience
  • Ideally experience in the repair and maintenance of wastewater pumps
  • Readiness for orders in shafts and channels
  • Team-orientedness, organisational ability and IT user knowledge
  • Willingness to provide cooking services and work location reachable within 30 minutes
  • Category C driving licence with multi-year experience
