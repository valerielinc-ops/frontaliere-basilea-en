Full description

As a Logistics & Warehouse Compliance Specialist, you will ensure compliance with all GxP compliance requirements in the area of Logistic Solutions.

In addition to the logistics processes, this also includes ensuring compliance requirements for the global active ingredient warehouse in Basel and processing deviations and changes in the GxP environment. In cooperation with our quality functions, you will form the interface to the business functions as well as to our customers and partners.

Location: Basel

Duration: 12 months/possible extension

The perfect candidate

You are a positive-thinking, integrity-oriented and self-reflective personality with strong team skills. In this sense, you bring an open and collaborative attitude and mindset that is needed for successful work in your team and across departments. You trust yourself and others, take responsibility and live an open speak-up and error culture. Your solution-oriented attitude and "can-do attitude" determine your daily actions.

Ideally, you have several years of professional experience in the GxP sector, preferably in the pharmaceutical industry or pharmaceutical logistics, as well as a quick grasp of things. You recognize connections and are resilient and have good assertiveness, negotiation skills and a high level of teamwork.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Independent reading of transport temperature data from incoming goods and checking checklists for correct completion
  • Checking of all incoming goods for the specified transport temperature
  • Support of the Business Circles of Logistic Solutions in all GxP issues
  • Take ownership or responsibility for handling variances and changes in Veeva
  • Proactively participate in process optimizations and quality improvements

Must Haves

  • Completed vocational training or a completed bachelor's/master's degree, ideally in the field of the pharmaceutical industry or pharmaceutical logistics
  • Ideally 3-5 years of professional experience in the GMP / GDP area
  • Experience in GxP deviation and change management
  • Experience in processing GxP documents
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
