Role overview

Bed management functions as a central point for all stationary patient receptions and internal relocations within the Claraspital and the USB, with the exception of patient receptions in highly installed or specialized beds (e.g.

INT, IMC, SU, NCW, isolation station).

It acts as a contact point for all departments of the USB, for all bed stations of the Claraspital as well as for external assignees.
