Do you have experience in the field of hydraulic systems, do you like to work independently and give an active hand in the yard? Whether it’s new constructions, renovations or modernization of existing facilities, thanks to your technical expertise, you will ensure that all installation operations are performed according to art and reliably. Shipments

  • Installation of pipes for cold and hot water and sewer systems
  • Execution of hydraulic works in newly built buildings and renovations
  • Installation of sinks, toilets, showers, bath tubs and taps
  • Laying, connecting and fixing of different pipe systems
  • Assistance in repair and rehabilitation work
  • Performance of control and operation within your qualifications
  • Work execution according to installation schemes and technical specifications

Profile requested

  • Manual skills and technical skills
  • Knowledge of the most common hydraulic materials and installation systems

  • Accurate, reliable and team-oriented work style
  • Category B driving licence is preferential title
