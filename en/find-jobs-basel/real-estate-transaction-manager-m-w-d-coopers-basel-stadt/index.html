Full description

For our customers in Basel, we are looking for an experienced Senior Real Estate Transaction Manager to support demanding purchase and sales projects in the Swiss real estate market. In this versatile role, you take on the independent management of real estate transactions throughout the entire life cycle.

Your tasks

  • Identification and preliminary examination of investment opportunities in the Swiss real estate market
  • Preparation and submission of non-binding and binding purchase offers
  • Implementation and coordination of due diligence checks
  • Analysis, valuation and plausibility checking of real estate investments
  • Preparation of decision-making bases and panel motions
  • Conduct of contract and sales contract negotiations
  • Compilation, preparation and examination of object documents within the framework of vendor due diligence
  • Organisation, maintenance and maintenance of data rooms
  • Tendering, mandatement and management of external brokers and advisors
  • Coordination with notaries, landlords and other external stakeholders
  • Ensuring full documentation and user-friendly reporting
  • Independent management and control of purchase and sales projects throughout the entire transaction process
  • Close cooperation with internal departments and decision-making bodies
  • Responsibility for timely and high-quality implementation of real estate transactions
  • Cooperation in market, location and investment analyses to identify new opportunities

Your profile

  • University degree in business, architecture, jurisprudence or a comparable field of study
  • Additional training in real estate is beneficial
  • Several years of professional experience (at least 3 years) in Real Estate Transaction Management
  • Experience in real estate transactions and direct investments
  • Sound knowledge of the Swiss real estate market
  • Experience in property valuation and in the real estate advisory environment
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Strong analytical skills and entrepreneurial thinking skills
  • Experience in managing and managing transaction projects
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office, especially Excel, Word and PowerPoint
  • Practical experience with Wüest Dimensions and Immopac as well as knowledge of GaraioREM
  • Negotiation security in dealing with internal and external stakeholders
  • Experience with other real estate, valuation or research tools is advantageous
  • Affinity for digital working methods and AI applications is beneficial
  • High personal responsibility as well as strong communication and assertiveness
  • Professional appearance and safe handling of demanding stakeholders in the real estate environment
  • Fluent knowledge of German and good knowledge of English and French
