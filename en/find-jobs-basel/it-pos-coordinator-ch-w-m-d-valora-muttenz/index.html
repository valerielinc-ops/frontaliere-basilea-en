Full description

As an IT POS Coordinator, you are the central interface between our stores, business areas and IT. You ensure that our POS solutions are stable, efficient and user-friendly in day-to-day business. Tasks you will like

  • You are the central contact person for all POS and Store IT topics and combine the requirements of our stores and B2C organization with the responsible IT teams.
  • You take requirements from the store environment, evaluate and prioritize them together with the relevant stakeholders and translate business requirements into clear IT and technical requirements.
  • In the event of incidents and service interruptions, you coordinate the teams and partners involved and ensure a fast, pragmatic and sustainable solution.
  • You will analyze recurring faults and vulnerabilities along the customer journey and derive concrete measures to continuously improve the stability, service quality and usability of our POS systems.
  • You actively promote the further development of our business and IT processes in POS Service Management and ensure a transparent prioritization of requirements.
  • You support NPS (Net Promoter Score) initiatives in the operational business and thus contribute to a positive customer experience and long-term customer loyalty.
  • You accompany POS rollouts, store openings and other store IT projects from a business perspective and ensure IT and POS readiness.
  • You coordinate and accompany piloting of new technologies and applications in the store environment and ensure that new solutions are successfully transferred to a stable operation.
  • You work closely with Store Operations, Construction/Expansion, POS Product Teams, IT Service Center, Infrastructure & Network Teams and external service providers.
  • With your focus on business and technology, you ensure that our IT services create measurable added value for stores, employees and customers. Requirements that you bring
  • You have several years of experience in the retail IT environment, ideally with a focus on POS systems, store technology or comparable solutions.
  • You bring practical experience in store rollouts, service delivery or IT operations.
  • You have a good technical understanding and can take the perspective of the business at the same time.
  • You enjoy structuring requirements, bringing together different interests and driving forward topics independently.
  • You work in a solution-oriented, pragmatic and structured manner and keep track of critical incidents.
  • You communicate clearly and appropriately and feel comfortable in an interface role between business, stores, IT and external partners.
  • You have a good understanding of service quality and a high affinity for customer experience and user experience.
  • You take responsibility for your topics and convince through a hands-on mentality as well as a pronounced service and customer orientation. Benefits We Offer You
  • A dynamic, international working environment with plenty of room for ideas and creative participation
  • Various development and further education opportunities, including through our internal Valora Academy and live training courses
  • An uncomplicated corporate culture with direct exchange and a lively du-culture that promotes innovation and agility.
  • Exciting projects and varied tasks with a great deal of room for manoeuvre and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Enjoy attractive employee discounts at Valora formats and selected partners in the fields of leisure, sports, health and mobility
  • Attractive employment conditions with own pension fund and three flexibly selectable savings plans
  • Flexible working hours with flexibility and the possibility to work in the home office
