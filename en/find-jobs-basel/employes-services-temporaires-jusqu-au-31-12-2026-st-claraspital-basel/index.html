Full description

<b>Do you want to work in a modern, dynamic environment and make a real difference with your service? The Claraspital in Basel is looking for a motivated personality who contributes with commitment, heart and passion to the well-being of our guests and patients. Come to us and support us in our various service areas!</b>

Tasks

  • Service in the Personal Restaurant & Café Chiara: You look after our employees and external guests with a warm, professional service. From coffee in the morning to lunch – They make everyone feel comfortable all around.
  • Catering Assistance & Guest Relation: Taking care of our patients as a contact person They are not only concerned about individual meal wishes, but also about all other non-medical concerns that make the stay more pleasant. They collect feedback and thus guarantee personal, tailor-made care.
  • Care and support: In close cooperation with patients, you take care of their needs with empathy. This is not only about the food, but also about the small things that make the stay easier and more pleasant.
  • Hygiene and Occupational Safety: You are responsible for complying with our high hygiene and occupational safety standards in order to ensure that patients and employees are safe and well cared for at all times.

Requirements

  • Completed gastronomy or hotel training as well as in-depth professional experience in service in the upscale gastronomy or Hotel industry
  • Strong service orientation and excellent manners
  • Passion for gastronomy and a keen sense of detail
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage
  • Interest in dietetics and empathetic treatment of patients, some of whom are seriously ill
  • Secure handling of computer applications, in particular Word, Excel and PowerPoint

We offer

  • 28-33 paid leave days per year
  • Extensive opportunities for further education and training, e.g. own seminar program with a wide range of educational opportunities
  • Occupational health management with preventive measures such as ergonomic advice in the workplace
  • Part-time work and possibility of job sharing also in management functions
  • Bright, friendly rooms with modern infrastructure
  • Strong values and actively lived corporate culture
  • Characterisation and participation of the Claraspital by the employees

The Claraspital, the largest private hospital in Basel, is a university teaching and research hospital with a full service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and a supra-regional broadcast of its centers "Bäu" and "Tumor" as well as its special fields. In the Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and humanly. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously nurturing and developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria developed together "together - competently - responsibly".
