4132 Muttenz (Hybrid) Building Technology

  • Permanent position

Hybrid Salary range 90,000 - 110,000 CHF

01 Future employer

For our client in the Basel area, an innovative company that offers solutions for the efficient, sustainable and comfortable control of building technologies, we are looking for an experienced project manager in building automation. Are you an experienced, solution-oriented professional who independently manages projects in the field of building automation and leads them to success together with internal and external partners? Then do not hesitate and seize the opportunity to embark on a new career path.

02 Your task

  • Management and coordination of projects in the field of building automation, from the quotation phase through commissioning and handover
  • Coordination of all trades involved and external partners, including technical coordination and scheduling
  • Development, optimization and implementation of technical automation concepts, taking energy efficiency and sustainability into account
  • Monitoring project progress, costs, schedules and quality, as well as identifying deviations at an early stage
  • Supporting and carrying out commissioning, tests and acceptance procedures, including operator training and handover of the documentation

  • Completed training in electrical engineering, automation engineering, building services engineering or an equivalent qualification
  • Experience in project management, ideally in building automation, control and instrumentation technology or systems integration
  • Sound knowledge of automation systems, bus systems (e.g. BACnet, KNX, etc.) and the relevant norms & standards
  • Independent, solution-oriented and precise way of working
  • Teamwork skills and good communication skills
