The Position As the Global Lead Construction Safety , you will play a pivotal role in driving strategic leadership, global governance, and operational oversight across capital investment projects worldwide. Working within our Global Capital Investment team, you will cultivate a proactive safety culture that protects workers and the environment while empowering diverse teams to deliver impactful work. This is a unique opportunity to shape global safety standards and lead meaningful, sustainable change across our international sites network.

The Opportunity

  • Establish and enforce global standards: Drive the creation, maintenance, and enforcement of global construction safety guidance across all capital projects in strict alignment with corporate directives.
  • Cultivate a shared safety culture: Build a positive, unified safety culture centered on active employee and contractor engagement, positive recognition, and visible leadership.
  • Deploy standardized tools and frameworks: Develop and roll out best-practice toolkits—including Construction Safety Plans, risk checklists, and tender attachments—to equip project delivery teams for success.
  • Empower site members and stop-work authority: Enforce robust safety observation processes that empower site personnel to recognize hazards, promote safe behaviors, and exercise Stop Work Authority for unsafe conditions.
  • Lead incident response and continuous learning: Ensure rapid reporting of recordable cases and champion organization-wide lessons-learned initiatives from investigations to prevent future risks.
  • Monitor performance and evaluate project readiness: Track monthly leading safety indicators and participate in formal project reviews across all delivery phases to evaluate construction safety readiness.

Who You Are

  • Educational & Professional Foundation: You hold a Master’s degree (MSc or equivalent) in Engineering, Construction Management, or a related technical discipline, paired with a recognized professional safety credential such as Certified Safety Professional (CSP).
  • Proven Track Record: You bring at least 15 years of international construction safety experience across diverse corporate cultures, with demonstrated success managing safety for large capital investment projects.
  • Matrix Leadership & Communication: You are an influential, collaborative leader who excels in matrix organizations without direct reporting lines, offering exceptional cross-cultural openness and clear communication across all levels.
  • Systematic & Courageous Mindset: You demonstrate strong systematic thinking, high self-reflection, and transparent decision-making, with the passion and autonomy needed to independently shape organizational processes and drive continuous improvement.
  • Language & Mobility: You are fluent in English (with German skills or a willingness to learn), and you are open to international travel up to 20%.
