The Position At our location in Basel, we offer an exciting internship opportunity. In the Basel Drug Substance Manufacturing division, we produce active ingredients for cancer therapy in the hybrid single use (SUT) and antibody drug conjugate (ADC) production plant. These biotechnological processes take place with the help of highly automated plants in compliance with Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP). As an intern in production, you work independently on optimization and improvement projects and learn how GMP production works from start to finish.

The possibility

  • Implementation and support of interdisciplinary projects for process optimization.
  • Analyze process flows and develop optimization possibilities.
  • Collaboration with various interfaces, such as quality, engineering, manufacturing science & technology.
  • Active co-creation and advancement of digitalization projects for the optimization and further development of existing production processes.
  • Cooperation in the processing of root cause analyses within the framework of process deviations and in the implementation of the associated measures.
  • Get to know and support routine tasks (e.g. document maintenance, raw data evaluation, etc.).
  • Cooperation on compliance and SHE topics as well as continuous improvement processes (CIP) and support material management.
  • The GMP-compliant implementation and documentation of their tasks in the area with focus on quality of the product and processes.

Who you are

  • You are currently enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree program, preferably in the fields of natural sciences (e.g. pharmacy, biology, chemistry, life sciences) or in the field of engineering (e.g. biotechnology, pharmaceutical technology, process engineering), or you have completed your studies within 12 months before the start date.
  • Proactively share knowledge with excellent organizational and communication skills.
  • Good knowledge of MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and of Google applications (Drive, Docs, Tables, Presentations) - a general IT affinity (e.g. interest in automation using Google scripts) is required.
  • Very good oral and written German as well as good English skills.

Application process

  • Please upload your CV and a matriculation certificate (if you are currently studying) or your diploma (if you have already completed your studies) with your application.
  • Due to regulations, non-EU/EFTA citizens must, as part of the application documents, provide a certificate from the university stating that an internship is mandatory, as well as the duration of the mandatory internship.

Additional information

  • Please note that this position is designed purely as a practical internship. The writing of an academic thesis in parallel is therefore unfortunately not possible.
  • The preferred start date for this internship is February 2027 or by appointment.
