The possibility As an engineer in heating, ventilation, air conditioning (HLK), you work in the field of “Energies & Utilities” and mainly in the “Site Infrastructure Engineering” district at the Basel/Kaiseraugst site. Your area of responsibility includes the following exciting activities:

  • Self-responsible handling of HVAC projects as project manager, in compliance with the specifications in terms of costs, deadlines, quality and safety.
  • Responsibility in investment projects with pronounced complexity and development of innovative and economical solutions for HVAC, also in the GMP environment.
  • Creation of targeted specifications and commissioning and coordination of qualified specialist planners, service providers and suppliers taking into account various processing models (e.g. GP, individual awards).
  • Supervision of HVAC plants in laboratory, infrastructure and administration buildings as well as in the field of chemical and galenical research, development and production.
  • Maintenance of project and work documentation including electronic storage (Comos system).
  • Development of ventilation concepts and their optimization.
  • Support for official inspections.

Furthermore, you are responsible for shaping your personal and professional development. You are actively involved and take care of the participation in self-organizing teams in the interest of the OneRoche thought. Setting up and expanding a customer network is also one of your responsibilities.
