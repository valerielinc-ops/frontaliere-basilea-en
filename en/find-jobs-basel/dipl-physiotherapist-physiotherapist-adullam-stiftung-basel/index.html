We focus on people – day after day, it is the people who make the difference – both our patients and our team. In your role as head of physiotherapy, you ensure that patients receive careful and individually coordinated physiotherapeutic support and that the team works well together. You lead with clarity, quality awareness and humanity and combine professional responsibility with everyday life in practice.

Become part of our team and help us to strengthen the mobility and joie de vivre of our elderly patients. We are looking for a motivated and committed employee for the Basel location as a dipl by 01.11.2026 or by agreement. Physical therapist 80 - 100% | Location Basel Your tasks

  • Physiotherapeutic examination and individual therapy and support: You create targeted therapy concepts that are tailored to the needs and abilities of older people and work both individually and in groups
  • Treatment of acute medical patients as well as rehabilitation after surgical and orthopaedic procedures as well as neurorehabilitation
  • Mobility and fall prevention programs: Together with the interdisciplinary team, you will perform exercises to promote balance and strength and actively participate in fall prevention
  • Active interprofessional cooperation with the medical and nursing team and other therapeutic services
  • With empathy and competence, you contribute to optimizing mobility and independence in order to make everyday life as active and self-determined as possible for our patients.
  • Documentation of therapy progress and participation in interdisciplinary case discussions Your profile
  • Completed training as a physiotherapist:in (BSc, FH or an equivalent foreign diploma with completed SRC recognition)
  • Professional experience in geriatric rehabilitation or great interest in this field
  • Joy, empathy and patience in dealing with elderly people
  • Strength of communication and the ability to build motivating and trusting relationships with patients: inside and their relatives
  • Independent, structured and networked working method Do you want to work in an environment where your work has an impact and your commitment is appreciated? We look forward to your application!
