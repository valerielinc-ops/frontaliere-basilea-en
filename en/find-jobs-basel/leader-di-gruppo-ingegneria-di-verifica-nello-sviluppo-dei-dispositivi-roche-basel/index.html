The Position

At Roche, you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring in a culture that encourages open dialogue and genuine connection. Within our Device Development Department, the Verification Engineering Section generates the objective evidence that ensures our medical devices and combination products perform reliably for patients—spanning pre-filled syringes, auto-injectors, injector pens, infusion pumps, and breakthrough platform technologies. As Group Leader, you will lead, coach, and inspire a multidisciplinary team of around 10 Verification and Device Engineers, driving both design verification strategies and broader device development activities from initial requirements to commercialization.

The Opportunity

  • Team Leadership & Development: Lead, coach, and develop a team of ~10 Verification Engineers and Device Engineers, balancing a primary focus on design verification with broader device design and development across the portfolio.
  • Technical Guidance Across Lifecycle: Provide people and technical leadership to Device Engineers supporting requirements definition, design development, experimental characterization, and technical problem solving.
  • Verification Strategy & Planning: Provide strategic direction and technical oversight for design verification planning, test method implementation, and validation in alignment with Health Authority expectations.
  • cGMP Execution & Oversight: Oversee experimental characterization and cGMP verification/qualification activities for medical devices and combination products.
  • Regulatory & Standards Translation: Translate industry policies and external regulatory and normative standards (cGMP, USP, Ph. Eur., ISO, ASTM) into compliant, practical laboratory processes.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Build strong partnerships with Development, MSAT, Quality, and Regulatory teams across both verification-specific and broader device engineering topics.
  • Operational Excellence: Optimize laboratory workflows, manage project timelines, and ensure all technical documentation (protocols, reports) meets strict cGMP standards.

Who You Are

  • Educational Background: PhD or Master’s degree in Engineering or a related scientific discipline (e.g., Biomedical, Mechanical, Materials, Pharmaceutical, or Natural Sciences).
  • Inspiring People Leader: Proven track record in people management, talent development, and cross-functional collaboration, adept at guiding engineers across both specialized verification and broader development roles.
  • Medical Device & Combination Product Expert: Demonstrated experience in device design development, design verification, and test method qualification, ideally for parenteral delivery systems.
  • Broad Lifecycle Perspective: Strong technical grasp of device engineering across the full product lifecycle, capable of advising on both deep verification challenges and general device design.
  • Regulated Environment Experience: Hands-on experience operating within a pharmaceutical, medtech, or similarly regulated environment governed by cGxP and Quality Management Systems (QMS).
  • Proactive & Collaborative Mindset: Accountable, results-driven, and eager to shape your own career path across functions and geographies while empowering others to thrive.
  • Bilingual Communicator: Outstanding interpersonal and negotiation skills with fluency in both English and German.
