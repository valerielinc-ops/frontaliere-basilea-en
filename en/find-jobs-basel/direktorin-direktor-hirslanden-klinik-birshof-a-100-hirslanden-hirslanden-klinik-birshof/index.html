Full description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Birshof | Münchenstein - Kt. Basel-Land

Occupation by 1 April 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74137

Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

It offers you a versatile, shaping role at the interface between medicine, management and the market, at one of the most renowned addresses for the musculoskeletal system in the region. In this dynamic, professional and value-oriented environment, you will have the space to realize your entrepreneurial ideas and, together with a dedicated team, shape the future of integrated healthcare.

ITS TASKS

  • Strategic, operational and economic overall responsibility for Klinik Birshof within our Mittelland supply network
  • Future-oriented positioning of the clinic in the regional market and realization of sustainable growth opportunities
  • Targeted market development and medical acquisition as well as development of strategic cooperation with relevant service providers
  • Ensuring a consistent focus on patients and a medical quality at the highest level in cooperation with the management team
  • Promoting a trusting culture of leadership and cooperation as well as convincing representation of the clinic in the political and social environment

YOUR PROFILE

  • University degree at university or university level, preferably in medicine, economics or a comparable discipline, as well as several years of successful management experience at management or management level in the Swiss health care system
  • In-depth understanding of clinical core processes and the interrelationships of a hospital operation as well as very good knowledge of the regulatory and health policy environment in Switzerland
  • Motivating, appreciative leadership style at eye level with exemplary function as well as targeted promotion of teamwork and innovative spirit
  • Convincing communication and negotiation skills, strong skills in the analysis, planning and implementation of strategies and financial and qualitative results orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

We offer you an inspiring and professional environment with a lot of creative leeway and a high degree of personal responsibility as well as the integration into a strong, Switzerland-wide network of the Hirslanden Group with short decision-making paths. She expects an appreciative working atmosphere with motivated, interdisciplinary employees, attractive employment conditions and diverse opportunities for professional and personal development.

Take the opportunity to actively shape the future of Klinik Birshof and decisively shape the next development phase with foresight and an entrepreneurial sense. We look forward to your application and getting to know you personally.
