Property management / Mortgages / Brokers, Customer advisory – Sales Workload: 50-100% Workplace: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: On site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, that means more than simply working through tasks. Here, you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards and bring the courage to pursue new paths. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, your initiative and your contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • You provide private customers with comprehensive and independent advice on real estate financing for single-family homes, condominiums and apartment buildings – online, by telephone and in person on site.
  • You actively approach customers, guide them through the entire financing process and are their central point of contact from the initial consultation through to the payout.
  • You prepare property appraisals and capitalization valuations using WUP and IAZI.
  • You develop sustainable and individual financing solutions and take into account tax aspects, amortization strategies and pension planning topics of the 2. and 3. Pillar Central Office.
  • You find suitable financing solutions and negotiate terms with financing partners in the interests of your customers.
  • You identify potential for insurance solutions and specifically refer customer needs to our insurance advisers.
  • You ensure an efficient and smooth financing process – from the initial contact to the successful completion.

Your qualifications

  • You have several years of experience in mortgage advice with a bank, insurance company or financial intermediary.
  • You are confident in complex financing solutions and enjoy the combination of consulting and sales.
  • You convince with pronounced negotiation skills and stylish German skills
  • English, French or Italian are an additional plus.
  • You are characterized by a high customer and service orientation as well as a winning and communicative personality.
  • You have a strong digital affinity and move safely in modern consulting and work environments.
  • You work independently, in a structured and reliable manner and have high standards of quality and professionalism.
