In order to strengthen our Vista teams at our site in Binningen, we are looking for a qualified and ready-to-performance personality as an optometrist 40 - 60%. Vista Eye Practices & Clinics, with its 35 locations and approximately 500 employees, are the largest provider of eye medicine in Switzerland. We offer first-class medical services to our patients and set standards in the disciplines of ophthalmology, eye surgery and laser eye medicine. Our practice team ensures a customer-friendly and efficient consultation hours. A versatile task awaits you, where you can contribute your skills and competencies and develop further. Their tasks

  • The welcome and reception of our patients

  • The independent performance of targeted preliminary examinations such as autorefractometer, fields of view, biometrics, corneal topography, endothelial cell measurement and subjective refractions for our medical team
  • Performing OCT of the retina and optic nerve
  • Patient education in the field of special lenses (IOL)
  • The organization of everyday practice is also one of your tasks This is what they bring with them.
