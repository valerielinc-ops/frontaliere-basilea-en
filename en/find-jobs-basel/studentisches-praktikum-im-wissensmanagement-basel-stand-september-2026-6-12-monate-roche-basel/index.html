At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position

A healthier future. It’s what drives us to innovate. To continuously advance science and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for generations to come.

Creating a world where we all have more time with the people we love. That’s what makes us Roche. Make a difference – as an intern for Established Data Solutions. We are looking for Knowledge Management (KM) and Computer Science (CS) enthusiasts with a keen interest in delivering and supporting advanced solutions at the interface of the Computer- and Life Sciences. In this role, you will specifically contribute to our Virtual Product Home (VPH) —a mission-critical, standard internal document management solution that supports over 5,000 active users and manages data for 800+ Roche products across our global manufacturing network.

KM increases the productivity and efficiency of every one of our company’s employees. We deliver services, processes and solutions that help an organization of several thousand researchers to manage and analyze their information. During the last years we have, for example, developed state-of-the-art solutions for semantic and numerical integration, automated processes for large-scale document management, and AI based information retrieval and classification tools. All our project teams follow the agile method within a very large IT organization, but embedded in a research & development function. Our interns are part of the team – which means you share all the fun & all the pain.

The opportunity

  • Be a part of a global team that delivers technical solutions to knowledge management problems

  • Develop a high level of understanding of global business and IT processes

  • You have ability to present ideas in a clear, comprehensive manner and independent working style, yet also in a very high performing team

  • Experience in software development and programming is a plus
