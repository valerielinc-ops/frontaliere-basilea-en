Full description

BERNER pushing the limits - for those who dare more

Deciding for your profession and your employer is a decision for your future – a future in which you will invest your passion and a large part of your time, energy and capacity.

You want to take the first steps in sales with the opportunity to develop yourself? Then the job of the merchandiser in our company is exactly what you are looking for!

What to expect

  • After your training, you will manage a permanent customer base and become an indispensable part of your team.
  • Your main tasks consist of maintaining our shelf customers and securing stocks at major customers and car dealer groups
  • Regular team meetings in your region offer you the opportunity for intensive exchange
  • Constant support from your executive and our in-house service – all just a phone call away
  • You benefit from a modern sales and service technology that optimally supports you

What sets you apart

  • Artisanal or commercial training
  • Training in the car industry is an advantage
  • High commitment and team spirit
  • Strong communication personality with an open air

What we offer

The values and sustainability of a family business. At the same time, we are setting ourselves up for the future today: customer satisfaction and digitalization are a strong priority for us. Become part of this unique transformation process and pave the way for your and our further development with your successful work.

In addition to a collegial environment, we offer you a company car (also for private use), an attractive service package of fixum and travel expenses as well as diverse career opportunities in sales and modern digital equipment.

Who we are 165 employees throughout Switzerland. Over 36,000 articles for the construction and automotive trade. Professional advice and individual service with over 50 years of experience. In short: one of Europe’s leading sales companies. So much for the pure facts. But behind the BERNER brand is something even more valuable: a family business with just as much heart as mind. We look forward to your meaningful application documents (cover letter with salary presentation, CV and employment certificates).

As an employer, the BERNER Group dares to differentiate itself and give every employee the opportunity to dare to be more. Together, we push our own boundaries and help create a better tomorrow.
