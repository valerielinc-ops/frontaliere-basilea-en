Full description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for the total cantonal processing of wages for around 11,000 employees including monthly and annual accounts as well as the control processes relevant to wage and social security
  • Personnel, professional and organizational leadership of the four-member team as well as continuous development of the department
  • Strategic development of the wage and social security sector as well as SAP HCM
  • Advice and cooperation with internal and external stakeholders
  • Management and cooperation in cantonal projects as well as implementation of legal, organizational and technical innovations
  • Ensuring compliance, quality and process responsibility as well as continuous optimization of specialist processes

Requirements

  • Basic commercial education with higher education in HR, wage or social security
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in the field of wage and social insurance, ideally in a complex organization
  • Very good knowledge of SAP HCM as well as high IT affinity and interest in digitalization and process optimization
  • Experience in the management and implementation of projects as well as in the further development of processes and systems
  • Strong business understanding and analytical and conceptual skills

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and specialist areas, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
