As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Hirslanden Klinik Birshof in Münchenstein / BL specializes in the treatment of the entire musculoskeletal system.

The ENDO team is an independent practice within the clinic and specializes in orthopaedic surgery and traumatology.

YOUR TASKS

  • Organization and support of the consultation hours of the assigned doctor and for the team
  • Fully administrative support of the assigned doctor, such as preparation of office hours, medical histories, findings and referrals
  • Outpatient office hours
  • Finalizing and sending, partly also writing medical reports and general correspondence

Your profile

  • Completed training as a medical assistant or medical secretary with several years of professional experience
  • Organizational talent with a winning appearance and high service orientation towards patients as well as reliability, commitment and flexibility
  • Sound PC and very good language skills (German mother tongue, other national languages as well as English of advantage)
  • Secure writing of reports and correspondence using medical terminology

You can expect a modern workplace within the Hirslanden Klinik Birshof. You work in a pleasant environment within a well-established practice team and take on a varied and responsible job. We also offer you attractive employment conditions.

If this activity appeals to you, we look forward to your application.
