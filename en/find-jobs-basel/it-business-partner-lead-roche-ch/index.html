The Position The RDT DIA R&D Site Partner Lead is accountable for IT Business Partnering for the Roche Diagnostics Motherwell (LumiraDX) sites in Scotland, UK. This role drives local value realization for both R&D and Site Services as well as other business functions on site like Global Operations. It demands a distinctive combination of informal people leadership, deep domain expertise, technical proficiency, and business insights.

Positioned at a critical transition point as the LumiraDX sites move from an M&A integration phase to standard RDT (Roche Digital Technology) operations, you will work alongside M&A integration leaders to ensure a smooth handover, while actively advising stakeholders through organizational change and driving alignment toward long-term Roche RDT standards.

The IT R&D Site Partner acts as the strategic liaison between RDT and local business functions, with focus on R&D, but also partnering with Site Services, Manufacturing, recognizing that supporting specialized local capabilities, unique instrumentation, and site-specific workflows is essential for the wider organization's success and competitive advantage in diagnostic development. The role operates across a multi-entity site ecosystem, guiding local environments through the final stages of M&A integration and establishing sustainable, standard operational models.
