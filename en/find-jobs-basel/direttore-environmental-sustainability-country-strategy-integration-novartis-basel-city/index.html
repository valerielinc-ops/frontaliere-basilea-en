Job Description Summary Job Title: Director, Environmental Sustainability Country Strategy & Integration

#LI-Hybrid

Location: Basel, Switzerland

Shape the connection between global environmental sustainability strategy and meaningful country-level impact. As Director, Environmental Sustainability Country Strategy & Integration, you will play a pivotal role in translating enterprise ambitions into effective country strategies, in line with Novartis global priorities, external developments and stakeholder expectations. The role will independently develop, lead and implement key strategic initiatives for country commercial organizations; and engage on these initiatives with health system partners.

This role reports to the Global Head, Social Impact & Sustainability Strategy and works closely with the Chief Sustainability Officer, Public Affairs, regional and country leaders, and the global ESG Committee (the Novartis executive-level decision-making body for Social Impact & Sustainability).

Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Cascade Novartis global Social Impact & Sustainability (SI&S) strategy – with a focus on environmental sustainability – across commercial business units and country organizations, translating enterprise priorities into clear, practical guidance and locally relevant implementation roadmaps.
  • Develop scalable sustainability solutions, turning country-level proof-of-concept initiatives into approaches with broader global potential (e.g., circularity programs and patient-care-pathway decarbonization initiatives).
  • Monitor emerging environmental market expectations and requirements - including those of regulators, payors, HEOR/HTA organizations, health system partners and customers (e.g., through tenders) – and translate developments into insights, risks and recommended actions.
  • Partner with Market Access, HEOR, Policy and Commercial teams to integrate environmental considerations into value propositions, market access strategies and healthcare-system engagement.
  • Build partnerships and new initiatives that advance care-pathway decarbonization, climate-resilient healthcare and environmental sustainability; build relationships with health system stakeholders.
  • Support country policy and advocacy activities focused on environmental sustainability, climate and health.
  • Coordinate regional and country disclosure needs, including tender and green-procurement responses; support global Finance, Operations and Disclosure teams on data requests.
  • Lead and support priority Sustainable Markets Initiative (SMI) and Pharmaceutical Environmental Group (PEG) workstreams, including circularity efforts, the Pharma Lifecycle Assessment (LCA) Consortium and patient-care-pathway decarbonization initiatives, coordinating internal country teams and external partners to deliver tangible outcomes.
  • Support country-level implementation of Scope 3 strategies and associated transition plans, partnering with relevant functions to translate global priorities into locally actionable initiatives such as business travel, local distribution and electric-vehicle adoption.
  • Guide country and global commercial teams on decarbonization and environmental sustainability priorities. Build country capabilities through practical guidance, playbooks, training and communities of practice.

Essential Requirements

  • Master’s degree in Strategy, Engineering, Sustainability or Life Sciences, or equivalent relevant professional experience.
  • Strong knowledge on key environmental sustainability topics, incl. Scope 3 emissions, life-cycle assessment and product environmental footprints, circularity.
  • Proven success delivering strategic environmental sustainability initiatives and influencing corporate sustainability agendas.
  • Pharmaceutical industry experience with strong understanding of market access, health economics, public procurement and tender processes.
  • Extensive experience leading complex strategic projects and programs involving senior leaders (incl. executive management, executive-1) and cross-functional teams.
  • Ability to translate complex sustainability topics into clear, decision-oriented insights, recommendations and executive-level communications.
  • Demonstrated success navigating complex matrix organizations and influencing across functions without formal line authority.
  • Ability to balance strategic intent with operational reality; experience in enterprise integration, transformation or program orchestration roles.
  • Strong experience developing multi-stakeholder partnerships and collaborations with health systems, policymakers, industry associations, customers and external partners.
