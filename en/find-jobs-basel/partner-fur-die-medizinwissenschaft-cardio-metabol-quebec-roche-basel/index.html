Vision

Roche embarks on a bold journey to transform the care of obesity and cardiometabolic disorders. As we broaden our scientific focus towards these interconnected therapeutic areas, we are setting up a dedicated medical team to lay the foundation for our emerging clinical portfolio.

Addressing metabolic health requires navigating complex and evolving care pathways within both traditional clinical care and emerging patient-centred health care models. To support this expanding portfolio, we are looking for medical science partners (MSP) dedicated to early scientific exchanges and ecosystem preparation.

This team will have an impact on health paradigms, establish fundamental partnerships with key health sector leaders and ensure that Canada's health system is ready for the next generation of metabolic innovations. This is a rare opportunity to develop our presence at Roche, during the critical period of pre-launch where the field work you do determines the difference we will make.
