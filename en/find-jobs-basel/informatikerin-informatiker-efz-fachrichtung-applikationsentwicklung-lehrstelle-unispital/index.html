Full description

Tasks

  • As a computer scientist with a focus on application development, you are a software specialist
  • You get to know different operating and file systems such as Windows, Linux and IOS
  • You will realize solutions for new or existing software that are used in medical operations, in clinical research or in the administrative world of the USB
  • You learn to develop software (Java, Java script, PHP and more on the situation), test it and train the users
  • The first two years are focused on getting to know the departments and processes
  • From the third year of apprenticeship, you will carry out your own projects, which serve to learn to work independently in order to actively prepare for the final apprenticeship exam.

Requirements

  • You have completed compulsory schooling, level P (or E) with good performance in mathematics and English
  • You have a great interest in technology and contact with customers
  • Logical-abstract thinking is a strength of you and you have a good understanding
  • You enjoy programming, work structured and focused
  • You are reliable, service-oriented and team-capable
  • You are very curious, patient and willing to learn

We offer

  • free sports courses (including gym), discounts for shopping, sports and leisure, employee parties, learning events and, and, and!
  • Soft start thanks to introductory week with getting to know the USB and the other learners
  • Active support from the vocational trainers, your team and older learners – #TeamUSB is there for you
  • Location in the middle of the city – fast on the banks of the Rhine and in the city centre
  • a beautiful hospital garden for chilling and refueling
  • Cozy restaurants & cafes with discounted prices for you
