Metal construction project manager 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people - now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by appointment, we are looking for a trained metal construction engineer for a temporary or permanent position

Your challenge

  • Technical and administrative project processing from order placement to final billing
  • Independent planning, coordination and implementation of projects
  • Contact person for clients, architects, engineers and our internal departments
  • Development of economical and technically convincing design and execution solutions in cooperation with architects and clients
  • Deadline, cost and quality monitoring of projects

What you bring to the table

  • Completed training in metal construction or comparable basic construction training
  • Several years of professional experience in metal and façade construction
  • Experience in project management is an advantage
  • Good IT and CAD/3D skills

What we offer you

  • Varied projects
  • High level of personal responsibility and short decision-making processes
  • Collegial working environment with a motivated team
  • Modern work equipment and attractive employment conditions
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development
