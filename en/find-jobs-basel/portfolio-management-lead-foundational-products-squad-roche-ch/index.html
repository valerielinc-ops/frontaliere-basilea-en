The Position Portfolio Management Lead – Foundational Products Squad The Foundational Products Squad (FPS) is a location-agnostic squad composed of members from affiliates across multiple regions. FPS is accountable for the management and performance of a dynamic late-life-cycle portfolio, ensuring sustainable patient access, operational excellence, and long-term enterprise value creation. As the Portfolio Management Lead, you are accountable for the end-to-end stewardship of the FPS portfolio and the deliberate expansion of FPS scope across markets, products, and portfolio clusters. You operate with a strong enterprise mindset, shaping portfolio strategy, orchestrating product and affiliate transitions, and identifying internal business development opportunities that allow FPS to scale impact in a planned, value-driven, and system-ready manner. Your role is not simply to manage what is already in FPS, but to anticipate what should come next — ensuring the right products enter FPS at the right time, under the right conditions, and with a clear value case for the enterprise. Your accountability spans:

  • Portfolio analysis, prioritisation, and performance management
  • Product and affiliate readiness and transition orchestration
  • Internal development to expand FPS scope across markets and products Your day may involve analysing portfolio trade-offs, shaping transition strategies with affiliates, facilitating decision forums on FPS expansion, or engaging regional and global stakeholders to align on future onboarding opportunities.
