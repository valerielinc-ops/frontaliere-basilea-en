05.10.2026 Allschwil

Become part of our team!

We, Raiffeisenbank Allschwil-Schönenbuch, a successful and locally oriented bank, are looking for a service-oriented and committed personality to strengthen our successful team in the corporate banking business.

What do you expect?

  • Comprehensive and needs-based advice for an own corporate and real estate portfolio (individuals, SMEs, real estate customers)
  • Preparation of individual solution proposals for the entire Raiffeisenbank consulting and service offering in corporate banking, taking into account the specific needs of corporate customers as well as the use of information for targeted cross-selling
  • Independent preparation of meaningful credit analyses and development of innovative financing solutions
  • Active expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Performing representation tasks at events and active network maintenance

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education and further education with deepening in Banking & Finance
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of corporate client consulting and complex property financing (return / commercial properties)
  • As a corporate customer consultant with a strong sales competence, you convince through a sustainable, needs-based and individual customer support
  • Resilient, socially competent flexible personality with team spirit, initiative and a high level of independence
