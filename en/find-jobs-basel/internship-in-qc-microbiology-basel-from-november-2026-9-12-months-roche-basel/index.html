The Position At our location in Basel we offer an exciting internship opportunity from November 2026. The site is located in the “QC mEMT Basel and Water Systems” unit, which belongs to the QC Microbiology department. The main task of the unit is to ensure reliable and efficient microbiological environmental monitoring for the production areas at the Basel site (production of active ingredients and development products).

The possibility As an intern in the field of QC microbiology at the production site in Basel, you support the team in a wide range of aspects. In this internship you will gain insights into the versatile project work of QC Microbiology. Especially in the environmental monitoring of the clean rooms and ultra-clean media of the production areas in Basel. In addition, you can expect the following exciting activities:

  • Support and cooperation in ongoing projects. Independent processing of projects including comparison of documents and data evaluation.
  • Creating and presenting concepts and study plans for new methods
  • Document creation and editing
  • Literature searches, creation of overview lists
  • Accompanying and coordinating professional and organizational routine meetings
  • Grade: Routine laboratory activities are not part of the task area

Who you are

You will finish your master’s degree (preferably in the field of natural sciences: biology/microbiology/molecular biology/biotechnology/pharmaceuticals) in a timely manner or have already completed it within the last 12 months and are interested in using your knowledge in a modern work environment as part of an internship of at least 9 months as well as getting to know the pharmaceutical industry.

The following competencies complete your profile

  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • High degree of initiative and rapid comprehension
  • Enjoyment of constructive cooperation with internal and external interfaces
  • Very good knowledge of English and German
  • Good MS Office knowledge
  • Google Suite knowledge is an advantage

Further details The preferred start of the internship is in November 2026 or by arrangement. The minimum duration of the internship is 9 months and can be extended up to 12 months. For the application, we need a valid enrollment confirmation (if applicable), a letter of motivation and a CV. Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must accompany the application with a confirmation from the university that they must complete a mandatory internship as part of the training. Part-time employment or the writing of a thesis is not possible at this position.
