Full description

Asset Management Workload: 80-100% Location: Level of experience: Manager FlexOffice: on-site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than just working through tasks. Here you will work with people who take responsibility, have high standards and have the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment in which trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development. As Head of Data Governance, you will lead our Data Governance team and be responsible for Data Governance, Data Quality Management and Data Operations in Asset Management. Together, you will create the basis for high-quality data, regulatory compliance and the responsible use of artificial intelligence. With your expertise, you will combine business, technology, risk and compliance and drive the further development of our Data & AI capabilities.

What you do

  • Further development and implementation of the data governance framework as well as the associated roles, standards and processes
  • Responsibility for data ownership, data stewardship, data catalogue, business glossary, metadata management and data lineage
  • Management of master data management as well as external data providers with a focus on data quality, service levels and governance
  • Responsibility for data quality management as well as for stable and efficient data & reporting operations
  • Implementation of group-wide data & AI governance regulations and ensuring regulatory requirements
  • Drive standardization, automation, and continuous improvement
  • Consulting and coaching of data owners, data stewards, management and other stakeholders
  • Active participation in transformation and digitalization initiatives as well as close cooperation with business, risk, compliance, operations and IT

What you bring to the table

  • University degree in economics, finance, data management, computer science or comparable education
  • Several years of experience in data governance, data management, data quality or related fields
  • Several years of leadership experience and enjoyment of developing teams
  • Experience with governance and control frameworks as well as regulatory requirements in the financial services or asset management environment
  • Good understanding of modern data platforms as well as data-driven business and reporting processes
  • Experience in the implementation and operation of master data systems (e.g. SimCorp DMS, Neoxam) and in the connection of market data (e.g. Bloomberg, Refinitiv, SIX, MSCI etc,)
  • Strong analytical, conceptual and communication skills as well as strong stakeholder management
  • High level of initiative, implementation orientation and enjoyment of change
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, French is an advantage
