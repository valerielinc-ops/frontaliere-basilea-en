Full description

You ensure that our production facilities can work with the right raw materials, materials and systems at all times. You combine procurement, goods management and production systems into efficient processes. Together with your team, you drive digitalization forward and provide an economical supply for our production facilities. 🚀📦

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for procurement and ensure that our production facilities are always supplied with the right raw materials and materials
  • You conduct tenders and supplier negotiations and continue to develop existing supplier relationships
  • You will continue to supervise and develop production-relevant systems and drive the digitization and standardization of processes
  • You are responsible for the merchandise management and master data processes and ensure high data quality in the relevant systems
  • You will analyze processes, identify potential for optimization and lead projects to increase efficiency and profitability
  • You lead a team of two employees and promote their development and knowledge transfer in the field

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in food production as well as in procurement, goods management or supply chain management
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of purchasing, procurement or supply chain management
  • Entrepreneurial, assertive personality with leadership experience as well as strong communication and networking skills
  • High system affinity as well as experience with ERP, goods management or production systems
  • Strong process understanding, analytical skills and pleasure in continuously optimizing processes
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work: Well accessible by public and private transport.
  • Modern infrastructure: Inviting and functional office world, modern IT infrastructure
  • Flexible work location / home office: possibility of home office when fulfilling the function
  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance AN share 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Food: Rich selection and discounted prices, food discount in M-Restaurants and TakeAways
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
