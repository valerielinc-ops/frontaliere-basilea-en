Job description

Team leader primary packaging (m/f/d)

Location: Allschwil, Switzerland Work Schedule: Two-shift operation Environmental Conditions: Compliance with all applicable GMP, hygiene and safety regulations

As part of the Thermo Fisher Scientific team, you can expect a meaningful job that will allow you to make a positive contribution worldwide.

Together, we pursue our mission to help our customers make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. We offer our employees the opportunity to develop personally and professionally and actively contribute to scientific progress.

At the Allschwil site, an important location of our Clinical Trials Division, we support international pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies in the production, packaging and worldwide delivery of clinical trials.

Their function

As a Team Leader Primary you take over the technical and personnel management of a production team in two-shift operation.

They are responsible for the efficient, timely and GMP-compliant preparation, execution and conclusion of manufacturing and packaging contracts.

You ensure compliance with our quality and safety standards, promote the further development of your employees and actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our production processes.

Their tasks

Production management and operational responsibility

  • Ensuring efficient, timely and GMP-compliant execution of manufacturing and packaging orders, including in-process controls (IPCs).
  • Professional management and coordination of production cabins and ensuring smooth production processes.
  • Monitoring the proper cleaning of production rooms, machinery and equipment.
  • Ensure complete, correct and timely GMP documentation.
  • Compliance with and enforcement of all relevant SOPs, work instructions and hygiene, quality and safety regulations.

Staff management and personnel development

  • Professional and disciplinary management, support and further development of the assigned production team.
  • Conduct employee meetings, performance reviews (PMD), regular 1:1 discussions and support in case management.
  • Participation in recruitment, training and qualification of new employees.
  • Responsibility for needs-based personnel, holiday and shift planning.
  • Coordination and supervision of temporary employees.
  • Promoting an open, respectful and performance-oriented team culture.

Quality and continuous improvement

  • Responsibility for compliance with relevant quality indicators and training requirements.
  • Support and processing of quality deviations (investigations), CAPAs and change controls.
  • Identification of process deviations, inefficiencies and potential for improvement as well as implementation of suitable measures.
  • Actively promote continuous improvement through PPI activities and Gemba Walks.
  • Creation and updating of SOPs, work instructions and forms in the electronic document management system (EDMS).

Communication and cooperation

  • Conduct and lead daily stand-up meetings and shift meetings.
  • Ensuring structured shift transfer and transparent communication within production.
  • Close cooperation with Quality Assurance, Engineering, Planning and other interfaces.
  • Early escalation of quality problems, production deviations and resource bottlenecks.
  • Collaboration and management of sector-specific projects and improvement initiatives.

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the pharmaceutical, food industry or equivalent technical field with relevant professional experience. An equivalent combination of education and professional experience is also possible.
  • Initial experience in the professional or disciplinary management of employees.
  • Professional experience in a regulated production environment, ideally in pharmaceutical manufacturing or packaging.
  • Good knowledge of GMP requirements and a strong quality and safety awareness.
  • Strong communication and leadership skills as well as pleasure in the development and motivation of employees.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method, also in a dynamic production environment.
  • Good technical and organizational understanding as well as the ability to set priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Good MS Office skills
  • Experience with ERP systems, TrackWise and EDMS is an advantage.
  • High sense of responsibility, reliability, assertiveness and teamwork.
  • Readiness to work in two shifts.

Our mission

At Thermo Fisher Scientific, your daily work makes an important contribution to the development and delivery of clinical investigational drugs.

Become part of our team and support us in realizing our mission:

Empower our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer.
