Full description

For our CMF (Craniomaxillofacial) division in Basel, we are looking for an experienced senior global product manager. In this role, you are responsible for a global product segment throughout the entire product life cycle - from product strategy development and new products to international market launch and continuous development of the existing portfolio. You work closely with product development, engineering, QM/RA, production, logistics, marketing and sales, and are also close to our customers and markets. By regularly exchanging ideas with doctors, monitoring technological developments and analysing the competition, you ensure that market and customer needs are consistently integrated into our product development. Would you like to accompany innovative medical technology from product ideas to the international market and actively shape the further development of our CMF portfolio? Then we look forward to your application.

Senior Global Product Manager CMF

Tasks

  • You are responsible for a global product segment in the CMF segment and continue to develop its product strategy and portfolio throughout the product life cycle.
  • You manage product development projects and bear responsibility for deadlines, costs and quality as well as the planning and control of the project budget.
  • You will continue to develop new products and existing product solutions together with product development, engineering, production, logistics, marketing and sales and will accompany them from the product idea to the implementation.
  • You plan, coordinate and control international product launches and provide the necessary product knowledge in the context of training courses and further activities in the course of the year.
  • You will monitor the market, competition, scientific developments and new technologies and derive relevant impulses for product strategy and product development.
  • You build an international network of doctors and medical opinion leaders and consult them regularly, through exchange, business visits, congresses and other customer contacts.
  • You systematically get market and customer feedback after product introductions and initiate product improvements, corrective measures and changes if necessary.
  • You work together with QM/RA and engineering to create the required product and approval documentation and ensure a reliable flow of information between internal and external partners.

Requirements

  • You have a degree in business administration, technology, science or a similar subject.
  • You have at least 7 years of experience in product management in medical technology, ideally in an international environment.
  • You have a good understanding of the medical market and the relevant regulatory requirements.
  • You combine strategic and analytical thinking with the ability to structure and independently advance product development and launch projects.
  • You bring with you strong organizational and project management skills and can effectively coordinate different internal and external stakeholders.
  • You convince by a communicative and team-oriented personality and build long-term relationships with international customers, medical experts and internal partners.
  • You feel comfortable in an international and intercultural environment and are ready to visit customers, operations, congresses and relevant markets regularly
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing; other languages are of benefit. Good knowledge of Microsoft Office and experience with PLM systems complete your profile.

About Medartis Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of osteosynthesis products in the area of facial skull and extremities. Medartis has committed itself to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and offering the best service for the benefit of the patient.
