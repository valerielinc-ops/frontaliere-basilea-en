Specialist/in payment transactions • Basel, CH • Full time • Perpetual Their role As a specialist in payment transactions at Bank J. Safra Sarasin, you will support our team in a wide range of activities and receive the opportunity to further develop in various areas of responsibility. Their tasks

  • Clarification of payment transactions with internal bodies and domestic and foreign banks
  • Processing of incoming and outgoing payments of our international customers
  • Processing of check-in collections as well as credit cards and Maestro cards
  • Advising internal offices and our customer service representatives in Switzerland and at all our international locations in the area of payment transactions, checks and cards
  • Readiness for occasional assignments on holidays (1 to 2 times per year) on a regular basis with other teammates
  • Deputy assignments at Kasse Basel, alternately with other teammates

Your profile

  • Commercial education (o.o.)
  • In-depth industry knowledge in the field of payment transactions
  • Excellent Microsoft Office knowledge, Avaloq knowledge is an advantage
  • Freight forward and structured working methods as well as high customer orientation
  • Ability to work independently and collaborate in a team and with various stakeholders
  • Quick comprehension and ability to recognize connections
  • High awareness of quality and responsibility as well as great willingness to learn and motivation
  • Good knowledge in German and English, in word and writing
