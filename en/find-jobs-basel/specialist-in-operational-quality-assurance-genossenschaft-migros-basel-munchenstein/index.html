Full description

Quality and food safety are more than just regulations for you? In our "Caribbean" Marcheale, the central kitchen of the Migros Basel cooperative, you are responsible for the compliance and further development of quality, hygiene and safety standards. As a central contact person for quality assurance and food safety, you are actively contributing to the optimization of our processes and products.

Mansioni

  • You ensure compliance with and further development of the company's quality management system and the HA-Boogo concept
  • You carry out operational, hygiene and process controls and monitor the implementation of GHP, GMP and food safety standards
  • You analyze quality deviations, initiate corrective measures and accompany their sustainable implementation
  • You organize and evaluate quality checks as well as microbiological and chemical investigations and accompany audits and official controls
  • You are responsible for topics such as all management of the company's management,

Declarations, traceability and the release of raw materials, semi- and finished products - You train employees in the fields of hygiene, quality and food safety and support the departments as a competent contact person

Requisiti

  • multi-year professional experience: in quality assurance or in quality management of a food production company
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): food technology, food science, food engineering or comparable education and training in the food sector
  • Well-founded knowledge of the HA 152 principles and Swiss food legislation
  • Experience in handling quality management systems as well as in hygiene, all gene and declaration management
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working methods

combined with a high quality awareness

  • Communicative personality with assertiveness and joy in raising and developing employees
  • German (flowing)
  • French (advantage)

Cosa offriamo

  • Central place of work: Good access by public and private transport.
  • Modern infrastructure: inviting and functional office world, modern IT infrastructure
  • Professional pension: pension fund with above average performance AN share 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Catering: Rich selection and discounted prices, food discount in M-Restaurants and TakeAways
  • Cum-Points: Additional Cum-Points for Employees
