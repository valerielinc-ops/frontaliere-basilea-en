Operational engineer or technician maintenance passenger transport Basel 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Operational engineer or technician for maintenance of passenger transport in Basel Basel, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In your role, you will help ensure that our Astoro long-distance transport fleet runs safely and reliably. In the maintenance of SBB passenger transport vehicles, you will be responsible for the technical support of the assigned fleet and, in complex cases, offer technical support in maintenance and diagnostics. You combine conceptual work with the practical implementation of technical solutions. You contribute to safe and punctual rail operations and keep an eye on the economic conditions. As a subject matter expert, you are a central contact person and take over the technical and/or methodological management of cross-departmental technical topics. The function is also carried out in shifts depending on operational needs. Night and weekend work is paid additionally. In addition, you will receive time allowances, which give you additional free time and more flexibility compared to regular working hours. You can find further benefits such as information regarding GA 2nd class travel, discounts for travel at home and abroad (also for families) and child allowances on our website under “Benefits”. You can move that.

  • You carry out complex fault diagnoses on control technology, electrical and electronic components as well as pneumatic and electromechanical systems.
  • You determine repair measures, carry out complex work on rail vehicles and their systems and are then responsible for putting the vehicles into operation.
  • You develop repair measures and support your team in their tasks, especially the operational fleet technician and work preparation.
  • As a maintenance technician, you provide technical guidance to other employees in carrying out orders, instruct repair processes and ensure the necessary documents are provided.
  • You ensure effective stakeholder management in your area of ​​responsibility and promote trusting, targeted and cross-departmental collaboration through appreciative, addressee-appropriate communication. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by the ability to work in a team and work in an interdisciplinary manner. Your communicative style supports you in this central function.
  • You have strong analytical thinking skills and can quickly gain a clear overview, even in complex situations. You like to work independently and develop practical solutions on your own responsibility.
  • We require 4 years of electrical engineering or mechanical vocational training EFZ with further training to become an engineer FH or technician HF in electrical engineering or mechanical engineering.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of rail vehicle technology with at least 2 years of experience in maintenance or commissioning.
  • You communicate confidently in German (minimum B2), Italian is an advantage.
