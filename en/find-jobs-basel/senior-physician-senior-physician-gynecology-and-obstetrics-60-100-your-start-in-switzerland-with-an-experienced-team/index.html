Would you like to start your specialist career in Switzerland and learn from an experienced team? In Basel, you can expect a versatile senior doctor position with a broad gynecological and obstetric spectrum, professional support and plenty of space for your personal development. We are happy to tell you more about this possibility in a personal conversation. The established women’s clinic in the border region of Basel combines high-quality medical care with a personal and appreciative working atmosphere. With around 1,800 births and 2,200 gynecological procedures every year, you can expect a varied clinical everyday life. Especially for doctors who want to take the step to Switzerland after their specialist training in Germany, this position offers attractive prerequisites: You work with experienced specialists, expand your operational skills and develop professionally in a collegial environment.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of patients in gynecological and obstetric consultation hours
  • Accompaniment and guidance of births, including risk births from the age of 35. Week of pregnancy
  • Performing gynecological and obstetric surgery and pre- and post-operative care
  • Opportunity to get involved in special fields such as uromynecology and develop your own focuses
  • Participation in the training of assistant doctors
  • Participation in night, weekend and emergency services

Your Competences

  • Completed or advanced specialist training in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Enjoying surgical gynecology and obstetrics as well as motivation to further develop your skills
  • Interest in a long-term career in Switzerland
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least C1)
  • Team-oriented, empathetic personality with pleasure in professional exchange
  • MEBEKO recognition of your medical diploma or willingness to initiate the recognition procedure

Their advantages

  • Ideal entry to Switzerland: Experienced specialists accompany you when entering the Swiss healthcare system
  • Strong team: Collegial exchange, professional support and the opportunity to expand your operational experience
  • Broad medical spectrum: obstetrics, surgical gynecology and special consultation hours under one roof
  • Personal development: Attractive further education and training as well as space for your own professional focus
  • Flexible employment: Workload between 60 and 100% at an attractive border location in Basel
  • Additional benefits: Free sports offerings such as yoga, Pilates and CrossFit, plus a gym
