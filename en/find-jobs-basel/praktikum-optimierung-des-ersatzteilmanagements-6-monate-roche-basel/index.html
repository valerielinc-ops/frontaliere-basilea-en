The Position

In our state-of-the-art production in Basel and Kaiseraugst, we manufacture a variety of medicines that improve the lives of millions of patients every day. The reliability and efficiency of our plants is of crucial importance. In this context, predictive spare parts management plays a central role for efficient maintenance and maximum plant availability.

The possibility

As an intern (m/f/d), you get the unique opportunity to participate at the interface of data analysis and practical application in maintenance. You support us in significantly improving our processes for the identification, classification and inventory management of spare parts. This is an ideal opportunity to apply your knowledge in a real, highly relevant industrial environment and actively shape the future of maintenance.

Your tasks

As an intern (m/f/d) in the area of optimization of spare parts management, you will take on exciting and varied tasks in which you can actively shape the following topics:

  • Data-driven digitalization: You contribute significantly to the digitalization and structuring of spare parts and parts lists. Your goal is to create a consistent database for our SAP system – a central tool for managing our company resources, which is essential for efficiently planning and executing maintenance and maintenance work. Here you use your understanding of data structures and models.
  • Intelligent data harmonization: You compare spare parts from the plant planning phase with the actual data stored in SAP for maintenance and maintenance activities. You identify discrepancies and contribute to data quality and consistency, which is crucial for effective maintenance planning.
  • AI-assisted spare parts identification: You develop and implement innovative approaches for intelligent recognition of whether necessary spare parts are already present in the SAP system. You can use modern techniques such as data science and natural language processing (NLP) for text analysis.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work closely with experts from engineering, plant operators, workshops and suppliers. Your task is to derive well-founded recommendations for the inclusion of new spare parts in SAP and to evaluate their relevance and the optimal time – always with regard to plant availability and operational reliability.
  • Identification of Critical Components: Helps identify critical spare parts that require special attention in stock to minimize unexpected downtime and ensure asset reliability. Here you apply principles of risk management.
  • Inventory optimization through analysis: You evaluate whether the spare parts in the SAP system are in stock in sufficient quantities. In doing so, you take into account the frequency of use and the installation cases of the spare part in various plants in order to develop an optimal inventory strategy and optimize the capital commitment.
  • Process innovation with advanced technologies: You will be actively involved in the use and building of modern tools, including data science approaches, possibly the use of large language models (LLMs) and the development of your own local app
