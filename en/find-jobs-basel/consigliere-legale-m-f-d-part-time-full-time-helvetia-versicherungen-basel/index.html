Full description

Shaping the future together – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. At our corporate headquarters in Basel, you will work with colleagues who take on responsibility, have high standards and have the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our internal customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • In procurement, IT and outsourcing projects, you advise on contractual, corporate and supervisory issues and answer corresponding inquiries from the organization. Depending on your seniority, you also contribute to the structuring and planning of larger procurement projects of the Helvetia Baloise Group.
  • In the field of intellectual property and competition law, you assume primary responsibility at group level and coordinate closely with those responsible in the individual countries.
  • In addition, you are interested in other areas of law that your colleagues focus on, such as sustainability and AI regulation.
  • In your areas of responsibility, you develop and maintain internal instructions and processes and independently carry out exercises and training.

Requirements

  • Prerequisite for this position is a completed legal degree and preferably a lawyer's patent.
  • You already have several years of professional experience as an in-house counsel or lawyer, ideally in regulated markets or in an international environment.
  • In-depth knowledge of contract, company and supervisory law distinguishes you as well as very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
  • As a team-capable, solution-oriented and communicatively strong person, you understand how to bring different interests together and develop pragmatic solutions.
  • Self-responsible work is a matter of course for you, and you represent your work results sovereignly to the management.
  • Working in a dedicated in-house legal team gives you pleasure, and you want to continuously develop and improve both yourself and the team.

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
