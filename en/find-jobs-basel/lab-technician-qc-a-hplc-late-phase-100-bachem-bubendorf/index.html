Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. For our QC Late Phase department, we are currently looking for a dedicated Lab Technician QC with a focus on HPLC (m/f/d) for independent and precise analytical work in a specialized team. We currently have a permanent vacancy that offers exciting insights into the late phase of quality control.

Their tasks

  • Analytical activities: Self-conducting HPLC/U(H)PLC analyses in the context of release, stability and development projects. Professional preparation, operation and maintenance of the equipment, if necessary incl. Troubleshooting to ensure reliable results.
  • Documentation & Evaluation: Careful documentation of all work steps as well as evaluation of raw data in compliance with relevant quality standards (GMP).
  • Quality Assurance & Compliance: Ensuring that all laboratory activities comply with quality specifications and safety guidelines. During quality events (OOX cases) support in processing and documentation.
  • Teamwork: Close cooperation with QA, production and other internal areas for continuous improvement and quality assurance in the laboratory. Good communication and precise working methods to support efficient and smooth work in the team.

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a chemical laboratory technician, preferably focusing on analytics
  • Professional experience in the chemical or pharmaceutical industry ideally in the GMP environment
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English
  • In-depth knowledge of HPLC/U(H)PLC and experience in the independent implementation of analytical methods
  • Knowledge in the field of peptides/oligonucleotides desirable
  • Safe use of computerised systems
  • Experience with LIMS and Chromeleon is beneficial
  • Strong organisational ability and high level of care in documentation

Our offer

  • Option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Additional free days (bridge days), which are fixed in autumn by the Bachem
  • Company-internal KITA and staff restaurant on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our election savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
