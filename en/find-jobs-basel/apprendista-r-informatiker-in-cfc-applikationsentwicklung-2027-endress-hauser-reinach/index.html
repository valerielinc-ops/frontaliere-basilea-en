Shaping the future together – your apprenticeship at Endress+Hauser At Endress+Hauser, you don't just work anywhere – you're part of a team that works on exciting technologies for industry worldwide.Our approximately 18,000 employees develop, build and improve measuring devices that are used all over the world. What is important to us? Cooperation, trust and team spirit. Because together we achieve more – and every idea counts. As a family-run company, we think in the long term and invest in your future.

With us you get not only a top education, but also many opportunities to develop and shape yourself.

What is the job about? Do you like to program and tinker with your own ideas? Do you want to know how apps, websites or software solutions are created? And are you interested in working with modern programming languages and digital technologies? Then training as an IT scientist is just right for you! In this training you learn how software solutions are planned, developed, tested and implemented. You work on your own projects, develop prototypes, configure applications and support users:inside.

You use the methods of the company and are in close contact with internal customers and professionals.
