Full description

Finance / Controlling / Audit Pensum: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Do you want to not only accompany technical accounting operations, but also further develop them in a targeted manner? In this role, you combine sound accounting expertise with your sense of processes, data and systems. Together with Technical Accounting (Insurance/ Reinsurance), IT and Financial Reporting, you create clear standards, drive automation and strengthen the quality of underwriting financial data.

What you do

  • You shape the further development of the processes in technical accounting and passive reinsurance – with clear standards, targeted automation and less manual effort.
  • You bring expertise and technology together. With Finance and IT, you translate professional requirements into suitable solutions and drive system and transformation projects forward.
  • You ensure reliable financial data and are responsible for the correct and complete mapping of business transactions in the technical accounting book and improve the quality of balance sheet and earnings data.
  • Together with Financial Reporting, you develop booking logics, ensure the coordination between pre-systems and secondary book and clarify challenging final questions.
  • You strengthen process reliability by developing effective controls, identifying risks along the process chain and accompanying internal and external audits.
  • You make improvements measurable: with meaningful metrics, you show progress in process quality, efficiency and receivables management and derive the next steps.
  • You stay close to the operational business, take over selected processes or contract portfolios and find solutions for demanding specialist questions.

What you bring

  • You have completed studies in business administration, accounting, finance or business informatics, or a comparable qualification.
  • You bring several years of experience in the insurance or reinsurance industry and know the technical accounting very well.
  • You will be familiar with IFRS and local accounting standards and understand how transactions, sub-books, ledgers and financial reporting work together.
  • You have experience in process management, governance or business analysis and enjoy working with data and systems. Knowledge of SAP FS-RI and SAP FS-CD are a plus.
  • You penetrate complex relationships, recognize dependencies and develop solutions that convince professionally and prove themselves in everyday life.
  • You like to take on responsibility, work independently and with high quality standards. With clear arguments, you win different specialist areas for joint improvements.
  • You communicate very well in German and English and bring specialist topics clearly to the point in conversation as well as in writing.
