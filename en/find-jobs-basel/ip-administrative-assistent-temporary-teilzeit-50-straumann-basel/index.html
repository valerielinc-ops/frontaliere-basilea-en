IP Administrative Assistant (50%)

Basel, Switzerland | Fixed-Term Contract (2 Years)

At Straumann Group, innovation is at the heart of everything we do. To support our global intellectual property activities, we are seeking a highly organized and proactive IP Administrative Assistant to join our Group IP Department in Basel on a 50% basis for a fixed term of two years.

This role offers a unique opportunity to work at a Global IP Department ensuring the smooth operation of our IP operations function by supporting administrative processes.


Your • Responsibilities

As a member of the Global IP team, you will:

  • Provide day-to-day administrative support to the Group IP Department.
  • Coordinate and maintain documentation and records related to patents, trademarks, and other IP matters.
  • Assist with portfolio administration activities, including record updates, address changes, and information requests.
  • Communicate with internal stakeholders and external intellectual property law firms worldwide.
  • Monitor and follow up on administrative tasks and project activities.
  • Assist with special projects and initiatives within the IP Department.

Your • Profile

You are an organized and service-oriented professional who enjoys working with complex information and supporting a global team.

• Qualifications and Experience

  • Previous experience in an administrative or IP services environment.
  • Experience in IP services or as an IP administration person is an advantage but not required.

Skills

  • Excellent organizational and multitasking abilities.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Structured and proactive working style.
  • Ability to manage confidential information with discretion.
  • Experience working with databases or enterprise systems.

Languages

  • Fluent English is essential.
  • Additional languages are an asset.

Please submit your job application CV in English language.
