You can look forward to this. If you love challenges and are looking for an expert role in an innovative environment, you have come to the right place in the control room for our fleets in Basel. Your support is crucial for efficient maintenance and reducing throughput times in our production. You can also look forward to a collegial environment in which know-how is shared, as well as interesting opportunities for further development.   • You contribute your resources, skills and material knowledge to efficiently prepare maintenance and repair work with the help of the most modern remote diagnostic data and operational information.
  • In this function, you will create precise disruption analyzes on a daily basis in order to ensure the essential basis for demanding contractually owed services from our suppliers.
  • You take responsibility for monitoring and ensuring the accuracy and quality of S1 reports to ensure a smooth process and the highest standards.
  • Prove your professional competence by carrying out complex repair work independently, even without specific instructions. You report these back in accordance with the established guidelines.
  • Diagnosis of high-complexity malfunctions to optimize maintenance: You bring in your expert knowledge to diagnose high-complexity malfunctions.

You use information from various vehicle and system documents as well as specialist documentation to report fault reports professionally. This is what you bring with you.

  • You have a high degree of independence, initiative and a sense of responsibility. Networked, analytical thinking and actions, a high level of resilience and flexibility characterize you as a person.
  • Teamwork is our key to success. You see yourself as a team player and promote collaboration.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are in digital form, you have no fear of contact with common modern work tools and IT programs such as MS Office and SAP.
  • For this exciting and demanding position, you need to have completed a 4-year EFZ apprenticeship in electrical engineering. In your career so far, you have been able to gain several years of solid professional experience in the field of rail vehicle maintenance.
  • You are physically fit and willing to work in a 7x24 hour company. Of course, you will be compensated accordingly for this.
  • We expect very good oral and written German skills (C1/C2). For professional exchange with the Geneva location, we also require good oral and written knowledge of French (at least B2).
