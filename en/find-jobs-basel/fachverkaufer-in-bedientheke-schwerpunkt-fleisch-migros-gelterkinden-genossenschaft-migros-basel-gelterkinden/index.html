Full description

Are you passionate about fresh food, especially meat, fish and cheese? As a specialist dealer*in control counter you can experience your craftsmanship and sales talent. You are also inspired by your tips and tricks. Join now and become part of our team!

Mansioni

  • The advice and service of our customers at the operating counter (meat, fish, cheese) is one of your main tasks.
  • You prepare meat, fish, and cheese for the service sale and present them appealing. You make sure that all hygiene regulations are complied with.
  • You make sure that our goods are available at the operating counter in the required quantity (goods disposition). In addition, you regularly check the goods of the counter, fill automatically and sort them in.
  • You are responsible for the freshness and durability of our goods and always check them at regular intervals.

Requisiti

  • 1 -2 years of professional experience: in the area of meat / fish / cheese / service
  • Complete professional teaching (EFZ): as butcherin, meat retailerin or chef
  • You are a customer-oriented personality and have pleasure in dealing with our customers.
  • You have a great skill in planning and organizing tasks.
  • You work reliably and have a high quality awareness.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Social benefits: In case of illness, the wage advance payment is 100% for two years, in case of accident we are insured SUVA
  • Catering: rich selection and discounted prices, catering in M-restaurants and TakeAways
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Recreational and cultural offerings: Various discounts or free cultural offers
  • Club school Migros: cost participation of 70% in club courses up to max. CHF 1'000.- per year
