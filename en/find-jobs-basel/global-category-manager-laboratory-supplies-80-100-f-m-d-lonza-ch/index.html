As Global Category Manager Laboratory Supplies, you will play a key role in shaping global sourcing strategies that support research, development and manufacturing activities across Lonza. You will collaborate with stakeholders across the business to drive value, strengthen supplier partnerships and ensure reliable access to critical laboratory supplies and services.

What you will get

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance

What you will do

  • Develop and implement global category and sourcing strategies for laboratory supplies, equipment and related services
  • Lead sourcing initiatives, supplier negotiations and contracting activities to deliver value, improve service and reduce risk
  • Partner with stakeholders across R&D, Quality, Manufacturing, Finance and Operations to align procurement strategies with business needs
  • Build and maintain a pipeline of cost savings and value creation opportunities, ensuring accurate forecasting and tracking
  • Manage strategic supplier relationships and drive performance through governance, business reviews and continuous improvement initiatives
  • Monitor market trends, supplier capabilities and emerging technologies to identify opportunities for innovation and optimization
  • Ensure compliance with company policies, regulatory requirements and risk management standards across the category.

What we are looking for

  • Bachelor or Master Degree in Economics / Supply Chain Management / Procurement or a related field
  • Extensive experience within category management, strategic sourcing or procurement within the pharma or life sciences industry or comparable industry
  • Proven ability to lead complex sourcing initiatives and deliver measurable business value
  • Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to build partnerships and influence decision-making
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities, combined with a customer-focused mindset
  • Fluency in English is required
