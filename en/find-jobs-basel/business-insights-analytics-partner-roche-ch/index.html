The Position Positioned at the core of digital transformation and the evolving data ecosystem in the pharmaceutical industry, this role seamlessly integrates Commercial Excellence with business analytics, data-driven insights, and CRM architecture. As a Business Insights & Analytics Partner, you will optimize customer engagement, targeting, and segmentation strategies for field teams through a data-driven approach. Beyond traditional performance reporting (IQVIA, CRM, etc.), you will leverage advanced analytics, artificial intelligence (AI), and data modeling to shape strategic decision-making across commercial teams. You will act as a primary architect of next-generation commercial excellence through advanced dashboard design, AI-driven automation, and data integrations.

Your Responsibilities

  • Strategic Partnership & Decision-Making Architecture: Act as a strategic partner to Disease Area Teams, establishing the tailored data architecture, insights framework, and decision-making mechanisms required for commercial strategy execution and strategic Planning
  • Commercial Excellence & Business Analytics: Drive targeting, segmentation, route optimization, and call planning for field teams; define, track, and optimize performance metrics (KPIs).
  • AI & Advanced Analytics Integration: Leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools, machine learning, and advanced analytical approaches to automate insight generation, enhance predictive capabilities, and drive innovation in commercial operations.
  • CRM & Customer Engagement Management: Oversee the effective utilization, data quality, and system integration of CRM platforms (e.g., Veeva, Salesforce) with neighboring systems.
  • Data Architecture, Dashboard & Datawarehouse Design: Design, build, and maintain user-friendly, dynamic, and automated BI/Dashboard architectures (e.g., PowerBI, Tableau) for decision-makers and field teams.
  • Market & IQVIA Insights: Deep-dive into IQVIA, market share, and sales performance data to identify growth opportunities, market dynamics, and performance gaps.
  • Data-Driven Culture & Process Automation: Drive a data-driven mindset across the wider affiliate; streamline and automate routine insight processes to maximize operational efficiency.
  • Supporting & enabling organization on adopting CRM capabilities.Partnering with global teams with close collaboration
  • Data stewardship and master data management ownership, applying and maintaining data governance & standards.
  • Contributing to the efficiency and effectiveness of insight lifecycle in the organization
  • Maintaining standard BI & analytics services, handling & funneling ad-hoc BI & analytics service requests of business units
  • Delivering the following analyses such as: Patient Flow, CX & Commercial Data Analysis, Market & Competitive Intelligence, Trend Forecasting, Target & Contribution Analysis, Patient/HCP Behavior Modeling
  • Designing VoC, Research, Survey, NPS, PS, adboard, segmentation, external PMRs based on existing data (patient flow, cx & commercial data) & journey requirements and managing the related internal & external stakeholders.
  • Delivering timely risk identification alerts/notifications.

Reports to: Business Excellence Lead
