Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a Senior Project Manager Data Management (f/m/d) for Endress+Hauser Flow. In this role, you actively shape the digital transformation of our development processes and make an important contribution to the further development of our product data and lifecycle management. What tasks will you take on? They shall:

  • Leading PLM and data management projects in research & development.
  • responsible for the introduction and further development of a PLM system.
  • Plan and implement workshops, project reviews, training sessions and stakeholder formats.
  • Build and maintain an active stakeholder management.
  • work closely and interdisciplinary with various specialist departments and colleagues within the Endress+Hauser Group.
  • Continuously improve processes and standards in technical data management. What do we expect from you? They:
  • have completed studies in engineering (e.g. mechanical engineering, electrical engineering) or a comparable field of expertise.
  • bring at least five years of experience in project management, ideally in the field of PLM, PDM or technical data management.
  • have a deep understanding of product development processes, product structures and engineering workflows.
  • have experience with common PLM systems and relevant IT architectures.
  • work in a structured, solution-oriented and self-responsible manner.
  • are a strong, team capable and flexible personality and communicate safely in German and English What can you expect from us? We offer you:
  • Diverse training and further education opportunities as well as internal knowledge transfer and e-learning offers.
  • Support for sustainable mobility, especially in public transport.
  • A stable, family-run company with a long-term perspective.
  • Modern premises and ergonomically equipped workplaces.
  • Comprehensive occupational pension provision and attractive health services. How do you apply? Please upload your application documents via our career page. Applications by e-mail cannot be considered for data protection reasons. For this position, we do not currently work with external service providers. Unsolicited applications are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
