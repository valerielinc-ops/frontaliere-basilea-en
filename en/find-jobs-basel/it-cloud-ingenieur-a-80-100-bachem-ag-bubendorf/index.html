Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in Global IT, we are looking for a dedicated IT Cloud Engineer (a), 80-100%

Your tasks

  • Manage and maintain cloud operations in alignment with security, compliance, and company policies
  • Design, build, and support CI/CD pipelines using tools such as GitHub Actions, AWS Code Pipeline, and Jenkins
  • Develop and maintain Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) solutions using Terraform and AWS CloudFormation
  • Enforce cloud engineering standards through governance, peer reviews, and best practices
  • Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions to ensure system reliability and performance
  • Automate operational processes, including patching, backup validation, and compliance checks
  • Collaborate with application owners, IT teams, and the CISO organization to meet business and regulatory requirements
  • Support change management processes, including CAB participation and technical documentation
  • Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and contribute to cloud governance standards, policies, and operational runbooks

Your profile

  • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, IT, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience
  • 3+ years of hands-on AWS cloud engineering experience in a production environment
  • Experience working in regulated industries (e.g., pharmaceutical, biotech, medical devices)
  • Strong skills in Infrastructure-as-Code (preferably Terraform) and scripting (Python, Bash, or PowerShell)
  • Solid knowledge of AWS security services and compliance frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF)
  • AWS Certified Solutions Architect (Associate or higher) or equivalent expertise
  • Experience with DevOps and cloud-native technologies, including Docker, Kubernetes/EKS, and CI/CD practices
  • Knowledge of disaster recovery, business continuity, and hybrid/multi-cloud environments
  • Additional advantages: AWS DevOps/Security certifications, AWS Bedrock, Veeva Vault or validated systems, NIS2 knowledge skills
  • Fluent in English, German is a plus

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal development and training opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with home office days and an option for obtaining additional vacation days through workload reduction
  • Company‑run daycare center on the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as option for extra-mandatory pension provision with our Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to benefits platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
