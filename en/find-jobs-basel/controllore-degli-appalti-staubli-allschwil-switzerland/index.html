Full description

As part of the Controlling Division, you are the central interface between Finance and Procurement within our Electrical Connectors Division. In this internationally oriented role, you will work closely with the Global Head of Procurement, the local purchasing teams and Divisional Controlling and contribute directly to increasing the competitiveness and profitability of our division. Their tasks

  • Responsibility for global procurement controlling as well as the continuous development of standardized control and reporting processes, methods and KPI definitions across international locations
  • Controlling the periodic controlling cycle (budget, forecast, monthly financial statements, reporting)
  • Implementation and further development of price variance analyses (PPV), cost savings trackers and tracking of relevant measures across all locations and product groups
  • Monitoring and analysis of material, supplier and purchase price developments as key drivers of the gross margin as well as development of decision-making basis for sustainable improvement of profitability
  • Sparring partner for Global Head of Procurement, local purchasing teams, finance and supply chain organizations
  • Support strategic purchasing decisions through in-depth analysis and business cases, as well as cooperation in supplier, cost and make-or-buy decisions
  • Successfully completed studies in Finance, Controlling, Business Administration, Supply Chain Management or Business Engineering
  • Several years of professional experience in controlling, ideally in procurement controlling or purchasing in an international industrial environment
  • In-depth understanding of cost structures, purchasing processes and margin mechanisms combined with strong analytical skills as well as high numbers and data affinity
  • Very good SAP knowledge (FI/CO, CO-PA, MM; further modules are advantageous)
  • Digital competence for data analytics and AI solutions for the analysis of material, supplier and purchase price developments as well as for the creation of predictive basis for decision-making
  • Very good knowledge of Excel (including Power Query) as well as experience with BI tools such as Power BI or Qlik for the analysis of complex data structures, automation of reporting processes and creation of meaningful management dashboards
  • Strong communication personality with a strong business partner mindset and a structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented working method
  • Negotiable knowledge of English (C1)
