Kaizen Coach (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Kaizen coach (m/f/d) Basel, 01.01.2027, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a Kaizen organization, affiliated to the rolling stock maintenance, you are an important support for the divisions "Market passenger transport" and "Production passenger transport". With methods and coaching for continuous and sustainable improvement throughout the value chain, you make a significant contribution to increasing safety, quality and efficiency. Your contractual place of work is the service facility Basel, but you are also regularly in use in other service facilities such as Lucerne and Brig. We work process-oriented and develop work processes, products and services together with our employees. You can expect an innovative working environment in which you can implement major changes and thus make a decisive contribution to increasing the efficiency of SBB. You can move that.

  • You plan and run Kaizen workshops independently. With skillful moderation techniques, an exciting workshop setup and the application of your Kaizen toolbox, you motivate the participants to achieve sustainable solutions.
  • By conducting training courses, you empower managers and employees to independently identify potential for improvement and to realize the planned changes with the help of Kaizen methods and tools.
  • As a sparring partner, you advise and support organisations with the aim of increasing productivity and implementing Kaizen measures in a sustainable manner.
  • You carry out goal-oriented Kaizen activities according to requirements, check sustainability and prepare regular reports. You also perform administrative tasks such as creating weekly plans and efficiency controls.

You bring that with you.

  • As a convincing personality and team player: with good written and oral expressiveness, you inspire others for common goals and solutions and drive their implementation.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of Kaizen or related philosophies of continuous improvement (e.g. Lean Management or Six Sigma) and practical experience in conducting Kaizen workshops.
  • Your holistic way of thinking, strong analytical and conceptual skills and a high level of process understanding characterize you.
  • We presuppose a technical or business education and training at the level of HF (e.g. operating technology or company processes). Ideally, you have completed additional training in the field of process management or lean management.
  • For this position, you have a reliable oral and written knowledge of German.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in Requirement Level I, Regional Allowance Level 1.
