HR Administration Assistant (60%) – Basel

We are here to make you feel sexy, confident & UNSTOPPABLE. Since our founding in 1984, we have been celebrating fashion, independence, fun, love and community. Today, we are represented in 35 markets with around 400 stores, an online presence and over 3 million fans on our social media channels.

About the position

We are looking for an HR Administration Assistant with a 60% workload as of December 1, 2026. Your place of work is Basel. From there, you will support our team in Germany in personnel administration, taking into account German labour law.

This is an entry-level position: work experience is not required.

Your tasks

  • Assistance in drawing up and managing employment contracts.
  • Administrative processing of entries and exits.
  • Collection and updating of sickness reports and absences.
  • Maintenance of personnel documents and support in the daily HR administration business.

Your profile

  • Fluency in German is absolutely necessary.
  • Knowledge of the German labour market as well as an apprenticeship completed in Germany or a degree programme completed in Germany are mandatory requirements.
  • A careful working method and good organizational talent.
  • A reliable and discreet handling of confidential information.
  • Willingness to learn and joy of working together in a team.
  • No previous HR experience required – Beginners: Women are welcome.

What we offer

  • A position with a workload of 60% at the Basel location.
  • Start on 1 December 2026.
  • Practical insights into various areas of personnel administration.
  • Guidance and support in your first steps in HR.

Would you also like to be part of our fashion story and become an enthusiastic member of our TALLY Family? Then show us your individual talent and send us your application! Or contact our internal recruiter directly at c.barone@tally-weijl.com .
