Full description

Entrance by 1. January 2027 or by appointment · Liestal, Care · Job reference 13038 · Permanent employment

Tasks

You are a leader who takes responsibility, creates orientation and leads people with clarity and appreciation? We look forward to meeting you.

  • Personnel, organisational, professional and financial management of the station with 24 beds and around 25 employees with a focus on clear structures and binding framework conditions in everyday life
  • Strategic planning and sustainable implementation of station goals as well as future-oriented projects
  • Design and development of structures and processes to ensure professional patient-centered and high-quality care
  • Promoting an open, appreciative and at the same time binding station culture characterized by clear communication, mutual respect and constructive cooperation
  • Responsibility for securing and continuously developing the quality of care, care and treatment
  • Active participation in team development processes and strengthening positive team dynamics
  • Identification and targeted promotion of the strengths of employees as well as support of development processes with clarity and commitment
  • Predictive budget and resource management for optimal control of station operation
  • Ensuring the sustainable implementation and implementation of Lean Hospital principles
  • Active participation in the station management committee with joint responsibility and participation in the sense of shared governance

Requirements

  • Diploma as nurse HF, FH or with equivalent recognition
  • In-depth professional experience in vascular and thoracic surgery and ENT
  • Several years of leadership experience in an acute hospital, ideally supplemented by training in the health care sector
  • Enthusiasm for versatile management tasks as well as the ability to communicate clearly, appreciatively and bindingly and at the same time act in a solution- and result-oriented manner
  • Negotiation skills, innovation and the ability to inspire people and actively shape change
  • Sovereign and winning appearance in interprofessional teams as well as strong networking and cooperation competence
  • Enjoying a dynamic, varied and lively hospital everyday life

Experienced, hands-on, reliable and heartfelt: KSBL stands for these four values - you too? Then we look forward to meeting you and actively shaping the future of care together.

Benefits

All-round care: discounted day care on site, full parenting allowances, paid short absences in case of emergency care
