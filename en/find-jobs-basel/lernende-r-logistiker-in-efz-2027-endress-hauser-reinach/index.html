Full description

Shaping the future together – your teaching at Endress+Hauser At Endress+Hauser you don’t just work anywhere – you are part of a team working on exciting technologies for the industry worldwide. Our approximately 18,000 employees develop, build and improve measuring instruments that are used all over the world. Collaboration, trust and team spirit. Because together we achieve more – and every idea counts. As a family-run company, we think long-term and invest in your future. With us you not only get a top education, but also many opportunities to develop and shape yourself.

What is the point about? Are you interested in the world of goods flows? Do you like to work organized, physically active and in a team? And do you want to know how products are moved efficiently and safely from goods receipt to shipping? Then the training as a logistics specialist is just right for you! In this training, you will learn how goods are professionally accepted, stored, distributed and prepared for shipping. You work with modern conveyor systems, carry out inventory checks and ensure that our internal and external customers are supplied reliably at all times.

What tasks will you take on?

You will

  • Complete in-depth training in professional goods management
  • Accepting, checking and recording goods in the system
  • Coordinate internal transport with funding
  • Prepare products and delivery documents for shipping
  • Vehicles correctly and safely loaded
  • Checking stocks and carrying out inventories

What do we expect from you?

You

  • are in middle or upper school
  • brings an interest in logistical processes and technical understanding
  • have craftsmanship and pleasure in physical work
  • has a strong sense of order and sense of responsibility
  • are team capable, reliable and like to work independently

What can you expect from us?

  • Security and stability of an internationally operating family business
  • Very good takeover opportunities after training
  • A motivated and dedicated training team that will accompany you
  • Costs of the U-Subscription (Public)
  • Delicious and cheap lunch menus from 5.50 CHF in the company's restaurant

For your application we need

  • Letter of motivation
  • CVC
  • All upper secondary education certificates
  • Multicheck or S2-Check with evaluation in the professional field
