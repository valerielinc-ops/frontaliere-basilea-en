Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a process technician to continuously develop our logistics processes and technologies. In this role, you take care of operating resources and plants, drive Kaizen and TPM forward and support our employees in introducing and optimizing new processes.

What is the role about?

Are you enthusiastic about logistics, technology and continuous improvement? Do you want to analyze processes and derive sustainable optimizations from them? Do you enjoy working with people and support them in successfully using new technologies and processes?

Then you can expect a varied role at the interface between logistics, technology and process optimization, in which you can contribute your ideas and actively shape the further development of our logistics.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • ensure the technical support of the equipment and equipment as well as the reliable operation of our AGV systems.
  • promote the implementation and further development of Kaizen and TPM, create process analyses and derive targeted improvement measures from them.
  • support the introduction of new products, processes and equipment.
  • Develop new solutions for packaging, storage, transport and kitting as well as optimise storage layouts and land use.
  • support the management team in the development of employees, create training materials and carry out training and training.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have basic training as a logistician EFZ or technical training with several years of experience in logistics.
  • have ideally completed further training as a logistics specialist or process specialist.
  • are inquisitive, affine with process and technology, think analytically and have experience with project and lean methods.
  • master MS Office and have experience in dealing with ERP systems such as SAP.
  • communicate clearly, organize sovereignly and enjoy working with people.
  • have very good knowledge of German, English is an advantage.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support the work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly offers, including childcare and childcare services during the holidays.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
