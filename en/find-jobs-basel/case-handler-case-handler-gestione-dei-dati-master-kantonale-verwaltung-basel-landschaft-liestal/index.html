Full description

Tasks

  • Maintenance and management of master data as well as tax obligations of natural and legal persons in the IT system NEST
  • Allocation, validation and post-processing of electronic messages
  • Processing of internal reports from various business areas of the tax administration as well as reports from the commercial register
  • Recording of powers of attorney, delivery and succession representative addresses
  • Management of postal returns and corresponding post-processing
  • Ensuring high data quality and ongoing review of data stocks
  • Information provision and contact with taxpayers, their representatives and local and cantonal authorities
  • Participation in IT projects and the further development of processes

Requirements

  • Commercial education with proven professional experience in the commercial sector
  • Good MS Office knowledge and affinity for complex IT applications
  • Precise, structured and fast working methods – even with a high volume of work
  • High customer orientation and strong willingness to provide services
  • Quick comprehension and reliable and careful working methods
