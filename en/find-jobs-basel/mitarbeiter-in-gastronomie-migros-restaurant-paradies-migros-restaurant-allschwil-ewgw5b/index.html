Full description

You love to welcome guests with a smile and offer them a great experience? As a restaurant staff, you are an important part of our team and ensure smooth processes in the service and buffet area. Bring your passion for gastronomy – we look forward to seeing you!

Mansioni

  • Service of the cash register and execution of daily cash registers
  • Competent advice of guests and active sale of products
  • Service and management of buffets and display cases including attractive presentation of goods
  • Clean-up and cleaning work in compliance with HA-Booge guidelines

Requisiti

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in the catering industry
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail trade
  • Pleasure with the handling of guests and a strong service orientation
  • Reliable, structured and careful working methods
  • Flexible readiness for use and openness for changing areas of work
  • High awareness of responsibility and independent work
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Occupational pension: Migros Pension Fund guarantees the financial security of Migros employees in the third phase of their life

  • Migros Bank: Private and investment account with preferential interest rates and free bank account management
  • Employee offers: Professional home with one of the most successful and financially sound Swiss companies.
  • Individual continuing training: promotion of vocational training
