About the Company

The purpose of Idorsia is to discover, develop, and commercialize innovative medicines to help more patients.

Headquartered in Switzerland – a biotech-hub of Europe – Idorsia is a high-potential biopharmaceutical company, specialized in the discovery, development and commercialization of innovative small molecules, with the aim of transforming the horizon of therapeutic options.

We are now looking for a highly motivated Postdoctoral Researcher in the Drug Metabolism and Pharmacokinetics (DMPK) section of our Nonclinical Development department (2 years).

Job Responsibilities

  • Establish and evaluate New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) such as advanced in vitro hepatic systems to improve the prediction of human clearance, with a particular focus on low-turnover compounds.
  • Compare intrinsic clearance data generated in conventional systems, such as human liver microsomes and cryopreserved hepatocytes, with data from more complex liver-cell models such as HepaRG, HepatoPac, TruVivo or related systems.
  • Develop and apply robust LC-MS/MS bioanalytical methods to quantify parent compound depletion and, where appropriate, metabolite formation in those assays.
  • Investigate the impact of nonspecific binding, compound stability, incubation recovery and free concentration on the interpretation of clearance data and support in vitro–in vivo extrapolation in collaboration with DMPK modelers.
  • Assess the suitability of advanced hepatic systems for drug-metabolizing enzyme phenotyping in comparison with human liver microsomes, integrating metabolite identification where appropriate.
  • Document, analyze and present experimental results in multidisciplinary project teams, and contribute to scientific reports, internal presentations, conference communications and peer-reviewed publications.

Candidate’s Requirements

  • PhD in pharmaceutical sciences, analytical chemistry, biochemistry, biology, pharmacology or a related discipline, with solid scientific training and a strong interest in applying experimental and analytical skills to DMPK questions.
  • Hands-on experience with cell culture and LC-MS/MS bioanalysis is required; experience with high-resolution MS and/or metabolite identification is an advantage.
  • Experience with in vitro ADME/DMPK assays, such as microsomal or hepatocyte stability, protein binding, drug-metabolizing enzyme phenotyping, is desirable.
  • Good understanding of drug metabolism, hepatic clearance mechanisms, enzyme phenotyping and in vitro–in vivo extrapolation, or strong motivation to develop expertise in these areas.
  • Proactive, collaborative and reliable personality with strong analytical thinking, excellent organizational and communication skills, and the ability to work effectively in a multidisciplinary and multicultural environment.
  • Excellent knowledge of English.
