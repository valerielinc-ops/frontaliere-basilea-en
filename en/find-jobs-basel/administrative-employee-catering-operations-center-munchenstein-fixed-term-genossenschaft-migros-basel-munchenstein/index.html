Full description

You have an education as a chef/chef EFZ and would like to use your gastronomic knowledge in an administrative function? In this temporary position, you support our team with organizational and administrative tasks. With your reliable working method and your sense of planning, you contribute to smooth processes at the Munichstein headquarters. 📋🍽️

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, BZM GMBS, Ruchfeldstrasse 15, 4142 Munichstein

Important information

Working days from Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:00 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Transfer and integration of existing recipes from different systems into a central master system
  • Testing, maintaining and ensuring the completeness of recipe data
  • Critical review of recipes in terms of composition, plausibility and quality
  • Technical evaluation of ingredients, quantities and manufacturing processes
  • Identification of inconsistencies, missing information or optimization potential in existing recipes
  • Close cooperation with specialist areas to clarify and complete recipe data
  • Ensuring a uniform and structured data quality in the master system

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience as a chef & in administration: gastronomy
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as cook or food sector
  • High affinity for digital systems and enjoyment of structured work
  • Good knowledge of recipe preparation, food processes and ingredients
  • Good knowledge of common Microsoft Office applications
  • German (flowing)
  • French (advantageous)
