The Position In Basel Biotech Manufacturing, we manufacture pharmaceutical active ingredients. For the production of antibodies, we have modern, highly automated production facilities at our Basel site. The production and implementation of these processes take place in compliance with cGMP.

The possibility – Your tasks

  • You take full operational action and accompany our biopharmaceutical production processes from approach to harvest.
  • Thanks to your laboratory background, you understand both worlds and work closely with the laboratory team to optimally coordinate process flows and sampling.
  • You carry out process steps precisely and in strict compliance with all hygiene and safety requirements.
  • You control automated plants on a production scale and always keep an eye on all process parameters.
  • You document your work steps seamlessly and comprehensibly, including electronic documentation in the Manufacturing Execution System (MES).
  • You take over the first troubleshooting in your own area of responsibility and actively support the maintenance and maintenance of plants and equipment.
  • Shift work (USP 6/4): You work as an operator in the continuous 365-day shift operation fully in production.

Who are you – Your profile

  • You have completed vocational training as a biology laboratory, chemical laboratory or in a comparable profession and are motivated to work fully as an operator in production.
  • You bring a sound understanding of GMP and ideally already have experience in pharmaceutical or biotechnology production.
  • You have experience in handling automated production facilities on a production scale.
  • You are open to new ideas, actively participate and also question conventional thought patterns.
  • You work performance-oriented, team-ready and respectful in a cooperative environment – both within the shift and at the interface to the laboratory.
  • You act in a quality and safety-conscious manner and always keep our high standards in mind.
  • You are flexible, adaptable (especially with regard to the 365-day shift plan) and have very good knowledge of German in words and writing (English is an advantage).
