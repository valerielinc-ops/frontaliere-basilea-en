Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in drug production, we are looking for a dedicated Lab Technician R&D (a), 100%.

In our production facility you produce pharmaceutical peptides under GMP-operations. Through the practical training you will gain the necessary safety in order to work independently and independently on our production plants. You like to actively participate and have pleasure in varied tasks.

Tasks

  • preparation, implementation and evaluation of laboratory tests
  • development and optimization of the manufacturing process from synthesis to purification to final active substance and production of peptides
  • responsibility for the analysis of experiments and interpretation of results
  • active participation in the improvement of our equipment and work processes and maintenance of laboratory infrastructure
  • involvement in scale-up projects and optimization of the manufacturing process as well as evaluation of new technologies and reagents
  • ensuring clean documentation

Requirements

  • Completed training as a chemical laboratory technician (EFZ) or comparable training or professional experience
  • Enjoyment of the preparation of organic chemistry in an innovative environment
  • Flair for handling technical equipment and software
  • A structured, clean and independent working method
  • Flexibility, communication and willingness to learn
  • Very good German knowledge, English knowledge are advantageous

We offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements with possibility for home office and option for additional holidays with reduced work costs
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% acceptance of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG and possibility for over-compulsory pension provision with our electoral savings plan
  • Access to the benefit platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our

Staff restaurant - Varied free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
