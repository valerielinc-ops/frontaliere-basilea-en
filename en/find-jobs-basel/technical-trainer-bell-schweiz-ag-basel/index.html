Full description

Are you looking for a role where you can apply your technical expertise and professionally guide young people on their training path? Found it! As a Technical Trainer, you will support our apprentices on their journey into their professional future and actively shape their training at Bell Schweiz AG in Basel. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Supervise, together with the vocational and practical trainers of the technical workshop, apprentices in the professions of Automation Technician EFZ, Production Mechanic EFZ, and Electrical Installer EFZ
  • Teach practical skills—particularly in the field of automation
  • Assist in apprentice recruitment, conduct site interviews, oversee learning documentation, and handle additional administrative tasks
  • Plan, alongside the team leaders of the technical departments, the deployments of in-company training
  • Contribute to the creation and updating of the training program
  • Support apprentices in preparing and executing their Individual Practical Assignment (IPA)
  • Organize and conduct trial days and create suitable programs
  • Represent Bell Schweiz AG at career fairs and other vocational training events
  • Maintain contacts within Bell Schweiz's vocational training and with professional associations, actively contributing to the development of technical training professions ##
  • Requirements
  • A completed apprenticeship as an Automation Technician or in a related field
  • Practical training skills and ideally experience in supervising apprentices
  • Strong social skills and enjoyment in working with young people
  • Independent, proactive, resilient, and reliable work approach
  • Teamwork skills and willingness to follow defined processes while taking responsibility
  • Motivation to continuously expand and share your knowledge and skills
