The Position At our location in Basel, we offer an exciting internship opportunity. In the Basel Drug Substance Manufacturing division, we produce active ingredients for cancer therapy in the hybrid single use (SUT) and antibody drug conjugate (ADC) production plant. These biotechnological processes take place with the help of highly automated plants in compliance with Current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP). As an intern in production, you work independently on optimization and improvement projects and learn how GMP production works from start to finish. The possibility

  • Implementation and support of interdisciplinary projects for process optimization.
  • Analyze process flows and develop optimization options.
  • Collaboration with various interfaces, such as quality, engineering, manufacturing science & technology.
  • Cooperation in the processing of root cause analyses within the framework of process deviations and in the implementation of the associated measures.
  • Get to know and support routine tasks (e.g. document maintenance, raw data evaluation, etc.).
  • Cooperation on compliance and SHE topics as well as continuous improvement processes (CIP) and support material management.
  • The GMP-compliant implementation and documentation of their tasks in the area with focus on quality of the product and processes. Who you are
  • You are currently enrolled in a master’s programme, preferably in the fields of natural sciences (e.g. pharmacy, biology, chemistry, life sciences) or in the field of engineering (e.g. biotechnology, pharmaceutical technology, process engineering) or you have completed your studies within 12 months before the start date.
  • Proactively share knowledge with excellent organizational and communication skills.
  • Good knowledge of MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and of Google applications (Drive, Docs, Tables, Presentations) - a general IT affinity (e.g. interest in automation using Google scripts) is required.
  • Very good oral and written German as well as good English skills. Additional information
  • Your application must include a CV and a letter of motivation.
  • Please note, due to regulations, non-EU/EFTA citizens are required to submit a certificate from the university as part of the application documents, stating that an internship is mandatory, as well as the duration of the mandatory internship.
  • The internship is not suitable for a master thesis. Ready for the next step? We look forward to hearing from you. Apply now to discover this exciting opportunity!
