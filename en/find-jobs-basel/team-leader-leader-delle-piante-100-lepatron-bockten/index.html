Le Patron is part of the internationally active ORIOR Group with around 2300 employees and headquarters in Zurich. Family cooperation and a positive team spirit create the basis for an open and team-oriented work culture. At Le Patron, with a team of around 130 employees, we are committed every day with pleasure and passion to the production of our premium products.
