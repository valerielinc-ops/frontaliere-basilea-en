Full description

Looking forward to work: 100 % healthy Steinentorstrasse 13, 4010 Basel Our diversity is our capital: CONCORDIA brings together people from different cultures, professions and areas of responsibility. Will you soon enrich our team too? For our agency in Basel, we are looking for a versatile and open-minded person like you.

What you can expect

  • You acquire new customers and sell needs-based health insurance and pension solutions.
  • You independently look after existing policyholders and maintain daily contact with customers.
  • You prepare quotations, carry out administrative tasks and proactively arrange appointments.
  • You support trade fair and stand activities and represent CONCORDIA on site.

What you bring

  • You have completed vocational training in a service- or sales-oriented field.
  • Customer contact, sales and consulting are your passion, and your administrative skills complete your profile.
  • You have very good German language skills, and any additional language is an advantage.
  • You have completed the VBV training or are willing to complete it - we support you in doing so.

Your place of work

Why CONCORDIA As a health expert, CONCORDIA is committed to a working environment where employees feel comfortable and can fulfil their potential. It is characterised by varied tasks, diverse teams and a family-like atmosphere. We also motivate our employees to develop further and progress. Pension fund We offer you an extremely attractive and secure pension fund with above-average employer contributions and generous benefits. Health insurance premium contribution You and your family benefit from reduced health insurance premiums. Appreciation In the CONCORDIA family, you experience appreciation and team spirit.
