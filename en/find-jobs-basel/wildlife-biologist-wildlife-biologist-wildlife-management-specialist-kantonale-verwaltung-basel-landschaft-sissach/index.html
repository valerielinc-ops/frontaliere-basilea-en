Office for Forest and Wildlife of both Basel

Our mission is to preserve the forest and wildlife for current and future generations. As a service of the Cantons of Basel-Landschaft and Basel-Stadt, we implement forest, hunting and fishing legislation, advise governments and directorates on forestry and wildlife policy issues and promote dialogue between stakeholders. Together we create a diverse, resilient, value-adding and accessible living space. We see ourselves as a service provider for the benefit of humans and animals. We are committed to the conservation and development of the landscape and the protection of native animal species. Contemporary and animal welfare-compliant hunting and fishing based on wild ecological criteria is a central concern.
