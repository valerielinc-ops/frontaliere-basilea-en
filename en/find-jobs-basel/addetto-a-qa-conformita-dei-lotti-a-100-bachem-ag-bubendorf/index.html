Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in quality assurance, we are looking for motivated Associates QA - Batch Compliance (a) in a 100% effort.

Their tasks

  • Examination of GMP production documentation on paper or in the electronic system PAS-X for the release of our active substances
  • Controlling documentation from intermediate and output stages for GMP compliance and compliance with internal rules
  • Discussion of abnormalities with the operators in the production areas and agreement on the necessary corrective measures
  • Determining the eligibility of customers to purchase our active ingredients, reviewing shipping documentation of our GMP products
  • Implement the GDP (Good Distribution Practice) policy in the area of provision, handling and distribution of our GMP products

Your profile

  • Vocational apprenticeship as a chemical laboratory technician, chemical-technical assistant (CTA), chemical and pharmatechnologist or comparable training
  • At least 2 years of professional experience within the chemical/pharmaceutical industry, preferably in the GMP environment resp. In the control of GMP production documentation
  • They are used to creating documents, checking them and implementing corrective measures. Experience in dealing with electronic quality management systems is beneficial
  • You are an independent and responsible person with a careful and team-oriented way of working and a high understanding of quality assurance aspects
  • You have a very good oral and written language in German and have a good knowledge of English
  • You have interest and experience with electronic systems for batch documentation (MES) and secure handling of Microsoft Office. SAP knowledge is an advantage

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements possible
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
