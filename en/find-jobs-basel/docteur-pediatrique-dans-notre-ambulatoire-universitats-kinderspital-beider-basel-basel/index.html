Full description

Our interdisciplinary emergency department at UKBB treats around 40,000 patients of all ages and severity levels every year. To strengthen our team in the affiliated ambulatorium, we are looking for 1. November 2026 or by arrangement a dedicated and flexible personality for assignments by individual arrangement. You can expect a varied job with a high level of personal responsibility in a collegial environment. The function is ideal for doctors who want to make their clinical activity flexible or expand their experience in pediatric care.

Entry date: 1 November 2026 or by arrangement

Their tasks

  • Independent assessment and treatment of outpatient walk-in emergency patients
  • Counselling and support of patients and their families during the treatment process
  • Close cooperation with medical practice assistants and the team of the emergency department
  • Contributing to high-quality, efficient and patient-oriented care
  • Targeted and resource-saving use of the diagnostic and therapeutic possibilities of the emergency unit
  • Support for a smooth operation in the ambulatory

Your profile

  • Federal medical diploma or an equivalent recognised degree
  • Professional experience in a pediatric environment is beneficial
  • Independent, responsible and patient-oriented working methods
  • The joy of caring for children, young people and their families
  • Flexibility for assignments by individual arrangement

Our offer

  • Independent participation in the motivated team
  • Specialist supervision if necessary
  • Perfect for a gentle re-entry and to develop practical skills in paediatric medicine
  • Gathering experience in the tertiary medical environment
  • Admission to a job pool for enquiries for planned or short-term vacancies in the ambulatory
