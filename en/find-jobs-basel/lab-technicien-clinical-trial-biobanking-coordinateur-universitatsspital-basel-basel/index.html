Department Biomedizin

Das Universitätsspital Basel nimmt eine führende Rolle in der (inter-)nationalen Medizin- und Forschungslandschaft ein. Unser Campus im Herzen der Stadt Basel steht für Innovationskraft, Weiterentwicklungsmöglichkeiten und interdisziplinäre Zusammenarbeit. Start date: by arrangement One year with option to extend Petersgraben 4, 4031 Basel Wer beim Unispital Basel arbeitet, profitiert von attraktiven Arbeitsbedingungen und vielen Vorteilen.

Das können Sie bewegen

  • Performing laboratory work for translational research projects and clinical studies, including the processing and storage of biological samples
  • Processing blood, tissue and other biomaterial samples in accordance with established laboratory standards and study-specific protocols
  • Organizing, managing and ensuring the quality of biobanking processes, as well as maintaining the associated databases
  • Supporting the planning, coordination and implementation of clinical studies
  • Ensuring complete and traceable documentation of laboratory and study data in accordance with GCP guidelines

Das bringen Sie mit

  • A qualification as a Biomedical Laboratory Scientist (BMA HF), Medical Laboratory Technician (MTA), Lab Technician or an equivalent qualification in the natural sciences
  • Solid practical experience in processing biological samples and working with modern laboratory procedures
  • Knowledge of biobanking processes and an interest in translational research and clinical studies
  • A structured, meticulous and quality-conscious approach to your work
  • Enthusiasm for working in an interdisciplinary and international research environment
  • Very good German and good English language skills

Benefits dieser Stelle

  • A varied laboratory role with a high level of responsibility in translational immuno-oncology, connecting research and clinical studies
  • The opportunity to contribute to innovative research on cancer, infection and immune-based therapies
  • An international and collaborative team with close links to physicians, study teams and research partners
