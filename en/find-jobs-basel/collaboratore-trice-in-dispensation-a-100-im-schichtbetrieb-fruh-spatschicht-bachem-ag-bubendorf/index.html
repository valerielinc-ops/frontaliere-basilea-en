Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in dispensation, we are looking for a dedicated employee in dispensation (a) 100% in shift operation (early/late shift)

Their tasks

  • Precise postponing of solid and liquid chemical raw materials, intermediate and final products
  • Conscious work and recording of the activities carried out
  • Provision of the necessary utensils for portioning
  • Carrying out cleaning and calibration work on equipment and premises
  • Ensure that all activities are carried out in strict compliance with GMP, occupational safety and environmental regulations

Your profile

  • Ideally, you will have an education in the chemical/pharmaceutical sector. However, we also accept dossiers from candidates with professional experience in the regulated environment (chemistry, pharmaceuticals, food, etc.)
  • Experience with cleanroom activities and working under GMP is desirable, but not necessarily
  • They are extremely reliable, work independently and are physically resilient
  • You have a structured way of working that allows you to keep track of things at all times
  • Your German skills are good

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
