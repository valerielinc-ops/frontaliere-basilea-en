Full description

In our rehabilitation clinic in Basel, we treat patients in the field of post-acute internistic-oncological and geriatric rehabilitation through modern and patient-centered care. Your work thus remains versatile and varied. The team maintains open and clear communication, the decision paths are short and your ideas and suggestions are welcome. So here you have the opportunity to get involved and actively participate.

Your tasks

  • Medical care: Take over the medical care of the inpatient patients in close cooperation with the medical cadre.
  • Rehabilitation planning: Design the planning of the individual rehabilitation of patients and accompany them from the entrance examination, through regular visits to the exit examination
  • Communication: You are the contact person for medical questions for the involved professional groups such as care and therapy as well as for assignment and follow-up doctors and relatives during the stay

Your profile

  • Qualifications: Federal or MEBEKO-recognized state examination, of advantage already experience in the field of general internal medicine
  • Personality: You bring professional and social competence for a good interdisciplinary cooperation and work in a structured and independent manner

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Join a new modern work environment and get actively involved
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies and benefit from benefit@work discounts
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the Wellness - Therme FORTYSEVEN
