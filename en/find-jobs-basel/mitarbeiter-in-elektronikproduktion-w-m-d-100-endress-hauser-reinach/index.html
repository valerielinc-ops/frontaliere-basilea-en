Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for an employee in sensor manufacturing (m/f/d) to complement our team and make an important contribution to the production of our Coriolis flowmeters. In this role, you ensure with your quality awareness and care that our products meet the highest standards. Endress+Hauser Flow develops and produces innovative flow meters for customers worldwide at its location in Reinach. Together, we create solutions that make an important contribution to safety, efficiency and sustainability in a wide range of industries.

What is this point about?

Do you like working in a modern production environment? Do you want to make precision and quality visible every day? Do you appreciate working together in a well-established team that takes joint responsibility for high-quality products?

Then this position could be just right for you. As part of a team of around ten people in sensor production, you contribute to the production of innovative Coriolis flowmeters. The position is located within the production of Endress+Hauser Flow.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Produce Coriolis flow meters as part of a team of around ten
  • Loading and operating test, blasting and welding equipment
  • Assessment of welds and surfaces with regard to quality and design
  • Working flexibly in two-shift operation and ensuring a smooth production process
  • Maintaining facilities and workplaces in very good condition through the application of 5S and TPM methods
  • Actively participate in the continuous improvement process and contribute optimization potential

What do we expect from you?

They

  • Have completed basic education, ideally in a technical profession
  • Bring experience in a manufacturing environment
  • Have a strong quality awareness, especially when assessing surfaces
  • Ideally have experience in operating manufacturing plants
  • Ideally have experience in assessing welds
  • Master the German language well in word and writing and are characterized by flexibility, personal responsibility, willingness to learn and team spirit

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision
