Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the Global Supply Chain Management Network & Demand Center, we are looking for a dedicated Demand Manager (a), 80-100%.

Your tasks

  • Develop, maintain, and continuously improve short-, mid-, and long-term demand forecasts based on market trends, historical data, and business intelligence
  • Lead the monthly Demand Review process of your assigned product group as part of the S&OP cycle.
  • Analyze forecast accuracy and deviations and implement corrective actions
  • Collaborate with Sales, Project Management and Supply Planning to align on demand change
  • Utilize forecasting tools and ERP systems (SAP/IBP) for data analysis and forecast generation
  • Prepare reports and KPI analyses to support decision-making

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in Demand Planning, Forecasting, or Supply Chain
  • Strong knowledge of S&OP processes and forecasting methodologies
  • Proficiency in ERP systems (SAP/IBP) and forecasting tools
  • Analytical mindset, strong numerical skills, and structured working style
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Fluent in English (written and spoken), German is a plus

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with home office days and an option for obtaining additional vacation days through workload reduction
  • Employee development through numerous internal and external training opportunities
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as option for extra-mandatory pension provision with our Pension Plan Plus
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant

Would you like to drive innovation together with us?
