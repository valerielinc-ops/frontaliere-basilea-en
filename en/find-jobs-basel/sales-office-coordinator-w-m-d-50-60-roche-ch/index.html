About the entity For our Restaurant & Catering division, we are looking for a motivated and service-oriented personality as Sales Office Coordinator (50-60% - temporary). In this versatile function, you support our Sales Office in the professional, timely and customer-oriented processing of daily administrative business. You will form an important interface between our customers and the operational teams on site.

Your task n

  • Administrative order processing: Professional processing and coordination of customer requests, reservations, offers and catering orders
  • Data & order management: Careful collection, maintenance and tracking of relevant customer and order data as well as ensuring high data and order quality.
  • Interface coordination: Reliable and traceable forwarding of orders to the responsible operational areas
  • Customer communication: Active and friendly communication with customers in connection with ongoing bookings, adjustments and requests.
  • Operational support: In the event of stress peaks, events or personnel bottlenecks, you support the team flexibly and actively in event and seat catering

Your professional l

  • Training & experience: Completed vocational training in the commercial sector, in the catering or hotel industry as well as practical experience in a comparable service-oriented function
  • Further education: in the area of administration, sales or customer service are advantageous
  • Professional competence: experience in administrative order, reservation or customer processing
  • Knowledge of catering or events is an advantage
  • Organizational skill: Very good administrative skills, structured working methods and the ability to set priorities and act calmly even in parallel tasks and last-minute changes
  • IT affinity: Secure handling of digital systems (e.g. ERP, reservation and offer tools) as well as good knowledge of Google and Microsoft Workspace
  • Languages: Very good oral and written knowledge of German and English
  • Personality: High customer and service orientation, independence, reliability as well as team spirit and flexibility for occasional operational assignments
