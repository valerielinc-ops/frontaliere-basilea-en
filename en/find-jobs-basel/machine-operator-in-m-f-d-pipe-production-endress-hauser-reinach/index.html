They process mechanical components precisely and operate modern production facilities within efficient manufacturing processes. In addition, you ensure stable plant operation and actively promote continuous improvements in quality and processes. What tasks will you take on?

  • They take over the mechanical processing of components by means of sawing, bending and erosion and ensure precise production.

  • They work consistently according to LEAN principles and actively contribute to the continuous improvement of processes and processes.
  • They ensure the high availability of the systems in 2-shift operation and correct faults including clean documentation in 1st level support.
  • They actively promote quality and process assurance measures such as 5S, CIP and employee training. What do we expect from you?
