Full description

Contribute expertise. Take responsibility. Develop together. Financial and Church Directorate Tax administration Frenkendorf By agreement

Your tasks – versatile, responsible and with creative leeway

  • Support of the divisional management in operational management as well as taking over the deputy in day-to-day business and co-responsibility for the technically correct and timely completion of the tasks of the division
  • Independent processing of objections and drafting of objections in the fields of real estate taxes and inheritance and gift taxes
  • Personal and written advice for customers, notaries and their representatives
  • Independent processing of complex tax law issues as well as assessment of tax rulings in the assigned specialist area
  • Examination of requests for the benefit of the Cantonal Decree and Taxation Commission and preparation of well-founded written opinions
  • Active participation in the implementation of the vision, strategy and annual goals of the cantonal tax administration as well as in cross-divisional projects
  • Joint development and implementation of business objectives with division management
  • Assumption of management and decision-making tasks within the framework of the deputy regulation and the possibility of direct human and professional management of a small department

Your profile – professionally experienced, communicative and leadership-strong

  • Bachelor or Master of Law
  • Experience in tax law, ideally in real estate taxes and/or inheritance and gift tax law
  • Open, team-oriented and responsible personality with an independent, structured and exact working method
  • Enjoying direct contact with different stakeholders as well as strong negotiating skills
  • Appropriate oral and written language and the ability to convey even challenging issues in an understandable way
  • Persuasion, assertiveness and a safe presence
  • Ability to quickly grasp economic and legal contexts, to assess them in a sound manner and to develop pragmatic solutions, as well as experience in accounting law and accounting
  • Interested in assuming leadership responsibility, actively supporting the divisional management and helping to shape the further development of the business area

Tax administration

The Basel-Landschaft Tax Administration is responsible for the correct collection of taxes from legal and natural persons. Your employees check the tax returns, make the assessments and receive the state and direct federal tax as well as the municipal tax in the contractual relationship. Similarly, the assessment and receipt of property profit, change of hands, inheritance and gift taxes and the processing of withholding taxes belong to the field of activity of the cantonal tax administration.
