We are hiring four Associate Directors, AI Engineering to join BR Data & Digital, Software & Workflows in Basel. In this role, you will be a senior technical authority for production-grade agentic AI.

This is a hands-on, deep-technical individual contributor role, not a people-management position. You will own complex architecture and design decisions, establish engineering standards and reference architectures, and remain actively involved in engineering.

The Software & Workflows organization partners with scientists, data experts and AI specialists across Novartis Biomedical Research to transform scientific workflows and laboratory processes. Our ambition is to make advanced digital and AI capabilities usable, scalable and impactful, helping teams generate better insights, improve decisions and accelerate the discovery of innovative medicines.

You will set the technical direction for agentic AI across a broad portfolio of software delivery initiatives, leading through technical influence. Working across engineering, science, architecture, operations, security and enterprise technology, you will translate complex needs into scalable solutions while ensuring high standards for quality, performance, security, compliance and reliability.

Job Description What you'll work on You will help shape agentic AI for drug discovery, scientific workflow automation and lab-in-the-loop systems. This includes the architecture behind complex multi-agent platforms supporting target identification and the broader discovery pipeline, automation of cross-functional research workflows, and systems that integrate AI reasoning with wet-lab experimentation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own the architecture and design of agentic AI platforms and solutions end to end, remaining hands-on through development, deployment, monitoring, reliability and continuous improvement.
  • Take complex technical problems from ambiguous business needs through to hardened production solutions.
  • Transform existing products, processes and workflows into scalable agentic AI-enabled capabilities.
  • Own architecture decisions for agentic systems and LLM inference at scale on AWS, including Amazon Bedrock and AgentCore, balancing reliability, latency, cost, security and governance.
  • Establish engineering best practices and reference architectures for AI system performance, scalability, security, governance and maintainability.
  • Define DevOps, MLOps and AgentOps standards, including CI/CD, infrastructure as code, containerization, reproducible pipelines, model/data/prompt versioning and experiment tracking.
  • Guide the evaluation, integration and optimization of AI models and orchestration frameworks such as Strands, LangGraph, CrewAI or custom frameworks.
  • Define evaluation and observability standards, including agent test sets, LLM-as-judge or human-review loops, tracing and cost/latency monitoring.
  • Partner with product, data, architecture, security and business stakeholders to define technical roadmaps, platform standards and priorities aligned with business goals.
  • Raise the technical bar through design reviews, coding standards and technical mentorship, leading through technical influence rather than direct reports.

Essential Requirements

  • 8+ years of professional software/AI engineering experience, including shipping production-grade AI solutions, with a track record as a hands-on technical lead.
  • Deep, hands-on expertise building AI agents on AWS, including Amazon Bedrock (models, Agents, Knowledge Bases and Guardrails) and Bedrock AgentCore (agent runtime, memory, tool access and identity).
  • Proficiency with at least one agent framework such as Strands, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, CrewAI or custom orchestration, with the judgment to determine when a simple workflow is preferable to a multi-agent design.
  • Strong Python expertise and experience with AWS services commonly used in agent architectures, including Lambda, Step Functions, ECS/EKS, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3 and IAM.
  • Experience with RAG, including chunking, embeddings and vector stores such as OpenSearch or pgvector, together with tool-use/function calling connecting agents to APIs, databases and enterprise systems. MCP experience is preferred.
  • Deep DevOps/MLOps expertise, including CI/CD, infrastructure as code using CDK or Terraform, containers/orchestration, reproducible pipelines, ML evaluation and observability, with experience setting these standards for a team.
  • Ability to take an ambiguous business problem through to a hardened production solution incorporating security, guardrails and robust error handling.
  • Demonstrated ability to set technical direction, drive design decisions across engineers and communicate technical trade-offs clearly to product and business partners.

Desirable Requirements

  • Experience operationalizing AI coding tools across engineering teams and working with vendor or external engineering partners.
  • Experience with AI governance and compliance in a regulated or life-sciences environment, including awareness of the EU AI Act.
