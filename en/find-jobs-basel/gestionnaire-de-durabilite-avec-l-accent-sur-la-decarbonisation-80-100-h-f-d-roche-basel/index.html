The Position

At Roche, we not only develop life-saving therapies – we also take responsibility for the future of our planet. Our goal is clear: We want to achieve net zero greenhouse gas emissions across the entire value chain (scope 1-3) by 2045 at the latest.

For our locations in Basel and Kaiseraugst, we are therefore looking for a driving force that translates our global sustainability strategy into tangible, local reality. In a location that combines our entire value chain – from early research to development and production to delivery of the medicines – you build the bridge between ambitious sustainability goals and operational excellence.

As part of the global group function “Corporate Strategy, Sustainability & Sites”, the organization “Site Network Basel/Kaiseraugst” forms the foundation of our location. We not only provide state-of-the-art infrastructure and services, but also develop them in a future-oriented way. We network with all stakeholders along the entire value chain – from early research to patient care.

What makes us special is the way we work together: You can look for classic supervisors in vain. Instead, you expect a modern organizational model that offers you maximum freedom of design and decision-making. With us, you have the freedom to use your talents independently for an absolutely meaningful mission.

Strategy meets real impact

Within the organization “Site Network Basel/Kaiseraugst” we are responsible for the implementation of CO2 neutrality and the sustainable operation of the energy infrastructure in the cluster “First Net Zero Site”. As a decarbonisation manager, you are at the heart of our local decarbonisation agenda in a central interface function.

Your opportunity

  • Strategic Project & Concept Management: You will develop the site-specific decarbonization strategy (Scope 1, 2 & 3), proactively advance the topic-specific roadmaps and translate them into pragmatic action plans.
  • Stakeholder management at all levels: You act as a bridge builder between site management, real estate management, production teams and various global organizations such as the Corporate Sustainability team.
  • Change Management & Communication: You create awareness of sustainability among employees and make our progress transparent for site management, committees and stakeholders.

Who you are

We are looking for a personality who sees challenges as opportunities. We rely on an entrepreneurial personality who takes a proactive approach as a real maker and who prefers to achieve their goals through teamwork.
