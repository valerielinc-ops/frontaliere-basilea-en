Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for an employee in Coriolis Production High Volume to complement our team and contribute with care, technical skill and quality awareness to the production of our high-quality flow meters. DRSMA is an important production site within the Endress+Hauser Group and stands for the highest quality standards, innovative manufacturing technologies and close cooperation across teams.

What is this point about?

Do you enjoy creating high-quality technical products with your own hands?

Do you like to work in an experienced team where precision, quality and mutual support count?

Do you want your work to help ensure that measuring instruments can be used reliably in industrial processes worldwide?

In this position, you will be part of a dedicated production team and take on a wide range of assembly and testing activities along the manufacturing process of our Coriolis flowmeters. The office is located in the DRSMA production area and offers you the opportunity to actively participate in the production of technologically demanding products.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Mount complete Coriolis flow meters mainly in small series.
  • Perform complex assembly activities on high-quality measuring instruments.
  • Sensors weld, solder and test at test stations.
  • As part of a team of around 12 people, we contribute to the production of our Coriolis flow meters.
  • Work flexibly in 2-shift operation and, if necessary, make individual assignments on Saturday mornings.
  • Actively shape the continuous improvement process and contribute optimization potential in the work processes.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • Have completed technical vocational training.
  • Bring knowledge in assembling and ideally have experience in welding.
  • Have good eyesight for processing and soldering small parts.
  • Have very good knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • Working independently, reliably and quality-consciously.
  • Contribute with your team spirit and perseverance to a successful cooperation.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We offer you a careful familiarization with a versatile and responsible activity.
  • We work in a modern production environment with high quality standards and modern infrastructure.
  • We promote an open and appreciative team culture in which cooperation and mutual support are a matter of course.
  • We offer attractive employment conditions as well as long-term prospects in an internationally successful company.
