The Adullam Foundation is a leading institution for age medicine, therapy and care in northwestern Switzerland. In Basel and Riehen, it operates a hospital and an affiliated care centre. The approximately 800 employees offer elderly people a regionally unique all-round care, from acute medicine to rehabilitation to transitional and long-term care. The holistic care in Adullam includes a high quality standard, lived humanity and great respect for old age.
