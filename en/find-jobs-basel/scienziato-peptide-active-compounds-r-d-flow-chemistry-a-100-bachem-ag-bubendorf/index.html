Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified specialists. To strengthen our team in Research & Development in Bubendorf/BL, we are looking for a dedicated Scientist Peptide Ingredients R&D Flow Chemistry (a), 100%. The field of work includes numerous different steps of the modern synthesis of peptide active ingredients, peptide fragments and amino acid building blocks in solution, as well as various technologies for the concentration and isolation of the products.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for the planning, analysis and interpretation of experiments as well as the presentation of the results obtained
  • Planning and timely implementation of development work or process optimisation in cooperation with other departments, taking into account quality, cost-effectiveness and safety
  • Scaling chemistry and equipment from the lab to pilot scale
  • Focus on the LPPS flow chemistry area (organic chemistry in solution, peptide couplings, protective group chemistry, extraction, precipitation, crystallization)
  • Writing reports and creating presentations on the work carried out for internal and external customers
  • Active contribution to the further development of the Bachem Flow Chemistry Platform
  • Active participation in the continuous improvement of our equipment and workflows as well as the maintenance of the laboratory infrastructure

Your profile

  • Master's degree or doctorate in chemical engineering or chemistry
  • Experience in preparative organic synthesis including flow chemistry and a soft spot for process technology
  • Structured, accurate and independent way of working that allows you to keep track of things at all times
  • Strong communication and organizational skills
  • Reliability, independence and ability to work in a team
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, both written and spoken
  • Knowledge of peptide chemistry and common analytical techniques such as UHPLC and LC-MS is an advantage

Our offer

  • A dynamic and fast-growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the possibility of working from home and the option to take additional vacation days with a reduced workload
  • Employee development through numerous internal and external training opportunities
  • 60% of pension fund contributions are covered by Bachem AG as well as the possibility of extra-mandatory pension provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
