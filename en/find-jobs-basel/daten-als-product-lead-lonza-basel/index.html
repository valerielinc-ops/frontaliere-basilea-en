Full description

Today, Lonza is a global leader in life sciences operating across five continents. While we work in science, there’s no magic formula to how we do it. Our greatest scientific solution is talented people working together, devising ideas that help businesses to help people. In exchange, we let our people own their careers. Their ideas, big and small, genuinely improve the world. And that’s the kind of work we want to be part of.

We are looking for Senior Director, Data-as-a-Product Lead to join us!

Data-as-a-Product Lead is accountable for the successful establishing delivery of the strategy, governance, and has end-to-end responsibility during execution of this multi-year transformation, leading the different workstreams required to establish scalable data products, customer-facing data services, and the foundations for future growth and differentiation.

This role sits at the intersection of business strategy, emerging technology, and operational transformation and owns the program vision, business case, scope, cost, schedule, value realization and overall program outcomes, ensuring alignment with the One Lonza Advanced Industry Program and digital business platform objectives.

The role provides overall leadership to the integrated Data-as-a-Product program and ensures effective coordination across all Lonza Business platforms, Engineering, Global IT/OT, Quality, Supply Chain and external delivery partners. Working closely with the Project Sponsor, Steering Committee and Program Leadership Team, the Data-as-a-Product Lead ensures that Lonza implements a future proof technology architecture, operating model and commercial model that supports scalable Data Products (e.g. ODH, Customer portal, etc)that deliver measurable business value.

The aim is to establish the operational capabilities, reusable data products, scalable operating models and a deployment blueprint that is supporting future network- wide adoption.

As a core member of the Advanced Industry Leadership Team, this role is critical to establishing Data-as-a-Product as a strategic capability that strengthens operational performance and customer relationships, differentiates Lonza in the market, and creates new opportunities for value creation.

  • Work closely with all stakeholders to monitor external trends and translate emerging opportunities into strategic recommendations and roadmap priorities
  • Act as primary program representative towards Sponsors, Steering Committee and Executive stakeholders. Scope, Cost & Schedule Accountability
