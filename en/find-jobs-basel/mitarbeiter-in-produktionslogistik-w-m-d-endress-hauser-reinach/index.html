Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for an employee in pipe production (m/f/d) to complement our team and to make an important contribution to our production processes with your technical know-how and your quality awareness. In this role, you support the reliable production of high-quality components and help ensure that our products meet the high quality standards of our customers. The office is located at DRS MR1.

As part of a dedicated production team, you will work in a modern manufacturing environment characterized by collaboration, continuous improvement and mutual appreciation.

What is this point about?

Do you want to actively shape modern production processes? Do you enjoy carrying out precise mechanical work and producing high-quality products? Do you like working in a team that promotes continuous improvement and personal responsibility?

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • carry out mechanical processing such as sawing, bending and erosion.
  • Operating and monitoring machines within our manufacturing processes.
  • work according to the principles of the LEAN philosophy and support continuous improvements in the working environment.
  • Maintain plants within the framework of Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) and contribute to their availability.
  • work flexibly in 2-shift operation.

What do we expect from you?

  • work independently and are equally committed to team tasks.

What can you expect from us?
