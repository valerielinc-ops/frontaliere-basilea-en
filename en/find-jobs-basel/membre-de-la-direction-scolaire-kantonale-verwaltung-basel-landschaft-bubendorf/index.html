Your responsibility

  • Together with the existing school management team, they take over the pedagogical, personnel, organizational and administrative leadership of the Bubendorf primary level.
  • They guide and accompany teachers and specialists in an appreciative, binding and solution-oriented manner.
  • Together with the team, they shape school development and implement development processes in a targeted and sustainable manner.
  • They are responsible for good cooperation with pupils, guardians, teachers, specialists, school board and community.
  • They ensure clear structures, transparent communication and reliable processes in everyday school life.
  • They are actively involved in a dedicated school management team and take responsibility for the further development of the entire school.

Your background

  • They have a teaching diploma for the primary level and several years of teaching experience.
  • They have a recognised school management education or are willing to take part in it.
  • They are a communicative, empathetic and leadership-strong personality and understand leadership as a combination of relationship, clarity and commitment.
  • They can take different perspectives, make decisions and represent them in a comprehensible way.
  • They enjoy school development and bring the ability to attract people to common goals.
  • Even in demanding situations, you will remain solution-oriented, reflective and professional.
  • They value cooperation in a committed and experienced team and contribute to an open and appreciative school culture.

Primary level Bubendorf

The school Bubendorf is a dedicated and well-organized primary school with clear structures and responsibilities. Our mission statement forms the common orientation framework: We want to be a school in which children and adults feel safe, accept responsibility, learn together and treat each other with respect and appreciation.At our school, around 380 students are taught in six kindergarten and 15 primary school classes by about 60 teachers.As a member of the school management, you can expect a professional environment with clear management structures, short decision-making paths and the opportunity to actively shape the school.

Organisation of school management

The school Bubendorf is run in the rector model. The Rector bears the overall responsibility for the educational, personnel and organizational management of the school. Together with the school management and other leaders, she or he ensures a future-oriented further development of the school.
