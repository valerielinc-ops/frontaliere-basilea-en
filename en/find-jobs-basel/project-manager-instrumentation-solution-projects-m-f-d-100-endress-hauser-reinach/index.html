Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a project manager instrumentation and solution projects 100% to complement our project team in Reinach. In this role, you combine sound product and application knowledge with customer-oriented consulting and contribute to converting technical inquiries into suitable, economical solutions.

What is at stake here?

Are you enthusiastic about sophisticated measurement technology? Do you like to find solutions for individual customer applications? Do you want to combine your expertise with consulting, sales and practical support? As a project manager for instrumentation and solution projects, you are the technical contact person for your product range. They support our customers and colleagues in the in-house and field service from the first request to the right solution and thus make an important contribution to customer satisfaction and sales development in the Sales Center Switzerland.

What tasks will you take on?

They

  • are responsible for the technical and commercial management of projects in the fields of automation and measurement technology for the process engineering industry – from project acquisition to project acceptance
  • manage and implement customer-specific solutions projects such as analysis panels, calibration cars and other individual measurement and automation solutions
  • create professional offers for projects in cooperation with internal and external bodies and negotiate them together with our sales teams
  • Ensure the technical and commercial implementation of customer requirements with our strategic measurement and automation solutions in various industries
  • hold all project threads in your hand and coordinate the internal and external project participants throughout the entire project process
  • ideally have knowledge of the chemical and/or pharmaceutical industry
  • Further industry knowledge is desirable
  • actively support our sales teams in the development and implementation of customer and project solutions
  • have the possibility to perform special tasks such as e.g. B. Embedded Engineering and strategic internal projects

What do we expect from you?

  • have completed technical studies in the fields of process engineering, electrical engineering or chemistry or have a comparable technical training
  • ideally look back on several years of professional experience in project management and/or in comparable technical tasks
  • already have experience in automation, metrology and project management
  • Commercial understanding is beneficial
  • are customer-oriented and enjoy working and communicating with internal and external customers and project partners
  • are characterized by analytical and conceptual skills as well as an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • are team-oriented, flexible and happy to take on responsibility
  • speak fluent German and have a good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We attach great importance to your work-life balance – with flexible working hours and the possibility of home office.
  • We offer a modern working environment with amenities such as an in-house canteen.
  • We support you with family-friendly offers such as childcare during the holidays and day care places.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.
