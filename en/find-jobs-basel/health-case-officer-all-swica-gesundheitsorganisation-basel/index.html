Full description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. This is how you shape health

  • You independently examine and process service cases in the ambulatory care area, in particular invoices, cost codes and service requests
  • You support our policyholders competently, solution-oriented and customer-oriented
  • You coordinate and correspond with customers, service providers, other insurance companies and authorities
  • You arrange for trusting medical clarifications and accompany them administratively
  • You support with the training and introduction of new employees and are happy to pass on your expertise

This sets you apart

  • You have a completed commercial education with medical knowledge or a completed medical education with commercial experience
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the processing of a health or accident insurance.
  • You are characterized by an exact, structured and reliable working method as well as a quick understanding
  • You will quickly find your way around various application programs and digital tools and enjoy working with different systems
  • You have a stylish oral and written expression in German
  • Good knowledge of French or Italian is required

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. With annual working hours, at least 5 weeks holidays and on request also holiday purchase. With part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job, you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work – with a market-based wage, up to 1,500 francs annual fee for personal wishes and a generous participation in the basic and additional insurance for you and your family.
  • We offer you prospects. With a generous training regulations, we support your professional and personal development – for your role today and for your goals of tomorrow.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being. And if it comes otherwise: 100% net wage continued payment.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.
