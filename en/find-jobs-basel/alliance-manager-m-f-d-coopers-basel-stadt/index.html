Full description

Join an innovative, cross-functional environment where you will play a key role in managing strategic public-private partnerships within the healthcare and life sciences sector. This position offers the opportunity to collaborate with international stakeholders, support complex research and development programs, and contribute to projects with global impact.

Hard Facts

  • Start Date: ASAP
  • Latest Possible Start Date: 01.03.2027
  • Duration: 12 months
  • Extension: Very likely
  • Workload: 100%
  • Location: Basel, Switzerland
  • Remote Work: Up to 2 days per week
  • Travel: Occasional international travel (approximately once per year, including the USA)
  • Work Model: Hybrid

Your Responsibilities

  • Support the execution of strategic partnership programs and ensure compliance with contractual obligations.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional project teams to identify partnership-relevant activities and reporting requirements.
  • Maintain and oversee comprehensive project plans across multiple workstreams.
  • Coordinate the creation, maintenance, and submission of key project documentation, including study plans, protocols, reports, and vendor-related documentation.
  • Monitor project budgets and track costs associated with funded activities.
  • Collaborate closely with finance teams to support monthly financial reporting.
  • Monitor project progress and contractual deliverables and contribute to periodic reporting activities.
  • Support the development of project plans, timelines, and budgets for new initiatives.
  • Identify project risks and issues, support mitigation planning, and proactively escalate concerns when necessary.
  • Facilitate effective communication between stakeholders and maintain sensitive partnership information and processes.

Your Profile

  • Master's degree or PhD in a scientific, biomedical, healthcare-related, or comparable discipline.
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant industry experience within healthcare, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, diagnostics, or related sectors.
  • Strong understanding of drug development and/or diagnostic assay development.
  • Experience in infectious disease research and development is advantageous.
  • Proven experience managing projects funded by governmental grants or working within public-private partnerships, alliances, or governmental collaborations.
  • Knowledge of health authority documentation and contractual compliance requirements.
  • Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive working style.
  • Strong project coordination and stakeholder management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively in an international and cross-functional environment.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.

What We Offer

  • Opportunity to contribute to globally relevant healthcare initiatives.
  • Exposure to complex, international partnership programs.
  • Collaborative and highly skilled interdisciplinary teams.
  • Flexible hybrid working model.
  • Dynamic environment with long-term project prospects.
