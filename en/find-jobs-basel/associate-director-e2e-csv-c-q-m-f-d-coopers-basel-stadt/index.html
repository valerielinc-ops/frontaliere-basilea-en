Full description

We are looking for an experienced E2E CSV/C&Q Lead to take ownership of the integrated qualification and validation approach for a connected manufacturing data solution. This is a senior, cross-functional assignment for someone who combines strong pharmaceutical validation expertise with a broad understanding of IT, OT, automation and manufacturing data platforms.

Hard Facts

  • Start Date: 1 November 2026
  • Duration: 12 months
  • Workload: 100%, 41 hours per week
  • Location: Basel, Switzerland
  • Contract Type: Temporary contract assignment
  • Language: Business-fluent English

Your Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain an integrated, modular and risk-based CSV/C&Q strategy for a connected manufacturing data solution.
  • Define intended use, GxP boundaries, system classifications and validation principles across source systems, integrations, platform services, data products and operational applications.
  • Establish a reusable validation framework for the initial implementation and future deployments.
  • Coordinate qualification and validation activities required for production deployment.
  • Ensure complete requirements traceability, testing coverage, validation evidence, data integrity and release readiness across the end-to-end solution.
  • Coordinate validation deliverables across Business, Engineering, Automation, IT, OT, Quality and external partners.
  • Define the qualification approach for operational applications such as batch traceability, process monitoring and controlled data sharing.
  • Assess data lineage, transformations, interfaces, auditability, access controls and data-integrity risks across connected systems.
  • Ensure compliance with ALCOA+ principles and applicable GxP requirements.
  • Define supplier-assurance and testing-reliance strategies for technology providers and implementation partners.
  • Ensure that validation deliverables are complete, internally consistent and ready for Quality review.
  • Embed validated-state maintenance requirements into the future operating model.
  • Develop reusable qualification archetypes, minimum validation deliverables and validation prerequisites for future deployments.
  • Facilitate risk assessments, intended-use evaluations and validation decisions across multiple workstreams.
  • Proactively manage and escalate validation risks, dependencies and issues.
  • Establish a pragmatic and defensible validation approach without unnecessary documentation or duplicated testing.

Your Profile

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering, Automation, Computer Science, Information Technology, Life Sciences, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Quality Management or a related discipline.
  • Proven experience developing and coordinating end-to-end validation strategies for integrated GxP systems.
  • Strong pharmaceutical or biopharmaceutical industry experience.
  • Broad understanding of IT and OT environments, including automation, MES, historians, infrastructure, integration platforms and enterprise applications.
  • Experience validating data flows, transformations, interfaces, contextualisation logic, data products and software applications used in regulated operations.
  • Strong knowledge of intended use, GxP impact assessment, system criticality and risk-based validation.
  • Practical experience with requirements management, traceability matrices, risk assessments, test strategies and controlled validation documentation.
  • Strong understanding of data integrity, ALCOA+, data lineage, auditability and access controls.
  • Experience assessing supplier capabilities and leveraging vendor documentation and testing appropriately.
  • Strong knowledge of GMP, CSV/CSA, C&Q, change control, deviations, CAPA and validated-state maintenance.
  • Demonstrated ability to align Business, Quality, IT, OT, Engineering, system owners and external partners.
  • Business-fluent English.
  • A pragmatic approach to compliance, with the ability to establish proportionate controls and avoid unnecessary requalification.
