Your Opportunity

As an Engagement Lead - Data Science , you will provide technical leadership across two sub-teams: the Predictive AI team and the Causal & Experimentation AI team. In this role, you will architect advanced enterprise AI solutions, mentor data science talent, and serve as a strategic consultant across global business lines including Commercial, R&D, Diagnostics etc.

Beyond team delivery, you will play a pivotal leadership role within the broader GATE organization—actively driving GATE-level people initiatives, talent capability enablement, strategic product development roadmaps, and cross-center innovation activities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Technical Leadership & Mentorship: Lead, mentor, and guide Senior Data Scientists and Data Scientists across Predictive AI and Causal & Experimentation AI sub-teams, fostering technical excellence, modular coding standards, and agile delivery practices.
  • Strategic Business Consulting: Act as a trusted AI consultant to global business partners across to translate complex business challenges into actionable Data Science and AI strategies.
  • GATE-Level Leadership & Product Development: Champion GATE-wide initiatives by actively shaping center-level AI product development strategy, contributing to talent enablement and mentorship programs, and driving standard methodologies across the GATE ecosystem.
  • Rapid Prototyping & Business Value: Rapidly design and build Proofs of Concept (POCs), prototypes, and business case studies to demonstrate tangible ROI and secure executive buy-in.
  • Predictive & Causal AI Solutions: Architect and oversee the development of robust predictive models, causal inference frameworks, and experimental designs (A/B testing, quasi-experiments) to drive decision-making.
  • GenAI & Agentic Frameworks: Drive the strategy and implementation of enterprise Generative AI architectures, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), fine-tuning techniques, and Agentic AI workflows.
  • End-to-End MLOps & Infrastructure: Partner with MLOps and Data Engineering teams to design, operationalize, and maintain end-to-end ML/AI pipelines, ensuring scalable model deployment, continuous monitoring, and automated lifecycle management.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with global cross-functional stakeholders in an agile environment, conducting demos, reviews, and retrospectives to continuously improve team deliverables.
