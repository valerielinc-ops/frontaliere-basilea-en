Full description

To support our continued growth and digital transformation, we are looking for a highly qualified leader to strengthen our Global IT Department as Head of MES & PI Competence Center (a), 80 - 100%.

This global leadership role is pivotal in shaping the future of our Digital Manufacturing capabilities. Reporting to the Head of Operations IT and serving as a key member of the Digital Manufacturing Council, you define the strategic direction and ensure the sustainable evolution of our MES and PI landscape as a core enabler of operational excellence, scalability, and data-driven manufacturing. Together with our internal and external partners, you translate business priorities into a clear application roadmap, strengthen governance and partner ecosystems, and ensure that our Digital Manufacturing solutions create measurable value across the organization.

Your tasks

  • Define and maintain the application strategy and roadmap for the Digital Manufacturing landscape in alignment with business unit priorities, business objectives and the Bachem IT application strategy.
  • Collaborate with business units to manage their demand, understand their requirements, ensure prioritization, and align solutions with business needs.
  • Oversee the implementation and operation of Digital Manufacturing solutions, from planning and development to deployment, continuous improvement, and ongoing support.
  • Ensure compliance with established processes for IT service management and computer system validation during application development, maintenance, and support.
  • Collaborate closely with project managers, IT, OT, and business SMEs to ensure Digital Manufacturing projects deliver the expected value.
  • Lead and mentor a high-performing team, fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
  • Evaluate, select, and manage third-party vendors and partners with the support of IT Operational Excellence and GRC.

Your profile

  • Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field.
  • Proven experience in a leadership role overseeing Digital Manufacturing applications and projects in a regulated pharmaceutical, chemical or manufacturing environment.
  • Experience with MES, process historian, or shopfloor-related IT/OT applications such as Körber PAS-X or AVEVA PI.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to build relationships at all levels.
  • Leadership: Inspire and guide teams towards achieving ambitious goals.
  • Communication: Articulate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
  • Problem-Solving: Address challenges with innovative and effective solutions.
  • Prioritization: Ability to focus on activities that create measurable business value.
  • Collaboration: Foster a collaborative, inclusive work environment and a can-do attitude.
  • Adaptability: Navigate and lead through change in a dynamic business environment.
  • Excellent written and spoken English and German skills

  • A key leadership role with global impact, offering the opportunity to shape our MES & PI Competence Center and drive the digital transformation of our manufacturing landscape
  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal development and training opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with home office days and an option for obtaining additional vacation days through workload reduction
  • Company‑run daycare center on the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
