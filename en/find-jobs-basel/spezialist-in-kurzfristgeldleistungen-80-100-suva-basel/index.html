Full description

You also keep track of complex billing and thus contribute to the financial stability of our customers? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • As a specialist, you make sure that our recipients receive their daily allowances correctly.
  • You check the completeness of the documents, assess the entitlement to daily allowance and determine the insured earnings.
  • You create complex daily allowance statements and coordinate the daily allowances with the benefits of other social insurance.
  • You process demanding daily allowance corrections and make recoveries.
  • You bring your expertise to the further development of processes and systems and actively shape the automation of case handling. What you bring
  • You have completed basic commercial education and experience in the social security environment. Knowledge in the area of daily allowances and/or further education as a social security specialist (SVS) is a plus.
  • You have a good understanding of numbers, think analytically and networked and like to deal with complex questions.
  • You are open to new things, are interested in digital and technical topics and are happy to participate in changes. You certainly use modern work equipment.
  • You work efficiently and precisely, take responsibility and act independently.
  • Kund:innen are the focus for you. You communicate in writing and verbally convincingly, work service-oriented and share your knowledge in the team. Knowledge of French and/or Italian is a plus.
