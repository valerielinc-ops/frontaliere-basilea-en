Full description

Your tasks

  • You gain new customers in the facility management market in the Basel / Berne region and build long-term relationships
  • You edit and qualify leads from the region and lead them to the finished offer
  • You clarify the needs of customers (e.g. industry, service, public sector) in Basel / Bern, structure their requirements and translate them into suitable IFM solutions
  • You coordinate the preparation of the offer with the internal areas and ensure a coherent, complete offer
  • Prepare customer appointments in the region before and after (agenda, documents, minutes, next steps) and ensure a professional customer experience
  • You keep offers, contacts and activities in the distribution system up-to-date and ensure transparency
  • You use your network in the Basel / Bern region as well as campaigns and events to generate new leads and actively work on the market

Your requirements

  • Completed commercial or technical education and/or studies (FH/uni), ideally in business administration or facility management
  • Several years of sales experience in the facility management market in the B2B environment, with proven success in customer acquisition
  • Very good knowledge of the regional market Basel / Bern and a resilient network in the FM, real estate or industrial sector of the region
  • Strong sales and customer orientation as well as enjoyment of customer acquisition, offer processes and structured cooperation with specialist areas
  • Very good oral and written communication skills and safe appearance with customers: inside
  • Analytical, structured and reliable way of working – even with several parallel offers you keep the overview
  • Secure handling of MS Office, especially Excel and PowerPoint
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, good English is an advantage
