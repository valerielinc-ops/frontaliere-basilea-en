Full description

You want to do a commercial education, but not always do the same tasks for three years? Then you are exactly right at Valora! During your three-year KV apprenticeship at the headquarters in Muttenz, you change department every six months. You will get to know very different areas and get a comprehensive insight into how a large and versatile company works. Tasks you will like

  • Practical, varied and versatile commercial training in the trade sector
  • Getting to know a dynamic and exciting corporate environment and everyday work
  • Insights in various departments
  • Assistance with projects and events Requirements you bring
  • Compulsory school completed with good to very good performance
  • Interest in commercial and economic connections
  • Enjoyment of working on the computer and digital activities
  • Reliable, independent and careful working methods
  • Organizational talent and ability to keep track of multiple tasks
  • Quick comprehension and a high willingness to learn
  • Open and friendly personality in dealing with other people
  • Very good manners and well-groomed and professional appearance
  • Team-oriented and committed
  • Enjoyment of foreign languages (very good German as well as good French and English skills)
  • Interested in how a large company with different brands and sales formats works Advantages we offer you
  • Varied vocational training with insight into different departments
  • Competent support from our dedicated vocational trainers
  • Participation in internal and external events
  • Development opportunities and future prospects after successfully completed basic education
  • Foundation stone for higher vocational training and study
  • Valora offers the basic commercial education in the two profiles E and M.
