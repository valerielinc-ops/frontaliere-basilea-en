Full description

Klinik Arlesheim is an acute hospital with 90 inpatient beds in the fields of oncology, internal medicine and psychiatry/psychosomatics as well as an oncology daily clinic with 24 beds. Outpatient primary care services include gynecology, dermatology, gastroenterology, family medicine, cardiology, neurology, pulmonology and sleep medicine. The emergency is open around the clock. Medical diagnosis and therapy is based on the modern possibilities of scientific medicine. As the first anthroposophical clinic in the world, Klinik Arlesheim has more than 100 years of experience with the integrative concept of anthroposophic medicine.

Their tasks

  • Assumption of treatment care for acutely ill patients: inside at home
  • Co-development and design of the treatment process in the interprofessional team
  • Ensuring the care process
  • Goal- and solution-oriented cooperation in an interprofessional treatment team
  • Implementation and guidance of integrative medical care measures

They bring with them

  • Training for/m dipl. nursing specialist HF (HF, FH, DNII, AKP)
  • Openness for an integrative treatment concept, focus on anthroposophical care and medicine
  • Team-oriented, process-focused working method
  • Interest in modern forms of work organization (New Work)

We offer

  • A collegial working atmosphere with a good and lived culture of encounter in a value-oriented hospital
  • Easily accessible place of work with excellent public transport connection (5 min to the tram or S-Bahn)
  • TNW Job Ticket and Free Use of Pick-e-bike
  • Careful training in a supportive team
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation

We look forward to meeting you. Only direct applications shall be considered for this call.
