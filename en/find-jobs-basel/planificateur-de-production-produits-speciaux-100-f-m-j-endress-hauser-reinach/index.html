Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a production planner* in special products (f/m/d) to complement our team and to efficiently design and further develop the complex and exciting production processes. What is the role about? Do you love to keep track? Do you feel comfortable at the interface between technology, production and logistics? And you also keep a cool head when it comes to individual customer requirements? Then this role is made for you! As a production planner (f/m/d) for special products, you are right in the middle of when unique customer requirements turn into tailor-made solutions in close cooperation with different teams and departments. What tasks will you take on? They shall:

  • as part of the work preparation, create production contracts for individual special technical products.
  • Define delivery dates, monitor them and process offers taking into account throughput times.
  • Test and maintain production documents, production master data for customer-specific special products and assemblies.
  • Configure customer-specific parameters and support production for special orders.
  • act as an important interface between production, procurement and technology.
  • actively participate in the continuous optimization of our processes, the further development of our processes and the digitalization of special product processing. What do we expect from you? They:
  • have completed basic technical training – ideally supplemented by further training as a process specialist.
  • bring several years of experience in the production environment.
  • know SAP/R3 well or are ready to get involved quickly.
  • have a good understanding of digital production systems and their connections.
  • communicate safely in German (minimum C1 level).
  • gladly take responsibility and act independently and solution-oriented.
  • think and act customer-oriented, have a networked mindset and keep an eye on the big picture as well as the details. What can you expect from us?
  • We are a family-run company that cares about kindness, appreciation and long-term thinking.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support your work-life balance through flexible working hours and the possibility of remote work (25%).
  • We promote sustainable mobility and support your use of public transport.
  • We offer family-friendly solutions – from childcare to children’s holiday programmes. How do you apply? Please upload your full application documents via our career page. For data protection reasons, we unfortunately cannot consider applications by e-mail. Note for agencies and personnel service providers: Please refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles. For this position, we do not work with external service providers. Apply now.
