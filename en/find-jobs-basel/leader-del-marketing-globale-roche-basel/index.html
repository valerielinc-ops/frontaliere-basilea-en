Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position

Our Global Pharma Strategy (GPS) team plays a pivotal role in shaping the future of healthcare. As a Global Commercial Marketing Lead (CML), you are a champion of our global brands and maintain central ownership for execution leadership. You will act as a "Global Conductor" and strategic orchestrator, synthesising insights from priority markets and global functions to co-create cohesive strategies, scalable content, and a unified customer engagement approach. In this role, you will bridge scientific innovation with market reality to maximise patient uptake, leading the development of Brand Strategy Guidance, the Brand Book, launch readiness, and market shaping across the Disease Area (DA) portfolio. This spans core campaign narratives, scalable modular content, and insight-driven customer journeys, alongside coordinating omnichannel engagement and measurement. You will also orchestrate alignment between global functions and local affiliates, tracking performance signals and KPIs to continuously optimise brand impact. Recent experience in Ophthalmology is a plus.

Key Responsibilities

Global Strategy Co-Creation & Execution Leadership

  • Integrated Strategy & Brand Championing: Leads the development and realisation of the Brand Strategy Guidance, Brand Book, scenario planning and Go-to-Market model.
  • Launch Excellence & Market Shaping : Maintains ownership for driving execution of global brands, including co-positioning, market shaping, and best-in-class launch readiness for new molecular entities and line extensions.
  • Leadership & Influencing: Acts as a cross-functional leader for Strategy Outcomes and Work Package owners, facilitating high-impact, "consent over consensus" trade-off decisions to maintain agility. Adept at working effectively in a matrix leadership environment.

Content Foundations, Campaigns & Story Flow

  • Campaign & Story Design: Defines the value proposition and develops the core content for global campaigns, omnichannel execution, and major congresses.
  • Modular Content & Scale: Leads the design of tailored modular global content and templates built for scale and reuse across the network of Affiliates.
  • Customer Experience: Designs customer-centric omnichannel strategies, leveraging digital platforms, data analytics, and AI to deliver personalised, insight-driven customer journeys. Establishes global content standards and reuse guidance, while applying the Risk Framework to streamline approvals
  • Market Insight Integration: Informs strategic decisions with deep DA customer and market insights, ensuring global strategies reflect patient realities. Helps Roche identify how to best serve customer needs, including addressing internal and external barriers.

Global–Local Orchestration & Governance

  • Ecosystem Orchestration: Operates in close partnership with Affiliate marketers, from both Early Launch Markets and Rest of the World, to co-create value and ensure global strategies are executable in local realities.
  • Strategic Connector: Actively partners with the Disease Area Commercialisation Strategy Lead (CSL), Commercialisation Engagement Lead (CEL), and other senior stakeholders. Challenge and hold partners accountable to ensure the highest impact for patients.
  • Network Catalyst: Coordinates between commercial functions (Ecosystem Insights, Competitive Intelligence, Marketing) and engages with US and Key Affiliates (I8) leads to drive organisational pull-through and outstanding execution.
  • Performance Optimisation: Defines global KPIs, synthesises performance signals across markets to identify trends, and drives portfolio-level optimisation and insights to include in strategy evolution. Responds rapidly to market signals.

Who You Are

Experience & Expertise

  • Experienced Marketer: You are a confident and expert marketer. You bring at least 10 years of experience in marketing in complex ecosystems, with a proven track record across the full product lifecycle (pre-launch, launch, and value maximisation).
  • Strategic & Technical Proficiency: Deep expertise in the "science of marketing," customer experience, omnichannel strategies, and leveraging data-driven insights to connect the dots in a fast-paced, matrixed environment.
  • Foundational Knowledge: A strong understanding of the AI landscape and Responsible AI, with the ability to shape direction, enable scale, and guide ethical adoption.

Mindsets & Behaviours: You show up and drive the execution of work by embodying the following core mindsets:

  • Accountable: Takes end-to-end ownership of business outcomes, ruthlessly prioritising high-impact initiatives and acting with commercial rigour and a bias for action.
  • Globally Collaborative: Works across markets and functions with clear ownership, sharing and reusing best practices to drive scale and efficiency.
  • Competitive: Strives to win by setting a bold ambition, acting with speed and agility, and balancing risk with opportunity.
  • Customer-Centric: Puts customers and patients at the centre of decisions, using insight and partnership to co-create value.
  • Curious: Learns continuously, seeks new ideas, and acts on feedback to adapt, innovate, and drive business impact.
  • Early Adopter: Proactively embraces AI, technology, and platforms to improve speed, quality, and decision-making.
  • Collaborative Influencer: Exceptional interpersonal skills with the ability to lead through influence in virtual and matrixed environments.
