Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress happens through collaboration. As the world's leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether innovating and implementing new technologies as a team, collaborating on the development of new devices or strengthening key relationships with various global industries, our work is based on building trusting relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Project Engineer Solutions (m/f/d) to complement our team and implement innovative automation and software solutions for demanding customer projects. At Endress+Hauser Flow in Reinach, we develop and produce high-precision flow measurement technology and actively shape the digital future of the process industry.

What is the position about?

You accompany sophisticated automation solutions throughout the entire life cycle - from the initial idea to successful commissioning. The focus is on high-precision transfer measurement and billing solutions, such as refueling or loading and unloading ships.

They operate at the interface of automation, software and hardware and combine them to create powerful overall systems. Are you interested in converting complex technical requirements into well-thought-out solutions and experiencing their effects directly in real use?

What tasks will you take on?

You will

  • Take technical responsibility for solution projects from the idea to implementation and actively promote their implementation
  • ensure technical feasibility and work at the interface between instrumentation, electrical hardware, communication as well as software and visualization
  • Develop and implement automation solutions with PLCs (according to IEC 61131-3, e.g. CoDeSys).
  • Create and be responsible for electrical drawings in control construction
  • Develop and expand visualizations and user interfaces using modern web technologies such as Angular or React
  • Support sales with technical issues and solution designs
  • handle service cases as level 2 or 3 support

What do we expect from you?

She

  • have a degree in electrical, automation or systems engineering or a comparable qualification
  • have experience in PLC programming (IEC 61131-3) as well as a solid understanding of control technology
  • have gained practice in electrical planning and creating electrical drawings
  • have an interest in or initial experience in web technologies such as Angular or React
  • have a good understanding of the interaction between IT, software and automation and, ideally, initial experience in system integration
  • communicate confidently in German and English and work independently, solution-oriented and enjoy interacting with customers
  • are willing to travel up to 15%

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision
  • wr promote lifelong learning through a variety of training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning
  • We offer you a varied role that combines classic automation with modern software development and gives you a lot of creative freedom.
  • We enable you to work on real, complex systems and experience your solutions directly in action
  • We promote close collaboration between engineering and software and create an environment in which ideas are developed together
  • We support your professional and personal development in a future-oriented environment
