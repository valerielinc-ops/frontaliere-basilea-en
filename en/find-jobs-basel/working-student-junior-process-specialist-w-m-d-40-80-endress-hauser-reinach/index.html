Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a Working Student: Junior Process Specialist to complement our global team and actively contribute to the data-based analysis and further development of production processes and thus to sustainably improve quality and efficiency. What is the point about? Do you want to develop test programs for an optical paint inspection system and thus contribute visibly to product quality? Does it appeal to you to understand requirements together with the development department and implement them in a practical manner? Do you want to actively support the quality team in introducing and implementing a new inspection process? In this role, you combine technology, analysis and collaboration and actively shape a central process along production. What tasks will you take on? You will:

  • Create test programs and further develop them in a targeted manner.
  • Support the process engineer in daily tasks and projects.
  • accompany the quality team in analyses and optimisations.
  • Evaluate data sets and analyze them in a structured manner.
  • derive concrete improvement measures for the painting process from the data. What do we expect from you? You:
  • studies electrical engineering, automation, mechatronics or a similar degree program with a specialization in automation, engineering or optical systems.
  • are at the beginning of your studies or in an ongoing semester (also possible as PiBS).
  • bring basic knowledge of EDP and CAD.
  • have good knowledge of German, French and/or English in words and writing.
  • work carefully and independently, are flexible, responsible, willing to learn, have a quick apprehension, communication skills and are open to new things. What can you expect from us?
  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e‐learning.
  • We support your work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport. How do you apply? To apply, please upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons. Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding. Apply now.
