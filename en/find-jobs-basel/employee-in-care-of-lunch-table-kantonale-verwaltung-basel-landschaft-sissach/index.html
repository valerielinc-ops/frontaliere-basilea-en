The Sissach secondary school is a future-oriented school in the upper Basel bid. Around 730 pupils from the 17 municipalities of the catchment area around the district capital of Sissach are taught in 39 classes in the three performance lines A, E and P of secondary school I by around 100 teachers. The lunch table is visited daily by around 10-40 students. Further information about the Sissach secondary school can be found on our website: www.seksissach.ch.
