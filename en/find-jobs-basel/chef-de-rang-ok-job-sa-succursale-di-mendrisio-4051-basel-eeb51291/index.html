Chef de Rang Back to the list For our client, a renowned gourmet restaurant in Basel with excellent cuisine and highest standards of service quality, we are looking for an experienced and guest-oriented personality as a restaurant manager. Job description You live hospitality at the highest level, lead your team with passion and want to offer your guests unforgettable culinary experiences? Then we look forward to meeting you. Their tasks In this responsible role, you take over the operational management of the restaurant and together with your team ensure first-class service.

  • Management and coordination of restaurant operations
  • Professional and warm welcome as well as care of the guests
  • Ensuring a smooth service flow at the highest level
  • Leadership, motivation and support of the service team
  • Close cooperation with kitchen and management
  • Co-responsibility for compliance with quality and service standards
  • Active participation in day-to-day operations
  • Ensuring a well-kept and representative appearance of the restaurant

Profile

  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable management function in the upscale gastronomy
  • Experience in gourmet or fine dining gastronomy
  • Very good knowledge of German, French and English
  • Safe and well-groomed appearance with a pronounced host mentality
  • High service orientation and passion for upscale gastronomy
  • Responsible, structured and independent working methods
  • Leadership skills and the ability to motivate and develop employees
  • Team spirit, commitment and high quality standards

This is what you can expect

  • Fixed working days from Tuesday to Saturday
  • Only evening service
  • Sunday and Monday free
  • No room hours and no shared services
  • Professional and respectful working environment
  • Possibility to participate in a gastronomic business
  • Time and space for quality, personality and first-class service
  • Conditions of employment according to L-GAV

Interested? Do you have experience in upscale gastronomy, are you an authentic host personality and would like to perform gastronomic excellence together with a dedicated team?
