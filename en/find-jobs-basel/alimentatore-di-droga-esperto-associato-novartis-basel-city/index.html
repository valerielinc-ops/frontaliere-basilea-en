Description

The position supports the manufacture of active pharmaceutical ingredients and solid dosage forms for clinical studies within the Novartis TRD PHAD ORAL Supply Team. Specifically, the activities relate to the GMP-compliant production of active pharmaceutical ingredients and solid dosage forms, operation of manufacturing equipment, support for maintenance & qualification, as well as cleaning and documentation of the activities performed.

Main Responsibilities

  • GMP-compliant manufacture of solid dosage forms, including Precise Drug Processing (PDP), encapsulation, tableting and/or granulation, as well as performing in-process controls (IPC) or process flow controls (PAK) and entering data into the MES system
  • Mixing, sieving, and milling of active pharmaceutical ingredients
  • Operation of manufacturing equipment, including setup, cleaning, and disinfection of the manufacturing equipment and rooms
  • Support maintenance & calibration of manufacturing equipment and participate in assigned investment projects (e.g., as part of qualification and commissioning)
  • Perform delegated sampling
  • Responsible for orderliness and cleanliness in the assigned areas of responsibility and rooms
  • Responsible for ensuring the availability of the assigned equipment
  • Personal responsibility for ongoing training/development
  • Participate in the implementation of and compliance with 5S and IQP/Continuous Improvement (CI) measures
  • Participate in preparing internal and external audits and actively participate in conducting the audits
  • Contribute to meeting the Supply Team-specific process performance indicators (KPI)
  • Contribute to the creation / revision of procedural documents (SOP, WP, FRM)
  • Compliance with the cGMP, HSE, Data Integrity and ISEC (Information Security) guidelines

Vocational Training

  • Minimum: Completed vocational training
  • Preferred: Vocational training as a chemical and pharmaceutical technologist, vocational training in the food sector or in a hygiene operation, or technical vocational training with experience in a GMP-regulated environment (e.g., mechanic, mechatronics technician, etc.)

Language Skills

  • Minimum: Fluent German language skills (spoken and written)
  • Preferred: Good English language skills (spoken and written)

Professional Experience

  • >5 years of experience in a GMP-regulated environment if no specialized training as a chemical or pharmaceutical technologist has been completed.
  • Experience in a development operation for chemical or pharmaceutical manufacturing is desirable.
  • Experience in the manufacture of active pharmaceutical ingredients (sieving, mixing, and milling activities) or solid dosage forms (mixing, granulating, sieving, milling, compacting, extruding, tableting, encapsulation, Sorting, Coating)
  • Forklift license desirable
