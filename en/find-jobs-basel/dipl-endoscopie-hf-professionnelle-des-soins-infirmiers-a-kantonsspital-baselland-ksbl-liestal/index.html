Tasks

Would you like to work in an exciting field where modern technology, team spirit and patient-oriented care come together? In our endoscopy, you can expect a varied day-to-day work with great personal responsibility, clear processes and a committed team.

  • Care of patients before, during and after endoscopic examinations
  • Assistance and sedation in diagnostic and therapeutic interventions in the fields of gastroenterology and hepatology
  • Performing routine examinations such as gastroscopies, colonoscopies and functional diagnostics
  • Working according to defined processes and standards (SOP) as well as independent and responsible action
  • Organization and coordination of work processes – here your organizational talent and flexibility are required
  • Varied activities in a dynamic working environment
  • Documentation of investigations by electronic systems

Requirements

  • Certified nurse (HF, FH or equivalent)
  • Experience in endoscopy or comparable functional services
  • Readiness for in-depth incorporation into endoscopy
  • Technical understanding & joy in dealing with medical devices
  • High patient orientation, empathy and communication strength
  • Teamwork, flexibility and personal responsibility
  • Readiness for continuous further education & development
