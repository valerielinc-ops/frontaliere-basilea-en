Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 29.10.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus topics of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system and rehabilitation as well as palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients annually benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

The Clinic Rheumatology and Pain Medicine is the largest clinic of its kind in northwestern Switzerland. We work closely interdisciplinary with the clinics Spinal Surgery, University Orthopaedics, Internal Medicine, Rehabilitation and Neurology. Our MPA team is looking for a medical assistant (100%) immediately or by appointment. As part of the centralization of our clinics, we are looking for flexible personalities who enjoy varied activities and versatile assignments in our ambulatories.

Their tasks

  • Reception of patients in the ambulatory and telephone triage
  • Organisation, coordination and preparation of consultation hours (scheduling of appointments, opening of cases, obtaining documents, etc.)
  • Assistance in osteoporosis consultation hours (bone density measurements)
  • Preparation, independent implementation and monitoring of infusions and injections in the ambulatory
  • Assistance in sonography-controlled joint and soft tissue infiltrations
  • Assistance in X-ray-controlled spinal column infiltrations in the intervention room
  • Preparation and monitoring of patients in the IMC
  • Postal and e-mail processing and telephone inquiries
  • General administrative tasks
  • Special delegated tasks

They bring with them

  • Completed training as an MPA
  • Knowledge of German at least. C1 Level
  • High social skills and good communication skills
  • Flexible personality and enjoyment of cross-department cooperation
  • Independent, resilient, forward-looking and reliable personality
  • Teamwork, commitment and friends in a very dynamic working environment

We offer

  • Innovative field of work
  • Organizational structures that promote interprofessional cooperation
  • Team atmosphere of togetherness and for each other
  • Attractive internal training programme
