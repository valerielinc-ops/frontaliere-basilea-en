Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities
  • In this key position, you will be responsible for the operational excellence of our state-of-the-art diagnostic systems:
  • Troubleshooting: You will independently perform complex error analysis and repairs on our systems, with a particular focus on AXELIOS and its digital interfaces ;
  • Installation: You will manage the installation and commissioning of our new sequencing platforms - from checking the customer's technical infrastructure to the final handover ;
  • Maintenance: The planning and execution of preventive maintenance intervals will be in your hands, ensuring the maximum performance of the systems at all times ;
  • Advisory: As a technical expert and competent point of contact, you will provide sound advice to our customers regarding system adjustments and the appropriate service portfolio.

Key requirements

  • You possess strong analytical skills and enjoy diving deep into complex mechatronic and molecular systems:
  • Education: You hold a degree in Engineering (University/Applied Sciences), are a state-certified technician, or possess a comparable qualification in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Medical Technology ;
  • Experience: Ideally you have between 3 and 5 years of professional experience in technical service and you have already had exposure to Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) or similar molecular techniques ;
  • IT Expertise: You are proficient in the configuration and diagnostics of IT interfaces as well as in handling Linux and modern network infrastructures ;
  • Communication: You communicate fluently in Spanish and have very good English skills for working within an interdisciplinary, international team of experts ;
  • Mindset: You are customer-oriented, business-minded, and characterized by a structured, independent way of working in a dynamic, innovative environment ;
  • Mobility: Due to regional deployment planning and travel times, you should ideally live in close proximity to an international airport (Madrid or Barcelona).
  • Furthermore, you hold a valid driver's license and have a high willingness to travel to support our customers within the EMEA-LATAM region.
