Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With our services we set a benchmark in inheritance consulting. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our central location in Basel.

  • You advise our private clients, plan estates and work out wills, marriage and inheritance contracts.
  • You independently lead mandates in the areas of will execution and inheritance.
  • As a lawyer, you are the contact person and interface for our financial advisors on the subject of estate.
  • In parallel with your work, you will complete a practical training program in which you will build up a broad knowledge of all financial topics.
  • Master's degree at a university or FH in the field of law / business law
  • Professional experience in the field of marriage and inheritance law is a plus, but not a must
  • Analytical and structured thinking and working methods
  • Joy of contact with customers
  • We promote your personal development individually and you can take on more responsibility on an ongoing basis.
  • Most of the positions are also open for a part-time stay, so that you have more time for further education, family, sports and projects that are important to you.
  • On our expansion course, we want to maintain our uncomplicated culture. This means for you the opportunity to shape and develop a unique company.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
