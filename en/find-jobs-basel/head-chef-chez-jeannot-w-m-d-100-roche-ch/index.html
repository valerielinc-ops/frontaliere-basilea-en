As of January 2027, we are looking for a:n Chef: Chef for the Museums Restaurant, chez Jeannot.

Your tasks

  • Operational kitchen management: Professional and organizational overall management of kitchen staff and ensuring a smooth operation in the restaurant chez Jeannot .
  • Staff management & personnel development: service and deployment planning, professional training, training and continuous development of the kitchen team.
  • Offer & menu planning: Conception and further development of the food offer, the daily menus as well as tailor-made banquet and catering proposals (in close coordination with the restaurant manager).
  • Calculation & Profitability: Economic menu calculation, management and maintenance of the inventory management system as well as regular implementation of the inventory.
  • Goods Management & Quality Assurance: Responsibility for ordering, professional goods acceptance and quality control of fresh food products in consistent compliance with the highest quality standards.
  • Event & banquet management: organization, coordination and implementation of exclusive special events and banquets in the restaurant and in the context of the museum.
  • Partnership interface: Close and cooperative cooperation with Museum Tinguely and a reliable contact person for internal and external partners.
  • Deputy & Administration: Reliable operational deputy of the restaurant manager in case of absence including support of the e-mail box and reservation management.
  • Safety & Maintenance: Responsibility for compliance with and enforcement of the internal guidelines on hygiene (HACCP), accident prevention and fire protection, as well as monitoring the function and maintenance of the kitchen inventory.
  • Working hours: Usually during the day between 09:00 and 17:30 (Tuesday to Sunday, with occasional assignments at evening events).

Your profile

  • Professional qualification: Completed vocational apprenticeship as a chef/chef (EFZ) as well as a successfully completed further education as a chef/chef with a federal certificate (Federal School) or an equivalent qualification.
  • Management experience: Several years, proven successful management experience as chef, ideally in upscale gastronomy, museum or event gastronomy.
  • Economic thinking: Pronounced entrepreneurial thinking and acting, sound business understanding as well as an experienced handling of menu calculation and goods management.
  • Leadership & organizational competence: Strong assertive, structured leader with a high sense of responsibility, strong organizational talent and role model for the entire kitchen team.
  • Working method & mindset: Independent, solution-oriented working method, absolute reliability as well as a pronounced team spirit and lived host qualities.
  • Professional & legal knowledge: Sound and proven knowledge of Swiss food legislation and relevant hygiene and safety guidelines (HACCP).
  • Language Competence: Very good German and English skills in word and writing for a confident communication in the team as well as competent advice and support for international guests and customers.
  • Digital Affinity: Secure handling of common MS Office programs as well as experience in dealing with digital goods management and booking systems.
