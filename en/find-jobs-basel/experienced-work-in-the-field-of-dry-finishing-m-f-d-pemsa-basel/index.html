Pemsa is a leading Swiss human resource agency with over 30 years of experience in recruitment and placement of qualified personnel in building, civil engineering, electronics, plant technology and industry. Thanks to a complete and professional service portfolio, we guarantee a close proximity to the customer and personalized service in the management of the staff, regardless of the location of your yards in Switzerland. Our expertise guarantees you reliability, quality and tailor-made solutions for your projects.

Mansions

  • Assembly and disassembly of dry construction elements such as plasterboard panels, metal bearing structures and insulation materials
  • Responsibility for the implementation of technical projects and detailed drawings according to Swiss building standards
  • Planning and preparation of work on site, including the supply of materials and the maintenance of tools
  • Execution of internal finishing works, in particular stucco and sanding works for surface preparation
  • Collaboration with architects, hairdressers and other professional figures to ensure the performance of the work in the expected time and with a high quality standard
  • Compliance with safety standards and quality standards on site
  • Autonomous organisation of work activities and coordination with other shipyard teams

Profile required

  • Essential requirements: Valid category B driving licence to ensure transport safety in Swiss shipyards
  • Solid professional experience as a skilled worker in plasterboard, preferably with several years of activity in Switzerland
  • Great mastery of German at the native level, to accurately understand and apply technical instructions and safety standards
  • In-depth knowledge of materials, tools and machinery commonly used in the plasterboard industry
  • High resistance to stress and flexibility to safely manage the changing workplace situations
  • Ability to work in teams and independently in a dynamic environment
  • Knowledge of Swiss legislation on safety at work and environmental protection
  • Useful: Additional specifications in the field of fire protection or sound insulation are an advantage
  • Bonus: Experience with specific licenses for the use of machinery (e.g. from M1 to M7) to support the logistics of the yard
