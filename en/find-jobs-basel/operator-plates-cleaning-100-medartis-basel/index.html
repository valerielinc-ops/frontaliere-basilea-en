Full description

To support our plate production in Basel we are looking for a motivated person as

Operator Plates - Cleaning 100%

Your responsibilities

Manual blisters of implants and screws

Cooperation with automated cleaning machines incl. Maintenance work

Final inspection of finished products

Performing dimensional and visual samples for quality control

Preparation of material orders and shipping documents Support for the optimization of existing manufacturing processes

Your qualifications

Craftsmanship, technical vocational training is beneficial Professional experience in a mechanical workshop or production environment is an advantage Good knowledge of German (in word and writing, at least B2) Safe handling of the PC as well as knowledge of MS-Office applications Readiness for shift and piquet work

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.
