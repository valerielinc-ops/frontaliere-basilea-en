Full description

Are you a Cooper?

Recruiting is your passion and you are only looking for the right environment to live it out? You are a communicative and ambitious personality who enjoys working with people? - Then you are exactly right in our team in Bern. We offer you a great working environment, great colleagues and the opportunity to grow personally and professionally.

As a talent acquisition specialist in our team in Basel, you support our customers in filling exciting temporary positions and take full responsibility for the candidate side.

Your most important tasks

  • You handle the entire recruitment process on the candidate side: from the job advertisement to the selection of the most suitable professional, you take responsibility.
  • You operate active sourcing on various recruitment platforms and gain new talent for Coopers.
  • You conduct interviews, check the suitability of your candidates for the open positions of our clients and conduct salary negotiations.
  • You build up a trusting network at eye level with your candidates and accompany them on their career path in the long term.
  • You interact with senior management to develop and improve effective recruitment strategies.

Our requirements

  • Basic commercial training or equivalent professional experience is required for this position.
  • Experience in the recruitment of temporary positions (Life Sciences)
  • You love interaction with people and approach them openly and proactively.
  • A high degree of self-motivation, perseverance and sense of responsibility are a matter of course for you.
  • You feel at home in an environment with very high service/customer orientation.
  • You want to work in a humorous, competent and appreciative team.
  • Confident knowledge of German and good knowledge of English round off your profile ideally, every other language is an advantage.

Why Coopers?

We are different – and of course in a positive sense! We are a family and diverse team, connected by a common vision. We support each other and set teamwork as the highest priority. You work in good company!

What we offer you

  • Flexible working hours and a hybrid working model.
  • 30 days of vacation a year.
  • Workation: Work 10 days a year from your favorite destination!
  • A private accident insurance.
  • Varied, dynamic tasks and internal development opportunities.
  • International flair: Both our internal employees and our candidates: customers come from all over the world.
  • Regular events and events.

Want to know more about us? Then check out our blog, where we regularly report on internal company news, team members are introduced or you can find out more about our latest events.
