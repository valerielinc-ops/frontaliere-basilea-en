Full description

Tasks

  • With the aid of the process control system, they operate the process stages of the sewage treatment plants.
  • They monitor the operating facilities, put plants into operation, detect faults and take corrective measures.
  • They revise and maintain machines and equipment.
  • After a familiarization phase, they perform pikettdienst on their own responsibility.
  • They support the operations manager in management tasks, in securing operations and in various projects.
  • They alternately take over the management of one of the two wastewater treatment plants in Birsfelden and Therwil.
  • They shall perform deputy duties during the absence of the manager.

Requirements

  • EFZ in a technical / industrial profession or as a chemical and pharmatechnologist / chemical and pharmatechnologist or the like
  • oath Specialist certificate as a sewage treatment specialist or equivalent training
  • Secure handling of common MS Office applications
  • Category B driving licence
  • Leadership and social skills as well as good communication skills and decision-making
  • During the cooking service, you will be able to be at the place of work within 30 minutes.
