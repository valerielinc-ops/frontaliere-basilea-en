Pemsa is an agency for the selection and supply of active personnel throughout Switzerland, specialized in the search for qualified personnel in the fields of construction, industry and plant technology. With over 30 years of experience, Pemsa offers its customers a complete and professional service. Thanks to its decentralized organization, Pemsa guarantees excellent proximity to the customer and fast reaction times, regardless of the location of your yards throughout Switzerland.

Mansions

  • Assembly and disassembly of art of fixtures and doors according to Swiss standards and design specifications
  • Autonomous execution of assembly details, sealing and fittings in the yard
  • Quality control and compliance with technical specifications during assembly process
  • Autonomous organization of your vehicle park and transport of materials to intervention sites
  • Coordination with heads and other workers on site for the execution of the works
  • Compliance with directives on safety and protection at work in accordance with Swiss standards and company standards

Profile required

  • Professional training completed as an AFC or AFC window installer with at least one year of proven work experience in Switzerland (depensable)
  • German mother tongue or excellent mastery of German written and spoken to ensure communication in the yard (indispensable)
  • Availability of its own vehicle park (e.g. van or suitable vehicle) for the transport of materials and persons (depensable)
  • Knowledge of Swiss building standards, assembly and safety standards
  • Good awareness of safety and safety experience on site according to SUVA directives
  • Conscientious and autonomous working methods, as well as the ability to work in teamwork on site
  • Flexibility with regard to accessibility routes and places of intervention (the proximity to the intervention area is an advantage)
  • Basic knowledge of technical design and installation projects are desirable
  • They benefit from additional qualifications in building enclosure or civil engineering (bonus)
