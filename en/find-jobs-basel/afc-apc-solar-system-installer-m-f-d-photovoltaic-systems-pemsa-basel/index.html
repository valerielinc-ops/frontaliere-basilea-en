Pemsa is a leading national employment agency, specialized in recruiting and providing qualified personnel in building, industry, electronics and plant technology. With more than 30 years of experience, we have a complete know-how and a versatile service offering, which guarantees excellent proximity to the customer, regardless of the location of your yards. Our expertise and commitment allow us to offer tailor-made and competent solutions that meet the needs of our customers and provide highly qualified personnel.

Mansions

  • Installation, installation and commissioning of photovoltaic systems on different types of buildings and on free areas
  • Mounting solar modules, inverters, fixing systems and electrical components in compliance with safety and quality standards
  • Execution of installation and wiring works in accordance with Swiss electricity regulations and regulations
  • Verification of plant operation, fault diagnosis and on-site resolution
  • Documentation and testing of the works performed according to the indications
  • Collaboration with project managers, electricians and other specialists on site
  • Compliance with safety directives on work and environmental protection during all assembly operations
  • Care and maintenance of tools and materials to ensure efficient work execution

Profile required

  • Vocational training completed as an installer of AFC or AFC solar systems or equivalent technical training in the field of electronics, building or building technology
  • Knowledge of the German language at the native level for precise and safe communication in the yard
  • Valid category B driving licence to move to yards
  • Experience in installing photovoltaic systems and knowledge of Swiss safety and electrical systems (e.g. NIV, NIN)
  • Ability to use electrical tools, measuring instruments and mounting techniques
  • Autonomous, meticulous and solution-oriented working style, combined with teamwork
  • Availability to perform occasionally outdoor activities and in different locations within Switzerland
  • Basic knowledge in the use of technical drawings, mounting patterns and documentation are an advantage
  • Commitment for sustainable energy solutions and interest in further development in the photovoltaic sector
