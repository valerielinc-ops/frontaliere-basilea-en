The Position Quality assurance for the production of clinical products is the quality partner for all manufacturing companies in the pharmaceutical-technical development in Switzerland. This includes all products that are manufactured and packaged for clinical trials in Switzerland, i.e. synthetic molecules, parenteral drug forms and related analytics at the Basel site.

The possibility

They support the Quality Systems team as well as the Swiss Technical Development sites.

Their main responsibilities are defined as follows

  • Support the implementation of global Roche standards and requirements as defined in the PQS (Pharma Quality System) within IMP Switzerland.
  • Assisting in the continuous improvement of quality systems support and collaboration with quality assurance managers within quality assurance for clinical device manufacturing
  • Processing of projects that are free in scope and orientation and are based on your strengths. The aim of these projects is to support the team in its work.

Who you are

  • You complete a scientific degree (e.g. pharmacy, chemistry, biology engineering) and are interested in gaining initial experience in the pharmaceutical industry as part of an internship.
  • An initiative and flexible personality with a strong desire to work and learn in a constantly changing environment.
  • A positive mindset driven by creativity, curiosity and passion.
  • Results-oriented person who likes to work independently.
  • Excellent planning and organizational skills to handle multiple tasks simultaneously, analytical skills and an innovative way to solve problems
  • High competence in building and maintaining constructive and effective relationships with team members, internal and external partners
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills in German as well as in-depth knowledge of English.

Additional information

  • Start: from 1 February 2027 for 6-12 months in Basel
  • Your application must include a CV and a letter of motivation.
  • Please note, due to regulations, non-EU/EFTA citizens must submit a certificate from the university as part of the application documents, stating that an internship is mandatory, as well as the duration of the mandatory internship.
