Apprenticeship Laborant/in EFZ Department of Chemistry as of 01. August 2027 (100%)

Lonza is now a world leader in life sciences, operating on three continents. We work in science, but there is no magic formula for how we approach work. Our most important scientific solution is talented employees who collaborate and develop ideas to help other companies help people. In return, our employees actively shape their careers, because their ideas, big or small, improve the world. And that's the kind of work we want to be part of.

Lonza in Basel offers you an exciting and practical training as a laboratory assistant / in EFZ Chemie. In this three-year apprenticeship you will get to know different chemical fields of work. The company language is English, which allows you to use your language skills on a daily basis and continuously expand them. You will conduct experiments, produce results, work with modern laboratory equipment and gain a comprehensive insight into the world of the chemical and pharmaceutical industry. You will develop a deep understanding of chemical reactions, analytical methods, safety and environmental regulations, and regulatory requirements. During your training, you will be supervised by experienced vocational trainers and work closely with various specialist areas.

Theoretical training takes place at the vocational school in Muttenz (BL). There you will learn all the basic knowledge in the respective subject areas. The training is complemented by intercompany courses (üK), in which you acquire additional practical skills.

Your tasks

  • Performing chemical and physicochemical experiments and analyses
  • Working with modern laboratory and analysis equipment
  • Documentation and evaluation of test results according to scientific and regulatory standards
  • Sample preparation and handling of various chemicals and substances
  • Participation in the development and optimization of chemical processes
  • Support for research, quality control and production

Your requirement profile

  • Higher or middle-level education or graduation
  • Interest in chemistry, natural sciences and laboratory work
  • Precise, careful and responsible work
  • Joy of practical work and handling of technical equipment
  • Teamwork and reliability
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Benefits for learners

  • 6 weeks holidays and additional paid holidays
  • General subscription (GA) for CHF 1.- per day
  • Personal IT equipment
  • Lunch Check is a food benefit that allows you to get a discount or pay for meals for free
  • E-bikes for private and business trips
  • Free, unlimited access to online learning platforms and language courses

Are you interested in our apprenticeship and want to become part of Lonza? Then we look forward to your online application via our recruiting tool with the following documents:

  • Certificates of the last three years of school (secondary school)
  • CV with photo
  • Letter of motivation

Success for us means morally acceptable progress.

Lonza is joined by people who take on challenges and use their creativity to develop new ideas for complex problems. Together we make a contribution that can improve the lives of many people around the world. This offers them the certainty and satisfaction of really making a difference with their own work.
