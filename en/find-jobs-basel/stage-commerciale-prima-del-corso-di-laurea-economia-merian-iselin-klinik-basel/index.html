Full description

Economy meets health care – Your internship with a future perspective! What if you had the opportunity to improve people’s health well-being even if you weren’t in care? Imagine gaining deep insight into managing one of the most important areas of our society while gaining valuable work experience for your future professional development.

Commercial internship Mandatory before the Bachelor's degree program Wirtschaf t This is like the “future to touch” – or? Our team at the Merian Iselin Clinic is there for you and offers you first-class opportunities for a successful entry into your professional career. The Merian Iselin Clinic is a state-of-the-art private clinic in Basel that specializes in treatments in the fields of orthopaedics, surgery and urology. Interested? Then apply now for the internship start from August 2027.

Your main tasks

  • You will carry out and coordinate administrative work/correspondence both in the area of real estate management and accounting as well as in the CFO assistant area
  • You process outpatient and inpatient cases from collection to billing and support in report management
  • You communicate with patients, medical doctors, health insurance companies and with internal areas
  • You get the chance to work in many exciting areas and share responsibility for your tasks

Your training path

  • Commercial internship in health care, duration 1 year
  • Before the start of the internship: mandatory trial day to get to know the team and work

You bring with you

  • Reliability and motivation to participate in an exciting working environment and learn new things
  • Communication ability, high comprehension and ability to work focused and accurately
  • High willingness to work with the team, at the same time the ability to work independently
  • An appreciative and friendly way of dealing with people
  • Successful high school graduation

Our commitment to your education

  • Structured induction and comprehensive support to achieve the internship goals
  • You are part of a well-established team
  • 40-hour week, 5 weeks holidays
  • Excellent lunch in the restaurant at discounted prices as well as discounts on fitness training and sports at Merian Santé

Your direct contact person Do you have questions? Please contact: Nicole Bammerlin, assistant CFO, telephone +41 61 305 12 39. We look forward to your application!
