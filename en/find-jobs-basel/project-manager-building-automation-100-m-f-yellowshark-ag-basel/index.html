Project Manager Building Automation 100% (m/f)

4000 Basel Building Services Engineering

  • Permanent position

Ofis Salary band CHF 95,000 -120,000

01 Future employer

For our client, an established company in the field of technical building services and automation, we are looking for an experienced, solution-oriented professional to work as a Project Manager Building Automation (m/f).

The company implements modern automation solutions for demanding building services projects, combining control, regulation and automation technology. In this position, you will play a key role in the planning, implementation and commissioning of projects, maintaining close contact with customers, specialist planners and other trades involved.

02 Your task

  • Independent planning and execution of projects in the field of building automation.
  • Development and implementation of automation solutions for heating, ventilation, air-conditioning and refrigeration systems, as well as for room automation.
  • Project design and programming of control and regulation systems.
  • Conducting commissioning and functional tests of the implemented systems.
  • Coordination of the various specialist areas involved and close collaboration with the heating, ventilation, refrigeration, plumbing and electrical trades.
  • Responsibility for the technical, scheduling and organizational implementation of the projects.
  • Monitoring the economic project objectives and assuming commercial responsibility.
  • Direct support and advice for customers throughout the entire project execution.
  • Close collaboration with Sales in supporting existing customers and developing new projects.
  • Developing practical solutions to technical and project-specific issues.

04 Your profile

  • Completed basic technical training, for example in electrical engineering, building services engineering or automation.
  • Further training at HF or FH level, or a comparable qualification, is an advantage.
  • Several years of practical experience in building automation.
  • Good knowledge in at least several areas of technical building equipment, in particular heating, ventilation, air conditioning, plumbing and electrical systems.
  • Alternatively, in-depth experience in industrial or process automation.
  • Experience in programming and commissioning automated systems.
  • Good technical understanding as well as a structured and solution-oriented way of working.
  • Strong organizational skills and enjoyment of independently managing projects.
  • Resilience and a sense of responsibility.
  • Customer-oriented manner and enjoyment of working with different specialist departments.
  • Team-oriented personality with excellent communication skills.
