Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in the Health, Safety department. Environment (HSE) we are looking for a dedicated HSE Specialist – Projects (m/f/d), workload (80-100%)

Their tasks

  • Contact person for HSE topics in project teams of investment projects, in particular infrastructure assets.
  • Advising project managers in all planning, execution and commissioning phases regarding plant safety and environmental protection.
  • Moderation and implementation of risk analyses of process and infrastructure facilities.
  • Review of the implementation of measures and compliance with safety standards in the commissioning phase of plants.
  • Responsible for thorough review and release of security-related documents.
  • Active cooperation with internal specialist organisations and external partner companies

Your profile

  • Basic technical or chemical training in process engineering/plant engineering or equivalent training
  • Specialist for occupational safety and health protection AS/GS or expert AS/GS
  • Several years of project experience in the field of plant construction and plant safety
  • Experience in the implementation and moderation of the most important risk analysis methods (e.g. HAZOP, Zurich risk analysis).
  • Quick understanding, technical understanding and a solution-oriented way of thinking
  • Secure, competent appearance and strong communication skills in words and writing (flowing knowledge of German and English)
  • Flexible and independent working methods, as well as high personal responsibility
  • Good MS Office knowledge and openness to digital working methods

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
