Full description

More than 1000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The CSD Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventiveness, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal solutions for our customers with a plus for the quality of life and the environment are our trademark. Do you enjoy working in a professional and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency? Become part of our success!

We are looking for a geologist/atlast specialist 80-100% (m/f/d) for our infrastructure division in Pratteln.

Your responsibilities

  • Leading our geologist team
  • Independent implementation of geological-geotechnical ground investigations, contaminated sites, groundwater investigations from offering to project completion (exploratory concepts, procurement of third-party services, construction management and field work, reporting)
  • Writing of offers, concepts (investigations, material management, water management, substitute measures for groundwater protection, soil protection), technical reports, opinions, etc.
  • Support for the preparation and partial processing of tender documents
  • Engineering geology, waste management, hydrogeology, soil protection
  • Supporting the infrastructure engineering team

Your profile

  • Completed study of earth sciences: Msc od. doctorate
  • Desirable: Further education in the civil engineering sector od. Environmental sciences
  • At least five years of experience in the desired professional field
  • Initiative Personality with rapid comprehension and skillful in dealing with customers
  • Oral and written expression in German
  • First leadership experience already made
  • Driving licence Cat. B

We offer

  • Exciting tasks in projects
  • Integration into the network of various departments
  • Professional and personal development
  • A dynamic environment and a respectful approach
  • Flexible conditions for optimal work and leisure
