Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a production engineer (m/f/d) to complement our team and actively shape the industrial implementation of innovative sensor solutions. In this role, you will make an important contribution to the successful introduction of new products, the further development of manufacturing processes and the sustainable improvement of our production processes.

What is this point about?

Are you excited about accompanying new products from development to series production? Do you want to sustainably improve production processes and systematically solve technical challenges? Does it appeal to you, together with colleagues from development, quality and production, to shape the future of our sensor production?

Then this position could be just right for you. In this function, you play a central role at the interface between development and production. They work closely with various specialist areas and actively shape the further development of our production processes.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Introduce new products and manufacturing processes from the development phase to successful series production.
  • Manage projects for process development as well as for the sustainable increase of quality, productivity and profitability.
  • represent sensor production in interdisciplinary projects and work closely with the areas of development, quality and production.
  • Analyse production processes, identify potential for improvement and implement effective optimization measures.
  • carry out technical root cause analyses of quality and process deviations and establish sustainable solutions.
  • Support production in technical issues and drive continuous improvements.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed studies in mechanical engineering, mechatronics, production engineering or a comparable technical discipline.
  • have several years of experience in the production environment, ideally in process development or manufacturing technology.
  • bring in-depth experience in project management as well as in lean and continuous improvement methods.
  • have good knowledge of MS Office and SAP or comparable ERP systems as well as a strong understanding of technical data analyses and process key figures.
  • convince through analytical thinking, a structured working method, a high level of initiative as well as strong communication and team skills.
  • German and English are very good at word and writing.

What can you expect from us?

We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.

  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We value a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working hours and the opportunity to work up to 25% mobile.
  • We support sustainable mobility offers such as public transport to make it easier for our employees to commute.
  • We support family-friendly concepts, including childcare and childcare during school holidays.
  • We offer you an appreciative, international working environment where collaboration, innovation and personal growth are encouraged.

Thank you for your understanding.
