Full description

For a modern, customer-oriented trust company in the Basel region with a focus on personal advice, high quality and digital processes, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as a trustee. In a collegiate environment with flat hierarchies, you independently manage your own mandate portfolio, benefit from extremely flexible remote work models and actively shape the future of the company. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Mandate support: Independent and holistic support of an own customer portfolio including general trust and administration tasks.
  • Financial and payroll accounting: Independent management of financial and payroll accounts as well as processing of VAT accounts.
  • Financial statements & audits: Assistance in and preparation of financial statements (individual companies, GmbH, AG) as well as support for audits and tax audits.
  • Taxation: preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • Real estate: management of mandates in the field of real estate accounting.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Completed basic commercial training as well as further training in trusting (e.g. clerk:in trust or in training for/to the trustee:in with federal certificate of advantage).
  • Experience: at least 5 years of in-depth professional experience in the Swiss trust system
  • Experience in real estate accounting is a plus.
  • Expertise: Very good knowledge of Swiss accounting, tax law and VAT.
  • Languages: stylish knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Personality: Independent, exact and reliable working method combined with a customer-oriented appearance and team spirit.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Maximum flexibility: Work independently of location (flexible remote work) and free time allocation as well as the possibility of increasing the amount of work required.
  • Culture & design: Flat hierarchies, short decision paths, a lot of personal responsibility and the perspective to actively shape the company.
  • Conditions: Permanent employment in the Basel region with direct client contact, modern digital processes and attractive employee discounts.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
