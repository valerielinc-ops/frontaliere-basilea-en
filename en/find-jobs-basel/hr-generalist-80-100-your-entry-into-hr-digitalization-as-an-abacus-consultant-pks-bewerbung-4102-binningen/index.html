ID: Category: HRM Region: Basel Reference: HR generalist 80-100% – Your entry into HR digitalization Actively shape the future of human resources. The HR world is constantly evolving. Digital processes, modern ERP solutions and automated processes are becoming increasingly important. Companies therefore need specialists who not only understand HR, but also use their experience to optimize and further develop processes. If you are working as an HR generalist today and would like to use your practical experience in a varied consulting function in the future, this position offers you an exciting perspective. Our customer is a successful, ISO 9001 certified company with around 30 employees and many years of specialization in ABACUS solutions. In order to strengthen the team, we are looking for a committed personality as HR generalist 80-100% – Your entry into HR digitalization as an ABACUS Consultant

Their tasks

  • After a thorough induction, you will accompany companies in the introduction and further development of the ABACUS HR and wage modules
  • Advising customers on the digitalization and optimization of their HR and payroll processes
  • Analysis of customer requirements and parameterization of the software
  • Participation in introductory projects including workshops and user training
  • Creation of concepts, documentation and training materials
  • Support of customers in support and support of projects with regard to quality, deadlines and budget

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience as an HR generalist or in a comparable function with a focus on personnel administration and payroll
  • Interest in digitalization and modern HR and ERP solutions
  • Enjoyment of customer contact and the transfer of knowledge
  • Structured, independent and service-oriented working method
  • Networked thinking and strong communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • French and/or English are beneficial
  • ABACUS knowledge is not a requirement. Decisive are your HR expertise, your willingness to learn and the motivation to familiarize yourself with a future-oriented task.

This is what awaits you

  • Comprehensive and structured induction with targeted further training
  • Exciting customer projects in a wide range of industries
  • Collegial, family working environment with short decision paths
  • Modern office space at a central location in Basel
  • Progressive employment conditions with trusted working hours and 5 weeks holidays
  • Long-term development opportunities in a successful company
