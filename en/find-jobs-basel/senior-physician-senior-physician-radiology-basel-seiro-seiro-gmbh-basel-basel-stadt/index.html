Full description

For you, radiology is more than imaging – you recognize what is hidden to others and thus create clarity for patients. In Basel, as a senior physician or senior physician in radiology, you take on a central role in a highly specialized team that covers the entire imaging diagnostics at 80-100% by agreement. They work with the latest technology on site, in a collegial environment that combines professional depth with human closeness. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They assume a central function in radiological care and actively shape professional development
  • You work interdisciplinary with various specialist departments and surrounding hospitals
  • They are involved in supervision and training of assistant doctors and promote their professional development
  • Participate in telephone call readiness with second-level support instead of classic piquetting services, conveniently from home
  • They carry out daily reports and thus ensure a seamless handover

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title in radiology or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Interest in university teaching as well as in continuous further education and training

That sets them apart.

  • Engaged, flexible and team-oriented personality with self-responsible actions
  • High social and clinical competence as well as collaborative cooperation in a team and with other disciplines
  • Joy of passing on knowledge and continually deepening yourself professionally

This is what your future employer offers you

  • State-of-the-art imaging techniques, including 3-Tesla MRI and 64-line CT, for the highest diagnostic quality
  • Motivated, friendly team with an appreciative working atmosphere and high personal flexibility
  • Various possibilities for scientific work and active involvement in research projects
  • Generous holiday regime: up to 50 years 30 days, from 50 years 33 days, from 60 years 37 days

  • Family-friendly additional benefits: extended child allowances, public transport benefits, continued pay up to 730 days in case of sickness, maternity leave extended to 16 weeks at full wage
  • Staff discount in in-house restaurants

  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
