Full description

For <b>Raiffeisenbank Liestal-Oberbaselbiet</b>, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Liestal, Sissach and Gelterkinden, we are looking for a responsible and independent personality as a customer advisor:in.

Tasks

  • Ensuring smooth and customer-oriented operation at the Welcome Desk as well as efficient and needs-based operation and advice in the customer zone (including customer vault)
  • Ensuring simple, standardised advice on numbers, savings, investing and provisioning
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the customer’s living situation as well as within the segment-specific / customer-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular in contact campaigns

Requirements

  • Completed banking training or comparable training with several years of experience in banking
  • Team-capable personality with development potential
  • Self-employed, precise and responsible work
  • Enthusiastic and sales-oriented personality with a winning appearance
  • Enjoying active customer acquisition and developing long-term customer relationships
