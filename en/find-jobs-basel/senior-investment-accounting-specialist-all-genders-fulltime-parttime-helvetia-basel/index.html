What you'll be doing

  • Own Investment Accounting topics for your assigned market unit and act as a trusted business partner.
  • Ensure accurate investment accounting and period-end closings under IFRS and local GAAP.
  • Deliver meaningful analyses, reporting and insights for management and governance bodies.
  • Develop and maintain the internal control system and support regulatory compliance.
  • Contribute actively to our Finance transformation programmes including setting up SimCorp Dimension: designing processes, configuration and controls
  • Support internal and external audits
  • Partner with stakeholders across Finance, Investments and Risk.

What you'll bring to the role

  • Degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration or a related field.
  • 5-8 years of experience in Investment Accounting, Asset Management, Insurance or Financial Services.
  • Hands-on experience with SimCorp Dimension; SAP S/4HANA is a plus.
  • Strong knowledge of IFRS and local accounting standards.
  • Analytical mindset, collaborative approach and a passion for continuous improvement.
  • Fluent German and English skills.
