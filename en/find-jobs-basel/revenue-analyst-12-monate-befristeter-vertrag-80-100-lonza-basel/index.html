For our successful Drug Product (DP) focused branch located in Basel and Stein, Switzerland, we have exciting opportunities. Become part of a group that enables its customers to meet some of the greatest challenges in patient treatment and join us by applying for the Revenue Analyst fix-term contract position.

What you will get

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance

What you will do

  • Monitor Drug Product revenue and cost performance, ensuring the accuracy and integrity of financial data across SAP, Planview, and other systems
  • Partner with manufacturing sites, Program Managers, and supporting functions to track and coordinate activities impacting revenue and business performance
  • Perform deferred revenue and work-in-progress analysis to support accurate and compliant financial reporting
  • Support month-end close activities, reconciliations, audits, and data validations in collaboration with Finance teams
  • Analyze revenue performance, identify risks and trends, and provide insights to Program Management and Leadership teams
  • Develop and maintain reporting tools and dashboards, including monthly leadership reports and ad hoc business analyses
  • Identify process improvement opportunities and enhance reporting transparency, efficiency, and automation

What we are looking for

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Project Management, Engineering, Life Sciences, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related field
  • Experience in project coordination, commercial operations, financial analysis, or business support within a life sciences or pharmaceutical environment
  • Strong financial and commercial awareness, with the ability to understand project costs, risks, and business impact
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Microsoft 365 applications; experience with Power BI, Microsoft Lists, Power Automate, SAP, or similar tools is an advantage
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to collaborate across different functions and organizational levels
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities while meeting deadlines in a fast-paced environment
  • Fluent written and spoken English, with a proactive, detail-oriented, and accountable approach to work
