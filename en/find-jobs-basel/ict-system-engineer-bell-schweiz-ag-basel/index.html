Full description

Are you passionate about IT, think in terms of processes, and would like to take on responsibility? Then help actively shape our Identity & Access Management service! We are a committed IT team that reliably operates central IT services and continuously develops them further. We are looking for a committed individual to take responsibility for our Identity & Access Management (IAM) service based on the One Identity Manager and actively drive its further development. In this role, you will be the central interface between IT, specialist departments, external partners and technical specialists. In addition to your IAM focus, you will also be happy to contribute to other exciting topics in our diverse IT landscape.

We can only consider applicants who are authorized to work in Switzerland (EU/EFTA). We only consider direct applications.

  • Contract type: Permanent
  • Workload: 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible working hours
  • Start date: Immediately
  • Language: German
  • Work location: Bell Schweiz AG, Elsässerstrasse 174 4056 Basel

Responsibilities

  • Responsibility for the IAM service: You ensure the stable operation of our Identity & Access Management and manage the further development of the One Identity Manager as well as the implementation of new requirements
  • Process management and automation: You design and optimize IAM processes, particularly the identity lifecycle (Joiner, Mover, Leaver), role and access rights management, and the automation of existing workflows
  • Interface and partner management: You are the central contact for internal specialist departments, connected systems and external IAM consultants. You coordinate the collaboration and ensure the quality of the results
  • Service management and governance: You ensure clear responsibilities, transparent processes and proper documentation. You also coordinate incidents, changes and technical dependencies with the responsible specialists
  • Participation in other IT services: Depending on your interests and our needs, you will also contribute to other topics within our team, such as client management, software packaging and distribution, Omnissa VDI, Citrix, mobile device management (MDM), monitoring, SharePoint or SQL management
  • On-call duty: You will occasionally take on on-call duties and be prepared to work weekends when needed

Requirements

  • Education and specialist knowledge: You have completed IT training or a comparable qualification and have experience in Identity & Access Management, service management or application management
  • IAM expertise: You understand the technical and process-related interrelationships in Identity & Access Management. Experience with the One Identity Manager is an advantage
  • Service and process understanding: You work in a structured manner, set priorities and enjoy sustainably improving and further developing services and processes
  • Independence and organization: You enjoy taking responsibility, coordinating internal and external partners, and driving topics forward independently
  • Communication and team spirit: You communicate clearly, enjoy working with various stakeholders, and find suitable solutions together
  • Interest in new technologies: You enjoy looking beyond your own area of expertise, are curious about other IT technologies, and are willing to familiarize yourself with new topics
  • Commitment and flexibility: You impress with your service-oriented approach to work and are willing to work on-call shifts and occasional weekends.

What we offer

  • Work-life balance: A balanced work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 vacation days and transparent, electronic time recording. This allows you to compensate for any overtime flexibly with additional days off.
  • Catering: Culinary experiences are very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu including soup and salad, water & tea, and free fruit as well as high-quality products at heavily reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient access to public transport, attractive ticket subsidies and sufficient parking spaces in the parking garage.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive training opportunities, high-quality training at the Coop Education Center and diverse personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness memberships – for health and leisure enjoyment!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our department event and strengthen team spirit at the company run – celebrating, exercising and making connections together!
  • Market-based salaries and social benefits: Secure your future with our above-average pension provision, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonus and attractive bonuses for employee referrals. You will also receive Coop's employee discount card and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Shape your working hours flexibly with part-time models and experience an inclusive working environment in which diversity is valued and promoted!
