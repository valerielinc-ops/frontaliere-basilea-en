Full description

You combine purchasing, marketing and sales and perfectly showcase our fresh worlds. With your sense of quality, presentation and trends, you create shopping experiences and enthusiastically pass on your know-how to the teams in the stores

Mansioni

  • With your expertise you supervise and advise our branches in the field of freshness competently and practically
  • You ensure that our concepts for product range and presentation are successfully and uniformly implemented
  • The professional education and training of our specialist and team leaders* is in your hands – you impart knowledge with enthusiasm and practical relevance
  • You keep an eye on spoilage, analyze causes and derive effective measures for optimization
  • In the further development of our presentation principles for the stores, you actively contribute your ideas and experience
  • You support in the planning and design of new openings as well as changes in our branches and thus contribute to a convincing appearance at the POS
  • You observe the market and our competitors, carry out price checks and use your findings for continuous development
  • You appreciate the varied combination of office work and assignments directly on site in our branches
  • You actively participate in projects, contribute your expertise and provide important impulses

Requirements

  • Mind. 2 years: in the retail trade in a similar function
  • Eidg. Professional ID: Koch/Köchin comparable training (butcher*in)
  • You have in-depth expertise in the field of freshness
  • Freshness, quality and an emotional presentation of goods are important to you and you have a good feeling for a convincing appearance at the POS
  • You are an assertive personality and convince with a strong communication ability in dealing with different stakeholders
  • You work independently and structured, organize yourself efficiently and tackle tasks in a solution- and customer-oriented manner
  • You have a valid category B driving licence
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central place of work: Well accessible by public and private transport.
  • Modern infrastructure: Inviting and functional office world, modern IT infrastructure
  • Flexible work location / home office: possibility of home office when fulfilling the function
  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance AN share 8.5%, AG share 17%
