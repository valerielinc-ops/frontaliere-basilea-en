Full description

Technology is your passion. Our state-of-the-art trains transport thousands of tons of goods reliably through the Alps every day, connecting tens of thousands of people. As part of a well-established team, you are an indispensable link in our transport chain, because a lot can happen on the long journey through Europe.

This is waiting for you

  • Perform technical checks on departure and train preparation on our freight trains
  • Manoeuvring missions
  • Checking purchased services for quality and completeness
  • Conducting technical audits in the field of wagon technology and dangerous goods at various stations of departure and tank depots
  • Regulating defective wagons and loads, including triggering the required transport orders

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as technical:r controller:in TKC, shunting companion:in Cat. Ai40 or train preparer:in
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Flexibility for irregular assignments throughout Switzerland, sometimes also in border countries
  • Readiness for regular cooking
  • Independent, resilient and initiative personality with a strong team spirit

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts possible
  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • You have free time when others work. For commutes at night and on weekends there are surcharges.
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health

About the BLS Cargo

We can't move mountains, of course. It doesn't matter. Because as experienced alpinists: In rail freight transport, we see mountains as a challenge and not as an obstacle. We transport heavy goods from the North Sea through the Alps to the Mediterranean, and that is a tricky task. We master them in a team with our approximately 450 employees in Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Italy. Our customers are international, our roots local. This is another reason why it is exceptionally exciting to work for BLS Cargo.
