Within Roche Pharma Research & Early Development (pRED), the Translational Drug Metabolism, Pharmacokinetics, and Pharmacodynamics (tDMPK/PD) department plays an important role in understanding how potential drug candidates behave in the body. Our work combines experimental research, computational modeling, and advanced analytical technologies to investigate drug properties and support decisions throughout drug discovery and development. We are expanding our capabilities in laboratory automation and digitalization, exploring how new technologies, including artificial intelligence, can improve laboratory workflows and make our research more efficient.

The Opportunity You will explore how large language models (LLMs) can be used to automate laboratory workflows in pharmaceutical research. The project focuses on connecting LLMs with laboratory equipment and software, allowing users to interact with laboratory systems through natural language and automate selected tasks.

Your work will involve evaluating existing tools and frameworks for LLM-based automation, developing a proof of concept using a laboratory liquid handling system, and assessing the potential and limitations of this approach in a real laboratory environment.

Specifically, you will

  • Research and compare existing tools and frameworks for connecting LLMs with external software and laboratory equipment.
  • Develop a proof of concept that connects an LLM to a laboratory liquid handling system through suitable software interfaces.
  • Explore how natural language instructions can be translated into executable laboratory workflows.
  • Design and perform tests to evaluate the reliability, accuracy, and limitations of the developed approach.
  • Collaborate with laboratory scientists and automation experts to understand existing workflows and identify suitable applications.
  • Document your implementation, summarize your findings, and provide recommendations for further development.

Who You Are

  • You hold a Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Bioinformatics, or a related field; or are currently enrolled in a relevant Master's or PhD program.
  • You have strong programming skills in Python and experience with software development.
  • You have experience with or a strong interest in large language models (LLMs), AI agents, and their integration with external tools.
  • You are familiar with APIs, software integration, and version control systems such as Git.

  • You enjoy exploring new technologies and solving open-ended technical problems.
  • You are comfortable working independently and collaborating with colleagues from different scientific backgrounds.
  • You have a good command of spoken and written English.
