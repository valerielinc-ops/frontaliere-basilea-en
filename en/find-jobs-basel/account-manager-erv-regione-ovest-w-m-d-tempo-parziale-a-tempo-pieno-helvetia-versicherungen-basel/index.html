Full description

The holiday is the best time of the year. So, if something goes wrong, it's all the more annoying! With your commitment, you will enable our customers to enjoy a careless holiday. Because the European Travel Insurance ERV is the leading provider of solutions in the areas of leisure, travel and mobility for private and corporate customers in Switzerland. From travel cancellation/SOS to luggage protection, to specialized solutions such as animal insurance, ski and sports covers and ticket protection – our customers are fully secure for every adventure and you contribute to this with your work.   • VBV certification at least non-life

  • Expressed sales and customer orientation as well as communication strength
  • Self-employed, Structured and goal-oriented working methods
  • Negotiable French and German (English of advantage)
