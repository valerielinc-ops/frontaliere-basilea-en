Your Opportunity

Planning and Financial Management

  • Proactively acquires a deep understanding of the external environment (healthcare system, policies and reforms) to anticipate future trends and formulates a strategic view of market, competition and products erosion in short-term and long-term
  • Localize and define life cycle management strategy for products in charge.
  • Develop business models in different channels for products in charge
  • Develop a deep understanding of key stakeholders, including key accounts, payers, patient advocacy groups, professional associations, and policymakers, and develop development plans for the top clinical opinion leaders for the therapy area.
  • Build marketing plans analyzing the relevant markets and defining long- and short-term qualitative and quantitative objectives.
  • Where relevant, implement the launch excellence framework to build leading launch plans.
  • Translates the brand plan into a strong actionable operational plan with clear goals, actions, accountabilities, and measures across the cross-functional team.
  • Sets responsible and challenging goals with appropriate KPIs for the brand that have a clear positive impact on business performance.
  • Ensure brand-in-charge performance

Resource Management / Marketing Excellence

  • Ensuring the good implementation of tactical plans by collaborating with cross-functional team (Sales Force/Market Access/Medical Affairs/SFE)
  • Ensuring supply and healthy stock.
  • Optimizing segmentation and targeting strategies for the products under management.
  • Build brand communication package implement for activities to make sure quality and brand engagement & reach
  • Key in charge for the Brand KPIs (Lead, Lag), sales performance, and A&P

Tactical Market Plan / Implementation

  • Achieving Marketing objectives as agreed with management, managing the development and implementation of marketing plans consistent with approval strategy
  • Ensuring the achievement of products’ budget in the approved BP and integrated marketing plan.

  • Innovation and MCM apply in activities
