Do you have experience in heating systems, like working with your hands and interested in modern building technology? PEMSA Basel seeks, on behalf of one of our customers, a qualified EFZ/EBA (m/f/d) heating system installer for versatile tasks in Basel and Basel Country. Whether it’s traditional heating systems, modern heat pumps or renovations of existing systems, you’ll benefit from your technical skills and, together with your team, you’ll ensure an art performance.

Tasks

  • Installation and installation of heating systems in newly built buildings and renovations
  • Installation and connection of heating and distribution pipes
  • Installation of radiators, floor heating and other components
  • Assistance in installing heat pumps and heat generators
  • Performance of adaptation, repair and remediation
  • Performance of control and operation within your qualifications
  • Work execution according to installation schemes and technical specifications

Profile requested

  • Complete training as an AFC heating system installer, AFC heating system technician or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in the installation of heating systems is preferential title
  • Manual skills and technical skills
  • Knowledge of common heating systems and installation materials
  • Good knowledge of German
  • How to work autonomous, accurate and reliable
  • Ability to work in team and physical endurance
  • Category B licence is preferential title

What do we offer?

  • Long-term gains
  • Support for continuing training
  • Collective events
  • Activities throughout Switzerland
  • Expert and competent staff consultants
