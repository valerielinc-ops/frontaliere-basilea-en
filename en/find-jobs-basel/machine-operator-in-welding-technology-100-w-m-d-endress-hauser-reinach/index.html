Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a machine operator in welding technology (f/m/d) to complement our team and to contribute with technical skill and care to safe, high-quality and efficient production at Endress+Hauser Flowtec AG. At the Reinach site, we use modern plants to produce flow measurement technology for customers worldwide. What is this point about? Do you like working with modern welding systems and automated processes? Do you appreciate an environment where quality, safety and teamwork are at the heart of our efforts? Do you want to actively contribute to ensuring that our products are produced reliably and precisely? In this role, you assume responsibility in the ongoing production operation, work closely with various specialist areas and ensure that modern welding technology is used safely and efficiently in everyday life. The site is located in the production area. What tasks will you take on? You will:

  • TIG and MAG welding robots, helium leak esters and blasting systems in ongoing production.
  • Inspect and monitor rotating machinery and ensure stable operation.
  • Monitor manufacturing processes in terms of quality, safety and productivity.
  • Resolve minor faults and carry out simple repairs directly on the plants.
  • Actively participate in CIP, Lean and TPM activities and support continuous improvement.
  • Ensure cleanliness and order in the workplace with consistent application of the 5S principles. What do we expect from you? They:
  • are flexible in 2-shift operation
  • have completed basic mechanical training.
  • bring a further development and practical experience in the field of welding.
  • have good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • know the common MS Office tools.
  • work responsibly, independently and resiliently.
  • are characterized by their communication and teamwork ability. What can you expect from us?
  • A stable working environment in a family-run company with a long-term perspective.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e‐learning.
  • A modern production environment with contemporary welding and plant technology.
  • Careful familiarization as well as continuous training and further education opportunities.
  • An appreciative cooperation in the team and clear safety and quality standards.
  • Attractive employment conditions at the Reinach site as well as supporting offers for reconciling work and private life. How do you apply?
