Full description

Join us in shaping Bachem’s new global Quality organization. As Global Head Quality Systems & Services (a), 100% , you will provide global leadership for quality systems, eQMS, quality risk management, training, document management and global quality services across the Bachem network.

This is a unique opportunity to contribute your expertise to a strategic transformation and help define how this new organization will operate globally across Bachem. We encourage qualified colleagues from all Bachem locations to apply and bring their experience to this important journey.

Your key responsibilities

  • Lead the global Quality Systems & Services department across QMS, Quality Risk Management, Training, eQMS/document management and Global Quality Services & Support.
  • Oversee and continuously improve global QMS processes and platforms, ensuring compliant, efficient and harmonized execution across the business.
  • Define and maintain the global Quality Risk Management framework to support compliant, science- and risk-based decision-making.
  • Develop and oversee global eQMS, document management and GxP training standards, including governance, oversight and continuous improvement.
  • Build and develop a high-performing global team through clear accountabilities, strong collaboration, capability building and talent development.
  • Provide global quality support to sites and functions, including process guidance, system support, troubleshooting and knowledge sharing.

Your profile

  • University degree in life sciences, pharmacy, chemistry, biotechnology, engineering or a related scientific discipline
  • Master’s degree or PhD preferred.
  • Minimum 12–15 years of experience in the pharmaceutical, biotech or CDMO industry, with strong exposure to GMP-regulated environments.
  • Several years of global or multi-site leadership experience in Quality Systems, Quality Risk Management, Training, Operational Quality Support or related Quality functions.
  • Proven track record in driving quality system transformations and improving global QMS, eQMS/document management, deviation, CAPA, change control and training frameworks.
  • Strong expertise in Quality Risk Management, RCA methodologies, investigation standards and risk-based decision-making.
  • Strong understanding of global GMP requirements, quality system expectations and audit/inspection readiness.
  • Strong senior stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills across sites, functions and leadership levels.
  • Excellent English & German skills are required

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee development through numerous internal and external training opportunities
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as option for extra-mandatory pension provision with our Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to the benefits platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
