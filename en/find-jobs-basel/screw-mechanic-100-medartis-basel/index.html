Full description

To strengthen our production team in Basel, we are looking for a committed and professionally experienced personality as

Mechanist Screws 100%

Your responsibilities

  • Setting up and operating systems for producing implants in accordance with specified work and process documentation
  • Complete documentation of production processes in accordance with the applicable proof and quality requirements
  • Implementation of optical and dimensional controls on implants and their documentation
  • Monitoring the production process and ensuring compliance with all specified process standards
  • Active participation in the continuous improvement (CIP) and implementation and further development of the 6S process
  • Resolving minor disruptions to production facilities
  • Support of the maintenance team in maintenance and maintenance work
  • Compliance with GMP guidelines, quality standards and occupational safety regulations

Your qualifications

  • Completed technical vocational training as a polymechanic, precision mechanic, toolmaker or equivalent
  • At least 2-3 years of professional experience in CNC manufacturing, ideally on long lathes
  • Programming skills in Torno’s TB-Deco ADV and Citizen Long Turner Mitsubishi control are beneficial
  • Safe handling of MS Office and PC applications
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, basic knowledge of English
  • Independent, quality-conscious and reliable working method
  • main working hours in the day shift
  • Readiness for shift and Saturday missions desired

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.

We offer you a responsible and varied activity with high quality requirements in a growing, international company with progressive social benefits.
