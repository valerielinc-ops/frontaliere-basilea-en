Today we’re present in 35 markets with about 400 stores, online presence, and 3m+ fans across social channels.

About the Role

We are looking for a Junior Operational Allocation profile to support the distribution and allocation of merchandise across our retail network.

This role ensures that products are delivered to the right place, at the right time and in the right quantities, contributing to inventory optimization and overall business performance.

Your

  • Responsibilities

· Manage merchandise flow from warehouse and distribution centers to stores · Support the implementation and follow-up of new product launches in stores · Analyze product performance by country and trends to identify opportunities · Prepare weekly sales and inventory reviews and support re-forecasting activities · Optimize inventory levels and stock performance across stores · Support redistribution of high-potential products based on performance · Monitor core product availability and stock levels · Support pricing and promotional strategies to improve sales and stock efficiency · Collaborate with Merchandising, Planning, Operations and Logistics teams · Support assortment definition for new store openings · Contribute to the creation and monitoring of replenishment strategies · Analyze product performance to support decision-making and strategy extension

  • Profile

· Good knowledge of Excel · Strong analytical and problem-solving skills · Ability to work in a fast-paced environment · Team player with strong communication skills · Results-oriented mindset · Flexibility and adaptability · Interest in fashion and retail · Fluent in English; additional languages are a plus

What We Offer

· A dynamic, international and fast-paced working environment · The opportunity to gain experience in allocation and retail operations · Exposure to cross-functional teams and international markets · Competitive compensation and benefits package
