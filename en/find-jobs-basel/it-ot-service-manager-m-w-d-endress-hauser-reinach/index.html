Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

What is at stake here?

As IT/OT Service Manager (m/f/d), you work in a team that is responsible for the life cycle management of IT/OT Solutions or Services.

What tasks will you take on?

  • As a central point of contact, you will work very closely with our internal customers (production, engineering, development, etc.) in order to understand their needs and meet their requirements for IT/OT services.
  • You will analyze and model processes and translate business requirements into IT requirements for realization
  • You will develop IT/OT Services together with software architects and IT product managers
  • They become IT/OT services, responsible for their documentation, testing, monitoring and further development
  • You will take over the management of digitalization projects if necessary

What do we expect from you?

  • You have completed a technical degree with a specialization in electronics, computer science, automation technology or a comparable qualification
  • You have several years of professional experience with IT/OT systems (e.g. SCADA, MES, PLC, IoT platforms) in the industrial environment and ideally in the test environment
  • You have the ability to absorb and analyze customer requirements, create technical concepts and service suggestions
  • They have a good knowledge of English
  • Are a creative, communicative and team capable personality
  • You have experience in project management

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning
  • We support your work-life balance with flexible working hours and a working environment that takes your life reality into account
  • We offer support for sustainable mobility, e.g. B. Public transport grants
  • We promote family-friendly offers such as childcare and holiday programs for your children

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

*We kindly ask agencies, HR service providers or similar organizations to refrain from unsolicited submission of profiles and requests for cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. Unsolicited applications are not considered and deleted without further processing. Thank you for your understanding.
