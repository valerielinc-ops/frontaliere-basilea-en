Full description

Working independently. Carried in a team. Close to people. At Spitex Regio Liestal, we accompany people where they feel at home. Our catchment area includes 16 municipalities with around 49,000 inhabitants. To strengthen our nursing team at the Liestal site, we are looking for a Certified Nurse HF/FH (50-100%) Your task You will take on professional responsibility and accompany our customers holistically in their familiar living environment. Needs assessment and case management are essential components of your area of responsibility.

In particular, your tasks will include

  • Carrying out needs assessments and taking over case management
  • Holistic care and support for our customers at home
  • Advice for customers and their relatives on all aspects of home care
  • Close cooperation with family doctors and other partner organisations
  • Professional guidance and support of employees in an interdisciplinary team
  • Active participation in the creation of high-quality and needs-based care

You like to work independently and take on responsibility – at the same time, you can count on the professional exchange and support of a committed team. What you bring to the table

  • Diploma as a nurse HF/FH
  • Enjoyment of independent and responsible work
  • Professional competence and enjoyment of advising and supporting different people
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Category B driving licence
  • Ideally your own vehicle

What you can look forward to

  • Work independently with the backing of a dedicated team
  • Careful induction and personal support
  • A wide range of training and development opportunities
  • Appreciative cooperation, short distances and approachable leadership
  • Flexible and family-friendly employment conditions

Portrait SPITEX Regio Liestal is a non-profit organisation with a public mandate from 16 municipalities. For the approximately 49000 inhabitants of these municipalities, it provides a comprehensive range of non-hospital services in nursing, care and housekeeping with six interdisciplinary teams at three bases. In addition to help and care, it offers a meal service and a laundry service. SPITEX Regio Liestal employs 210 people and is an important partner in the regional health care system. Spitex Regio Liestal offers apprenticeships in the tertiary and secondary level and accompanies between 15 and 18 trainees every year, including external internships.
