Role overview

Tasks

  • You are responsible for taking over, preserving, developing and making available the archaeological collections owned by the Canton of Basel-Landschaft or left to him for care on the basis of the Treaty of Rome.
  • You initiate and accompany the key project for the organizational and spatial integration of the canton’s archaeological collections, including the re-inventory of the old stocks.
  • You develop the collection strategy for all archaeological collections as well as the collection concepts and processes derived from them and develop them continuously.
  • You are responsible for the lending as well as the management and further development of the Fund Depots.
  • You have the technical, human and administrative responsibility for leading a medium-sized team
  • You engage yourself creatively in concepts for the digitization of the cantonal collections together with the collections of the museum.
  • You get involved so that the collections are available for research and support the public companies, namely Augusta Raurica, in communicating scientific findings from the collection area.
  • You maintain relationships with national and international museum and non-museum collections as well as educational institutions

Requirements

  • You have completed university studies in Provincial Roman Archaeology, Classical Archaeology, Medieval Archaeology or Prehistory and Early History.
  • You will bring in-depth experience in the supervision and further development of archaeological collections.
