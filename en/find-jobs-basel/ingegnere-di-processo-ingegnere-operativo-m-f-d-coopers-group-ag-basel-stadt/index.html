Full description

For an international company in the pharmaceutical industry, we are looking for a committed personality as a Professional Process Engineer / Operations Engineer (m/f/d) to support engineering and investment projects in a highly regulated production environment.

In this role, you are responsible for the development and implementation of procedural solutions as well as the technical support of demanding production and supply plants.

Their main tasks

  • Development, optimization and implementation of process solutions
  • Planning and realization of investment and engineering projects from the concept phase to the transfer to the company
  • Ensuring deadline, cost and quality targets within the projects
  • Compliance and implementation of cGMP requirements in the engineering and production environment
  • Technical support and further development of complex plants in chemical and biotechnological production
  • Support for optimisation, conversion and capacity expansion of existing plants
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary project teams as well as close cooperation with production, quality assurance and other specialist areas
  • Support for active ingredient production in batch and continuous manufacturing processes

Must-haves

  • First professional experience in the GMP-regulated pharmaceutical or biotechnology environment
  • Sound IT user knowledge, especially with Microsoft and Google applications

  • Structured and independent working methods
  • Strong teamwork skills and high personal responsibility
  • Communication, decision-making and assertiveness

Nice-to-haves

  • Knowledge of the design of installations, pumps and heat exchangers
  • Experience in plant engineering or technical plant operation
  • Understanding production processes in the chemical or biopharmaceutical environment
