Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and create a long-term perspective for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a Head of Procurement Investment (m/f/d) for our Endress+Hauser Flow who will drive the strategic development of investment procurement and actively shape the future of our production site. What is this point about? Imagine being able to strategically shape global supplier networks, significantly influence complex investment projects and lead an experienced team into a modern, sustainable procurement organization. Do you enjoy international negotiations? Do you have a passion for optimized procurement processes? Do you like working as a strategic sparring partner for technology, production and management? Then this position offers you the opportunity to play a central role in the further development of our supply chain strategy. As head of department, you lead a team of seven employees, work closely with the strategic Purchasing Managers as well as technical and operational interfaces and shape the procurement landscape within our production center Flow in a sustainable manner. What tasks will you take on? They shall:

  • assume the strategic and operational management of the Sourcing Indirect department (including procurement investments, MRO, facilities management, IT services, auxiliary and consumables) – including management, coaching and further development of seven employees.
  • shape the future of the department and further develop an innovation-oriented purchasing strategy.
  • Take responsibility for the orientation of the product group and supplier strategy.
  • Be responsible for the suppliers at the Reinach site and support supplier development in the regions of China, India, USA and France.
  • Collaborate across departments with strategic Purchasing Managers as well as with construction, manufacturing and industrial engineering departments.
  • Develop optimized processes and ensure efficient integration of suppliers into project processes. What do we expect from you? They:
  • have a university degree with a technical background – ideally supplemented by a management education (e.g. MBA, MAS in Supply Chain Management or an equivalent qualification).
  • bring several years of experience in the supply chain management of a manufacturing industrial company.
  • have at least five years of leadership experience and proven success in developing teams.
  • have strong strategic, methodological and innovative skills.
  • have profound experience in project business as well as in indirect purchasing.
  • bring very good knowledge of German and negotiated English.
  • convince through organizational talent, social skills and a healthy assertiveness. What can you expect from us? We offer you:
  • Diverse training and further education opportunities as well as internal knowledge transfer and e-learning offers.
  • Support for sustainable mobility, especially in public transport.
  • A stable, family-run company with a long-term perspective.
  • Modern premises and ergonomically equipped workplaces.
  • Comprehensive occupational pension provision and attractive health services. How do you apply? Please upload your application documents via our career page. Applications by e-mail cannot be considered for data protection reasons.
