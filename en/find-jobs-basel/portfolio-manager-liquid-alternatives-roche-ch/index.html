The “Swiss Pension Asset Management” team is part of the Treasury & Financing department within Group Finance at Roche and manages the investments of the Swiss pension fund. To strengthen the team in Basel, we are looking for an individual for the newly created position of:

Portfolio Manager Liquid Alternatives

The opportunity

Your main tasks are

  • The design and implementation of the investment strategy in liquid alternative asset classes such as hedge funds, insurance-linked securities (ILS) and commodities
  • Liquidity and cash flow management
  • Selection and monitoring of external asset managers
  • Drawings, withdrawals and reconciliation processes
  • Regulatory compliance and risk monitoring
  • Creation of comprehensive reports and presentations in various committees

Who you are

  • Master's degree in (quantitative) finance, economics, econometrics or a related field
  • Professional qualifications such as CFA, AZEK or CAIA
  • 7 to over 10 years of experience in institutional asset management, with a particular focus on strategies in the areas of hedge funds, insurance-linked securities and/or commodities at a Swiss pension fund, an asset manager or an institutional investor
  • Proven success in managing liquid alternative investments in a regulated institutional environment
  • Sound practical knowledge of the Swiss pension fund landscape (BVG/BVV 2 regulations, ALM studies)
  • Strong analytical and digital skills as well as experience in data-driven decision-making
  • Ability to convey complex issues in an understandable way
  • Fluent in written and spoken German and English

If you would like to become part of a dynamic and future-oriented team, we look forward to receiving your application (including CV and letter of motivation).
