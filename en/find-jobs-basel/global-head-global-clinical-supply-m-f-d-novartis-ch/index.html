Role Purpose

The Global Head Global Clinical Supply is accountable for global, end-to-end clinical supply performance, ensuring Novartis medicines reach patients on time, every time and enabling successful delivery of the development portfolio. The role sets GCS strategic direction and translates it into supply strategies, investment priorities and organizational capabilities that deliver reliable execution and sustainable value. As the strategic interface between clinical and technical teams, the role aligns enterprise decisions and partnerships to anticipate risk, navigate complexity and respond to evolving portfolio needs. The role leads a significant digital and organizational transformation, moving GCS from manual coordination towards integrated, data-driven and AI-enabled supply orchestration. It owns adoption and business outcomes—not technology implementation alone—while safeguarding quality, compliance and uninterrupted patient supply. As a member of the TRD Global Leadership Team, the role shapes TRD strategy and builds the leadership, capabilities and culture required to sustain performance through transformation.
