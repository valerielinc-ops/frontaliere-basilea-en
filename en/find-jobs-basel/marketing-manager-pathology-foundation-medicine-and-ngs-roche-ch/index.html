Role Purpose

Lead marketing strategy and execution for Roche’s One Oncology portfolio in India, spanning Immunology, Molecular and Tissue diagnostics, with dedicated focus on Pathology, Foundation Medicine and Next‑Generation Sequencing (NGS).Drive brand growth, portfolio expansion, and KOL engagement to strengthen Roche’s leadership in precision oncology and advanced diagnostics.

  • Lead new product launches and strengthen the current portfolio across pathology, tissue diagnostics, Foundation Medicine and NGS.
