Full description

Future-oriented, professionally competent and professional – that is our non-profit organization Spitex Region Birs GmbH. Take the next step in your career in an exceptional and diverse working environment. With flat hierarchies and respectful interaction on an equal footing, we place great value on professional expertise and strong social skills. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you as Deputy Team Leader / Registered Nurse HF (70-100%) This versatile role offers you the ideal entry into a management position. You will continue to work actively in nursing while simultaneously being introduced to the role of deputy team leader of a somatic nursing team. Both areas of responsibility account for approx. 50% of your work.

What you can expect as Deputy Team Leader

  • You support and advise employees in complex care situations
  • You lead team meetings and client discussions
  • You assist with preparing assignment and duty rosters
  • You take on administrative tasks delegated by the team leader
  • You contribute your ideas to improving quality, create guidelines, and optimize processes and workflows
  • You plan and organize internal and external training sessions on nursing-related topics

What you can expect as a Registered Nurse HF

  • You provide independent, competent and resource-oriented basic and treatment care to our clients of all age groups
  • You are responsible for a defined number of clients
  • You are responsible for planning, managing and monitoring the nursing process
  • You conduct assessments and progress reviews using RAI-HC
  • You support our clients in maintaining a self-determined daily routine at home
  • You coordinate collaboration with family members, doctors and other services

What you bring

  • A completed qualification as a registered nurse HF
  • 3-5 years of professional experience and broad specialist knowledge
  • Experience in home care is an advantage
  • An independent, responsible approach to work
  • A team-oriented, open and empathetic personality with a positive attitude
  • Enjoyment in taking on responsibility, sharing knowledge and developing solutions together

Our offer for you

  • No night shifts
  • When arranging working hours, we take your personal needs into account
  • Room for initiative and professional development
  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working environment
  • A modern, rapidly growing and innovative organization

With us, you will find not only a job, but a meaningful role with prospects! Become part of our dynamic team and help shape the future of healthcare with us. Profile

Due to developments in healthcare, we have become an indispensable part of healthcare provision. With our team of over 140 employees, we care for and support people of all ages with a wide variety of medical conditions. One of our most important tasks is to enable the residents of the municipalities we serve to live a dignified life in their own four walls, whether this is in the case of a temporary acute need for support or in stable care situations. A meaningful task that offers variety and brings joy!
