SPITEX MUTTENZ AG – committed, well-founded and targeted We offer high-quality hospital care for the approximately 18,000 inhabitants of the municipality of Muttenz. We are looking for a Professional Health (FaGe) evening service 40-70 % You bring with you

  • A high sense of responsibility and reliability

  • A flair for digital media

Your tasks

  • Basic and treatment care for clients
  • Ensuring differentiated and specialized care

What we offer you
