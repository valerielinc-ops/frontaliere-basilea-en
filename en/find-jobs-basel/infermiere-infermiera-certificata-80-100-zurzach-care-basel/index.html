Full description

You want to make the difference? By expanding our modern rehabilitation clinic in Basel, we are looking for you as a committed nurse who wants to work in a varied and interdisciplinary environment. With us you combine the exciting world of internistic and oncological rehabilitation. Your expertise and ideas are invaluable.

Does that sound like your next professional challenge? Then we look forward to you!

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: You help our patients with internistic and oncological limitations to find their way back to an independent life
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: In close cooperation with the various disciplines, you promote holistic rehabilitation in order to achieve the best possible results
  • Various activities: In addition to the care and care of patients, dealing with modern medical technology is also part of your task, e.g. wound management, PEG probes and infusion management
  • Professional training: We promote your continuous development with regular internal and external training opportunities so that you always stay up-to-date

What you bring

  • Qualification: Completed vocational training as a qualified nurse or equivalent training with at least one positive SRC pre-check
  • Experience: You have experience in dealing with complex care situations
  • Personality: You show commitment in interdisciplinary cooperation and enjoy participation and bring the necessary flexibility

What we offer you

  • Co-creation: Work in a new modern working environment and actively participate
  • Working time model: You choose between the different working time models to create a balanced work-life model for yourself
  • Inspiring and collegial work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional shifts, which are also rewarded attractively.
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the Wellness - Therme FORTYSEVEN
