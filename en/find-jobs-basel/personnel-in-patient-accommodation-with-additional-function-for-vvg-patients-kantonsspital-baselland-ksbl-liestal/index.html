Full description

Entrance by 1. September 2026 or by agreement · Liestal, income management · Job reference 13175 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Recording and maintenance of the case data of inpatient and outpatient patients at the counter and on the telephone
  • Patient advice on administrative issues and class choice
  • Control and processing of various lists as well as responsibility for complete documents, as well as ensuring the cost recovery of the treatment
  • Management of administrative processes with internal departments and external bodies
  • Processing of mutations such as clinic change, class change and relocation
  • Care of the VVG patient in the administrative area as well as obtaining missing documents and signatures on the ward in the patient room.

Requirements

  • You like to work organized and accurately, enjoy direct contact with people and want to actively shape a new function.
  • You have completed a basic commercial education and bring experience in the medical or social security environment.
  • You have an education and/or experience in the nursing, care or medical field.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing and can also communicate orally in other foreign languages.
  • You are well versed in dealing with the common MS Office applications and work accurately and reliably.
  • You are capable of teamwork, flexible, independent, resilient and have a strong service mentality.
  • You enjoy personal contact with patients and convince through your safe and appreciative appearance.
  • You are an organizational talent, like to take on responsibility and enjoy shaping new processes.

The additional function for our VVG patients is newly created. You therefore have the opportunity to participate from the beginning and to develop together with us how we would like to shape the administrative care of our VVG patients in the future.

Benefits

All-round care: discounted day care on site, full parenting allowances, paid short absences in case of emergency care

Area: Revenue management
