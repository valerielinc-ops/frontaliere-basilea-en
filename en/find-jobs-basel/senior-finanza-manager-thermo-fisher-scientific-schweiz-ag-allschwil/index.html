Job Description

At Thermo Fisher Scientific, our Finance teams are important to our business and functional teams to make educated, sound decisions that drive our Mission and make us a stable and trusted industry leader. From Financial Planning & Analysis, Tax, Treasury, Financial Reporting, Audit, Investor Relations, Strategy & Corporate Development to Accounting, our Finance functions have a diverse and global presence, providing significant opportunities to develop outstanding career experiences and perspectives. Position Summary

Provide global commercial finance leadership and strategic business partnership to the General Manager (GM) of the Global Comparator Business. Shape and execute business strategy, including the Strategic Plan (STRAP), and drive profitable growth through commercial insight, disciplined resource allocation and cross-functional decision-making. Lead financial performance management across the global business, with accountability for driving EBITA delivery, cash generation and working capital performance in partnership with commercial, operational and regional leaders.

Key Responsibilities

Commercial Finance Leadership

Partner with the GM and global commercial leadership to shape growth priorities, pricing strategy, customer and portfolio profitability, commercial investments and margin expansion. Evaluate market and customer opportunities, challenge commercial assumptions and translate business decisions into measurable financial outcomes.

Global Financial Performance & Value Creation

Drive global business performance against EBITA, cash flow and working capital commitments. Identify performance gaps and lead cross-functional actions to improve revenue quality, gross margin, operating leverage, inventory and cash conversion. Partner with business owners to establish clear accountability and deliver sustainable improvements.

Business Partnership and Decision Support

Serve as a trusted strategic advisor to the GM and senior leadership, bringing forward-looking commercial and financial perspectives to key decisions. Lead business performance reviews, assess risks and opportunities, and recommend corrective actions across regions and functions.

Financial Planning, Forecasting and Insights

Own the financial planning and forecasting framework for the global comparator business, ensuring an integrated view of commercial drivers, EBITA and cash. Develop decision-oriented analyses, scenarios and models that support strategic choices and proactive performance management rather than reporting alone.

Global Cross-Functional Leadership and Transformation

Influence and align commercial, operations, supply chain and regional teams around shared business outcomes. Sponsor initiatives that improve pricing discipline, profitability, operational effectiveness and cash conversion; use data, automation and process improvements to strengthen decision speed and execution.

Financial Stewardship and Governance

Maintain robust financial controls, policy compliance and reliable management information. Partner with accounting, controllership and audit teams to address risks while keeping the finance organization focused on commercial value creation and business performance.

People Leadership

Lead, coach and develop the finance team, building strong commercial acumen, strategic business partnering capabilities, accountability and succession depth across the global organization.

Qualifications and Skills

  • Bachelor’s degree in finance or related field; MBA or advanced degree preferred.
  • 6+ years of progressive finance experience, including meaningful commercial finance and business partnering responsibility in a complex, international business environment.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence commercial strategy, pricing, profitability, resource allocation and senior leadership decisions through rigorous analysis and sound business judgment.
  • Experience leading strategic planning (STRAP or equivalent), translating long-term priorities into operating plans, performance actions and measurable financial outcomes.
  • Strong understanding of EBITA drivers, cash flow, working capital and the operational and commercial levers that improve business performance; proficiency with Excel, Power BI and presentation tools.
  • Proven track record of leading cross-functional performance improvement and navigating a global matrix organization through influence and collaboration.
  • Excellent executive communication, stakeholder management and people leadership skills; ability to challenge constructively and align diverse teams in a dynamic environment.
  • Experience with enterprise financial systems (e.g., Oracle or SAP) and data platforms is beneficial; demonstrated commitment to continuous learning and developing talent.
