Full description

As a leading real estate service provider, we offer our customers a comprehensive package of services for retail properties and areas in the area of center and mixed-use site management.

From commercial and technical management, through marketing and marketing, to temporary use and community management.

To support our team in Basel, we are looking for a dynamic and competent personality after agreement as

Personnel Center Management (m/f/d)

Basel (Stückipark), 80-100 %

This is what moves you

  • You support the commercial management and the Real Estate Site Manager in all administrative matters within the portfolio to be managed as well as in the rental of different types of use
  • Telephone and written contacts with tenants, housekeepers, craftsmen and offices are still part of your area of responsibility
  • You review applications, are responsible for obtaining references and creating rental contracts for secondary objects
  • You plan, carry out and take over the post-processing
  • You control, account and process various payments, you continue to charge creditors' bills and create debtors' bills
  • Reporting, such as sales reports and parking income, is created and monitored by you

This is why you succeed

  • A basic commercial education and at least. 3 years of commercial professional experience you bring (further education and experience in the real estate sector are an advantage)
  • You have stylish German and English skills in word and writing (French orally advantageous)
  • You have good MS-Office user knowledge
  • You are a team player and work independently and structured
  • Your flexible, resilient and customer-oriented personality with a lot of initiative distinguishes you

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us, you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. Accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.
