Full description

You want to not only keep existing systems running, but also help shape the production of tomorrow? 🔍 As an OT Engineer, you will become part of our 6-member OT team and work at the interface between production, automation, data and IT. Your sphere of activity extends significantly beyond a single production site: Together, we continue to develop our OT landscape throughout the Group and create the technological basis for more networked, automated and data-driven production processes. A special focus is on the question of how we can use data, automation and in the future also AI in our industrial environment. If you bring classic OT expertise and want to develop technologically, you will find a lot of room for it. 🚀 Tasks:

  • Together with the team, you ensure the reliable operation of our OT systems such as SPS, SCADA, HMI and MES/WMS at our production sites.
  • You will analyze technical disruptions, find sustainable solutions and continuously develop our OT infrastructure.
  • You will accompany automation and digitization projects from the idea through implementation to commissioning and optimization.
  • You will develop solutions with which we can automate, network and make production processes smarter.
  • You will work with data models and production data and help to lay the foundation for data-based applications and future AI connections in the industrial environment.
  • You will deal with new technologies around Industrial AI, Industry 4.0 and Smart Factory and help us to identify and implement meaningful use cases.
  • You work closely with production, technology, maintenance and IT – across locations and within our entire group.
  • You support the standardization of our OT solutions and help to develop common technical standards.
  • If necessary, you will coordinate external suppliers, integrators and plant manufacturers and bring together different technical perspectives.
  • You carry out error analyses and performance optimizations and derive concrete improvement measures from them. Requirements
  • Technical training or Studies at at least HF/FH level, for example in automation, electrical engineering, systems engineering, computer science or a comparable field of study
  • Experience with industrial OT and automation systems, such as PLC, SCADA, HMI or MES
  • Understanding how automation solutions are designed and implemented in a production environment
  • Good knowledge of data models and structured data
  • Enjoy trying out new technologies and developing yourself especially in the field of Industrial AI
  • Ability to analyze technical problems in a structured manner and make decisions independently
  • Very good German and English skills
