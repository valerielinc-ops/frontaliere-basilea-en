Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified specialists. To complement our controlling team in Bubendorf/BL, we are looking for a S enior CAPEX Controller (a), 100% with immediate effect.

Your tasks

  • Monitoring and reporting of project progress in terms of schedule, resources and costs as well as preparation of regular cash forecasts
  • Ensuring financial compliance and consistent compliance with Bachem standards and processes
  • Proactively display deviations while adhering to escalation levels
  • Identification of cost potentials and active support of the departments in the implementation
  • Monitoring and coordination of the approval process of project budgets and purchase requisitions, preparation of project activations
  • Contact person for all financial topics, coordination and responsibility of the change management process, coordination and support of payments to suppliers
  • Active participation in the optimization of project management processes and tools

Your profile

  • Completed studies in the field of industrial engineering, business administration or comparable training, further training in the field of finance / controlling
  • At least three years of experience in project controlling, preferably in large-scale projects
  • Ability to work in a team, as well as a confident appearance towards stakeholders
  • Strong communication and assertiveness, hands-on mentality and an independent, structured way of working
  • Strong motivation and perseverance in complex tasks and challenges
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, both written and spoken
  • Microsoft Office, especially very good Excel skills
  • Experience in dealing with SAP and BI systems

Our offer

  • A dynamic and fast-growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee development through numerous internal and external training opportunities
  • 60% of pension fund contributions are covered by Bachem AG as well as the possibility of extra-mandatory pension provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and varied food in our staff restaurant
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
