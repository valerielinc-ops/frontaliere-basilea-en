Credit Officer (w/m/d) — Raiffeisen

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are Raiffeisenbank Basel – successful, regionally anchored and close to our customers and employees. This closeness shapes our actions and our shared success.

We are looking for you to join our Credit Office, an important part of our credit administration. In a well-established environment in which the credit office and processing work together under one roof, you will take on responsibility and contribute your strengths in a targeted manner. We work in a structured, efficient manner and value partnership-based, uncomplicated cooperation at eye level.

Do you feel addressed? Then we look forward to getting to know you. Become part of our team and actively help shape it.

Tasks

  • You take on a central role in the lending business and use your specialist knowledge and structured way of working to ensure efficient and high-quality processing for private and corporate customers.
  • You are responsible for the creation, review and approval of loan applications as well as periodic new approvals within the framework of competence in compliance with all regulatory requirements
  • You create property valuations using the bank's own valuation tools (including Wüest Dimensions)
  • You manage the loan positions actively and sustainably: balance sheet analyses, processing construction loans, managing mortgage master data, taking on control tasks
  • You identify, assess and address credit risks at an early stage and identify viable alternative solutions
  • You act as a central specialist interface: You support the customer advisors, especially in complex cases, work closely with internal and external partners and ensure smooth processing with credit administration

Requirements

  • You have many years of experience in the lending area of a Swiss bank and know both corporate and private customer business from your daily practice
  • You have completed a banking apprenticeship or comparable training and have ideally been able to develop your professional skills through further training
  • You must have experience in appraisals, especially in the valuation of investment properties
  • You think entrepreneurially, contribute ideas and take responsibility, while actively looking for sustainable solutions and representing them convincingly
  • You are a team player and lend a hand where support is needed, get involved and thus contribute to mutual success

We offer

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and provide our employees with targeted support – with a wide range of internal educational offerings as well as cost sharing for external further training.
