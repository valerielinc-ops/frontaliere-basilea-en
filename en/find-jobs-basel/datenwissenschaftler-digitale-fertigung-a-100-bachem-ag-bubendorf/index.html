Full description

As Data Scientist Digital Manufacturing (a) 100% you will strengthen Bachem’s Digital Manufacturing and Data Analytics capabilities within Group TechOps. Using PI Historian, manufacturing data from diverse sources, and advanced data science methods, you will develop data-driven solutions, enhance reporting, and deepen process understanding across the production lifecycle. You will collaborate closely with Production, TechOps, Quality, Automation, IT, and external partners.

Your tasks

  • Develop advanced extraction capabilities and analytics to support CPV, root ‑ cause investigations, and process improvements. Support batch reporting and data extraction
  • Contribute to end-to-end concepts for data pipelines connecting PI Historian, MES (PAS-X), PCS, and other OT/IT systems to analytical or reporting platforms
  • Manage and expand Bachem’s data analytics capabilities, including dashboards, reports and ad-hoc analyses
  • Serve as a functional expert for the PI System (PI Data Archive, PI AF, PI Vision) and support end users in retrieving, structuring, and interpreting time ‑ series data
  • Support investigations, risk assessments, and continued process verification with data ‑ driven evidence and analysis
  • Analyze manufacturing and process data across the full production lifecycle and deliver meaningful insights for operations, engineering, and quality

Your profile

  • University degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Chemistry, Engineering, or a related technical field
  • Hands-on experience working with manufacturing or process data, ideally in a GMP or regulated environment
  • Solid programming skills (e.g., Python, SQL, R) and experience with analytical or visualization tools (PI Vision, Power BI, Spotfire, Tableau)
  • Ability to understand manufacturing processes and translate operational needs into data visualization, algorithms, and analytical solutions
  • Familiarity with GMP, data integrity (ALCOA+), and validation activities related to analytics and data systems
  • Dedicated, self ‑ driven personality who enjoys taking initiative and collaborating across a fast ‑ paced operational environment
