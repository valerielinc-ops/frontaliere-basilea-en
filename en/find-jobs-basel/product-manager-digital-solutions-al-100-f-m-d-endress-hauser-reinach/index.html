At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. What is at stake here? Are you enthusiastic about technical connections, keep an eye on market trends and develop innovative marketing approaches from them? As Product Manager Digital Solutions 100% (f/m/d) at Endress+Hauser (Switzerland) AG They take responsibility for our digital solutions and services and actively shape their positioning and development across different industries.   • Market observation and market research to identify future requirements
  • Product support throughout the entire life cycle (from market launch to phase-out) What do we expect from you?
  • University of applied sciences or studies in the technical field (Electrical Engineering / Information Technology)

  • Open-minded and team-capable personality with a communication-strong and secure appearance
