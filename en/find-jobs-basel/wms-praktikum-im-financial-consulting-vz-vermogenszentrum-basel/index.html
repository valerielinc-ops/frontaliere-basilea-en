Full description

Our customers retire well prepared, invest their money successfully, are optimally secured and save taxes, premiums and fees. This makes us the number 1 independent financial consultancy. For young and committed personalities, we offer an insight into customer advice as part of the WMS internship.

  • You support our team at the Basel office and get to know the everyday life of our consultants.
  • You will gain insight into various topics such as asset management, investment advice, tax planning, estate regulation and real estate financing.
  • You work with an experienced consultant, whom you accompany during customer projects and consultations.
  • You are attending the Wirtschaftsmittelschule and would like to obtain your vocational degree with this internship.
  • Financial topics such as shares and bonds as well as customer contact are fun for you.
  • You have a quick apprehension, a flair for numbers and are a motivated and established personality.
  • You learn every day from our experienced consultants.
  • After completion of the internship, there is the possibility of permanent employment (also part-time).
  • You are also right with us for your further education: The VZ supports you individually and supports you in external training within the framework of your job profile.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
