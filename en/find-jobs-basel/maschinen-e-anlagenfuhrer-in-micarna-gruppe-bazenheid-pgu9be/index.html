Full description

You ensure that our machines and plants run reliably. With your technical understanding, you monitor production, repair disruptions and ensure a smooth process. Do you like to take on responsibility? Then we look forward to you!

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Bazenheid, Neue Industriestrasse 10, 9602 Bazenheid

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You operate and monitor our plant(s), repair faults and carry out maintenance work so that everything runs smoothly
  • You make sure that our plant(s) produces in the highest quality
  • You bring fresh ideas for process optimization and help to constantly improve our processes
  • Safety, quality standards and productivity are a matter of course for you and you adhere to the requirements
  • You are flexible and also help in other areas when there is a need, always with a view to the big picture

Requirements

  • several years of professional experience: preferably in the food industry
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as a machine and plant operator or comparable
  • You enjoy working accurately and work independently and disciplined
  • You convince with team spirit, physical fitness and resilience
  • You are reliable, strong in communication and do not shy away from shift work
  • Whether operating systems or office applications, you are safe with it or ready to acquire these skills
  • German (very good knowledge)

  • Occupational pension: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages
  • Meals: Balanced lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for max. CHF 7.-
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Holidays: 5 to 7 weeks holidays per year depending on age and service
  • Club School Migros: refund of 70% of the course costs up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Sports and fitness offers: organisation of various sporting events with discounts
  • Du-culture: At Migros you smell – because it’s simply more relaxed
