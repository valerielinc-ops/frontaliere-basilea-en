Full description

Shaping the future together – your teaching at Endress+Hauser At Endress+Hauser, you don’t just work anywhere – you are part of a team working on exciting technologies for industry worldwide. Our approximately 18,000 employees develop, build and improve measuring instruments that are used all over the world. Collaboration, trust and team spirit. Because together we achieve more – and every idea counts. As a family-run company, we think long-term and invest in your future. With us you not only get a top education, but also many opportunities to develop and shape yourself.

What is the point about? Do you have a good imagination? Are you interested in technology, machines and how things are constructed? And do you like working on the computer with modern 3D programs? Then training as a constructor*in EFZ is just right for you!

In this training, you develop and construct mechanical, electrical and electronic devices, machines and plants. You work with a modern CAD system, create technical drawings and translate ideas into precise plans that are used for production. You work closely with development and production departments.

What tasks will you take on?

You will

  • Modelling and designing mechanical, electrical and electronic equipment, machinery and equipment
  • Drawings of components and sub-assemblies by design, manufacture or assembly drawings
  • Adapt and optimize existing technical drawings
  • Working out detailed constructions using CAD system

What do we expect from you?

You

  • are in middle or upper school
  • Shows good performance in mathematics, technical design and physics
  • brings understanding of abstract and technical connections
  • have good logical and analytical thinking skills
  • Work carefully, focused and are team capable

What can you expect from us?

  • Security and stability of an internationally operating family business
  • Very good takeover opportunities after training
  • A motivated and dedicated training team that will accompany you
  • Costs of the U-Subscription (Public)
  • Delicious and cheap lunch menus from 5.50 CHF in the company's restaurant

For your application we need

  • Letter of motivation
  • CVC
  • All upper secondary education certificates
  • S2-Check with evaluation in the professional field and/or multicheck
