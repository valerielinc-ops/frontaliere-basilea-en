Full description

Tasks

  • In your role, you support the Public Prosecutor’s Office Accounting Director in her daily management work and represent her in her absence.
  • At the same time, you yourself are part of the small team within the Central Services of the Public Prosecutor's Office, which is responsible for the entire debt collection system. This includes, in particular, the booking of incoming payments and the payment order and recovery procedure. In addition, you support the accounting team in all administrative work and have daily (telephone) contact with people who have to pay.

Requirements

  • Thanks to a commercial or equivalent education, practical knowledge in accounting, management experience, IT application knowledge and a very good written and oral expression in German, you will quickly find your way around your new job.
  • Even if the phone rings a few times in a row, you keep your nerves and with the help of your oral foreign language skills (French and / or Italian and / or English) you can communicate with the majority of the people calling.
  • You contribute an important part to the work of a small department by tackling it independently and doing your work quickly and accurately so that you can take responsibility for it at any time.
  • Integrity and secrecy are absolutely essential for this position.
  • With your personality you enrich a harmonious and helpful team and with your work attitude you realize the basic idea of Central Services to support the entire authority and its employees.

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and specialist areas, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
