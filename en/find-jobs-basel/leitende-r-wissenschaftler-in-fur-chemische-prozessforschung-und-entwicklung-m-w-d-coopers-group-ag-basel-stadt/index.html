Lead Chemical Process R&D Scientist (m/f/d) — Coopers Group AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In this function you will lead chemical process R&D activities to deliver safe, scalable, efficient and sustainable processes as well as drug substance material for projects in clinical development, with a focus on late stage development, leveraging internal and external CDMO capabilities.

Location: Basel

Home office: Yes, 2 days a week

Duration: 1 year/possible extension

Travel: Yes. 4 times a year - Europe or Asia (China, India)

Responsibilities

  • Lead Internal & External Chemical Process R&D activities: Direct chemical process research and development work, managing an internal team of 1–5 process chemists and/or leading external activities at contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs).
  • Oversee end-to-end process chemistry workflows, including synthetic route scouting, process optimization, scale-up, and support for supply activities.
  • Coordinate Cross-Functional Delivery: Synchronize internal laboratory efforts with CDMO timelines to guarantee the timely, safe, and cost-effective production of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and advanced intermediates
  • Ensure Quality & Compliance: Deliver the target compounds following internal quality processes and according to specifications, safety standards and sustainability objectives.

Must Haves

  • PhD or Postdoctoral background in Synthetic Organic Chemistry.
  • 5+ years of hands-on experience in pharmaceutical chemical process R&D, with demonstrated expertise in late-stage process development and scale-up.
  • Leadership & Project Management: Proven track record of leading, mentoring, and driving project deliverables within internal process chemistry teams.
  • CDMO Management: Direct experience managing external chemical development, technology transfers, and manufacturing activities at CDMOs.
  • Technical & Software Proficiency: High proficiency with standard office applications alongside core chemistry tools, including chemical drawing tools, literature/patent search engines, Design of Experiments (DoE) applications, analytical software, and project planning platforms. Proficiency in the use of kinetic modeling or scale-up simulation software is a plus.
