Full description

Tasks

  • Implementation of tort-preventive, psychotherapeutic treatments in adolescents and young adults both inpatient and outpatient (including diagnosis, prognostics, reporting, documentation and, if necessary, crisis intervention) in close support of the psychological cadre
  • Active cooperation and interdisciplinary networking, especially in the interdisciplinary core treatment team (social education and vocational training)
  • Advice and support of the socio-pedagogical and occupational field in risk-related and diagnostic issues and in the case of existing mental disorders
  • Organize, lead (with the division management) and document the initial interviews for new entries, including preparation and evaluation of the entry questionnaires
  • Contribute to group therapies
  • Cooperation in placement investigations

Requirements

  • Completed psychology degree
  • Started additional psychotherapeutic training
  • Interest in forensic issues and modern forensic treatment principles as well as in long-term and complex treatment processes
  • Risk-oriented working methods while at the same time encouraging and appreciative attitude towards the enrollees
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of interdisciplinary working methods
  • Possession of driving licence B and good repute
