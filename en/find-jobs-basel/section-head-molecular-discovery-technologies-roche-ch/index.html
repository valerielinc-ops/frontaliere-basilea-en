The Position The Small Molecule Discovery Team in Pharma Research and Early Development (pRED), located in Basel, is a center of excellence within Small Molecule Research. We are a community of drug-hunters dedicated to converting scientific hypotheses into innovative small molecules. Roche is committed to maintaining industry-leading, in-house drug discovery capabilities. We are seeking a highly experienced scientific leader to head our Biochemistry & Biophysics, DNA-Encoded Libraries (DELT), Mass Spectrometry, and Protein Science teams.

This group is responsible for the design, production, and biophysical characterization of high-quality protein reagents and the execution of advanced hit-finding strategies to support pRED’s small molecule portfolio. The ideal candidate will be an accomplished people leader and scientist who thrives in a matrixed environment and enjoys engaging with a diverse stakeholder community to transform science into medicines.

The Opportunity

  • Strategic Leadership: As a member of the Strategy and Enabling Circle, you help shape and drive the strategic development of the overall department strategy and ensure its effective implementation.
  • Scientific Oversight : Direct the activities of a multi-disciplinary group—including Biochemistry & Biophysics, DELT, Mass Spectrometry, and Protein Science—in the research and development of the organization's Small Molecule programs.
  • Portfolio Advancement: Align strategy with experts and senior leaders in Therapeutic Modalities (TMo) and Discovery Therapeutic Areas (DTA) to prioritize and advance small molecule projects.
  • Innovation & Automation : Oversee the miniaturization and automation of biochemical assays and the implementation of cutting-edge DELT and Mass Spec platforms to enable data-driven decision-making.
  • External Engagement: Build and maintain relationships with external partners to leverage emerging technologies and stay at the forefront of protein science and lead identification.
  • Operational Excellence: Make final decisions on administrative or operational matters, including budget development for capital expenditures.
  • People Development: Provide direct line management and mentorship to a team of scientists and associates, fostering a culture defined by curiosity, responsibility, and humility.

Who You Are

  • Education & Experience: You hold a PhD in Biochemistry, Mass Spec or Protein Science or a related discipline with extensive drug discovery experience (10 y+) in an industry setting with a focus on small molecule lead finding.
  • Technical Expertise: You are an expert in defining lead identification strategies (e.g., DELT, ASMS, biochemical screening) and possess deep knowledge of sequence to protein workflows and multi-dimensional data analysis.
  • Automation Mindset: You have a proven record of implementing end-to-end automation of laboratory processes and connecting informatics with biological experimentation.
  • Technology Innovation: You have demonstrated implementation of technology innovation to support next-generation targets and small molecule sub modality approaches
  • Leadership Style: You inspire, mentor, and coach diverse teams with curiosity, responsibility, and humility, equating personal achievement with collective success. You bring proven leadership in complex matrix environments, empowering others to collaborate across boundaries and solve challenging scientific problems.
  • Communication Skills: You have highly developed negotiation and influencing skills, with the capacity to clearly communicate complex ideas across organizational levels. You have the ability to adapt your communication to suit different audiences and situations.
  • Disease Therapeutic Area knowledge: A broad understanding of contemporary small molecule Oncology targets and subsequent biology is advantageous
  • Dynamic Mindset: You bring the flexibility and resilience needed to operate in a rapidly evolving research landscape, proactively flexing your leadership approach and shaping the scope of your team as new modalities, platforms, and organizational needs emerge.
