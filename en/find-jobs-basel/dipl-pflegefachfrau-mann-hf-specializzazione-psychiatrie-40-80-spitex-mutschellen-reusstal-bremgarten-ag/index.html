Dipl. Nursing professional HF psychiatry, 70% - 100% Your tasks

  • Trusted contact person for our clients in everyday life
  • Ensure implementation of the entire care process
  • Coordinates and plans needs-based care with the involvement of all stakeholders and supports them in their decision-making
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation and coordination with external persons and bodies
  • Ensure quality of care

Your profile

  • Very good PC knowledge

  • Independent, resilient and capable of teamwork
