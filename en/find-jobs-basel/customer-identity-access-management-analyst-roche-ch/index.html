The Position Customer Identity & Access Management Analyst

We focus on the long - term, leading investment in research and development, striving to improve lives today and for generations to come. In Roche Informatics we are a diverse team of open and friendly people, enthusiastic about technological novelties and efficient IT solutions.

The opportunity

  • Participate in all aspect of the implementation of Enterprise CIAM strategies, policies, and procedures, ensuring they align with overall security and risk management objectives
  • Assist with the delivery of CIAM projects and programs, ensuring they are completed on time, within budget, and to a high standard of quality
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure CIAM solutions are integrated with existing systems and processes.
  • Perform security assessments and identify potential improvements.
  • Participate in incident response and troubleshooting efforts
  • To stay aware of operational challenges and opportunities; works with support teams to provide internal escalation support as needed
  • Actively focus on self-development in creating actionable plans to improve.
  • Assist in the evaluation of solutions and their feasibility to Customer-Facing Application owners in a variety of environments including backend (PHP, Java, .NET), and client-side (REST, JavaScript) while ensuring procedures, processes and documentation are in compliance with regulatory guidelines and organizational standards,
  • Demonstrate business awareness and understand the needs of the Application owners, and any other supporting IT Partner organization.

Who you are

  • 2 years of experience in administering and/or implementing CIAM or IAM solutions
  • Experience in integrating with other solutions including APIs
  • Someone who wants to influence their own development, demonstrate intellectual curiosity, a self-starter and embrace continuous improvement.
  • Are execution-oriented with an agile mindset and looking for a company where you have the opportunity to pursue your interests across functions and geographies.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related field
  • Knowledge of cybersecurity principles and best practices
  • Ability to work independently and manage time effectively
  • Excited to help our customers to improve their consumer journeys

What you bring

  • Backend Development skills with at least one server platform such as .NET, Java, PHP.
  • Front-end Development skills with at least one server platform such as HTML, CSS, REST, JavaScript and JS Libraries.
  • SAP CDC (Gigya) Technical expertise.
  • Experience in integration projects involving identity management aspects and web development and customer facing experience.
  • Experience with SAML and OIDC
  • Understanding of Identity and Access Management, and Customer Identity Management,
  • Strong verbal and written English with excellent communication, negotiation and documentation skills.
  • Awareness of Agile techniques like Scrum, SAFe, etc are a plus for this position.
