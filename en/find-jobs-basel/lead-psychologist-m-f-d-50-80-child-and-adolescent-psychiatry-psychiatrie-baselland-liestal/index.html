About us

The Psychiatry Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, daily clinical, home treatment and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. She supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of living, work and day-to-day planning (www.inclusioplus.ch). She also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Center for Work and Mental Health. At several locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, PBL employs around 1,300 people and treats, supports and advises more than 15,000 patients per year (www.pbl.ch).
