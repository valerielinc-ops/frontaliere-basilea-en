Full description

To strengthen our team in the production of active ingredients, we are looking for a process specialist (a), 100% in the field of chromatography.

In our new building, we offer you a varied range of tasks in the production of pharmaceutical active ingredients at the latest plants. Already accompany the commissioning and the first productions.

Tasks

  • Ensuring a uniformly high level of training of GMP production staff and practical process implementation
  • Technical support for shift managers and masters in operational operations
  • Support for the commissioning of new plants in the HPLC area including recipe tests, engineering batches and PQ runs
  • Support for the introduction and application of the process control system PCS7 as well as paperless documentation using MES
  • Transfer of technical know-how and training of production employees in the new plant area
  • Development and implementation of technical responsibility training programmes
  • Implementation of onboarding programs for new employees and learners
  • Preparation and maintenance of training documents as well as process and plant-specific documentation
  • Training of HSE and GMP relevant topics in the production environment
  • Function as technical expert for the responsible plant area
  • Support in planning and implementing technical improvements in cooperation with QA, operational engineering and external suppliers
  • Support for repairs including the creation of LOTO plans, work permits and on-site support

Your profile

  • Chemical training and/or chemistry master training or other equivalent technical training, at least 5 years of professional experience in the chemical industry
  • Experience in chromatography, ultrafiltration or similar
  • Comprehensive knowledge of chemical handling, PPE, zone concepts and HSE regulations
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Secure GMP understanding and ability to convey compliance
  • Strong organisational talent and ability for independent work planning
  • Experience with paperless documentation
  • Experience with process control systems (e.g. PCS7)
  • Experience in commissioning new plants and processes

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
