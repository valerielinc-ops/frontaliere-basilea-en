Full description

Location Basel Tower, Switzerland. The place of work of this office is in Basel, Switzerland. Eligible applicants and their families will be assisted in moving if needed.

It includes ensuring the operational operation of the technical administration and assuming professional responsibility for defined special areas (WEF) within the pension fund. This position is mainly designed as an office activity. The cooperation on site enables close, timely collaboration and coordination in the team, so that we can achieve optimal results. You work primarily in the office, but you have the opportunity to work up to 20% of your working time (one day per week) in the home office.

What awaits you

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • A variety of advantages, depending on function and location.

The full list of our global benefits can be found here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits.

Their tasks

  • Independent handling of business cases & ensuring timely processing.
  • Support in day-to-day business at Peaks.
  • Professional responsibility for specialty WEF (checking of advance payments and pledges, ensuring correct calculations, compliance with legal requirements (e.g. blocking periods, repayments).
  • Contact person internal & external (insured, banks).
  • Implementation of ICS controls.
  • Documentation of processes, building know-how in a team
  • Testing at system introduction / releases.

What we are looking for

  • Several years of experience in occupational provision (especially technical administration).
  • training in the field of occupational protection is beneficial.
  • In-depth knowledge in at least one specialty area (WEF).
  • Experience with management systems (preferably Xplan).
  • Foreign languages English (mandatory), French, Italian (advantageous).
  • High accuracy and sense of responsibility, team player.
