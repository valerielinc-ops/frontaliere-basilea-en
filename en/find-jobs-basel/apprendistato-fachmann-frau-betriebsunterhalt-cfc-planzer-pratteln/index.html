Apprenticeship as a facility maintenance specialist EFZ — Planzer

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who think ahead, pitch in and want to grow with us. Become part of our family and help shape the future of transport and warehousing logistics with us.

What you move

  • You keep outdoor facilities and buildings in good condition – you clean, repair and remedy structural damage
  • You monitor machines and building services systems, service them regularly and carry out minor repairs
  • Replacing seals, lamps or door locks is also part of your everyday work
  • You carefully inspect heating systems, electrical installations and sanitary facilities
  • With tasks such as waste management, grounds maintenance, snow removal and surface repairs, you ensure order and safety on the premises
  • In this way, you ensure smooth operation and contribute to preserving the value of our buildings

Why you succeed

  • You have technical understanding and manual skills, and you always work conscientiously
  • You are physically resilient and are at the forefront when it comes to getting things done
  • You stand out through your ability to work in a team and your interest in cleaning work
  • You stand out through your independent and responsible approach to work

Your benefits

  • Family-oriented company culture: Respect, trust and fair treatment matter to us – we are on a first-name basis and personally support you on your journey.
  • Holidays: In the 1. Apprenticeship year, you have 8, in the 2. 7 and 6 weeks of vacation in the following years.
  • Events: During your training with us, you will take part in various events such as WelcomeDay, an in-depth seminar, an apprentice camp, an apprenticeship graduation ceremony, etc.
  • Financial support: With us, it is not just the pay that is good: You receive a fair apprentice wage plus a 13th-month salary. If you give your all at school, in intercompany courses and at the company, you can even earn a performance bonus. We cover the cost of the school materials and give you a financial contribution towards a laptop. On top of that, you receive a Half-Fare Travelcard for your entire apprenticeship.
  • Generous expense policy: With our expense policy, you are well provided for.
  • Continued employment: After completing your training, you have a very good chance of being offered a permanent position.

Who we are For us as a company, supportive collaboration and a positive working atmosphere are central. That is why we particularly value qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
