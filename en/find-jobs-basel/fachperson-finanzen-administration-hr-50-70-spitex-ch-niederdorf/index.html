Full description

As a non-profit organization with around 50 employees, we provide comprehensive outpatient help and care in the Waldenburgertal. We are looking for 1 December 2026 or by agreement Specialist Finance, Administration & HR 50–70% You want to take on responsibility in a versatile function and like working at the interface between finance, personnel administration and administration? Then you are exactly right at Spitex Waldenburgertal. In this varied function, you are an important hub for our employees, the management and the various stakeholders. You ensure that financial and administrative processes work reliably and that our employees are also competently looked after in personnel administration issues.

Your main tasks

Finance & Accounting

  • Self-employed management of finance and accounting
  • Accounting and control of ongoing transactions
  • Accounting and invoicing services
  • Preparation and preparation of half-yearly and annual accounts
  • Support for management in financial and administrative matters

Personnel administration and HR

  • Independent implementation of monthly wage processing incl. Year-end tasks
  • Supervision of the personnel administration from entry to exit
  • Further development of administrative processes
  • Contact person for employees and supervisors in administrative HR and wage issues

Administration and Organization

  • General administrative and organisational tasks
  • Participation in the optimization and digitalization of processes
  • Support of the management and team in various projects

Your profile

  • Commercial training EFZ with further education and professional experience in finance and accounting
  • Good knowledge of Swiss wage and social security
  • Independent, structured and exact working methods
  • Number flair, sense of responsibility and discretion
  • good IT user knowledge
  • Abacus, Perigon and M365 are beneficial
  • High degree of independence and pleasure in taking on responsibility within a team
  • Flexible working hours
  • Contemporary employment conditions and good social benefits
  • Opportunities for further education and personal development

We offer you

  • Meaningful activity in a regionally anchored nonprofit organization
  • Short decision-making processes and direct cooperation with the management
  • Possibility to actively shape and further develop processes

We look forward to your application!
