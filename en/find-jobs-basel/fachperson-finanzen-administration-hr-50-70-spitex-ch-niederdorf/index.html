Role overview

As a non-profit organization with around 50 employees, we provide comprehensive outpatient help and care in the Waldenburgertal. We are looking for 1 December 2026 or by agreement Specialist Finance, Administration & HR 50–70% You want to take on responsibility in a versatile function and like working at the interface between finance, personnel administration and administration?
