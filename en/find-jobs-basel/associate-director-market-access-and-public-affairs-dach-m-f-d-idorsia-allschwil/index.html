Main responsibilities

  • of the Associate Director Market Access and Public Affairs DACH (m/f/d) include:
  • Focuses on coordination and execution of Idorsia’s access strategy and its implementation (project management, oversees dossier writing, timelines, risks etc.) for defined therapeutic area.
  • Ensures local fit of product value proposition (incl. evidence) to obtain ideal pricing and reimbursement scenarios and outcome.
  • Represents Idorsia vis-à-vis local external agencies and other stakeholders , e.g. pharma association working groups and patient advocacy groups.
  • Actively contributes to the internal Idorsia processes and contributes significantly to the conceptual development and implementation of country Market Access and Public Affairs strategies.
  • Regularly exchanges information with the Senior Market Access and Public Affairs Director DACH and cross-functional project teams.
  • Drives development and implementation of country strategies to support DACH Market Access and Public Affairs.
  • Monitors the relevant regional policy environment and understands key healthcare trends and their impact.

  • Successfully completed a degree ideally in health economics, pharmacy, or science, or equivalent qualification.
  • Minimum of 7 years of successful professional experience in DACH Market Access, and 10 years in the pharmaceutical industry.
  • Profound knowledge and broad experience of Market Access (German AMNOG is a must, Switzerland an advantage) ideally in an innovative specialty pharma environment.
