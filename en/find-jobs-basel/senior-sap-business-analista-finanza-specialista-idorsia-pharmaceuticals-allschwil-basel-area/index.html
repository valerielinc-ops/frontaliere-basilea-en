Full description

The purpose of Idorsia is to discover, develop and commercialize innovative medicines to help more patients. To achieve this, we will develop Idorsia into a leading biopharmaceutical company, with a strong scientific core.

The Role

We're looking for a Senior SAP Business Analyst with a Finance (FI/CO) specialization to become the system and process owner for our SAP Finance modules.

If you love translating complex financial business needs into robust, compliant SAP solutions — and enjoy being the trusted bridge between IT, Finance and Controlling — this is your role.

You'll own end-to-end SAP Finance processes, drive data governance, keep us audit-ready (SOX, GDPR, GxP), and mentor junior analysts along the way.

What You'll Do

  • Lead requirements workshops and process mapping across SAP Finance (FI: GL, AP, AR, Asset Accounting

CO: cost/profit centers, product costing, profitability analysis)

  • Configure and validate SAP Finance solutions, supporting period-end close and financial reporting
  • Define and enforce data governance and master-data harmonization for Finance data
  • Own SOX / GDPR / GxP compliance and CSV documentation within your solutions; support internal & external audits
  • Manage Finance projects and cross-module integrations (procure-to-pay, order-to-cash, record-to-report, reporting)
  • Run user access reviews upholding segregation of duties (SoD)
  • Mentor junior analysts and act as trusted advisor to Finance & Controlling

What You Bring

Must-haves

  • Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business, or a related discipline
  • 5–8 years of SAP business analysis experience, with proven ownership of one or more SAP modules — ideally in Finance (FI/CO)
  • Solid experience with data governance and regulatory compliance (SOX, GDPR, GxP)
  • Working knowledge of SAP role design and segregation-of-duties principles
  • Strong stakeholder management — able to explain technical matters to technical and non-technical audiences
  • Very good written and spoken English

A plus

  • SAP FI/CO certification
  • Experience in pharma, life sciences or another regulated industry
  • Exposure to S/4HANA and adjacent modules (MM, SD, Coupa touchpoints)
  • German language skills

What We Offer

  • The chance to shape the SAP Finance backbone of an innovative biopharma company
  • A collaborative, high-trust culture where your ideas are heard and your expertise valued
  • Real ownership and room to grow — technically and as a mentor/leader
  • A modern campus in the Basel area, one of Europe's leading life-sciences hubs
  • Competitive salary with performance bonus and attractive benefits
