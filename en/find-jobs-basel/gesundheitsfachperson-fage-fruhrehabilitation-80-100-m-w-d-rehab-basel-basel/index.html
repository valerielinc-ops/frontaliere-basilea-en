Full description

About us Anything but everyday At Station 1, early rehabilitation and wakeful coma, we treat patients after accidental and/or disease-related serious brain injuries with an impact on a wide range of areas such as breathing, communication, mobility and cognition. The ward takes over patients from the Intermediate Care Unit (IMCU) or directly from the acute care hospital. In the first rehabilitation phase, vital signs are monitored, patients are treated with tracheal cannula including ventilation, and weaning is carried out. At the same time, bedside therapy and early mobilization are important approaches for activation of wakefulness and awareness. Are you open to an anything but everyday challenge? Then you are right with us and we look forward to your application for Station 1, early rehabilitation and waking coma.

For this challenge, we are looking for a professionally experienced, committed and initiative health professional (FAGE) 80% - 100% m/f/d
