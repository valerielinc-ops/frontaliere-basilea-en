Full description

Part of the Energiewende gfeller elektro is part of ETAVIS Bern‐Mittelland AG based in Bern and is one of the leading companies in the Swiss electrical installation and telecommunications market. We provide all services for consulting, project planning, execution, support and maintenance for electrical and building technology, telematics, ICT infrastructure and automation. For this position, however, we are looking for a personality based in the Basel area, who is on the road throughout Switzerland and competently accompanies our projects on site. That's what you do.

  • You can realize simple to complex TV system projects in the HF or IPTV area for our hotels, hospitals and residential cooperatives.
  • You are a competent partner for new or existing hospitality TV solutions.
  • You install TV satellite terminals in the broadcast or IPTV process.
  • You install and program interactive hotel TV systems.
  • You repair disturbances on site or by remote maintenance.
  • You mainly move in hotels of different categories in German and French Switzerland.
  • You are ready to provide periodic pikettdienst on weekends. This will make it easier for you to get started.
  • Successfully completed training as a multimedia electronics technician or electrical installer with further training in the field of telecommunications or equivalent ICT training.
  • Knowledge of the following technologies: Hotel TV.Systems, Network (LAN/WLAN), HF technology.
  • Independent and structured working method with great personal responsibility, as well as solution-oriented thinking and behavior.
  • Good German and French skills in word and writing as well as solid technical English skills are an advantage.
  • You have a ticket Cat. B. This is waiting for you
  • You are based in the Basel area and travel throughout Switzerland.
  • Long-term perspective, further education support and operational promotion of professional development.
  • Sustainable and trusting corporate culture.
  • Open, family and collegial working environment with established employees.
  • Competent managers ensure exciting projects and functioning workflows.
  • We will contribute to our success – with employee shares and bonuses. Still questions? Your manager, André Lüthi, has the answers (+41 79 948 91 57). Apply with us, we are in a spirit of optimism and looking for you! ETAVIS has the right expertise for all topics in the field of electrical engineering. Whether electrical installations, building automation, event service, renewable energies, etc. - we are the ideal partner for all these services in Switzerland. As part of the international VINCI Energies Group, we have power for innovation to remain the best solution provider in the future. We are strongly anchored locally and know the people, cultures and the market in all regions of Switzerland and Liechtenstein.
