Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. With us, you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. You will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our internal customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • Further development and operational operation of the actuarial database used within the framework of the IFRS 17 Financial Statement Process of the Helvetia Baloise Group
  • Coordination of the actuarial final process at group level incl. Coordination with other financial functions such as accounting and controlling and group companies
  • Support for actuarial system integrations within the framework of the Helvetia-Baloise integration project
  • Monitoring and quality assurance of the database functions as well as support for actuarial reporting from the database (e.g. via power BI dashboards)
  • Further development and optimization of actuarial life insurance monitoring at group level, including participation in reporting to Group management
  • Support and analysis of group companies regarding actuarial issues and KPIs incl. Actual monitoring

Requirements

  • Studies in mathematics, physics or other quantitative orientation
  • In-depth database knowledge and IT affinity
  • Experience/knowledge in IFRS 17, actuarial and life insurance products
  • Secure communication, independent working methods, team-oriented and ready for performance
  • Project management competences
  • Very good German and English skills

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
