The IT Service Continuity Manager (ITSCM) is responsible for planning, implementing, and continuously improving IT service continuity within the organization. The goal of the role is to ensure the availability of critical IT services even during severe disruptions, cyber attacks, or disasters, and to guarantee recovery times according to business requirements.

The role works closely with IT Operations, Information Security, BCM (Business Continuity Management), Risk Management, IT Architecture, Cloud/Infrastructure teams, and external providers, aligning with current best practices according to ITIL, ISO 22301, ISO 27001, and modern resilience and cyber-recovery approaches.

What you achieve

  • Establishment and further development of the IT Service Continuity Management (ITSCM) framework
  • Managing and ensuring IT service continuity management, promoting a resilience-oriented corporate culture
  • Development and maintenance of resilience-oriented IT service strategies for on-premises, cloud, and hybrid environments
  • Conducting consultations and training
  • Elaboration and maintenance of the ITSCM process, including interfaces
  • Ordering and supervising tests / preventive measures according to defined strategies and goals
  • Maintenance / ensuring current and complete recovery procedures and recovery plans to manage extraordinary situations
  • Ensuring compliance with regulatory, audit-relevant, and internal requirements

What you bring

  • Completed degree in computer science, information security, or a related field, or proven multi-year experience in corporate IT environments
  • Several years of experience in the IT operations environment
  • Very good understanding of IT processes, especially IT infrastructure processes
  • Great interest in deepening knowledge in ITSCM, experience in ITSCM is an advantage
  • Assessment and derivation of potential risk situations in reporting
  • Understanding of ITIL and other frameworks for IT service management
  • Persuasive communication, quick grasp, and analytical mindset
  • Good knowledge of German and English
