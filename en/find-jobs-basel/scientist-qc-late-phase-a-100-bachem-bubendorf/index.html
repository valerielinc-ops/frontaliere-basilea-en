Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. Become part of a dedicated team that is responsible for quality control in the late development phase with the highest standards of precision and reliability. To strengthen our team in the QC Late Phase, we are looking for a dedicated Scientist Quality Control with focus GC, GC-MS & IC (a), 100% .

Their tasks

  • Monitoring and professional evaluation of GC, GC-MS and IC analyses for approvals, development projects and customer orders
  • Contact person for analytical questions of internal/external customers and authorities
  • Ensuring data quality through technical reviews and second checks
  • Development, validation and transfer of analytical methods according to cGMP requirements
  • Professional cooperation and responsibility in interdisciplinary project teamsProcessing and conclusion of Q-Events (e.g. OOXs, Deviations, Incidents) including cause analysis and documentation
  • Preparation, review and maintenance of QC documents, reports and GMP-relevant documents
  • Continuous optimization of QC processes and promotion of knowledge exchange and quality culture in the team

Your profile

  • Completed studies MSc / PhD in Natural Sciences (FH, Uni)
  • Professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry and in the GMP environment
  • Expertise in the analytical environment in dealing with chromatographic methods, GC, GC-MS & IC desirable
  • Knowledge of Analytical Method Validation & Transfers in the Regulated GMP Environment
  • Strong analytical thinking skills
  • Communicative and well-structured organizational talent
  • Convincing and safe appearance
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
