Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

To strengthen our team, from October 2026 we are looking for a committed and analytically thinking personality as a Trainee Strategic Business 50% for 12 months.

What is this point about?

Are you enthusiastic about strategic issues, the development of long-term customer relationships and the analysis of markets and business potential? Do you want to understand how sustainable growth is achieved through close cooperation with customers and internal departments? Do you like working at the interface of sales, strategy and business development?

Then you could be exactly the person we are looking for. In our trainee program, you will learn the basics of Strategic Account Management and gain insights into the development and implementation of growth strategies for key international customers. You work closely with experienced colleagues and gradually take on your own tasks and projects.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Support experienced strategic account managers in the support and strategic development of key customers.
  • Use modern sales, analysis and collaboration tools to make workflows more efficient and support data-based decisions.
  • Perform market, customer and competitive analyses as well as identify growth and business potential.
  • participate in the creation and implementation of account plans and strategic customer concepts.
  • Support and actively shape strategy meetings and cross-divisional projects.
  • Coordinate activities between sales, service, product management and other internal stakeholders.
  • Create presentations, reports and decision bases as well as get to know Endress+Hauser’s solution, product and service portfolio.

What do we expect from you?

See also

  • have a completed degree in business administration, industrial engineering, economics or a comparable discipline.
  • are interested in strategic sales, business development and the development of long-term customer relationships.
  • think analytically, recognize connections and identify potentials and growth opportunities.
  • work in a structured, self-responsible and solution-oriented manner and gladly assume responsibility.
  • Convincing through communication strength, secure appearance and pleasure in working with different stakeholders.
  • bring a pronounced affinity for digitality and working with the provided AI tools and have very good German and English skills in word and writing.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We value a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working hours and the opportunity for home office
  • We support sustainable mobility offers such as public transport to make it easier for our employees to commute.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications that reach us by e-mail cannot be considered for data protection reasons.
