Full description

Let’s build a healthier future, together! By combining our unique strengths, we are redefining healthcare through sustainable action and innovation. For a behind-the-scenes look, check out this video to see how we at Roche are making personalized health care a reality.

The mySugr GmbH was acquired by Roche in 2017 and our Vienna office is one of Roche's global sites. You will be located here, in the heart of Vienna, no matter if you work on a mySugr®, Accu-Chek®, navify® or any other Roche product or solution. You can read more about the history of mySugr & Roche here .

Here's what we're looking for

We are looking for a Scrum Master who shares our passion for Agile product development and is able to drive it forward, supporting up to two teams in an Agile Release Train (ART). You’ll be working on a chronic care solution, enabling teams to deliver value that really impacts patient care.

We don’t just practice Agile on paper. We constantly reflect, learn, and improve how we work, with the goal of enabling high-performing, focused, and sustainable teams.

You will act as a delivery and team-effectiveness coach, helping teams deliver value, stay focused, improve flow, and collaborate better across the ART.

What you’ll do

  • Help teams deliver value by keeping a clear focus on Sprint Goals, the Team Backlog, and ART priorities.
  • Facilitate decision-making, remove blockers, and make risks and impediments visible, helping the team resolve them early.
  • Support coordination across teams and ART-level ceremonies, especially around dependencies.
  • Use team metrics to learn, reflect, and continuously improve how teams deliver.
  • Improve ways of working by evolving roles, ceremonies, processes, and tools; always aiming to reduce complexity and wasted effort.
  • Encourage experimentation, learning, and adaptation across teams.
  • Coach teams on Agile, Lean, Scrum, Kanban, and SAFe principles, promoting high performance and agile best practices.
  • Help teams build trust, autonomy, and strong collaboration, enabling them to perform effectively and sustainably.
  • Support teams in achieving the right capacity and skills to deliver effectively and contribute to overall ART performance.

Essential Skills & Experience

  • 5+ years of hands-on experience as Scrum Master or Agile Coach.
  • 7+ years working in software product development.
  • Solid experience with Scrum, Kanban, and SAFe (XP is a plus).
  • SAFe 6.0 Certification.
  • Strong interpersonal, problem-solving, communication, facilitation, and change management skills.
  • Comfortable handling team and cross-team conflicts and impediments.
  • Technical background: experience in software development and/or a technical degree.
