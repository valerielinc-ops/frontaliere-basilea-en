Would you like to actively contribute to ensuring that life-changing medicines are reliably manufactured to the highest quality? As an Equipment Operator at Novartis, you are an essential part of our production team and contribute every day to ensuring that our products are manufactured safely, on time and to the highest quality standards. If you enjoy hands-on work, take responsibility and would like to be part of a dedicated team in a dynamic environment, we look forward to receiving your application.

Job Description

Main responsibilities

  • Carrying out manufacturing activities in accordance with the production plan and quality requirements
  • Careful and timely review of batch documentation
  • Cleaning, preparation and disinfection of production equipment and facilities
  • Carrying out sampling, aliquoting and in-process controls
  • Monitoring equipment operation and identifying deviations at an early stage
  • Reporting malfunctions and irregularities to the responsible specialist departments
  • Compliance with quality, safety and environmental regulations in day-to-day production

Minimum requirements

  • Experience in a chemical or pharmaceutical environment or relevant training, ideally as a chemical and pharmaceutical technologist
  • Very good understanding of Good Manufacturing Practice is mandatory
  • Technical understanding and the ability to work independently and reliably according to specifications
  • Experience with process control systems, ideally DeltaV (Emerson) or Siemens control systems
  • Careful, structured and quality-conscious approach to work with a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Business-fluent German and basic English skills

Desired requirements

  • Proficient use of Microsoft Office and process control systems
