The position You will be part of the global Diagnostics Export Control & Sanctions Compliance Team. You are instrumental in ensuring the integrity of our global operations and ensuring the distribution of vital diagnostic products to patients worldwide. This position is globally oriented and requires international interaction and expertise in multinational regulations. We are looking for a dedicated Export Control & Sanctions Manager to ensure compliance at Roche Diagnostics worldwide. You are an experienced expert in the field of export control & sanctions and ready to tackle complex, worldwide challenges and requirements in the field of medical devices and diagnostics?

Responsibility | This is what awaits you As an Export Control & Sanctions Manager, you set guidelines and support compliance worldwide:

  • You are responsible for the timely recording and implementation of export control changes in internal guidelines and solutions.
  • Your focus is on reviewing business partners in SAP GTS across divisions and worldwide.
  • You develop and implement comprehensive, cross-divisional IT solutions, such as SAP GTS, to comply with export control requirements.
  • You will work on the permanent development of the cross-location processes of export control. You are also involved in the development of the internal export control audit program and carry out on-site audits in the affected company units.
  • You plan internal training courses, design training materials and carry out qualification measures. Of course, you are available as a specialist consultancy of the unit and operate best practice sharing.
  • You represent the company’s interests vis-à-vis relevant authorities and associations.

Qualifications | This is what you bring with you

  • You have a degree in law. Alternatively, study business administration, if possible with a focus on supply chain, foreign trade/export.
  • You have at least 5 years of relevant experience in an international group with a focus on foreign trade, export control or customs.
  • Experience in business partner screening is indispensable.
  • You have deep operational knowledge of complex international export control and sanctions regimes, in particular the regulations of the EU, US, CH, UK and CN.
  • Experience in diagnostics, life sciences or medical technology is a strong advantage.
  • Negotiable English in word and writing is mandatory. Knowledge of German B2 an advantage
  • You have strong skills in teamwork and collaboration and can inspire and convince international stakeholders.
