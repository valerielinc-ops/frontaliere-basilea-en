Full description

You love contact with people and keep an overview even in a lively environment? Then you are exactly right with us: You coordinate guest requests, organize reservations and events, create guest information and support in the preparation of events. With your commitment you contribute daily to a smooth process and a first-class guest experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Restaurant Seegarten, Rainstrasse 6, 4142 Münchenstein

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Independent processing and coordination of written inquiries by e-mail, in particular on reservations, events, general guest questions, found objects and internal concerns.
  • First contact person for guests and customers on the phone as well as professional receipt, processing and forwarding of requests to the relevant specialist areas.
  • Support in the organisational preparation of banquets and events (mise-en-place, quality controls, provision of infrastructure and materials).
  • Responsibility for creating and updating labels and information materials, such as daily menus, offer labels and guest information.
  • Situational support of the operational restaurant operation in the guest areas of the restaurant Seegarten to ensure a smooth operation.

Requirements

  • At least 2 years of professional experience: in the commercial sector with regular customer contact.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): basic gastronomic training EFZ or equivalent training.
  • Strong service and customer orientation
  • Organisational capacity and prioritisation
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Communication Strength in Word and Writing
  • Flexibility and readiness
  • Experience in dealing with digital work equipment (MS Office)
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • English (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo
