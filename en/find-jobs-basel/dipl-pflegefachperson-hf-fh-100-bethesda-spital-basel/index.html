Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 17.09.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus topics of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system, rehabilitation and palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

For our surgery / orthopaedics department we are looking from 01. January 2027 or by arrangement a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH (70 - 100%).

Their tasks

  • Control and implementation of the care process
  • Creating a trusting professional relationship with patients and their relatives
  • Coordination and organisation of daily operations
  • Co-responsibility for the process and result quality of the department
  • Active participation in interprofessional case meetings
  • Co-responsibility in the practical support of students and students
  • Electronic documentation and performance measurement

They bring with them

  • Diploma as nurse HF or a completed bachelor's degree in nursing FH
  • A noticeable identification with the guidelines of professional care and the perception of the care process as a core task of nursing trade
  • The ability to form relationships, which is characterized by empathy and affection
  • A strong willingness to learn and perform (digital competence)
  • The ability and willingness to cooperate in the interprofessional treatment team

We offer

  • An innovative field of work focusing on the continuous development of care quality
  • Organizational structures that promote interprofessional cooperation
  • Independent work in collegial teams
  • Promoting personal and professional development through an attractive training programme
  • Access to free sports facilities such as Pilates, Yoga and CrossFit as well as the use of our gym for your health and well-being
