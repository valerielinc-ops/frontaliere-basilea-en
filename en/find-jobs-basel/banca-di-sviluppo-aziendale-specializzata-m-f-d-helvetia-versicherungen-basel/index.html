Bank, Corporate Development / Strategy Busy: 80 - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site We are the bank of Helvetia – an innovative financial services provider that intelligently combines banking and insurance. Setting new standards together and shaping the future – this means more for us than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment in which trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, your initiative and your contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • You shape the future of our company and develop corporate, sales and insurbanking strategies.
  • You will analyze market, customer and company data and derive sound decision-making bases for management.
  • You will continuously develop our unique insurbanking model along the customer journey.
  • You design innovative consulting and sales approaches and tap into new growth and earnings potential.
  • You lead or accompany strategic projects, design organizational development initiatives and actively promote change and transformation projects.
  • You develop key figures, dashboards and reporting for sustainable management of the business.
  • You ensure the effective implementation of strategic initiatives and create understanding and alignment across all levels.

What you bring

  • Completed studies (economics, business administration or comparable training)
  • Several years of professional experience in corporate development, sales development, consulting or controlling with proven experience in the management or support of strategic projects as well as change and transformation projects
  • Very good business and analytical understanding
  • Experience in structured data analysis and in dealing with the creation of key figures, reports and business cases
  • Confident use of presentation tools, MS Excel and other project-oriented tools.
  • High degree of independence and personal responsibility
  • Structured, conceptual working method and high implementation competence
  • Languages: German, English, French (advantageous)
