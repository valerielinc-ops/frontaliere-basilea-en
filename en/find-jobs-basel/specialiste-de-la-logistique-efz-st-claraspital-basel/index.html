About the Claraspital

The Claraspital, the largest private hospital in Basel, is a university teaching and research hospital with a full service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and supra-regional broadcasting of its centers “Bäu” and “Tumor” as well as its special fields. In the Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and humanly.
