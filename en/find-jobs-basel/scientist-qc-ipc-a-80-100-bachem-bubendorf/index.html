Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. In order to strengthen our team in quality control IPC, we are looking for a dedicated Scientist QC - IPC (a) (80 - 100%) immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • Professional leadership and support of the team
  • Testing and release of IPC analysis results
  • Active participation in analytical development projects
  • Competent contact person for analytical questions
  • Preparation, testing and maintenance of QC documents, reports and GMP-relevant documents
  • Critical review of existing processes and processes as well as development of well-founded optimization proposals
  • Independent processing and tracking of quality events (e.g. OOX and Deviations)

Your profile

  • Completed studies MSc / PhD in Natural Sciences (FH, Uni)
  • Expertise in analytical environment with emphasis on chromatographic methods such as (U)HPLC
  • High service orientation as well as convincing and motivating appearance
  • Proactive, solution-oriented and quality-conscious workflow
  • Strong analytical thinking and rapid comprehension
  • Secure handling of common GMP-relevant IT systems (e.g. LIMS, Chromeleon, SAP) as well as high software affinity
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Team-oriented, reliable, flexible and enthusiastic personality

  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
