Your responsibility

  • Together with the lunch table management, they supervise the students on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 11.30 a.m. to 1.45 p.m.
  • They assist in dispensing meals and provide a pleasant and respectful atmosphere during lunch and the lesson-free lunch time.
  • At the lunch table, an average of 20 to 25 students are fed and looked after every day.
  • They approach young people with appreciation, patience and a clear, respectful attitude.

Your background

  • They enjoy dealing with young people and ideally bring experience in their care.
  • They are a reliable, communicative and resilient personality who keeps an overview even in lively situations.
  • They have assertiveness, enjoy working in a team and are actively involved in the design of the lunch table.
