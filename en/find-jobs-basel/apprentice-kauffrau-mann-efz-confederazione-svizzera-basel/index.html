Put to the Point

We are looking for a motivated and talented learner who supports the EJPD in the Asylum Directorate in various commercial areas. You will work in the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) in the administrative building of the Federal Asylum Centre Basel and will be accompanied and supported by our experienced practical trainees. Not only will you have the opportunity to work with other learners and interns during the annual learning camp, but you will also be able to network in various ways.

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: 401-19384
  • Location: Freiburgerstrasse 50, 4057 Basel

You can make this contribution

  • Record personal data, register asylum applications and implement administrative processes in the event of entry and exit
  • Process dossiers, monitor deadlines and update documents in specialist applications
  • Prepare dates and meetings, coordinate and draw up minutes
  • Planning events, internal events and organisational processes
  • Process post, forward requests to competent authorities and make telephone clarifications
  • Control invoices, obtain offers and create working documents and documentation

This makes you unique

  • You are a motivated, ambitious and responsible person
  • You are team capable and have a communication ability
  • You have a high level of learning and commitment.
  • You master the ten finger system
  • You have basic knowledge of MS-Office programs
  • Good school grades (especially German and mathematics)
