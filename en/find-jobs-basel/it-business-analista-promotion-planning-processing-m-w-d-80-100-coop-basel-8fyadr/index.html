Senior IT Business Analyst SAP Retail - Promotion Planning & Processing (m/f/d), 80-100%

Coop Cooperative — Basel, BS, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Coop, Thiersteinerallee 12, 4053 Basel
  • Pensum: 80-100 %

Tasks

  • You will analyze and optimize end-to-end processes in the SAP retail environment and develop suitable solutions together with business and IT
  • You actively shape our SAP-based solutions around promotion planning, volume forecasting, volume consultation, volume distribution and division
  • You will analyze SAP processes, data flows and interfaces, especially in the environment of SAP CAR/PMR, and develop independent solution concepts
  • You question professional requirements and translate them into clear features, user stories and acceptance criteria
  • You work closely with specialist departments and development teams and accompany the implementation and quality assurance

Requirements

  • Several years of practical experience in the business analysis and further development of SAP-based business processes
  • In-depth SAP knowledge, ideally in SAP Retail, in particular merchandise management, procurement and allocation tables
  • Experience with SAP CAR/PMR as well as quantity processing and allocation is an advantage
  • Good technical understanding of SAP processes, data flows and interfaces
  • Experience with agile working methods, requirements engineering as well as Jira and Confluence
  • Analytical thinking skills, initiative and an independent, solution-oriented working method
  • Education or study in (business) information technology or comparable professional experience
  • Discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV)
  • Working time: 5 weeks holidays, flexible working time models
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company runs
