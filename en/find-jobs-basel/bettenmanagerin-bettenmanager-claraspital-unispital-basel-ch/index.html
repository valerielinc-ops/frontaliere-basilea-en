Full description

Tasks

  • You plan, coordinate and control elective and emergency entries as well as internal relocations and ensure optimal bed occupancy
  • They coordinate outpatient and inpatient operations (including entries on the day of operation) in close cooperation with the surgical management and the day surgical clinic
  • You are responsible for planning inpatient chemotherapies at the Claraspital site and ensure a smooth process
  • They carry out transfer rapports, participate in interdisciplinary rapports and work closely with doctors, nurses, case management, emergency surgery, as well as internal and external assignees
  • As a central contact person, you advise patients, external customers and internal departments in all questions regarding appointments, admissions and bed occupancy
  • They collect and analyze occupancy indicators, create reports for executives, ensure correct data maintenance in the CIS systems and participate in sector-related projects Requirements
  • You have training as a qualified nurse HF/FH as well as several years of professional experience in an acute hospital, ideally supplemented by experience in bed management
  • They have a high affinity for organizational and coordinative tasks and are characterized by a quick understanding as well as an efficient, goal-oriented and solution-oriented working method.
  • They communicate appropriately to the addressee, enjoy working interdisciplinary and convince through pronounced cooperation skills as well as enjoying a central interface function
  • They act service-oriented, treat people with appreciation and have a high level of social competence in dealing with different stakeholders.
  • Even in demanding situations, you remain sovereign, resilient and flexible and take on responsibility with a strong quality awareness
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing, good user knowledge in MS Office and ideally experience with CIS systems
