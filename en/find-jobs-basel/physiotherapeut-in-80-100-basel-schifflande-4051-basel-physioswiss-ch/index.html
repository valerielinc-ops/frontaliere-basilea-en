We are creating a firm framework for this:

  • Time for careful documentation: For a full working day of 8.5 hours, 30 minutes of documentation time are scheduled daily – within your working hours.
  • A team that exchanges: Once a month, a team meeting takes place. Here we clarify common topics, develop processes further and give your ideas space.
  • A personal interview each month: In the one-on-one with your manager, you can discuss concerns, receive feedback and plan your development together.
  • Advancing professionally: monthly internal training offers space for new findings and their implementation in everyday therapy.
  • Reflection on treatment issues together: In a monthly patient meeting, we exchange perspectives and discuss therapeutic challenges.
  • Two team days a year: time to strengthen our cooperation and work together on the further development of our team.
  • 28 holiday days per year at full time: so that recreation also has its permanent place.
  • Good employment conditions and a bonus model: We will explain the terms and conditions for the bonus in a personal conversation.

We want to develop our practice together. For this, your professional view, your questions and your ideas are welcome. Good therapy needs skill. And people who think ahead. You want to be able to accompany people attentively, treat them in a technically sound manner and recognize what your therapy is doing? Are you looking for a team in which you can get involved, learn and help shape something? Then we want to get to know you. At Helvetic Health, we are looking for a motivated physiotherapist who combines empathy with professional standards. A centrally located practice awaits you at the Basel Schifflände with treatment rooms, training areas and a secretariat. What is important to us in therapy We want to align our treatment with the best available scientific evidence and combine it with clinical experience and the needs of our patients. This includes asking questions, questioning procedures and applying new findings in a meaningful way. We want to make progress traceable. Objective findings, suitable measurement procedures and regular monitoring help us to do this. Our company is also developing. We want to use technical devices and digital tools where they improve therapy, documentation and collaboration. For this we are looking for people who remain curious and want to shape this development. Your contribution

  • You listen, meet patients with empathy and develop realistic therapy goals together with them.
  • You independently carry out findings, plan treatments and accompany the course of therapy.
  • You develop individual exercise programs and support people to become active themselves.
  • You document carefully and check treatment results against appropriate criteria.
  • You share knowledge, contribute ideas and help develop our team and our processes.

What you bring You are motivated, responsible and enjoy your profession. You want to integrate into a team, learn from others and at the same time contribute to the professional and human development of the team. You are interested in evidence-based physiotherapy, objective findings and the further development of our profession. Dealing with technical devices and digital aids gives you pleasure

  • You like to get to know new applications.

You bring a Swiss physiotherapy degree. In the case of a foreign degree, a completed SRC recognition is mandatory. You don’t have to have a ready answer to every question. We want someone who works carefully, is open to feedback and pursues technical questions.
