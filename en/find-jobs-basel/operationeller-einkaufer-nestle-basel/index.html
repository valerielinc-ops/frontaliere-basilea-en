Summary of entity

Fabrik Basel is a high-performance production company of Nestlé Suisse S.A. and produces THOMY culinary products for domestic and international use. For our engineering department, we are looking for a committed person as a technical:n buyer:in (m/f). You are responsible for the operational and administrative procurement of technical products and services. They ensure that materials and services are procured on time, economically and in the required quality

A day in the life of a technical:n buyer:in 50 %

  • Obtaining, comparing and evaluating offers based on technical specifications
  • Carrying out price and contract negotiations with suppliers
  • Creation and processing of orders in SAP
  • Monitoring delivery dates and ensuring timely material availability
  • Commercial management of the entire procurement process
  • Checking and processing of order confirmations, delivery notes and invoices
  • Maintenance of supplier and material master data in the SAP system
  • Support for supplier evaluations and supplier developments
  • Processing of complaints and deviations in cooperation with the specialist departments
  • Coordination and cooperation with technology, production, project management and finance
  • Participation in the optimization of purchasing processes and procurement strategies in coordination with our central purchasing department
  • Ensuring compliance with internal requirements, quality standards and compliance guidelines

What makes you successful

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (technical or commercial) or many years of experience in a similar position
  • Advantageously good SAP knowledge
  • Advantageously basic technical training or technical flair
  • Proactive organizational talent with a high service orientation
  • Good negotiating skills with suppliers
  • Very good knowledge of German, French and English is advantageous

What we offer

  • A varied and responsible activity
  • Flexible working hours within a 50% pension
  • Modern work equipment and contemporary working conditions
  • Collegial and appreciative working environment
  • Possibility for active participation and optimization of purchasing processes
  • Attractive employment conditions
