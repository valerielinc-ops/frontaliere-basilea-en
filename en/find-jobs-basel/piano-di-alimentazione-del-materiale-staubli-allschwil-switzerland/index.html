Full description

As a Material Supply Planner, you are responsible for the holistic scheduling and planning and control of materials along the supply chain with the aim of ensuring high material availability, identifying risks at an early stage and continuously optimizing the relevant KPIs such as OTIF, inventory and warehouse handling. Their main tasks include:

  • Disposition (quantities and dates) of the assigned materials in SAP along the supply chain
  • Planning and control of material, procurement and manufacturing contracts in SAP under ongoing monitoring of requirements, inventories and deadlines to ensure material availability
  • Active scheduling and prioritisation and taking action in the event of deviations, bottlenecks or risks
  • Coordination with suppliers and production as well as close cooperation with strat. Purchasing, QM, Customer Service, Finance and other interfaces
  • Maintenance and further development of master and planning data in SAP
  • Early identification of risks and supply bottlenecks as well as coordination of necessary adjustments with supply planning and participation in the monitoring and improvement of relevant KPIs (e.g. OTIF, inventory handling, complaints)
  • KPI: co-responsibility for the monitoring, analysis and continuous improvement of the relevant key figures in the area of responsibility, in particular warehouse value, warehouse handling, customer delivery reliability (OTIF)
  • Commercial education or basic technical education with commercial education
  • Sound experience in material management, material planning or operational purchasing
  • Experience in a manufacturing company is necessary
  • Accustomed to working process-oriented
  • Very good user knowledge in SAP (module PP and MM), further modules are advantageous
  • German negotiable in word and writing, English and French of advantage
  • Analytical thinking and pragmatic action
  • Enjoy working in a lively environment with a diverse range of tasks This is what we offer:
  • A globally active Swiss family company with a very good market position and innovative high-performance solutions
  • An appreciative company culture and motivated employees who meet the daily challenges with you
  • The advantages of a medium-sized company as well as that of an international group
  • The opportunity to participate in future-oriented technologies
  • Many other benefits such as flexible working hours, diverse internal training, parking, additional bridge days, modern workplaces and much more People, Passion, Performance, Partnership are our values that we live.
