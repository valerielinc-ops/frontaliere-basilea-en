Full description

Admission by appointment · Bruderholz, Liestal, Care · Job reference 13532 · Permanent employment

Tasks

We are not just looking for someone to coordinate processes. We are looking for a personality that leads people, plans resources ahead and makes development possible. As head of CNO staff, you design the nursing springer pool and the CNO office, develop educational offers and set new impulses for volunteer service. It's versatile. Demanding too. And that's why we're looking for you.

  • You lead and develop the Springerpool and the CNO office and give both units professional and organizational orientation
  • You are responsible for the operational planning of both units and ensure reliable, needs-based processes
  • You coordinate the Springerpool’s human resources and keep an overview even in the event of changing needs
  • You develop the training centre further, expand its offerings and integrate innovative forms of learning with virtual reality (VR)
  • You will design, coordinate and develop the pre- and onboarding processes for new nursing and therapies employees and ensure a structured start
  • You design the volunteer service and develop its offers and structures in a targeted manner.

Requirements

  • Well-founded: You bring practical experience with pool systems
  • Organized: You are experienced in administrative activities, planning systems and demanding appointment coordination – outpatient, stationary and external
  • Educational Strong: You plan, coordinate and develop further education and training offers
  • Practical: You have experience in vocational training at secondary and tertiary level
  • You are a qualified nurse with more than five years of professional experience. Leadership experience is beneficial
  • Personal: You think analytically, act reflectively and innovatively and bring assertiveness
