The Opportunity

As a Senior Associate - Genetics & Genomics Lab Expert, you play a pivotal role in accelerating early drug development within our Pharmaceutical Sciences team. This role serves as a vital bridge between high-throughput advanced wet-lab molecular biology and data-driven laboratory operations. You will spend your days executing hands-on laboratory work and leveraging modern digital tools to maximize scientific insights and advance our global therapeutic pipeline.

In this role, you will

  • Develop, validate, and implement advanced genetics and genomics-based biomarker assays (including epigenetics, gene expression signatures, and circulating DNA/RNA) to characterize disease mechanisms.
  • Evaluate and apply cutting-edge genomics technologies , such as single-cell sequencing, spatial-omics, and digital droplet PCR (ddPCR), to maximize biological insights from precious, low-volume samples.
  • Drive digital integration by utilizing advanced digital lab infrastructure, automation tools, and electronic lab notebooks (ELNs) to ensure high-fidelity data tracking.
  • Collaborate within an agile ecosystem , partnering seamlessly across matrixed project teams with project leads, pathologists, and computational scientists to turn samples into actionable insights.
  • Facilitate technology transfer by leading the robust technical delivery of validated methodologies to external collaboration partners and Contract Research Organizations (CROs).
  • Inspire and mentor other scientists, associates, and interns, fostering a laboratory culture of continuous improvement, curiosity, and learning agility.

Who You Are

We are looking for an engaged scientific professional who thrives at the intersection of laboratory science and digital innovation.

To be successful in this role, you bring

  • Education & Experience: A Master’s degree in Molecular Biology, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Genomics, or a related life-sciences field, alongside solid hands-on experience in biomarker assay development and a minimum of 3 years of working experience.
  • Technical Expertise: Deep proficiency in molecular biology techniques (qPCR, ddPCR) and next-generation sequencing (NGS) platforms, with a strong interest in executing high-quality experimental workflows.
