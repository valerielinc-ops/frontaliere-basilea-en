Full description

As a leading real estate service provider, we offer our customers a comprehensive range of services in the area of center and mixed-use site management for retail properties and sites.

From commercial and technical management, through marketing and commercialization, to interim use and community management.

To support our team in Basel, we are looking for a dynamic and professionally qualified person, starting on an agreed date, as

Center Management Administrator (m/f/d)

Basel (Stückipark), 80-100%

Your responsibilities

  • You support the commercial management and the Real Estate Site Manager with all administrative matters within the portfolio under your care, as well as with the leasing of spaces for various uses
  • Telephone and written contact with tenants, caretakers, tradespeople and authorities is also part of your area of responsibility
  • You review applications, are responsible for obtaining references and preparing rental agreements for ancillary properties
  • You plan and conduct apartment viewings and handle the follow-up
  • You check, code and process various payments, recharge accounts payable invoices and prepare accounts receivable invoices
  • You prepare and monitor reports, such as sales reports and parking revenues

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed commercial vocational training and bring at least 3 years of commercial work experience (further training and experience in the real estate sector are an advantage)
  • You have polished German and English skills, both written and spoken (spoken French is an advantage)
  • You have good knowledge of MS Office applications
  • You are a team player and work independently and in a structured manner
  • Your flexible, resilient and customer-oriented personality with a great deal of initiative sets you apart

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We embrace mobile working with individualized (annual) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% working from home is possible. We place great emphasis on work-life balance, because balanced and well-rested employees are important to us. Purchasing additional vacation is possible.
  • Mobility and meals: You receive a contribution toward your mobile phone. We also make a contribution toward your meals.
  • Education and professional development: Supporting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support for internal and external education and professional development. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project roles
  • Salary: We offer market-competitive compensation with absolute pay equality for all genders. Pension fund benefits are attractive, and you can choose the model that suits you from various options. Accident insurance covers the private hospital ward.

You can find more information about our benefits on our careers page.
