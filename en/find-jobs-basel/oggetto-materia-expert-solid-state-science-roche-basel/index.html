Therapeutic Modalities (TMo) organization, accountable for translating disease biology into high-quality drug candidates across small molecules, large molecules, oligonucleotides, and genomic medicines. As Subject Matter Expert (SME) you will lead the identification of the optimal solid-state form, ensuring that early-stage candidates possess the necessary solubility and stability to succeed in the clinic. You will serve as the central hub, coordinating complex screening and characterization activities across internal labs and global external partners. You will lead cross-functional efforts to de-risk drug candidates and define the "Golden Form" that carries our therapies from the lab to the patient.

The opportunity

  • Scientific Leadership: Act as the single point of accountability for the solid-state strategy of assigned projects, ensuring the selection of optimal salt, polymorph, and particle forms to support both stability and bioavailability.
  • End-to-End Ownership: Assume full responsibility for the lifecycle management of materials, from the initial integration of biopharmaceutical assessments to the final definition of robust crystallization processes.
  • Cross-Functional Integration: Orchestrate the interface between Chemistry, Analytics, and Formulation teams to harmonize the material science narrative throughout the drug development lifecycle.
  • Strategic Oversight: Spearhead the evaluation, technical vetting, and selection of global external CRO/CMO partners. You will orchestrate these collaborations, governing the seamless exchange of scientific methodologies while ensuring data generation meets our rigorous standards for quality and project milestones. You will manage the balance between internal technical services and external outsourcing partners, ensuring high-quality data generation and proactive risk mitigation.
  • Innovation Driver: Champion the adoption of digitalization and automated screening tools to enhance data analysis throughput and accelerate project cycle times.

Who you are

  • Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Chemistry, Material Science, or a related field, with a strong focus on solid-state characterization and crystallization.
  • Deep understanding of solid-state analytical techniques (e.g., XRD, DSC, TGA, DVS, Microscopy) and their application in a pharmaceutical development context.
  • Proven ability to lead complex projects in a matrix environment, with the communication skills necessary to translate technical data into strategic decisions for non-expert stakeholders.
