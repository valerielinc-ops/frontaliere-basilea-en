A natural leader and coach with a strong entrepreneurial and enterprise mindset, you will architect an environment that enables growth, fast decision-making, and cross-functional collaboration in a complex and volatile landscape. You will thrive in a fast-paced, high-performing organization, empowering your team to reach ambitious targets while partnering with peers to deliver overarching business goals. Acting as a central integrator across functions, you will ensure alignment with global and regional strategies, manage external stakeholders and legal partners, oversee contract and litigation processes, drive risk assessment, support audits, and coordinate with authorities and institutions.
