Additional responsibilities include implementing global Roche application standards, maintaining rigorous case reporting and follow-up protocols, and collaborating with the sales division to identify and capitalize on commercial opportunities.

Your Responsibilities

  • Provide high-quality, consistent application support aligned with evolving client requirements and strategic business objectives.
  • Deliver industry-leading support utilizing established systems and methodology to maintain high client satisfaction standards.

  • Conduct formal system commissioning, technical configuration and user instruction.

Who You are
