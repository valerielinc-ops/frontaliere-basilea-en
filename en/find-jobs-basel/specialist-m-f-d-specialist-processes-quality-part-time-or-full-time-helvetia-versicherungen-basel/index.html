Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the operational processing of withholding taxes
  • You will analyze claims, credits and open positions, coordinate the necessary coordination with the debt collection management and the relevant internal bodies and ensure a timely adjustment.
  • You supervise technically defined processes in the collection and operation environment and support in the preparation of decisions.
  • You coordinate the SUVA statistic dossier and are the central contact person for its preparation and submission.
  • You carry out internal controls, document them in a comprehensible manner and support leaders in audits and the implementation of the defined measures
  • You check reservations, case closures and the quality of data stocks.
  • You ensure that data protection requirements and authorization concepts are adhered to.
  • You participate in the further development of quality standards, key figures and control mechanisms.

Requirements

  • Basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in the insurance, financial or social insurance environment
  • Several years of experience in handling administrative, financial or insurance business cases
  • Understanding of Accident and Sickness Processes and Relationships
  • Experience with quality assurance, internal control systems or data protection topics
  • You work structured, carefully and reliably. In addition, you take responsibility for your topics and consistently bring them to a conclusion
  • You think analytically and networked and bring a high number, data and system affinity
  • You recognize optimization potential and implement improvements pragmatically.
  • You communicate clearly and enjoy working with different stakeholders.

We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
